# A Frozen 12B Beats Frontier Models on Verified Work: 100% Accuracy, 0 Tokens, Bit-Exact, Forever

> [← Daily summary](../2026-07-28.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2607.23806) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23806)


## One-sentence takeaway

The report claims that frozen open models can deterministically execute previously verified, parameterized methods at zero generation tokens, but its proprietary implementation, narrow family-based evaluation, and unmatched frontier comparison limit independent validation and generalization.

## Problem and motivation

The report targets recurring, verifiable work that language-model deployments repeatedly regenerate despite having solved equivalent problems before. The author argues that this wastes tokens and energy while retaining nondeterminism, complicating audits and reproducibility (§1).

The identified gap is a system combining exact memory selection, independent answer-key-free verification before storage, bit-exact reuse, and zero generation tokens (§2.9). Cold-start reasoning and autonomous discovery of new knowledge are explicitly outside the claimed scope (§§2.8, 4).

## Approach and contributions

- **Verified method memory:** Galahad stores parameterized solutions only after an independent, domain-specific gate accepts them; subsequent inputs from the same family trigger fresh execution of the stored method rather than answer replay (§§2.1, 2.6).
- **Deterministic storage and addressing:** The proprietary Merlin layer provides byte-exact uniqueness, exact content addressing, crash-safe storage, and bounded failure behavior (§§2.6–2.7).
- **Model-independent reuse study:** The same memory regime is evaluated across four frozen open models, including dense and mixture-of-experts architectures (§2.2).
- **Composition and movable context:** The report tests combinations of stored methods, merged knowledge items, and a storage-backed movable context window (§§2.4, 2.4b, 2.10).
- **Verification beyond numeric computation:** It reports formal-proof checking, consistency-gated reasoning, reasoning-method transfer, and preliminary factual-claim filtering (§2.5).
- **Public evidence interface:** A rate-limited testbench is offered for observing selected behavioral claims, while raw artifacts and reproduction details remain available only under NDA (§5; Appendix A).

## Evidence and main results

| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| Perfect reuse on the core battery | 180/180 fresh parameter instances across nine stored families, with 0 generation tokens per answer; repeated for each of four frozen models | §§2.1–2.2; Figures 2, 4 |
| One-time deposit amortization | Deposit required 16,579 generation tokens; assuming 500–1,000 tokens for each regenerated answer, computed break-even is 17–34 queries | §2.1; Figure 3 |
| Low measured reuse energy | 6.3–6.5 Wh for 180 answers per model, approximately 36 mWh/answer; self-sourced solve-and-verify campaign used 81.1 Wh | §2.2b; Figure 5 |
| Capability attribution to memory | Full memory produced 180/180; empty memory solved nothing. An externally authored stored method yielded 30/30 fresh instances at 0 generation tokens | §2.3 |
| Composition of stored methods | Compound tasks scored 5/5 at 0 generation tokens; removing one required item reduced performance to 0/5 | §2.4 |
| Limited merged-context composition | Up to nine small items were tested; seven were reached and correctly named under an output cap. Six items formed a 14,134-token context, while one two-item cross-domain computation produced the correct 44 N result | §2.4b |
| Gated reasoning and transfer | Consistency-gated reuse scored 88/88 across four models; method transfer scored 77/80; 63/66 measured deposits passed on their first attempt | §2.5; Figure 4 |
| Preliminary factual gate | Rejected 3/3 fabricated claims and accepted 5/6 genuine claims against a grounded corpus | §2.5 |
| Fast reuse and exact selection | Restore-and-reverify median was 6–23 ms by model; selection was 1.4 µs at the 95th percentile on a nine-item bench | §§2.5, 2.7; Figures 1, 6 |
| Exact versus lossy retrieval | Exact addressing had zero collisions over 4,500 items; the deliberately lossy approximate matcher chose a wrong neighbor for 94.3% | §2.7; Figure 8 |
| Storage and execution controls | Exactly 2,000 unique items retained from 6,000 candidates; 14/14 integrity guarantees passed; trusted execution was 93.9× faster and byte-identical on 140/140 cases | §§2.6–2.7 |
| Large movable stored context | On one 46 GB GPU, a six-million-token stored window retrieved 5/5 probes in 0.55–0.59 s with 263 MB GPU-memory drift | §2.10; Figure 9 |
| Serving-engine comparison | On the same GPU, vLLM stopped at 30,399 tokens and SGLang reportedly truncated beyond roughly 32,000; both used Qwen2.5-7B rather than the report’s 12B model | §2.10; Figure 9 |

These results support the author’s claim that, within the tested stored families, verified methods can be reused deterministically and cheaply across several frozen models. The negative and removal controls strengthen attribution to memory.

They do **not** establish superior cold-start reasoning, general accuracy on unseen problem families, global attention over millions of tokens, or direct quality superiority over frontier models. The frontier comparison is primarily architectural and economic: no frontier API was run on the same 180-instance battery (§2.8).

## Limitations and validity risks

**Author-stated limitations**

- Architecture, algorithms, configuration, and detailed methodology are intentionally omitted; raw artifacts and the reproduction environment are restricted under NDA (Introduction; Appendix A).
- Evaluation concerns solved-and-verified families, not novel cold-start reasoning, where the author acknowledges frontier models perform better (§§2.8, 4).
- Merged-context testing stops at nine small items or six larger items totaling 14,134 tokens; computation across merged items is demonstrated for two (§4).
- The six-million-token result is a movable bounded window, not global attention (§§2.10, 4).
- vLLM and SGLang used a different 7B model because they reportedly could not load the 12B architecture (§2.10).
- Large public-benchmark sweeps, production hit-rate measurements, and full held-out factual-gate evaluation are future work (§§4, 6).

**Analyst inferences**

- The unnamed nine problem families and absent instance-generation procedure prevent assessing difficulty, leakage risk, family diversity, or how far test parameters depart from deposited examples.
- The approximate-retrieval result cannot justify a general conclusion about vector retrieval: the matcher, representation, tuning, and query construction are undisclosed, and it is described as deliberately lossy (§2.7).
- “100% accuracy” is confined to 180 within-family instances; no confidence intervals, repeated batteries, or broader held-out family tests are reported.
- Cross-model invariance may chiefly measure execution of stored procedures, not equivalent reasoning capability, because the empty-memory condition solves nothing (§2.3).
- The frontier comparison lacks paired execution on identical tasks and hardware, while claims about API nondeterminism, latency, and verification are not experimentally evaluated here (§2.8).
- Hashes establish artifact identity, not experimental correctness; restricted artifacts and absent implementation details substantially constrain reproducibility (Appendix A).

## Open research questions

1. **Does reuse generalize beyond narrow parameter shifts?**  
   The paper tests new values within known logical families, not structurally altered or unseen families. A next experiment could stratify held-out inputs by increasing structural distance from each deposited method and report accuracy and gate failures.

2. **How does exact selection compare with strong retrieval baselines?**  
   Only an undisclosed deliberately lossy matcher is evaluated. A controlled study could compare exact addressing with tuned dense, sparse, and hybrid retrieval on the same store, including realistic paraphrased queries and latency–error tradeoffs.

3. **What is the system-wide value under real traffic?**  
   Break-even is arithmetic based on assumed per-query token ranges, while production family hit rates are unknown. A prospective traffic study could measure match frequency, deposit cost, fallback frequency, latency, and total energy over time.

4. **How reliable are verification gates outside executable tasks?**  
   The factual gate uses only nine claims, and open-ended correctness is represented mainly by consistency acceptance. A preregistered held-out evaluation could measure false acceptance and rejection against independently adjudicated factual and reasoning datasets.

## Confidence and missing information

**Confidence: medium.** The supplied text is complete and unusually explicit about reported outcomes and boundaries, but independent assessment is limited by the proprietary, input–output-only reporting format.

Material missing information includes the nine task families and their instances, sampling and randomization procedures, full verification specifications, matcher and baseline configurations, hardware and software details, statistical uncertainty, raw outputs, failure cases, frontier evaluations on matched tasks, public-benchmark results, and unrestricted reproduction artifacts.
