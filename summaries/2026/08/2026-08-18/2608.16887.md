# An Empirical Study of Training Pixel-Space Text-to-Image Diffusion Models

> [← Daily summary](../2026-08-18.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.16887) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16887)


## One-sentence takeaway

The authors find that direct large-scale pixel-space pre-training converges more slowly than matched latent-space training, but latent-to-pixel post-training combined with progressive patch enlargement and step distillation yields competitive text-to-image quality with reported 3.18×–4.75× inference speedups.

## Problem and motivation

Pixel-space diffusion avoids VAE reconstruction loss and decoding latency, yet practical large-scale training recipes remain underdeveloped: prior work largely concerns class-conditioned or smaller-scale settings. The paper asks how pixel- and latent-space models compare under matched large-scale training and how to make a pixel-space generator competitive in both quality and inference efficiency (§1).

## Approach and contributions

- **Controlled pre-training study:** Pixel- and latent-space models share the Z-Image backbone, conditioning, sequence length, corpus of more than 20B image–text pairs, and training configuration; only prediction space changes (§3; Appendix A.1).
- **Latent-to-pixel recipe:** Pre-train in latent space, then fully post-train in RGB space using latent weights, a 1:1 mixture of source-model-generated and real images, x-prediction, a lightweight DiP decoder, and noise scale \(\gamma=2\) (§4; Appendix A.2).
- **Efficiency optimization:** Progressively adapt patch size from ps16 to ps32, then apply Decoupled-DMD and DMDR step distillation to reach four function evaluations (§5; Appendix A.2).
- **Cross-family validation:** Apply the recipe to Z-Image and FLUX2-klein, comparing against corresponding latent models and prior latent-to-pixel methods (§6).

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Author-stated qualification:** The resolution-based argument for \(\gamma=8\) assumes average pooling and ignores differences between VAE latents and RGB statistics; empirical calibration instead favors \(\gamma=2\) (§4.5).
- **Author-observed trade-offs:** Synthetic-only training inherits source-model and VAE artifacts; direct ps32 training introduces local artifacts, and ps64 loses detail and benchmark performance (§§4.2, 5.1).
- **Analyst inference:** Generalization evidence is limited to Z-Image and FLUX2-klein, with the empirical study centered primarily on Z-Image (§6).
- **Analyst inference:** Hardware comparisons are not fully uniform: Table 1 and Table 3 cite H800, whereas Figure 12 cites H100. This complicates comparison across different reported latency results, though within-result comparisons may remain controlled.
- **Analyst inference:** The retrieved text omits table cells, curve values, training duration, final mixed-data scale, and most compute details, preventing effect-size verification and full reproduction.
- **Analyst inference:** No human-preference, safety, demographic, robustness, or deployment evaluation is described in the supplied text; quality evidence centers on automated benchmarks and qualitative examples.
- **Analyst inference:** The component studies mainly report one-factor comparisons; interactions among data composition, decoder, prediction target, noise scale, and patch adaptation are not established.

## Open research questions

1. **Does the recipe generalize beyond the two tested model families?** Evidence covers Z-Image and FLUX2-klein only. A next experiment could apply the fixed recipe to additional architectures and scales without retuning, reporting per-benchmark quality and latency.

2. **Which representation property causes slower pixel-space pre-training?** The VAE-as-structured-representation explanation is explicitly a hypothesis (§3). A next experiment could separately control dimensionality, spatial resolution, frequency content, and learned versus fixed compression.

3. **How do recipe components interact?** The paper varies major choices largely one at a time. A factorial ablation over initialization, data mixture, prediction target, decoder, and \(\gamma\) could reveal dependencies and whether the full recipe is necessary.

4. **Where is the patch-size frontier across resolutions and content types?** ps32 is favorable at 1024×1024, while ps64 degrades detail. A next experiment could test intermediate or adaptive patching across resolutions, with typography and fine-detail subsets reported separately.

## Confidence and missing information

**Confidence: medium.** The complete retrieved prose provides clear methods, settings, and directional findings, but the numeric contents of Tables 1–5 and plotted curves are unavailable. Material missing information includes most benchmark scores, uncertainty or repeated-run statistics, exact training budgets and dataset composition, final post-training set size and duration, detailed latency breakdowns, human evaluation, safety evaluation, and any explicit release status for models, code, or data.
