# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-13

## Executive summary

- Agent research dominates the day, especially persistent state, external skill or harness evolution, validation-gated autonomy, and long-horizon safety evaluation.
- OpenART (`2608.00677`) reports that environment evolution exposes delayed safety failures missed by instruction-only attacks; across converted trajectories, the median propagation distance was 37 actions.
- Test-time structure helps fixed models in two distinct settings: AI4AI (`2608.12307`) raises GPT-5.4-mini’s macro-average Theory-of-Mind accuracy from 0.488 to as high as 0.912, while structured composition (`2608.12836`) raises Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct Macro-F1 from 48.3 to 77.0 on Logical-CommonsenseQA-HV.
- Persistent representations improve both creative and embodied systems: StateFlow (`2608.12314`) maintains editable object-centric 3D scenes, while AtlasVLA (`2608.06729`) combines spatial and task-progress memories for wrist-camera manipulation.
- Several results expose long-horizon brittleness. In narrative interaction, the strongest tested model preserved only 42% of environments through 20 adversarial turns (`2608.08160`); the visual-tool audit (`2608.06270`) finds many crop-and-zoom calls causally inactive or poorly scheduled.
- Autonomous project work is increasingly validation-gated: Spark-to-Paper (`2608.11924`), Mechanist (`2608.12036`), Genesis (`2608.10450`), and AutoWorldModel-Bench (`2608.11216`) all organize iterative work around persistent artifacts, experiments, and acceptance checks.
- Efficiency gains come with important boundaries: C3PO (`2608.09805`) uses nearly the same FLOPs as GRPO but roughly 1.5× the wall time; Ready Cohorts (`2608.12123`) measures scheduling opportunity and a GPU mechanism benefit but does not demonstrate end-to-end service acceleration.
- Some apparently strong findings remain narrow: ToolHazard (`2608.11878`) is supported only by its abstract; MBA (`2608.11616`) relies chiefly on one automated judge; LDR (`2608.09926`) is evaluated on simple simulated ball dynamics; ReRound (`2608.11045`) covers small weight-only-quantized LLMs.

## Main results worth noting

- `2608.00677`: Under DeepSeek-V4-Pro and matched attack budgets, full EMHA achieved 94.7% Strict ASR versus 81.6% for instruction-only and 92.5% for workspace-only evolution. Across 10,000 converted trajectories, unsafe output appeared a median 37 actions after propagation began (IQR 20–66).

- `2608.11924`: On the same 36 seeded fabrication probes, detection was 14% (5/36; 95% Wilson interval [6,29]) for single-pass generation, 69% (25/36; [53,82]) with gates, 81% (29/36; [65,90]) with self-review, and 92% (33/36; [78,97]) with the full stack. Full runs averaged 11.9M tokens, $8.1, and 3.2 hours per manuscript across eight topics.

- `2608.12307`: Across 57 GPT-5.4-mini-target runs, mean ToM macro accuracy rose from 0.488 to 0.763; the best harness reached 0.912, below the 0.939 human-designed reference. The best scaffold fixed 1,717 baseline errors and broke 105 correct answers over 3,900 test items.

- `2608.12036`: On 133 LabSafety-Bench questions, unsafe-response rate was 48.6% for a student trained on filtered outputs from an unsafe teacher, versus 20.3% untuned and 18.3% with a regular teacher. This is author-reported behavioral transfer under text-only, safety-filtered training—not evidence of real-world laboratory harm.

- `2608.05604`: At the default 1K-skill SkillsBench setting, SkillZip achieved 3.46× compression, 1,941 rendered tokens, 99.2 dependency-preservation rate, 98.7 verifier reachability, and 33.3 reward. Matched-ratio text compression achieved 65.0, 60.0, and 25.5 on the latter three measures.

- `2608.08160`: GPT-5.2 averaged 32.92 turns but only 9.94% trajectory progress and 11.22% satisfied commitments. Across all 600 model–environment runs, only 3.5% reached the 100-turn cap without explicit conflict, and most still failed to satisfy every commitment.

- `2608.12314`: StateFlow’s video-level overall user/MLLM scores were 4.5/4.0, versus 4.1/3.3 for its VLM-only camera ablation and 3.6/2.1 for Seedance2. These are 5-point perceptual ratings, not direct geometric-accuracy measurements.

- `2608.10708`: Self-Geometry improved VGGT on ETH3D from 0.20/0.76 to 0.27/0.83 AUC@3/AUC@30, but reduced VGGT AUC@3 on HiRoom from 0.51 to 0.47 and unposed geometry F1 from 0.60 to 0.55. The mixed results argue for an adaptation-confidence gate.

- `2608.11350`: On the 231-question ESI-Bench subset, SHAPER reached 49.8% micro and 42.9% macro accuracy versus 32.5%/31.2% for the seed. Category effects were uneven: Specular Reflection rose 20.0%→60.0%, while Action Sequencing fell 40.0%→20.0% on only five questions.

- `2608.11216`: Mean state-model lift was +0.056 at horizon 1, +0.205 at horizon 10, and +0.215 at horizon 20 across 64 sessions. Editorially, this supports a long-horizon benefit within the structured-state benchmark, not downstream planning competence.

- `2608.11878`: Only qualitative claims are supportable: the authors report that attack timing and placement matter and that generated alignment data improves security on ToolHazard-Bench and AgentDojo while preserving benign utility. No numerical comparison can be responsibly reproduced.

- `2608.11562`: S2R-Removal obtained 28.84 dB PSNR, 0.859 SSIM, and 0.9740 temporal consistency on 60 S2R-Ref sequences. DSIT/RDNet had higher SSIM at 0.870, so S2R does not lead every metric.

- `2608.09926`: At 256² joint training, LDR’s average position error changed 0.050 ID→0.068 OOD; DiT-S changed 0.086→0.592. The result concerns 29-frame prediction in five simple simulated physics tasks.

- `2608.06270`: Online random-crop corruption left DeepEyes nearly unchanged on V* (83.3% clean versus 83.8% corrupted), but sharply reduced Mini-o3 (87.8%→23.6%) and Qwen3-VL-8B (91.1%→30.4%). This reveals different causal dependence on returned pixels, not a simple quality ranking.

- `2608.11616`: Against GPT-5 mini, MBA-k gained 0.413 Innovativeness, 0.317 Competitive Advantage, and 0.676 Market Size under the fixed InternVL2.5-78B judge, while losing 0.020 Specificity and 0.067 Technical Validity. All six differences survived within-comparator Holm correction.

- `2608.10450`: Genesis’s compiler passed 220/220 c-testsuite, 32/36 LLVM tests, 93/93 executed Csmith programs with 7/100 seeds skipped, and limited LZ4/SQLite checks. This demonstrates substantial formation but not complete C11 conformance or production readiness.

- `2608.11367`: The strongest numerical evidence concerns resources rather than accuracy: Gaze-Co has 119,525 training samples, and the private Child-SC set contains 326 clips from 40 children. Accuracy, latency, and ablation cells were unavailable.

- `2608.10615`: At entropy 5.44 and 1,024 neural-function evaluations on OpenWebText, Simplax reported generative perplexities 45.1/46.8/25.5 under GPT-2 Large/GPT-2 XL/Llama-2 evaluators. These are evaluator-based, entropy-matched operating points, not exact model likelihoods.

- `2608.09805`: On six OLMo-3 math benchmarks, C3PO averaged 44.04±0.46 Pass@1 versus GRPO 42.99±0.51. Across three seeds, the average gain was 0.89 points with paired-\(t\) \(p=0.027\), but this limited replication excludes the other model and code setting.

- `2608.11274`: The authors retrospectively classified 40 of 52 documented incidents as fully preventable and 11 as partially mitigable. Only 2 of 12 audited public systems documented submission-like evidence gates, though 9/12 captured file changes and 11/12 tool outputs.

- `2608.11215`: The EconAgent clone’s Phillips correlation was −0.569±0.138 versus the published −0.619, while a behavior-free policy reproduced an Okun-like correlation of −0.998 versus the published −0.918. The latter warns that some aggregate metrics do not identify agent behavior.

- `2608.12123`: In the primary replay—100,000 active sessions, route grouping, \(K=256\), 50 ms deadline—exact offline packing raised eligible event share from 30.19% to 43.00%, below a 45.85% local upper bound. Separately, device residency beat the matched host-mediated GPU path in all 36 mechanism cells, but no end-to-end result joins the two studies.

- `2608.08107`: For the default backbone, NeuPAT restored the 11-task language average from 43.92 after vanilla visual tuning to 49.06, compared with 48.99 before tuning, while the five-task multimodal average changed only 60.98→61.05. The 0.07-point multimodal difference lacks uncertainty estimates.

- `2608.06729`: AtlasVLA achieved 97.6% average LIBERO success, 70.8% over six RLBench tasks, and 69.5% on four real-world long-horizon tasks. Removing world or ego memory reduced the last figure to 54.0% and 56.5%, respectively.

- `2608.12836`: Relative-calibrated structured inference reached 77.0±0.1 Macro-F1 versus 48.3±0.5 for the strongest direct baseline on Logical-CommonsenseQA-HV, and 75.6±0.0 versus 47.0±0.8 on Logical-SATA. Neither/Nor accounted for the largest recovery.

- `2608.11574`: On HInt across three seeds, the detector achieved 0.931±0.000 mAP and 0.896±0.001 F1, versus 0.897±0.001 and 0.860±0.002 for Contact4D. Visibility weighting also reduced reprojection-error distributions, but true 3D joint error was not reported.

- `2608.11045`: Against matched group-wise RTN over five principal small models, ReRound improved four-task average accuracy by 0.1–0.9 points at W3A16 and 0.2–1.3 at W4A16. Offline diffusion inference required 3.65–9.55 hours per model on one GPU, while deployment inference was unchanged.

## Open research directions

1. **Prospectively test runtime safety contracts.** Motivated by `2608.00677`, `2608.11274`, and `2608.11878`: compare model-only, preventive-only, evidence-only, and combined controls on identical persistent-state attacks, reporting unsafe acceptance, benign completion, latency, and verifier failures.

2. **Measure harness gains outside narrow structured benchmarks.** Motivated by `2608.12307`, `2608.12836`, and `2608.11350`: hold validation budget and target calls fixed across ToM, coding, mathematics, embodied tasks, and open-ended planning; ablate routing, deterministic solving, calibration, and context construction separately.

3. **Establish causal value for persistent artifacts and recursion.** Motivated by `2608.11924`, `2608.10450`, `2608.12036`, and `2608.11216`: run factorial studies that remove persistent records, validation gates, hierarchical delegation, retrieval, or revision loops under matched compute and repeated seeds.

4. **Evaluate persistent spatial memory under controlled corruption.** Motivated by `2608.12314`, `2608.06729`, and `2608.10708`: perturb depth, calibration, correspondences, initial anchors, and edit length while measuring geometry error, task success, drift, and fallback decisions.

5. **Connect world-model scores to downstream decisions.** Motivated by `2608.09926` and `2608.11216`: place learned models inside the same fixed planner and test whether pixel- or state-rollout improvements yield higher task reward at 10-, 20-, and full-scenario horizons.

6. **Validate automated judgments against humans and external outcomes.** Motivated by `2608.11616`, `2608.11924`, `2608.08160`, `2608.06270`, and `2608.12036`: use blinded experts to assess business ideas, scientific validity, narrative conflicts, visual evidence use, and mechanistic reproductions; report agreement and criterion-level calibration.

7. **Quantify real compute–quality frontiers.** Motivated by `2608.09805`, `2608.10615`, `2608.11045`, `2608.12123`, and `2608.11215`: report wall time, memory, energy, hardware utilization, and time-to-target quality under matched implementations—not only FLOPs, neural evaluations, or offline opportunity.

8. **Prevent regressions from adaptation.** Motivated by `2608.10708`, `2608.11350`, `2608.08107`, and `2608.12307`: train or derive confidence gates that choose between frozen and adapted behavior, then evaluate positive gain, negative transfer, calibration, and failure detection across domains.

9. **Broaden the visual benchmarks beyond curated static settings.** Motivated by `2608.11562`, `2608.11367`, and `2608.11574`: collect independently labeled or paired real video with motion, parallax, demographic and capture diversity, temporal visibility, and trusted 3D or gaze ground truth.

10. **Test structural guarantees against semantic behavior.** Motivated by `2608.05604` and `2608.12836`: compare original and transformed systems using execution traces, state diffs, independently authored tests, probabilistic option distributions, and adversarially omitted guards—not merely structural recovery or exact Boolean feasibility.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All 27 supplied current papers were analyzed and appear in both the overview and sources.

Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical-connection stages were available. Their assignments and edges were retained only where the supplied endpoint analyses supported the claimed technical overlap. No chronology or influence was inferred from service-observation dates.

Collection failures affected 12 current papers: `2608.00677`, `2608.11924`, `2608.12307`, `2608.12036`, `2608.08160`, `2608.11878`, `2608.11616`, `2608.10450`, `2608.09805`, `2608.08107`, `2608.11574`, and `2608.11045` lacked usable Hugging Face Markdown. Most still had substantial alternative analysis material. `2608.11878` is the major exception: PDF extraction also failed, leaving abstract-only evidence and low confidence.

Other partial inputs include missing or malformed numerical tables for `2608.10708`, `2608.09926`, `2608.06270`, and `2608.11367`. The last paper’s comparative findings therefore remain qualitative. `2608.12036` has an unresolved Qwen3.5-9B versus Qwen2.5-7B description discrepancy. `2608.11562` contains inconsistent training-set totals. `2608.12307` contains a 5% versus 2% validation-language inconsistency.

Historical evidence was cached analysis rather than listing summaries, but several records were service-truncated. The retained historical edges rely only on concrete visible evidence from both endpoints; medium-confidence edges remain explicitly complementary or non-directional.
