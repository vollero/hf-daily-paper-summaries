# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-04

## Executive summary

- Agent reliability is a major theme. LongHorizon-Harness (`2608.01964`) externalizes and audits task state; SWE-Touch (`2608.02499`) measures failures after live code changes; ScrambleToolBench (`2608.02358`) exposes inefficient belief revision under tool drift.
- External skills appear at both ends of the pipeline: Skill-\(\alpha\) (`2608.01678`) learns to edit reusable skill files from downstream rewards, while SKT (`2608.02287`) trains agents to use supplied skills through verified synthetic trajectories.
- Several papers replace coarse supervision with better-matched evidence: VAD (`2607.28590`) attributes privileged visual corrections to interventions, DAPD (`2608.01735`) aligns privileged and deployable information conditions, and DEFT (`2608.01755`) hides future trajectories until after scene-first driving reasoning.
- Multimodal efficiency work spans retrieval, compression, routing, and generation. UEmbed (`2608.02583`) unifies sparse and dense embeddings; 3DZip (`2608.01185`) compresses projected 3D tokens; ReBA (`2608.00574`) balances MoE routing across changing modality mixes; DiffusionGemma (`2608.00146`) and LeapTalk (`2608.00079`) pursue few-step generation.
- WorldExam (`2608.02603`) finds that precise control, visual quality, and scene-conditioned reactivity remain uneven across controllable video models. WCM (`2607.29613`) and DreamTraj (`2608.00486`) instead use predictive latent structure for embodied value estimation and object-trajectory forecasting.
- Structured execution and verification recur in 3D work: CADENA (`2608.00799`) reconstructs CAD programs operation by operation, while Roomer (`2608.01973`) repairs indoor layouts through verified local patches. StyleForge (`2608.01954`) addresses the complementary problem of jointly selecting compatible furniture assets.
- Generative contributions cover unified speech and scene audio in SwanTale (`2608.02023`), cross-category motion transfer in OpenVMT (`2608.01628`), streaming talking heads in LeapTalk (`2608.00079`), and auditable human-image synthesis in Poplar (`2608.00440`).
- GradCuit (`2608.02585`) optimizes frozen-model latent prefixes at test time; GPTQ-2D (`2607.27042`) is the day’s proof-oriented outlier, providing exact cubic-time execution of a two-sided adaptive-rounding trajectory without empirical runtime evaluation.
- RecHarness (`2607.29241`) applies validation-driven, bandit-routed code mutation to recommender development, illustrating the broader pattern of optimizing persistent agent-facing artifacts from measured downstream outcomes.

- Agent state and robustness:

  - LongHorizon-Harness (`2608.01964`) raised WeaveBench PassRate from 51.8% to 80.7% and Overall from 0.702 to 0.835 with Qwen 3.7-Plus and Claude Code as executor on 114 tasks. On OSWorld, output tokens rose from 28.9K to 104K per task, and that comparison also changes GUI-only operation to hybrid GUI+CLI.
  - SWE-Touch (`2608.02499`) reduced mean SWE-bench Verified resolve rate by 7.7 percentage points across nine models, 200 tasks, three runs, and a 100-step budget. Among 526 solved-to-unresolved runs, 63.3% retained conflicting behavior.
  - ScrambleToolBench (`2608.02358`) saw mean episode completion fall from 0.93 in the unscrambled control to 0.03 under combined obfuscation, drift, stochastic failure, and temporal windows, across 15 no-memory models and 20 five-task episodes per model.

- Skill construction and use:

  - Skill-\(\alpha\) (`2608.01678`) achieved 55.83% mean tau2-bench pass rate with GPT-4o versus 49.17% for SkillPro. Removing RL reduced CL-Bench/SpreadsheetBench/tau2-bench scores from 10.38/27.50/55.83 to 3.46/15.50/44.17.
  - SKT (`2608.02287`) improved all 16 matched backbone–harness–benchmark comparisons by 3.20–18.91 points. Its largest reported gain was SkillEval with Qwen3.5-9B and DeepAgents, 51.62→70.53; verified data outperformed raw synthesis in the reported ablation.

- Evidence-matched learning and reasoning:

  - VAD (`2607.28590`) reached six-benchmark \(Avg_6\) scores of 78.32 at 4B and 79.93 at 9B, compared with 75.92 and 76.88 for matched Vision-OPD. The accuracy tables generally lack independent-seed uncertainty.
  - DAPD (`2608.01735`) scored 57.34 on the Qwen3-4B six-task mean, 2.00 points above OPSD. In its pooled behavioral probe, late-stage wrong claims fell from 33 for OPSD to 9 for DAPD.
  - GradCuit (`2608.02585`) averaged 64.5% accuracy across five backbones, three benchmarks, and two answer formats, 2.4 points above the strongest reported competitor. This is a single-seed result using model self-verification.
  - DEFT (`2608.01755`) found that exposing future trajectories raised severe hallucinations from 29.0% to 50.0% in a paired 100-scene human study. With Qwen3-VL-8B, DEFT-RLVR improved AD-MCQ-500 ACC from 56.6% to 77.9%, but the task remains open-loop candidate selection.

- Multimodal retrieval and efficiency:

  - UEmbed-9B (`2608.02583`) scored 71.8 dense and 71.0 sparse on MMEB-v2’s 78 datasets. In BrowseComp-Plus, sparse retrieval required fewer average search rounds at all three model scales, while accuracy did not uniformly favor sparse mode.
  - DeepVoyager-VL (`2608.01827`) raised the 30B-A3B ten-benchmark average from 20.7 without tools to 40.7 with the agent workflow and 58.6 after trajectory SFT. The added 7K vision-in-the-loop trajectories were cumulative rather than size-matched.
  - 3DZip (`2608.01185`) reduced LLaVA-3D’s average input from 1,410 to 128 tokens, retaining 94.7% of aggregate performance; on one RTX 4090, SQA3D latency fell 342→178 ms/sample, FLOPs 9.18T→0.90T, and KV cache 722→101 MB.
  - ReBA (`2608.00574`) reduced average mean layer CV on four split backbones from 0.43/0.26/0.21/0.20 to 0.16/0.19/0.16/0.15. Mean task-accuracy changes versus standard balancing were +0.3, +0.2, −0.2, and +0.6 points; the reported speedup remains an idealized compute proxy.

- Generation and media:

  - SwanTale (`2608.02023`) reported 4.22 MOS and 4.10 Scene Appropriateness on 180 SwanBench-Scene prompts rated by five professionals each. On zero-shot monologue it reported 0.95 timbre consistency and 0.086 Content Error, but specialized baselines led content or fidelity.
  - OpenVMT (`2608.01628`) improved HMF-All 0.7588→0.7707 and G-Mot. 3.436→3.976 when replacing in-category with cross-category Stage-II pairs on OpenVMT-T2V. Its main automated baseline table was unavailable.
  - LeapTalk Pro (`2608.00079`) reported HDTF FID/FVD 21/197 and Sync-C/Sync-D 8.38/7.69 at one function evaluation; Lite reached 200 FPS on one H200 at batch size 1. Long-duration stability is not numerically bounded.
  - DiffusionGemma (`2608.00146`) reported 1,479 TPS and 19.74 tokens/forward in diffusion mode on one H100, FP8, batch 1, excluding prefill. GPQA-Diamond fell from 79.8 in the Gemma 4 AR initialization to 73.2 in diffusion mode.
  - Poplar (`2608.00440`) retained 9,401 of 11,765 reviewed candidates, but supplies no controlled evidence that the accepted data improves downstream training.

- 3D, embodied, and predictive systems:

  - CADENA-RL (`2608.00799`) scored 67.0 GMS on CADENA-Bench versus 52.9 for the strongest reported learned baseline. On the hardest DeepCAD quartile, full stepwise inference added 21.3 GMS, but no matched same-data single-pass model was evaluated.
  - Roomer (`2608.01973`) reduced OOB 24.40%→8.66% and collision 29.52%→17.50%, while raising Practical 72.50%→82.98% on 1,100 scenes. Removing verification caused 54.27% of scenes to acquire a new hard error.
  - StyleForge (`2608.01954`) achieved 79.1% R@1 versus 44.5% for reproduced MetaFind on 800 fixed-layout 3D-FRONT rooms. Its retrieval prior began at 22.8%, and the result followed 200 room-specific optimization steps.
  - WCM (`2607.29613`) raised LIBERO-Plus totals from 39.1±2.1 to 72.8±1.9 for \(\pi_0\), 38.0±1.6 to 73.7±1.4 for \(\pi_{0.5}\), and 29.3±1.5 to 74.0±1.8 for OpenVLA-OFT.
  - DreamTraj (`2608.00486`) reported 6.4 cm ADE, 9.5 cm FDE, and 24.6° rotation error over 5,038 samples with grouped five-fold validation. Its 76-second inference was 4.6× faster than a matched 348-second generate-then-extract pipeline, but not real-time.
  - WorldExam (`2608.02603`) showed interface-specific strengths: NeoVerse scored 97.33 on Camera Control, while the best reported language-driven interaction scores came from different systems on terrain, object, social, and physical reactions. These metrics should not be combined into a global model ranking.

- Optimization and recommender systems:

  - GPTQ-2D (`2607.27042`) proves exact equivalence to vectorized two-sided adaptive rounding while reducing sweep work from \(O(m^2n^2)\) to \(O(mn\max(m,n))\) and parallel depth from \(O(mn)\) to \(O(\max(m,n))\). No empirical performance claim is available.
  - RecHarness (`2607.29241`) raised average HR@10 across four Amazon datasets from 0.2685 to 0.4990 for GRU4Rec and from 0.4723 to 0.5317 for HSTU, averaged over three runs. Its seven-day production test reported ADVV +2.084%, Revenue +0.534%, and Exposure +0.559%, without uncertainty estimates.

## Open research directions

1. **Equal-budget evaluation of long-horizon harnesses** (`2608.01964`, `2608.02499`, `2608.02358`). Hold model, tools, tokens, wall time, and action limits fixed while adding external state, auditing, change detection, or structured belief invalidation; measure success, recovery latency, false audits, and cost.

2. **End-to-end skill lifecycle evaluation** (`2608.01678`, `2608.02287`). Jointly vary skill quality and skill-use training, including misleading or partially correct skills, to separate artifact quality from execution competence on held-out harnesses and four-or-more-skill tasks.

3. **Independent verification of synthetic-data pipelines** (`2608.02287`, `2608.00440`). Human-audit stratified accepted and rejected outputs, estimate false acceptance/rejection by gate, and then compare downstream training utility against raw and size-matched alternative data.

4. **Generalizing evidence-matched distillation** (`2607.28590`, `2608.01735`). Test multiple noisy crops, retrieved documents, tool traces, and plans across unrelated model families; track whether attribution or bridge-consistency diagnostics predict downstream gains.

5. **Production-scale multimodal efficiency frontiers** (`2608.02583`, `2608.01185`, `2608.00574`, `2608.00146`). On uniform hardware, jointly report accuracy, index or cache memory, prefill, generation latency, throughput, energy, batch size, and workload composition; this would distinguish algorithmic savings from favorable operating points.

6. **Long-horizon evaluation of generated dynamics** (`2608.02603`, `2608.01628`, `2608.00079`). Apply repeated interventions and duration sweeps while measuring control fidelity, identity or scene persistence, implied reactions, and drift; include external benchmarks and full-video human inspection.

7. **Closed-loop validation of predictive embodied representations** (`2607.29613`, `2608.01755`, `2608.00486`). Feed critic states, selected driving trajectories, or predicted object trajectories into fixed controllers and measure task completion, collision rate, calibration, and final-pose error under sensor and dynamics shifts.

8. **Broader, independently evaluated 3D repair and selection** (`2608.00799`, `2608.01973`, `2608.01954`). Use unseen CAD kernels, room types, asset libraries, and accessibility or building rules; compare executable editing, continuous optimization, and joint retrieve–repair loops under matched compute.

9. **Verifier and reward robustness at test time** (`2608.02585`, `2608.01755`, `2608.01678`). Swap self-verifiers for independent models and human-validated rewards, inject calibrated label noise, and repeat runs across seeds to determine whether reported gains survive reward misspecification.

10. **Empirical validation of theoretical and proxy efficiency claims** (`2607.27042`, `2608.00574`). Benchmark GPTQ-2D across sizes, condition numbers, block widths, and accelerators; benchmark ReBA under real expert-parallel serving. Report numerical agreement, latency, communication, and downstream quality.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All 26 supplied current papers were analyzed and appear in the taxonomy or singleton notes, the at-a-glance table, and the sources.

Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical-connection stages were available. Their assignments and edges were retained only where the endpoint analyses supplied concrete support. Twelve cross-day edges survived checking against both the current-paper analysis and matching cached previous-paper evidence. Historical evidence was sometimes service-truncated; no retained edge relies only on a `listing_summary`, and no observation date was interpreted as publication order or influence.

Collection failures affected the preferred Hugging Face Markdown route for `2608.02499`, `2608.02585`, `2607.27042`, and `2608.00146`. Full or detailed paper analyses were nevertheless supplied for all four, so they remain covered; the failure limits confidence in collection completeness rather than removing their evidence.

Important partial inputs include missing numerical table bodies for parts of `2608.02023`, `2608.01628`, `2608.02287`, `2608.01827`, `2608.00486`, and `2607.29241`. Results quoted above are restricted to values explicitly present in their analyses. Several papers depend on author-created benchmarks or model judges, including SwanTale, WorldExam, VAD, SKT, OpenVMT, DeepVoyager-VL, Roomer, StyleForge, 3DZip, and DEFT; human validation ranges from absent to partial.

Low-confidence or especially qualified conclusions include OpenVMT’s broader state-of-the-art claim, LeapTalk’s open-ended stability claim, ReBA’s serving-speed implication, GPTQ-2D’s practical speedup, DreamTraj’s out-of-distribution geometric generalization, and RecHarness’s production durability. No numerical results were compared across incompatible metrics to declare an overall “strongest” paper.
