# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-06

## Executive summary

- Two papers refine selective on-policy distillation. Poly-OPD (`2608.04349`) separates capability-specific image-generation adapters, while OPD-V (`2608.05131`) filters token supervision using positive-versus-negative visual evidence margins.
- Poly-OPD reports simultaneous—but separately selectable—gains in preference and composition: its composition adapter raises GenEval from 67.30 to 73.30 on 553 prompts with four 512×512 samples per prompt (`2608.04349`).
- OPD-V reports 80.01% mean accuracy across six visual-reasoning evaluations with Qwen3.5-4B, versus 77.10% for the strongest matched post-training alternative, Vision-OPD (`2608.05131`).
- HelloWorld (`2608.05070`) adds timed, viewer-directed character interactions to camera-controlled video generation. On its 10-second benchmark clips, it reports 81.7 TimeAcc and 82.9 CamCtrl, though its 41.4 ActAcc is not the best reported.
- HelloWorld’s temporal mask is especially consequential: TimeAcc rises from 36.7 without masking to 81.7 with video-and-audio masking, while ActAcc slightly declines from 42.5 to 41.4 (`2608.05070`).
- TriGlue (`2607.22143`) tackles a distinct scientific task: jointly generating a molecular glue and assembling its ternary protein complex. Its evidence is computational, with no biochemical or cellular validation.
- Across the collection, results remain bounded by narrow model families, automated or surrogate metrics, limited uncertainty reporting, and—except for HelloWorld’s user study—little or no direct human evaluation.

## Main results worth noting

- **Poly-OPD (`2608.04349`) — compositional generation:** GenEval improves from 67.30 to 73.30 on 553 prompts with four 512×512 samples per prompt. Reported category gains over the base student include +14.93 points for two-object prompts, +8.25 for attribute binding, and +7.20 for position. DPG-Bench improves 84.51→85.80 on 1,065 prompts with four samples each, remaining 0.28 below Z-Image overall.

- **Poly-OPD (`2608.04349`) — preference metrics:** On 999 DrawBench prompts with one 512×512 sample each, ImageReward rises 0.922→1.168 and HPSv3 9.341→11.354, while PickScore is approximately unchanged at 0.869→0.866. These learned metrics should not be treated as interchangeable with direct human preference.

- **HelloWorld (`2608.05070`) — timing and camera control:** Across three seeds on matched 10-second HelloWorldBench generations, the method reports 81.7 TimeAcc and 82.9 CamCtrl; strongest baseline values are 52.6 and 70.0. TimeAcc excludes outputs judged to contain no interaction, so it is not a joint interaction-occurrence-and-timing measure.

- **HelloWorld (`2608.05070`) — trade-offs:** ActAcc is 41.4, below LingBot-World at 50.5 and the LTX-2.3 base generator at 42.5. GazeDev is 40.2° on 217 human-character samples, better than the five world-model baselines’ 52.8°–77.2° but slightly worse than LTX-2.3’s 38.1°. Lower GazeDev is preferable.

- **HelloWorld (`2608.05070`) — human evaluation:** Across 41 samples and 30 raters, reported pairwise preference spans 77.3%–90.9% for action naturalness, 71.7%–88.5% for interaction, and 82.6%–91.2% for scene quality against four competitors; all reported 95% confidence intervals remain above 66%.

- **OPD-V (`2608.05131`) — matched multimodal post-training:** With Qwen3.5-4B and a shared 6.2K-example training set, unweighted mean accuracy across V* Bench, ZoomBench, HR-Bench 4K/8K, and MME-RealWorld EN/CN is 80.01%, versus 77.10% for Vision-OPD and 64.30% for the base.

- **OPD-V (`2608.05131`) — scale and efficiency:** Reported mean accuracy changes are 68.00%→74.14% for Qwen3-VL-4B, 68.93%→73.03% for Qwen3-VL-8B, and 69.75%→77.63% for Qwen3.5-9B. Against OPSD, mean step latency falls 352→240 seconds at 4B and 451→340 seconds at 9B on four H200 GPUs, but the comparison also includes much shorter generated responses.

- **TriGlue (`2607.22143`) — joint computational design:** On 94 held-out TernaryDB complexes with no sequence-pair overlap with the 21,481-complex training set, ligand RMSD averages 5.22 versus 6.27–8.16 for three molecular generators. Its reported Vina/Vina-min/Vina-dock averages are −6.23/−6.77/−8.28, while PocketXMol has a slightly better Vina-min score of −6.88.

- **TriGlue (`2607.22143`) — assembly and interface estimation:** Docking acceptance at DockQ > 0.23 is 35.2%, versus 30.8% for DeepTernary. Removing interface correction reduces average DockQ from 0.22 to 0.15. Interface estimates report JSD 0.47 ± 0.15, W2 12.65 ± 7.89, Center-L2 10.99 ± 7.47, and LogDet error 3.83 ± 5.01, all lower than the converted PeSTo and ScanNet results in the supplied evaluation.

## Open research directions

1. **Test selective distillation without privileged localization** (`2608.05131`). Replace ground-truth evidence crops with detector-generated, attention-derived, and random crops under identical training budgets; compare accuracy, token selection, and causal visual-evidence annotations.

2. **Scale heterogeneous distillation beyond two capabilities** (`2608.04349`). Add several specialist teachers while measuring capability retention, adapter growth, gradient conflicts, human preference, and switching overhead against single-teacher and fully shared controls.

3. **Separate objective efficiency from rollout shortening** (`2608.05131`). Cap OPSD and OPD-V at identical response lengths and profile rollout generation, image transformation, teacher inference, and backward computation separately.

4. **Evaluate genuinely live social interaction** (`2608.05070`). Build a streaming version driven by unpredictable user inputs and measure control-to-frame latency, missed interactions, onset/offset error, and visual consistency rather than predetermined 10-second scripts.

5. **Measure long-term character identity and social memory** (`2608.05070`). Run repeated interactions across extended sequences, with controlled callbacks to prior events and metrics for identity preservation, remembered user state, and response consistency.

6. **Validate visual-generator gains with people and matched compute** (`2608.04349`). Conduct blinded pairwise studies for preference and composition while equalizing sampling cost across student and teachers; report latency, memory, adapter size, and uncertainty.

7. **Prospectively validate generated molecular glues** (`2607.22143`). Synthesize prioritized candidates and measure ternary-complex formation, ubiquitination, target degradation, selectivity, toxicity, and synthetic feasibility in biochemical and cellular assays.

8. **Relax TriGlue’s structural assumptions** (`2607.22143`). Compare ellipsoidal, residue-level, multi-patch, and surface-based interfaces, then add side-chain or backbone flexibility and evaluate DockQ, ligand RMSD, and prospective assay success.

9. **Test whether selection signals are causally meaningful** (`2608.04349`, `2608.05131`). Intervene on curriculum gaps or token margins—randomizing, reversing, or calibrating them—while holding sampled trajectories fixed to distinguish useful selection from correlated difficulty signals.
