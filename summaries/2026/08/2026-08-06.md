# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-06

## Executive summary

- Four papers refine on-policy distillation by deciding where teacher feedback is useful: SA-OPD filters weakly grounded, high-impact tokens (`2608.03632`); RSTG targets all-failed RL groups (`2608.00782`); Poly-OPD bridges heterogeneous image generators (`2608.04349`); and OPD-V favors visually grounded tokens (`2608.05131`).
- Long-horizon systems focus increasingly on memory quality, not merely memory capacity. OneDayAgent compresses execution state and verifies deliverables (`2608.05013`), while SpatialSTALE shows that outdated memory can be substantially more dangerous than no memory (`2608.04574`).
- Two diagnostics expose failures hidden by aggregate competence: NOLLI separates presentation-language effects from Hangul-jamo and kinship-task deficits (`2608.04397`), while Skill2-Bench measures difficulty introduced by switching among dependent skills (`2608.05139`).
- Robot-learning papers pursue complementary routes to data efficiency: Ego2Robot synthesizes multi-embodiment demonstrations from human video (`2608.02580`), whereas BridgeVLA++ adds heatmap-aligned control and episodic memory (`2608.05042`).
- Controllable video generation advances along three axes: reversible-cycle consistency in WorldCycle (`2608.04964`), temporally localized social interaction in HelloWorld (`2608.05070`), and geometry-conditioned large-baseline view synthesis in UniWorld-View (`2608.04701`).
- ToolArtist (`2608.04436`) and AVE-Compass/AVE-Agent (`2607.24821`) move multimedia production toward agentic workflows, but both remain dependent on learned judges, external tools, and relatively narrow evaluation suites.
- K-EXAONE 2.0 (`2608.04505`) reports broad gains from sparse-MoE upcycling, while TriGlue (`2607.22143`) addresses a distinct scientific task—joint molecular-glue and ternary-complex generation—with computational rather than experimental validation.
- Across the collection, many headline results lack repeated-run uncertainty, component isolation, or independent judge validation; comparisons should remain tied to their stated models, metrics, datasets, and inference settings.

## Main results worth noting

- `2608.04574`: In L2 text navigation over 250 episodes per model–strategy–regime cell, GPT-4o’s death rate was **74.4% with unfiltered stale memory**, compared with **28.0% without memory** and **31.6% after OMCD filtering**. This is unusually direct evidence that retained information can be worse than its absence in the tested environment.

- `2608.04570`: Across 12 models and 143,616 extracted claims, the claim-weighted over-inference mean was **41.8%**. Self-audit had record-level within-model AUROC of **0.58–0.83**, yet cross-model self-audit versus external-judge rankings had Spearman \(\rho=-0.60\), \(p=0.044\), with confidence intervals crossing zero.

- `2608.05013`: On the same 104 AgentIF-OneDay tasks with GLM-5.2 and memory enabled, DIRECT scored **0.771**, decomposition-only **0.8039**, verification-only **0.8044**, and FULL **0.821**. The comparison supports decomposition and verification, but not memory’s independent effect.

- `2608.03632`: With a Qwen3.5-35B-A3B teacher and 2B student, SA-OPD improved visual-understanding average **50.5→54.0** and visual-reasoning average **60.4→63.5** over Vanilla OPD. Combined grounding-and-impact filtering also beat single-criterion filters on Geo3K/MathVista.

- `2608.05131`: On the same Qwen3.5-4B backbone and 6.2K-example training set, OPD-V achieved **80.01%** six-benchmark mean accuracy versus **77.10%** for Vision-OPD and **64.30%** for the base. Its latency comparison against OPSD was **352→240 seconds per step**, partly because responses were shorter.

- `2608.00782`: RSTG’s reported improvements over a selected GRPO+OPD hybrid were **+0.89% to +4.02%** on aggregate math and **+1.97% to +3.05%** on code across three Qwen teacher–student pairs. The source does not resolve whether these are relative percentages or percentage points.

- `2608.04349`: For SD3.5-Medium distilled from FLUX.1-dev and Z-Image, the composition adapter raised GenEval **67.30→73.30** on 553 prompts with four samples each. Removing capability-specific adapters cost **11.0 GenEval points**, while direct OPD without warm start lost **24.1 points**.

- `2608.04397`: Across 12 non-floor models, matched direct-translation EN−KO gaps were only **−2.7 to +5.0 points** under a ±10-point equivalence margin. In contrast, DeepSeek-V4-Flash scored **74.3% English versus 5.7% Korean** on Cipher, and jamo-composition performance predicted Korean Cipher performance with \(R^2=0.91\).

- `2608.05139`: Cross-skill context lowered average-domain accuracy by **4.3–12.6 points** across 12 models. On Skill2-Bench, Skill-Entropy RL improved Qwen3-4B **58.8→68.4%** over answer-only GRPO and Qwen3-1.7B **32.2→40.1%**.

- `2608.02580`: Under matched sampled-frame and training-step budgets, a 1:1 synthesized/robot-data mixture improved RoboTwin Clean **62.2→68.1%** and Randomized **50.9→53.5%**. The best EBench mixture differed: 3:1 reached **51.7%**, versus **39.6%** robot-only.

- `2608.05042`: BridgeVLA++ reached **96.0%** on nine RMBench dual-arm memory tasks versus **18.9%** for its memory-free base; removing temporal memory reduced it to **21.3%**, whereas removing spatial memory left **95.4%**. This setting used one 100-episode evaluation per task after best-checkpoint selection.

- `2608.04964`: At 381 frames, full WorldCycle training reduced ESC **0.302→0.163** relative to its base. At 125 frames under out-of-domain composite controls, action accuracy rose **0.136→0.553** versus WorldPlay.

- `2608.05070`: On matched 10-second clips averaged across three seeds, HelloWorld scored **81.7 TimeAcc** and **82.9 CamCtrl**. Temporal masking drove TimeAcc from **36.7 without masking to 81.7 with video-and-audio masking**, while ActAcc slightly declined **42.5→41.4**.

- `2608.04701`: UniWorld-View reported the highest PSNR and SSIM among four listed baselines on RealEstate10K, CO3D, and DL3DV, but LPIPS was mixed—**0.1678** on RealEstate10K versus **0.1508** for Uni3C.

- `2608.04436`: ToolArtist reached **0.79 WISE Overall** on 1,000 prompts and **22.10 KCS** on 732 WorldGenBench-Humanities prompts, compared with **21.76** for Qwen-Image. Removing source-aware summaries lowered WISE Overall to **0.61**.

- `2607.24821`: Across 196 editing instructions, AVE-Agent’s **59.8 EI** exceeded Wan2.7’s **42.4**. Removing evaluator-driven retry reduced EI by **8.75 points**, while speech quality remained below Wan2.7 (**0.368 versus 0.428**).

- `2608.05000`: In the scaled 13.5B-MoE/2T-token comparison, the 70/25/5 mix improved language, VQA, DPG, and GenEval over 50/25/25, but worsened diffusion loss **0.261→0.272** and FID **5.131→5.234**. The result is therefore a measured trade-off, not dominance on every metric.

- `2608.04505`: K-EXAONE 2.0 reported **1.81–2.57×** DSpark speculative-decoding speedups on 8×H200 with FP8 and draft budget seven. Its predecessor comparisons improved SWE-Bench Verified **49.4→68.2** and OpenAI-MRCR **52.3→94.4**, though the latter evaluates through 128K rather than the model’s full 256K context.

- `2607.22143`: On 94 held-out TernaryDB complexes, TriGlue achieved ligand RMSD **5.22**, docking acceptance **35.2%**, and average DockQ **0.22**; removing interface correction reduced DockQ to **0.15**. These are computational structural results, not evidence of biological degradation activity.

- `2607.28048`: SKILL-KD improved ALFWorld from **30.6→86.6** and **59.7→96.3** for its two students. Trace-aware consolidation reduced one SpreadsheetBench library from **50 rules/2,930 words** to **6 rules/522 words**.

- `2608.05108`: PIMiner’s ASR@10 varied sharply by target: on IPIArena it ranged from **76.2%** against Gemini-2.5-Pro to **4.8%** against Claude-Opus-4.5 over 21 samples. Routing reduced attacker input from **236K to 92K tokens** with Sonnet-4.6 while average ASR changed **60.9%→68.4%**.

- `2608.04926`: CoCoEvolve reports improvements across chart, table, and code directions, but many headline values are maxima over task/model combinations. Its clearest operational result is that evaluation averaged **$0.2382 and 71.88 seconds per sample**, versus **$0.8637 and 128.72 seconds** for the reported baseline on four H100 GPUs.

## Open research directions

1. **Validate selective distillation signals directly** (`2608.03632`, `2608.05131`, `2608.00782`). Annotate whether selected tokens are genuinely task- or image-grounded, then compare prompt removal, masked evidence, entropy, teacher divergence, and verified token utility at matched selection budgets.

2. **Measure memory provenance and staleness jointly with utility** (`2608.04570`, `2608.04574`, `2608.05013`, `2607.28048`). Randomize evidence-linked writes, contradiction handling, read-time auditing, and compression; report task success, unsafe actions, false retention, and useful-memory loss.

3. **Test long-horizon harnesses under common judges and budgets** (`2608.05013`). Re-score all systems with one evaluator and equalize tools, time, tokens, and maximum actions; separately ablate decomposition, compression, checkpointing, verification, and repair.

4. **Distinguish skill-selection errors from answer errors** (`2608.04397`, `2608.05139`). Use matched cross-script and cross-domain tasks with independently manipulated skill labels, exact-transition rewards, and entropy-only rewards to determine whether better skill selection causally improves correctness.

5. **Map the fidelity–scale frontier for robot-free demonstrations** (`2608.02580`, `2608.05042`). Compare synthesized ego data, high-fidelity human capture, and robot teleoperation at matched episodes, collection hours, and training tokens across several embodiments and camera layouts.

6. **Stress-test robot memories in changing scenes** (`2608.05042`, `2608.04574`). Replace initial-only spatial memory with incrementally updated maps, introduce controlled object movement and occlusion, and measure memory calibration, retrieval accuracy, collision rate, and task success as horizon grows.

7. **Connect video consistency metrics to downstream control** (`2608.04964`, `2608.05070`, `2608.04701`). Use the same generated world models inside a planner or interactive agent and test whether cycle closure, camera fidelity, and interaction timing predict task completion under looping and non-monotonic trajectories.

8. **Run matched human evaluation for agentic media systems** (`2608.04436`, `2607.24821`, `2608.04349`, `2608.04926`). Hold prompts, tool budgets, inference compute, and judges fixed; collect blinded assessments of factual fidelity, edit compliance, preference, and failure severity.

9. **Establish generality of multimodal training recipes** (`2608.05000`, `2608.05131`). Repeat mixture, parameter-sharing, and modality-balance studies across unrelated architectures and temporal modalities with multiple seeds and parameter-matched dense/MoE controls.

10. **Move molecular-glue evaluation beyond surrogates** (`2607.22143`). Synthesize prospectively selected candidates and measure ternary-complex formation, ubiquitination, degradation, selectivity, and toxicity; stratify results by unseen protein family and compare rigid versus flexible docking.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All **22 supplied current papers** were analyzed and included. Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical-connections stages were available, but their claims were treated as editorial aids and retained only where supported by the corresponding paper evidence.

Collection failures affected `2608.04505` and `2608.05108`: Hugging Face Markdown was unavailable for the former and returned HTTP 404 for the latter. Both nevertheless had supplied paper analyses, so they are covered; the failed retrievals reduce confidence in collection completeness rather than eliminating the papers.

Several inputs were partial:

- Historical evidence for `2607.15686` was explicitly truncated, although it was not used in a retained edge.
- Several current and historical analyses lacked rendered numerical table cells, including important comparisons for `2608.00782`, `2607.28048`, and parts of `2608.04964`.
- `2608.00782` does not clarify whether reported improvements are relative percentages or percentage points.
- `2608.04701` contains a material inconsistency about which data train its two architectural stages.
- `2608.05013` compares new results and official baselines under different judge versions.
- Many papers report point estimates without repeated-run variance, confidence intervals, or significance tests.
- Several evaluations depend on LLM/VLM judges whose identities, validation, or human agreement are incomplete.

Historical edges use cached analyses at both endpoints; none relies only on a listing summary. Service first-seen dates were not used as evidence of chronology, priority, citation, or influence. Lower-confidence editorial conclusions include the proposed complementarity between WorldCycle and HelloWorld, cross-domain lessons from cycle consistency, and any implication that gains observed in synthetic or controlled environments will transfer to deployment.
