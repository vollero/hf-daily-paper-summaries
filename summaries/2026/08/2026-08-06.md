# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-06

## Executive summary

- Long-horizon agents were a major theme: `2608.05102` assigns step-level search credit, `2608.05013` manages decomposition and artifact repair, `2608.05466` synthesizes verified terminal tasks, and `2607.27853` supplies a finance-specific research harness.
- Persistent state helped only when it was selective and trustworthy. `2608.04574` found stale memory could be markedly worse than no memory; `2608.04530` improved GUI agents by separating retention, readout, and trust; `2608.04570` showed unsupported user-profile claims can accumulate.
- Several papers refined teacher supervision rather than applying it indiscriminately: `2608.03632`, `2608.00782`, and `2608.05131` filter or route token-level guidance, while `2608.04349` bridges heterogeneous image-generation teachers.
- Controlled benchmarks localized specific failures: English–Korean presentation alone explained little of the gap in `2608.04397`; scene-text conflict redirected all 20 MLLMs in `2608.04244`; `2607.21735` formalized limits on what finite red-team results can prove.
- Video-world research separated several control problems: interaction timing (`2608.05070`), reversible long-horizon consistency (`2608.04964`), and large-baseline camera control (`2608.04701`).
- Robotics work addressed complementary bottlenecks: synthesized cross-embodiment experience (`2608.02580`), explicit temporal memory (`2608.05042`), and adversarial robustness of flow-matching policies (`2608.03207`).
- Several strong numerical results are setting-sensitive. Examples include ABSeeker’s managed versus unmanaged BrowseComp scores (`2608.05102`), OneDayAgent’s substituted judge (`2608.05013`), and TriGlue’s computational rather than experimental validation (`2607.22143`).
- The collection also included distinct singleton contributions in multimodal pretraining (`2608.05000`), checkpoint compression (`2608.02162`), symbolic music (`2608.04378`), chart–table–code learning (`2608.04926`), foundation-model development (`2608.04505`), and workflow-resume semantics (`2608.03836`).

- `2608.05102`: On Qwen3.5-4B with up to 200 tool calls and three-run evaluation, BrowseComp was 37.3% without context management and 55.3% with a 256K discard-all strategy. The latter exceeds reported same-scale baselines, but the unmanaged score trails QUEST-4B’s reported 40.0%.
- `2608.05466`: Across 15 synthesis rounds, accepted yield stayed between 498.2 and 572.2 tasks per 1,000 attempts while fixed-solver pass@4 fell from 90% to 2.5%. This supports increasing difficulty under a fixed solver, not unlimited recursive scalability.
- `2608.05000`: In the 13.5B-MoE, 2T-token comparison, the 70/25/5 language/understanding/generation mixture achieved 54.31% language accuracy, 43.08% VQA, 0.689 DPG, and 0.482 GenEval, versus 52.86%, 41.42%, 0.676, and 0.467 for 50/25/25. Diffusion loss and FID were slightly worse.
- `2608.04570`: Across 143,616 claims, judge-labeled unsupported personalization averaged 41.6% across models. Self-audit had useful within-model AUROC of 0.58–0.83, but cross-model correlation with external judgments was \(\rho=-0.60\), with uncertainty intervals crossing zero.
- `2608.05013`: On 104 AgentIF-OneDay tasks, direct execution scored 0.771, decomposition 0.8039, verification 0.8044, and the full configuration 0.821. Ninety-five tasks passed initial verification; repair recovered six of nine failures.
- `2608.05139`: With common Qwen3-4B data and prompts, Skill-Entropy RL scored 68.4% on Skill2-Bench versus 58.8% for answer-only GRPO. External-benchmark gains were much smaller: 46.7% versus 46.0%.
- `2608.03764`: Persistent skills improved every evaluated aggregate configuration by 2.59–16.44 points, but the `tg018` case fell from 48.36% to 39.83% after scope conditions were dropped.
- `2608.02580`: Under a fixed roughly 19.2M-frame pretraining budget, a 1:1 Ego2Robot/robot mixture improved RoboTwin Clean from 62.2% to 68.1% and Randomized from 50.9% to 53.5%; the best EBench mixture was instead 3:1 at 51.7%.
- `2608.03632`: For Qwen3.5-35B-A3B teacher to 2B student, SA-OPD raised visual-understanding average from 50.5 to 54.0 and visual-reasoning average from 60.4 to 63.5 over vanilla OPD, with 2.64%–7.53% measured training-time overhead.
- `2608.04397`: Direct English–Korean presentation differences fell within a preregistered ±10-point equivalence margin for all 12 non-floor models, while DeepSeek-V4-Flash’s English/Korean Cipher scores were 74.3%/5.7%. The latter is associated with jamo competence but does not identify a causal mechanism.
- `2608.05070`: On 10-second HelloWorldBench clips, masking raised TimeAcc from 36.7 to 81.7, while ActAcc slipped from 42.5 to 41.4. HelloWorld’s 41.4 ActAcc remained below LingBot-World’s 50.5.
- `2608.04505`: K-EXAONE 2.0 reported SWE-Bench Verified 68.2 versus 49.4 for its predecessor, Terminal-Bench 2.1 43.8 versus 30.3, and DSpark speculative-decoding speedups of 1.81–2.57× on 8×H200.
- `2607.24821`: Across 196 edit instructions, AVE-Agent reached Editing Intent 59.8 versus 42.4 for Wan2.7. Audio preservation rose 48.1→74.8, but speech quality fell 0.428→0.368.
- `2608.00782`: RSTG’s reported gains over naïve GRPO+OPD span 0.89%–4.02% on math and 1.97%–3.05% on code across three Qwen pairs. Exact benchmark cells and whether these are relative percentages or points are unavailable.
- `2608.04574`: In 250 GPT-4o L2 text-navigation episodes, death was 74.4% with unfiltered stale memory, 28.0% without memory, and 31.6% after OMCD filtering.
- `2608.04530`: FocusMem’s five-web-benchmark mean success was 48.3% versus 33.7% for the matched fixed-memory baseline. On MMInA-Shopping, cumulative additions moved success 45.5%→50.5%→54.5%→59.0%.
- `2608.04964`: Full WorldCycle training reduced ESC from 0.076 to 0.026 at 125 frames and from 0.302 to 0.163 at 381 frames; composite-action accuracy rose from 0.136 for WorldPlay to 0.553.
- `2608.04349`: Poly-OPD raised GenEval 67.30→73.30 and DPG-Bench 84.51→85.80. A fully shared adapter lost 11.0 GenEval points relative to capability-specific adapters.
- `2607.28048`: SKILL-KD raised ALFWorld success from 30.6 to 86.6 for Qwen3.5-4B and from 59.7 to 96.3 for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B; these are single-run results.
- `2608.02162`: Brevis reduced 2.130 TB to 1.407 TB with exact reconstruction. On Llama-3.1-70B it reported 3.60 GB/s compression and 6.61 GB/s decompression under a 32-worker CPU configuration.
- `2608.05131`: On six visual benchmarks, Qwen3.5-4B averaged 80.01% with OPD-V versus 77.10% for Vision-OPD and 64.30% untrained. Final-ten-step response length was 140.9 tokens versus 553.8 for OPSD.
- `2608.05042`: On RMBench, BridgeVLA++ achieved 96.0% versus 18.9% for BridgeVLA; removing temporal memory reduced it to 21.3%, while removing spatial memory yielded 95.4%.
- `2608.04926`: CoCoEvolve reported up to 100% code execution success and substantial cross-domain gains, but most headline changes are maxima over model/task combinations rather than uniform effects.
- `2608.04701`: UniWorld-View scored 85.53 WorldScore-Static and led PSNR/SSIM on RealEstate10K, CO3D, and DL3DV among four reported baselines; LPIPS was mixed.
- `2608.05108`: With up to ten attacks per sample, PIMiner’s IPIArena ASR@10 was 76.2% against Gemini-2.5-Pro but 4.8% against Claude-Opus-4.5, showing substantial target dependence.
- `2607.22143`: On 94 held-out TernaryDB complexes, TriGlue reported ligand RMSD 5.22 and docking acceptance 35.2%, versus 6.27–8.16 and 30.8% for the relevant separate-task comparators. These are computational results.
- `2607.27853`: With the same Qwen3.6-27B backbone, finance search-only scored 25.3%, a naïve harness 29.6%, and the full harness 32.4%. GRPO added only 0.4 points.
- `2608.03392`: As a survey, it reports no pooled effect size; its main contribution is the five-target taxonomy and its warning that functional benchmark gains do not establish maintainability, security, or long-term reliability.
- `2608.03207`: DRIFT is reported at nearly 100% ASR against \(\pi_0\) across LIBERO suites, compared with average ASR of 13.2% for UADA and 25.3% for EDPA. The attack is white-box and evaluated by pasted simulated patches.
- `2608.04244`: Across 20 MLLMs and four datasets, mean weighted location accuracy fell 47.11→29.89 and median error rose 282→1,347 km under adversarial scene text.
- `2607.21735`: At a 1% unsafe rate, approximately 3,679 prompts per arm are required for 80% one-sided power to detect a reduction to 0.5%. At a 0.1% rate, more than 2,995 clean trials are needed to make a zero-event outcome occur with probability below 5%.
- `2608.04378`: Chord supervision raised root accuracy 0.24→0.59, joint chord recovery 0.18→0.54, and never-supervised 24-class key accuracy 0.16→0.70, while slightly reducing some phrase and temporal probes.
- `2608.03836`: Two-process concurrent delivery duplicated effects in 10/10 runs on both durable backends; the opt-in Remit gate produced one effect in each tested repetition. This evidence covers selected versions, paths, and failure models.

## Open research directions

1. **Validate selective teacher signals causally.** For `2608.03632`, `2608.05131`, and `2608.00782`, annotate whether filtered or routed tokens are genuinely task-supported, then compare downstream effects after selectively restoring or removing those exact signals.

2. **Evaluate memory under realistic conflict and partial observability.** Extend `2608.04574` and `2608.04530` from full symbolic grids or clearly irrelevant episodes to graded staleness, incomplete observations, and partially applicable memories; report calibration and downstream safety, not only aggregate retrieval accuracy.

3. **Separate recursive synthesis from scale and filtering.** For `2608.05466` and `2608.05139`, compare recursive versus independent generation at matched accepted-task count, difficulty, verifier budget, and training compute.

4. **Test persistent skills for provenance, revocation, and cross-domain transfer.** For `2608.03764`, `2607.28048`, `2608.03392`, and `2608.05108`, attach source traces and validity scopes to each skill, then measure transfer, negative interference, and whether deleting a source reliably invalidates descendants.

5. **Unify long-horizon agent evaluation under matched resources.** Compare `2608.05102`, `2608.05013`, and `2607.27853` with equal tool-call, context, wall-clock, judge, and retrieval budgets; measure artifact correctness, evidence quality, and cost separately.

6. **Connect video consistency metrics to usable interaction.** Put `2608.05070`, `2608.04964`, and `2608.04701` into a common interactive planner or user-control loop; test whether timing, cycle closure, and camera fidelity predict task completion and user-rated controllability.

7. **Disentangle robot data diversity from explicit memory.** For `2608.02580` and `2608.05042`, train a factorial grid varying synthesized embodiment diversity and temporal-memory capacity, then evaluate the same held-out embodiments and memory-dependent tasks.

8. **Stress-test robustness beyond benchmark-native attacks.** For `2608.03207`, run printed physical patches and adaptive defenses; for `2608.04244`, use naturally occurring misleading signage; for `2608.05108`, evaluate defended agents. Use the claim-calibration framework of `2607.21735` to choose sample sizes and bound conclusions.

9. **Replace proxy-only validation with domain evidence.** For `2607.22143`, synthesize and assay candidates for ternary-complex formation and degradation; for `2608.04378`, conduct musician co-creation studies; for `2608.04926`, add independently authored references and expert chart evaluation.

10. **Measure reliability across seeds and system versions.** Repeated runs are especially needed for `2608.04530`, `2607.28048`, `2608.05139`, `2608.05000`, and `2608.03836`; report paired uncertainty, versioned environments, and failure frequencies rather than point estimates alone.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All **34 supplied current papers** were analyzed and appear in the taxonomy or singleton notes, the at-a-glance table, the results section, and Sources.

Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical connections were available. The same-day table retains only edges supported by both current analyses. The historical table retains 12 sparse edges whose endpoint evidence was available as cached analysis; several historical records were service-truncated, so cross-day claims are limited to concrete retained passages. No `listing_summary` was used.

Collection failures affected Hugging Face Markdown retrieval for `2608.04505`, `2608.04530`, `2608.05108`, and `2607.27853`. Full PAPER_ANALYSIS blocks were nevertheless supplied for all four, so they remain covered; the failure reduces confidence in source-collection completeness rather than leaving them unanalyzed.

Other important partial inputs include inaccessible numeric cells or figure-only results for `2608.00782`, `2608.03207`, `2608.04436`, `2608.04926`, and several others. `2608.03836` is explicitly missing 33,583 characters, including substantial repair-system material. Its Remit conclusions are therefore limited to the reported tested behaviors.

Low-confidence or heavily qualified conclusions include ABSeeker’s cross-system ranking under different context management (`2608.05102`), OneDayAgent’s comparison under a substituted judge (`2608.05013`), RSTG’s image-only aggregate tables (`2608.00782`), CoCoEvolve’s maximum-gain summaries (`2608.04926`), DRIFT’s missing per-suite cells and simulated physicality (`2608.03207`), and all wet-lab implications of TriGlue (`2607.22143`). No service first-seen date was interpreted as publication order, priority, citation, influence, or dependency.
