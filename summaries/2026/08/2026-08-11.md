# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-11

## Executive summary

- Agent harnesses were a major theme. Macaron-V1 (`2608.09819`) and Ouroboros (`2608.08311`) implement versioned harness revision, while Evo-Bench (`2608.09096`) and A²E (`2608.07346`) show why held-out, process-aware evaluation is necessary.
- Coding-agent performance depends strongly on scaffolding. On SWE-Bench ProMax (`2608.09802`), GPT-5.2 rose from 21.8% with mini-swe-agent to 41.2% with OpenHands under identical 300-step and $10 limits.
- Two math papers refine on-policy distillation differently: u-OPSD (`2608.06296`) constructs supervision from rollout consensus, whereas SPOT (`2608.04419`) probes teacher-proposed branches and calibrates token targets with verified outcomes.
- External memory improved frozen agents through two distinct designs: hierarchical teacher-derived memories in AMD (`2608.07169`) and four persistent, outcome-updated utility coordinates in RoMeRL (`2608.02508`).
- Multimodal work increasingly tests dependence on specific evidence. Evidence-RL (`2608.08021`) uses counterfactual region interventions, while ConCor-1 (`2608.07886`) explicitly predicts paired language spans and image masks.
- Scientific and constrained generation remain difficult. Sci-VBench (`2608.09873`) finds that perceptually plausible videos often fail scientific-mechanism tests; MirrorWorld (`2608.07463`) and Atelier (`2608.06751`) add task-specific semantic, geometric, and anti-shortcut controls.
- Safety papers expose distinct infrastructure risks: replayable encrypted reasoning state (`2608.09867`), cross-user agent collaboration and governance failures (`2608.03499`), and benchmark fingerprinting under iterative optimization (`2608.08722`).
- Several contributions are deliberately narrow or preliminary: CEAA (`2608.09848`) is principally a reference architecture; VectraYX-Vision-1B (`2608.08477`) reports near-zero visual grounding in valid checkpoints; Steerling (`2608.07594`) cannot be fully assessed because central source sections were truncated.

## Main results worth noting

- `2608.09802`: GPT-5.2 resolved 41.2% of 170 SWE-Bench ProMax instances with OpenHands, versus 21.8% with mini-swe-agent, under the same 300-step and $10-per-instance limits. This is direct evidence that harness choice materially changes measured coding performance.

- `2608.09096`: With a fixed DeepSeek-V4-Flash policy on 448 held-out tasks, GPT-5.6-Sol raised Evo-Bench Overall from 29.7 to 46.3 after harness evolution. The result remained 1.2 points below the 47.5 composite human-engineered reference and came from one evolution run.

- `2608.08311`: On Terminal-Bench 2.1, Ouroboros/Opus 5 achieved 386/445 after audit, or 86.74%, with an estimated binomial SE of roughly ±1.7 points. This evaluates a frozen harness configuration, not the causal gain from self-evolution.

- `2608.06296`: In non-thinking competition-math evaluation, u-OPSD reported five-benchmark averages of 49.49 and 54.31 for Qwen3-4B and Qwen3-8B, respectively, 3.2% and 2.3% above supervised OPSD. For the 8B thinking setting, the OPSD margin shrank to 0.02%.

- `2608.04419`: Across six math benchmarks with eight samples per problem, SPOT’s macro Pass@8 was 54.30 for Qwen3-4B versus 49.57 for OPD. Its macro Avg@8 was 36.13, indicating that the clearest gain is solution coverage rather than a universally large per-sample improvement.

- `2608.06065`: Over three runs, Qwen3-VL-8B GHD achieved AndroidLab Pass@1 of \(54.11\pm1.11\), compared with \(37.43\pm0.42\) for the matched GRPO baseline. The next screenshot was used only during training.

- `2608.08021`: Under matched Qwen3.5-9B training, Evidence-RL averaged 73.80 across nine benchmarks, versus 61.22 for correctness-only GRPO. Its object boxes were weak COCO-derived priors rather than question-specific ground-truth evidence.

- `2608.07886`: ConCor-1 reached 88.8 JointF1 on COCONut-PanCap versus 59.9 for the strongest listed competitor. The result uses the paper’s joint span–mask correspondence representation and associated annotations.

- `2608.07565`: Structured verification reduced visual inconsistency from 3.7% to 0.9% on held-out sessions. In a concurrent 14-day randomized product test, the full system reported CTR +32.70%, image take-away +16.32%, and turns per user +39.90% versus prompt engineering, all with reported \(p<0.05\).

- `2608.09873`: On Sci-VBench testmini, low-level automatic perceptual scores occupied a narrow 3.79–4.12 range across 16 generators, whereas automatic scientific and causal correctness ranged from 1.24 to 3.34. This is author-reported evidence that visual plausibility and scientific correctness are poorly interchangeable.

- `2608.08097`: In a two-node disaggregated Qwen3-8B setup with 192 requests and 2,048 output tokens, OasisKV delivered 1,204–1,210 tok/s at 24K and 884–888 tok/s at 32K, versus dense ranges of 550–554 and 384–386 tok/s. These results are specific to H100/PCIe Gen5 and 400-Gbps RoCE.

- `2608.08621`: Across 150 runs in one shared marketplace world, model mean final net worth ranged from $20,856 to $188,488 from $80,000 starting capital; the strongest expert-designed strategy reached $436,195. Ten-run ranking reliability was ICC(1,10)=0.944, but cross-world robustness was not measured.

- `2608.09853`: Across four physical manipulation tasks with 20 trials each, RynnValue-shaped online DSRL averaged 72.5% success versus 52.5% with Robometer and 48.8% with sparse reward. Reward coefficients differed between the learned reward models.

- `2608.08722`: Of 53 promoted in-distribution GPU-kernel wins, 16 regressed or failed on held-out configurations. This 30% rate is an existence result for 65 single-search sweeps on one Apple M1 Pro, not an estimate for adaptive optimization generally.

- `2608.03887`: After code-to-prose LoRA tuning of Llama-3-8B, Omega-S raised HumanEval pass@1 from 0.173 to 0.238 over ten seeds. Wikitext-2 performance was not recorded, so the stability–plasticity trade-off is unknown.

Editorially, the collection suggests three recurring patterns: scaffold and evaluation design can rival model choice in importance; privileged or counterfactual training signals are most credible when gated by executable evidence; and adaptive systems require held-out evaluation because optimization can improve visible scores while degrading transfer. These are cross-paper inferences, not individual authors’ universal claims.

## Open research directions

1. **Measure harness evolution causally** (`2608.09819`, `2608.08311`, `2608.09096`). Branch identical seeds into mutation-enabled and frozen-harness arms, hold models and task budgets fixed, and report held-out gains, regressions, cost, and rollback frequency across multiple runs.

2. **Build a shared harness-audit protocol** (`2608.09802`, `2608.07346`, `2608.09096`). Register the same harness revisions in A²E and evaluate them on Evo-Bench plus repository-refactoring tasks, using larger task samples and process metrics to test whether diagnostics predict held-out success.

3. **Stress-test self-generated distillation labels** (`2608.06296`, `2608.04419`). Inject controlled rates of confidently wrong consensus and noisy branch-verifier outcomes; compare hard gates, uncertainty weighting, and repeated probing at equal rollout-token budgets.

4. **Combine structured grounding with causal dependence tests** (`2608.07886`, `2608.08021`). Use ConCor-1 correspondence masks as Evidence-RL proposals and compare them with COCO boxes and human evidence regions on both answer accuracy and intervention-margin calibration.

5. **Evaluate privileged visual supervision beyond one-step evidence** (`2608.06065`, `2608.08021`). Compare immediate-next-frame, multi-step future, and counterfactual-region teachers on tasks with delayed consequences, measuring task success and rationale faithfulness separately.

6. **Test external memory under nonstationarity and contamination** (`2608.07169`, `2608.02508`, `2608.09043`). Introduce stale procedures, conflicting facts, and distribution shifts while varying bank size; measure recovery, erroneous-memory persistence, retrieval cost, and causal present/absent memory effects.

7. **Standardize constraint-aware generation evaluation** (`2608.09873`, `2608.07463`, `2608.06751`, `2608.07565`). Hold duration, resolution, generation budget, and evaluator constant, then separately score semantic correctness, geometry, temporal consistency, unintended motifs, and requested transformations.

8. **Audit portable agent state end to end** (`2608.09867`, `2608.03499`). In a consented sandbox, inject replayable state across owner boundaries and measure acquisition, propagation, final harm, and mitigation effectiveness using deterministic checks plus blinded human review.

9. **Test adaptive-evaluation defenses outside kernels** (`2608.09096`, `2608.08722`). Factorially vary disclosed versus secret probes, fixed versus refreshed held-out tasks, and correctness-only versus performance gates in a tool-use or retrieval optimizer.

10. **Separate feasibility from capability in preliminary systems** (`2608.09848`, `2608.08477`, `2608.08285`, `2608.07594`). For CEAA, compare matched architectures; for VectraYX, complete the remediated NoPE experiments; for Ego-OSCAR, train downstream policies; for Steerling, reproduce the missing central scaling and steering evaluations.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All 38 supplied current papers were analyzed and appear in the taxonomy or singleton notes, the at-a-glance table, and Sources.

Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical-connection stages were available. Their assignments were treated as editorial aids and retained only where the paper analyses supplied bilateral evidence. Historical edges were checked against both new-paper and cached previous-paper evidence. No listing-summary-only historical edge was used.

Collection failures affected the Hugging Face Markdown retrieval for `2608.09819`, `2608.09867`, `2608.07594`, `2608.03499`, `2608.09766`, and `2607.27637`. Alternative analysis evidence was nevertheless supplied for all six. The analysis for `2608.07594` is materially partial because roughly 118,000 source characters—including central evaluation sections—were omitted. The analyses for `2608.09867`, `2608.06751`, and `2608.05136` also report source truncation; conclusions from missing tables or appendices were not upgraded.

Many papers report single runs, incomplete numerical tables, no confidence intervals, internally judged metrics, or unclear artifact availability. Especially low-confidence conclusions include causal benefits attributed to self-evolution (`2608.09819`, `2608.08311`), central scale claims for Steerling (`2608.07594`), broad visual competence for VectraYX (`2608.08477`), and architectural performance claims for CEAA (`2608.09848`). Cross-paper comparisons were not made by comparing raw magnitudes across different metrics or settings.
