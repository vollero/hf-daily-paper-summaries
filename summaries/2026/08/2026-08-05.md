# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-05

## Executive summary

- Persistent agent state is both an opportunity and a liability. MerchantBench (`2607.28956`) finds severe year-long coherence failures; PAST-Bench (`2608.04003`) measures modest, model-dependent cross-session gains; SkillJack (`2608.03509`) shows that poisoned experiences can survive source deletion as derived skills.
- PCSD (`2608.01837`) and TurnSight (`2608.04007`) both refine credit assignment for tool-using agents, using locally persistent token support and execution-conditioned turn-level hindsight, respectively.
- Visual generation is becoming more structurally editable: JoyAI-Video-Edit (`2608.03974`) targets causal video streams, Hunyuan3D-Buffalo (`2608.02711`) unifies several 3D tasks, and UniWorld-Design (`2608.03971`) generates ordered semantic RGBA layers.
- AURORA-LM (`2608.02602`) and Any-OPD (`2608.03316`) address continuous-latent flow models from different directions: faithful text representations versus distillation across incompatible image-model latent spaces.
- OmniPack (`2608.03812`) and GROVE (`2608.02392`) tackle long audiovisual context at different stages—token compression during inference and hierarchical persistent memory after ingestion.
- CAPEval (`2608.02589`) and ExplainBench (`2607.26451`) replace holistic description scores with checks grounded in independently constructed evidence.
- Knowledge-Geometry Decoupling (`2608.02738`) is the collection’s clear singleton: it targets refreshable transfer for streaming recommendation and includes both offline and large-scale production evidence.
- Comparisons remain setting-dependent. Several studies rely on automated judges, unmatched compute or sampling protocols, synthetic environments, small regression samples, or missing uncertainty estimates.

## Main results worth noting

- JoyAI-Video-Edit (`2608.03974`) reports **30.19 FPS** for an 81-frame, batch-1, 720×1280 end-to-end run on one Nvidia B200. On LongV2VBench’s **229 one-minute tasks**, it scores **3.30 overall**, versus **1.71** for the strongest listed baseline overall. These results do not establish behavior beyond one minute or a resolution-matched quality comparison.
- AURORA-LM-S (`2608.02602`) reports **Gen-PPL 23.56 and MAUVE 0.890** on **1,000 generated 1,024-token OpenWebText samples**, compared with AR at **39.40/0.851**. At eight ODE steps, self-trajectory consistency raises MAUVE from **0.116±0.006 to 0.672±0.038** in the reported ablation.
- MerchantBench (`2607.28956`) reports that Hermes Qwen3.7-Max ends the **365-day simulation** with **RMB 59.46k** mean final net assets, versus **RMB 217.61k** for three novice humans. The LLM configuration’s coefficient of variation is **55.1% over three runs**, so the mean should not be read as a stable ranking.
- Hunyuan3D-Buffalo (`2608.02711`) reports Edit3D-Bench average **CD 0.0091 and F1 0.6515** with 3D-VLM conditioning; the best listed baseline values are **CD 0.0684** and **F1 0.2729**, achieved by different baselines. Its text-to-3D preference study uses 100 prompts but lacks reported participant and uncertainty details.
- PCSD (`2608.01837`) reaches **90.6% ALFWorld Overall** with Qwen2.5-3B, versus **75.0% GRPO** and **84.4% SDAR**, under the shared evaluation protocol without inference-time skills. On `valid_unseen`, the corresponding result is **86.7%**, versus **70.9%** and **72.7%**.
- PAST-Bench (`2608.04003`) finds Overall persistence gaps of **+0.13 to +0.24** across seven models under Hermes. Hermes+ raises \(\Delta\) from **+0.13 to +0.15** and Mech from **0.64 to 0.73** with MiniMax-M2.7, but the gap change is smaller than three-run variability: **0.13±0.04 versus 0.15±0.06**.
- OmniPack (`2608.03812`) retains **52.2 versus 54.6** average benchmark score—**95.6%**—at **15% pre-LLM and 7.5% final retention**, using **10.0% of estimated LLM-token FLOPs**. On an Nvidia H20, reported prefill falls from **1.512 s to 0.339 s**; this is not full-system accounting.
- UniWorld-Design (`2608.03971`) improves four-layer Crello decomposition over Qwen-Image-Layered: **RGB L1 0.1264±0.0980 versus 0.2014±0.0937**, and **Alpha Soft IoU 0.7325±0.1622 versus 0.5454±0.1577**, across 512 designs. Its T2RGBA model leads CLIP Score but trails OmniAlpha on every reported reference-fidelity metric.
- CAPEval (`2608.02589`) reports conditional OLS associations across ten captioners: Coverage coefficients of **+0.118 (\(p=0.026\))** and **+0.215 (\(p=0.047\))** for two understanding pipelines; Precision coefficients of **+0.189** and **+0.235**, both **\(p<0.001\)**, for two generation pipelines. The sample is too small to treat these as settled causal effects.
- Any-OPD (`2608.03316`) raises **PickScore 0.846→0.884** and **HPSv3 9.12→10.97** when distilling FLUX.1-dev into SD3.5-Medium at 1024×1024. Teacher and student use 50 and 40 Euler steps, respectively, so this is not an equal-inference-budget efficiency result.
- TurnSight (`2608.04007`) reaches an overall average of **42.02** with Qwen3-8B, versus **39.03** for MatchTIR. Its component aggregates are **46.92 FTRL**, **37.56 BFCL**, and **41.58 ToolHop accuracy**, all higher than MatchTIR’s reported counterparts.
- SkillJack (`2608.03509`) reports **40.0% skill-based triggering** and **32.0% post-deletion triggering** across 25 attack-oriented tasks. This means **80.0% of skill-triggered cases persisted** after deleting source records, within the tested single-model proxy-based setup.
- GROVE (`2608.02392`) reports **EgoServe Macro-F1 12.6 versus EgoMemo 8.0**, **OVO-Bench overall 67.5 versus strongest listed non-human 62.6**, and **StreamingBench 65.1 versus 57.2**. Different backbones and task-specific information prevent attributing all gains solely to memory structure.
- Knowledge-Geometry Decoupling (`2608.02738`) reports a two-week Shopee A/B test with **GMV/user +1.75%** and **advertising revenue/user +1.53%**, both **\(p<0.01\)**, using control and treatment buckets each exceeding ten million users. Daily training grows from one to two hours and serving memory doubles.
- ExplainBench (`2607.26451`) finds that **79.30% of non-passing patches** were nevertheless described such that the evaluator predicted the bug-reproducing test would pass. ExplanationAuditAgent improves explanation scores for all five agents by **10.9% on average**, at a reported **$0.05 per explanation**.

## Open research directions

1. **Test persistent-agent memory over realistic durations.** Motivated by `2607.28956`, `2608.04003`, and `2608.02392`: run weeks-long, human-authored task streams with matched persistence-on/off conditions and measure performance, retrieval cost, staleness, interference, and pathway necessity.

2. **Make skill deletion transitive and auditable.** Motivated by `2608.03509` and `2608.04003`: maintain provenance graphs from experiences to derived and recursively transformed skills, then delete or quarantine sources and verify both artifact revocation and downstream behavior.

3. **Compare token-, turn-, and state-value credit directly.** Motivated by `2608.01837` and `2608.04007`: apply PCSD-style local weighting and TurnSight-style turn modulation to the same RL backbone, trajectories, rewards, teacher compute, and seeds; add controlled misleading or delayed feedback.

4. **Evaluate causal video editing beyond one minute.** Motivated by `2608.03974`: run 10-minute and one-hour streams with controlled revisits, camera cuts, instruction changes, and fast motion; plot source fidelity, edit strength, identity drift, memory, and latency over elapsed time.

5. **Separate representation gains from prior or distillation gains.** Motivated by `2608.02602` and `2608.03316`: swap autoencoders or frozen bridge representations while holding the denoiser, training tokens, solver evaluations, total parameters, and wall-clock budget fixed.

6. **Quantify editable visual structure, not only final appearance.** Motivated by `2608.02711` and `2608.03971`: construct hierarchical 2D/3D edit benchmarks with target-region, untouched-region, hidden-content, part-reassembly, and repeated-edit consistency metrics.

7. **Measure compression end to end.** Motivated by `2608.03812`: include encoders, projectors, selection operations, text tokens, decoding, peak memory, energy, and time to first output across multiple accelerators and open-ended generation tasks.

8. **Manipulate caption breadth and factuality independently.** Motivated by `2608.02589`: create matched captions differing only by controlled omissions or injected errors, then train understanding and generation models with multiple seeds to test whether Coverage and Precision are causal rather than proxies.

9. **Validate explanations with human reviewers.** Motivated by `2607.26451`: compare original and audited explanations in blinded code review, measuring faulty-patch detection, review time, confidence calibration, and function-local understanding.

10. **Test recommendation refresh under other drift regimes.** Motivated by `2608.02738`: reproduce test-before-train evaluation on another platform or domain, vary refresh cadence using measured drift, and compare orthogonal ACR with angular, norm-bounded, and unconstrained residual adaptations.

Particularly tentative conclusions include CAPEval’s ten-captioner regressions, PAST-Bench’s small aggregate Hermes+ difference, Any-OPD’s generality beyond one student and two teachers, SkillJack’s real-world execution risk, and Hunyuan3D-Buffalo’s generation-to-editing transfer claim based on one qualitative example.
