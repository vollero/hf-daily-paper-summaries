# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-05

## Executive summary

- Persistent agents are a major theme: MerchantBench (2607.28956), PAST-Bench (2608.04003), and ContinualSkillBench (2608.03874) distinguish long-horizon activity or sequential gains from genuine, attributable reuse of retained state.
- Persistence also expands the attack surface. SkillJack (2608.03509) finds that poisoned histories can survive as routable skills after source deletion, while AntiSkillBench (2608.03700) measures privacy leakage and impersonation from distilled persona histories.
- Several RL papers replace coarse trajectory credit with localized evidence: PCSD (2608.01837), TurnSight (2608.04007), DASH (2607.00482), and ReflectRL (2608.03972) use token neighborhoods, tool outcomes, verified answer checkpoints, or expert failures.
- Streaming video generation converges on bounded causal memory. JoyAI-Video-Edit (2608.03974) reports real-time 720×1280 editing, while MiniWorld (2608.01127) offers a lower-resource, from-scratch world-model baseline; both still show limits beyond tested rollout horizons.
- Efficiency work targets distinct bottlenecks: OmniPack (2608.03812) compresses audio-visual tokens, RestoreKV (2608.01247) synthesizes replacement cache states, and ARCHead (2608.02703) compresses the output projection. Their metrics and savings are not directly comparable.
- Editable output structure appears across modalities: Hunyuan3D-Buffalo (2608.02711) targets editable 3D objects, UniWorld-Design (2608.03971) produces semantic RGBA layers, and PosterMELD (2608.02218) exports editable poster elements.
- Grounding and verification recur as alternatives to text-only reasoning: STAMPlus (2608.02791) and CURV (2608.02833) bind language to spatial regions, while CALVER (2608.03506) verifies causal candidates symbolically.
- Strong headline gains frequently come with narrow settings, model-judge dependence, missing uncertainty estimates, normalized rather than absolute metrics, or incomplete table retrieval. Cross-paper numerical ranking would therefore be misleading.

## Main results worth noting

- **Long-horizon agents:** In MerchantBench (2607.28956), Hermes Qwen3.7-Max ended a 365-day simulation with mean final net assets of RMB 59.46k, 27.3% of the RMB 217.61k mean for three novice humans. Its coefficient of variation was 55.1% over three runs, so the mean does not imply stable superiority among LLM configurations.

- **Persistence attribution:** PAST-Bench (2608.04003) reports overall persistence-on/off gaps of +0.13 to +0.24 across seven models under Hermes. Hermes and nanobot both reached +0.13 with MiniMax-M2.7, but mechanism-evidence scores differed, 0.64 versus 0.57. ContinualSkillBench (2608.03874) found normalized gains in 14/15 model-domain combinations, yet pure ICL averaged 0.605 versus 0.602 for explicit skill maintenance in its limited three-domain GPT-5.3-Codex comparison.

- **Persistent-skill risks:** SkillJack (2608.03509) reports 32.0% post-deletion triggering across 25 attack tasks, equal to 80% of its skill-based triggers. AntiSkillBench (2608.03700) reports roughly 55%–66% overall Skill Coverage across the tested model/protocol combinations; its evidence is synthetic and model-judged.

- **Streaming video:** JoyAI-Video-Edit (2608.03974) reports 30.19 FPS for an 81-frame, 720×1280 batch-1 run on one B200, and 3.30 overall on 229 one-minute LongV2VBench tasks versus 1.71 for the strongest listed streaming baseline. MiniWorld (2608.01127) reports 7.29 versus 3.31 FPS and 4.86 versus 74.0 seconds first-chunk latency for rolling-cache versus full-window inference on its 1B DROID setting.

- **Diffusion language modeling:** AURORA-LM-S (2608.02602) reports Gen-PPL 23.56 and MAUVE 0.890 on 1,000 OpenWebText generations of 1,024 tokens. LLaDA MoE v2 (2608.03457) reports a 58.60 average across 15 benchmarks for a 30B-A3B model pretrained on 23.5T tokens. These results address different representation, compute, and evaluation questions and should not be ranked against each other.

- **Inference compression:** OmniPack (2608.03812) retains 52.2 versus 54.6 uncompressed average score across five benchmarks at 15%/7.5% retention, with 7.3T versus 73.2T estimated LLM-token FLOPs and 0.339 versus 1.512 seconds prefill on an H20. RestoreKV (2608.01247) raises Qwen3-4B RULER-4K accuracy from 38.2 to 73.2 at a 5% KV ratio under a matched final cache. ARCHead (2608.02703) reaches 1.007 relative perplexity at 25.6% of BF16 head storage on Qwen3-8B. Each compresses a different object.

- **Fine-grained RL supervision:** PCSD (2608.01837) reaches 90.6% ALFWorld overall success versus 75.0% for GRPO with Qwen2.5-3B. TurnSight (2608.04007) reaches 42.02 overall versus MatchTIR’s 39.03 with Qwen3-8B across FTRL, BFCL, and ToolHop. DASH (2607.00482) raises the four-benchmark math average from 58.13% to 59.45% over DR-GRPO but lowers AIME25 from 50.3% to 47.5%. ReflectRL (2608.03972) raises Qwen2.5-Math-7B GRPO OOD average from 20.9 to 40.0 while some individual tasks regress.

- **Executable selection:** CALVER (2608.03506) reaches 42.1% on 1,111 matched CLEAR candidate pools versus 30.5% for the closest generic selector, with a reported +11.6-point difference and 95% CI [8.2, 15.0]. At \(K=32\), it reaches 57.9% versus plurality at 32.5%. Exact solving remains better when graph extraction is reliable.

- **Robotics robustness:** Push-Wiper (2608.00730) reports gathering score 89.88±6.80 versus 44.98±19.44 for one-shot pushing and 32.64±34.76 for coverage wiping, across 40 trials per method. ST-WAM (2607.28993) reports 72.8% zero-shot LIBERO-Plus success versus Fast-WAM’s 51.5% over 10,030 shifted cases and 61.5% versus 25.8% across its physical shifted-condition evaluation.

- **Specialized video analysis:** ChildGait-Video (2608.00371) reaches 84% accuracy and 0.83 F1 for each limb on the patient-disjoint CGV split, versus VideoMAE-v2 at 69%/72% accuracy. V2N (2608.03419) raises PianoVAM 50-ms +Off F1 from 45.9% to 89.5% versus PPAN, but cross-dataset onset performance falls near zero under keyboard-geometry mismatch.

- **Structured artifacts:** UniWorld-Design (2608.03971) reduces Crello RGB L1 from 0.2014 to 0.1264 and raises Alpha Soft IoU from 0.5454 to 0.7325 versus Qwen-Image-Layered, while losing on alpha cleanliness. PosterMELD (2608.02218) reaches 81.3% print-ready rate across 621 requests at $0.38 mean cost, below GPT-Image-2’s 85.2% and Codex+Skill’s 82.8% in the reported comparison.

- **Domain-specific benchmarks:** FININDICES (2607.28661) shows Gemini-3.1-Pro Table-Index exact match falling from 70.70% with formula hints to 38.22% without. LegalPincite (2608.03756) contributes 1,639 case queries and 14,504 paragraph queries in its chronological test split; its strongest lexical method differs by retrieval level.

## Open research directions

1. **Causally identify useful persistent state** — motivated by 2608.04003 and 2608.03874. Delete, corrupt, or substitute individual memories and skills before matched later tasks, measuring both outcome changes and mechanism telemetry.

2. **Build provenance-aware descendant revocation** — motivated by 2608.03509 and 2608.03700. Maintain a history-to-skill dependency graph, delete or quarantine source traces, and test whether all derived, merged, and paraphrased descendants cease influencing behavior without destroying benign utility.

3. **Measure very-long video drift under a shared protocol** — motivated by 2608.03974 and 2608.01127. Run identical controlled streams for one minute, ten minutes, and one hour while varying sink retention, recent-window length, history corruption, and training on self-generated context.

4. **Separate retrieval from synthesis quality in research agents** — motivated by 2608.03979, 2608.02751, and 2608.02392. Use a frozen web snapshot and annotated evidence chains; independently score document recall, section selection, video grounding, citation fidelity, and final synthesis.

5. **Compare credit signals at matched compute** — motivated by 2608.01837, 2608.04007, 2607.00482, and 2608.03972. Hold policy, rollouts, rewards, and teacher-forward budget fixed while comparing token persistence, turn hindsight, executable checkpoints, and failed-expert reflection.

6. **Evaluate compression end to end** — motivated by 2608.03812, 2608.01247, and 2608.02703. Measure total latency, peak memory, energy, and output quality when audio-video token, KV-cache, and output-head compression are applied individually and jointly across several accelerators.

7. **Test editable-generation fidelity after real edits** — motivated by 2608.02711, 2608.03971, and 2608.02218. Ask users to move, replace, remove, or restyle generated components, then score hidden-content reconstruction, local preservation, structural validity, and repair time.

8. **Expand robustness beyond appearance changes** — motivated by 2608.00730 and 2607.28993. Cross friction, payload, rheology, geometry, lighting, viewpoint, and embodiment in a factorial robot study, reporting success, contact-force violations, latency, and failure mode.

9. **Validate grounding causally** — motivated by 2608.02791 and 2608.02833. Mask, perturb, or counterfactually alter predicted regions and test whether answers and masks change consistently; compare generated regions, human regions, attention maps, and no grounding.

10. **Disentangle reasoning from serialization** — motivated by 2607.28661 and 2607.26451. Score extracted values or behavioral claims independently from output format, then separately assess schema compliance and explanation readability with human reviewers.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All 38 supplied current papers were analyzed and appear in the taxonomy or singleton notes, the at-a-glance table, and the Sources section.

Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical connections were available. They were treated as editorial aids and retained only where both endpoints supplied concrete supporting evidence. Eight cross-day edges were retained from cached analyses; service observation dates were not used as chronology or influence evidence.

Collection reported unavailable Hugging Face Markdown for 2608.02602, 2608.02713, 2607.28661, 2608.03756, and 2608.02833. Full PAPER_ANALYSIS blocks were nevertheless supplied for all five, so they remain covered; the collection failures indicate source-acquisition fragility rather than absent paper analyses.

Several analyses note missing rendered table bodies, especially for 2608.03979, 2608.02738, 2608.01837, 2608.03457, 2608.03316, 2607.26451, 2608.03874, 2607.28993, and 2608.02751. Conclusions relying on prose summaries rather than visible complete tables should be treated as medium confidence.

Other material uncertainties include absent repeated-run variance, model-judge dependence, dynamic-web reproducibility, incomplete artifact-release verification, synthetic benchmarks, and narrow model or hardware coverage. Notable internal inconsistencies include VideoDR-Bench’s reported size (100 versus 200), FININDICES’ 70.70% versus 70.07% prose/table discrepancy, and unsupported broad wording around some “best” claims. Concrete table values and qualified settings were preferred where available.
