# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-18

## Executive summary

- Agent reliability is a central theme: StateM (2608.15089), ClawGym II (2608.16798), and ACID-Agent (2608.13900) respectively target runtime control, black-box policy training, and transactional execution.
- Evaluation increasingly moves beyond endpoint accuracy. AutoResearchEval (2608.14905), PACE-Bench (2608.14441), R³-Bench (2608.16033), HarnessEval-W (2608.16859), TRACE-Bench (2608.16765), and VideoGAIA (2608.14718) diagnose process or capability-level failures.
- Streaming-video work favors deployment-efficient internalization: MOSS-VL (2608.15045), StreamOPD (2608.16320), and IVT (2608.15869) improve causal or proactive reasoning without requiring explicit visual intermediates at inference.
- Generation research spans routing, representations, and structure: GenRouter (2608.16721) routes image workflows; the pixel-diffusion study (2608.16887) trades latent decoding for RGB post-training; GRNEdit (2608.16328) uses binary evidence; MegaParts (2608.14783) and HiFi-BRep (2608.16485) enforce explicit 3D organization.
- Several papers show that apparently favorable aggregate behavior can conceal a changed operating point or invalid structure: verifier leniency (2608.16003), MCQ order sensitivity (2608.11947), synthetic-survey invalidity (2608.14606), and selective-risk calibration failures (2608.14639).
- Scientific and mathematical search combines learned proposals with explicit optimization in LDM (2608.15669), TTP-D/LISA (2608.16435), and the newly certified matrix-multiplication bound (2608.16884).
- Dataset contributions are substantial but unevenly validated: WorldRover (2608.15659) supplies 21.9M annotated synthetic frames without downstream model results, while relational, enterprise-QA, document, and visual-world benchmarks provide direct comparative evaluations.
- Safety work emphasizes distributions and system context rather than binary refusal: HarmProfile (2608.14577), cognition-induced risk taxonomy (2608.15304), hosted-API verification (2608.16391), and RAG-poisoning detection (2608.06947).
- Many headline improvements remain environment-specific, use unmatched public baselines, omit uncertainty estimates, or bundle several interventions; cross-paper numerical rankings would therefore be misleading.

## Main results worth noting

- StateM (2608.15089) reports 424/445 raw successes, or 95.28%, on Terminal-Bench 2.1 with GPT-5.6 Sol xhigh and a frozen GPT-5.5-developed profile. This is not a finalized leaderboard result: rescoring four or nine disputed trajectories gives 94.38% or 93.26%.
- ClawGym II (2608.16798) reports harness-matched Pass@1 gains of 9.98 points under OpenClaw and 14.81 points under Claude Code on ClawGym-Bench. Cross-harness performance is weaker, supporting a harness-specific rather than universal interpretation.
- ACID-Agent (2608.13900) raises Qwen-backed KramaBench score from 64.0 to 74.6; on the Environment-domain ablation, removing failed-step isolation lowers 90.0 to 78.3.
- AutoResearchEval (2608.14905) identifies uncorrected self-awareness in 660/800 trajectories, overclaiming in 625/800, and method–conclusion disconnects in 620/800. These are judge-attributed frequencies from one rollout per system-task pair, not population prevalence.
- PACE-Bench (2608.14441) remains difficult under 20 attempts: Reflexion with Qwen3-14B reaches 35.9% Pass@2, while the strongest reported frontier configuration reaches 66.7% Pass@2 only on the 24-pair Statics subset.
- R³-Bench (2608.16033) finds the sampled offline oracle at least matches the contest policy in all 72 evaluated cells and strictly exceeds it in 71. The mean tool-free headroom is 1.16 correct answers, but the oracle is not an online policy.
- MOSS-VL (2608.15045) leads the listed OVO-Bench, OmniMMI, and ProactiveVideoQA averages under each system’s reported protocol. StreamOPD (2608.16320) raises its 4B student from 77.87% to 83.91% on StreamingBench, while IVT (2608.15869) reduces mean end-to-end latency from 6.56 seconds for Visual CoT to 1.22 seconds and beats matched answer-only training in all six reported settings.
- GenRouter (2608.16721) matches HybridGen on the mixed set—73.52 versus 73.53—while reporting $1.37 versus $6.27 in routing and primitive overhead. Terminal generator cost and latency are excluded.
- The pixel-space diffusion study (2608.16887) reports 0.20-second four-step pixel inference versus 0.95 seconds for its latent comparator at 1024×1024 on one H100. Most underlying quality scores are absent from the supplied extraction.
- MegaParts (2608.14783) sustains 0.77 valid-generation rate for 200–300-part objects but requires a mean 6,735 seconds of autoregressive generation in that bin. HiFi-BRep (2608.16485) reaches 72.20% DeepCAD validity, versus 43.20% for DTGBrepGen, but loses to that comparator on several ABC distributional metrics.
- ConceptFormer (2608.15698) reports 75.97 average NDCG@10 across six visual-document retrieval benchmarks, compared with 65.05 for the strongest listed visual baseline. Its evidence-box supervision is generated by another VLM and is not independently audited.
- NaviDC-OCR (2608.12898) reaches 96.87 overall on OmniDocBench v1.6, narrowly above OvisOCR2’s 96.58, but does not lead every degraded-document track and provides no component ablation.
- The verifier-context study (2608.16003) observes lower false-alarm rates in every one of 15 model–wording cells after audit–repair context. Criterion shifts survive correction in 13/15, while no \(d'\) change does; this supports threshold movement more strongly than improved discrimination.
- VerifyDoc (2608.14639) illustrates the cost of stronger validity: document-iid LTT averages only 0.060 coverage and accepts nothing in 19/40 splits, whereas a practical learned-score tier reaches 0.318 coverage at 0.096 risk but exceeds the 0.10 target in 47.5% of resplits.
- The synthetic-respondent audit (2608.14606) finds a best LLM psychometric score of 0.714 against a held-out-human ceiling of 0.825; models trained on synthetic responses average \(R^2=-0.18\) when predicting held-out human outcomes, versus 0.277 for human-trained models.
- D-SCAN (2608.06947) reports AUC above 0.83 and F1 above 0.77 on all three poisoning benchmarks, including with a single generation AUC above 0.80. This evidence is confined to white-box attention and the tested PoisonedRAG setup.
- PRISM (2608.15037) improves UrbanSound8K accuracy from 58.77% zero-shot to 71.71% under additive noise, but degrades on the DCASE device-shift test from 17.36% to 15.63%, marking a clear boundary of its affine-noise assumption.
- The matrix-multiplication work (2608.16884) reports the certified asymptotic bound \(\omega<2.371177\), improving the previous 2.371339 bound. It makes no claim of faster finite-size multiplication software.

## Open research directions

1. **Separate harness components from bundled profiles** (2608.15089, 2608.13900, 2608.16798). Run matched factorial ablations of state refresh, checks, isolation, recovery, token correction, and branch filtering using identical models, tasks, seeds, and budgets.

2. **Learn transferable shared-budget schedulers** (2608.16033, 2608.14441). Train an online controller from visible progress and remaining budget, then test it on held-out models, domains, suite sizes, and simulator shifts against both fixed schedulers and the sampled oracle.

3. **Measure long-stream memory versus recency under one protocol** (2608.15045, 2608.16320, 2608.15869, 2608.14718). Evaluate recent-window, latent-prediction, append-only, and selective-memory systems at matched frame rates, tool access, latency, and evidence gaps.

4. **Turn diagnostic generation operators into routing signals** (2608.16765, 2608.16721). Use TRACE Anchor/Disentangle/Apply/Compose demands and outcomes to route held-out prompts, then measure generator-inclusive quality, cost, latency, and human preference.

5. **Test synthetic visual-world assets end to end** (2608.15659, 2608.16859, 2608.15265). Train with and without matched WorldRover variants, evaluate controlled persistence changes through HarnessEval-W, and verify whether gains transfer to independently captured video or new 3D asset libraries.

6. **Protect satisfied objectives during adaptive RL** (2608.16072, 2608.16320). Combine saturation-aware allocation or cue-gated distillation with explicit constraints or gradient projection, then measure target improvement, auxiliary retention, abstention, and variance across seeds.

7. **Calibrate automated verifiers under both contextual and statistical shift** (2608.16003, 2608.14639, 2608.14905). Cross prior-context interventions with document-cluster-aware calibration and human adjudication to produce risk–coverage curves rather than a single accuracy score.

8. **Evaluate structured 3D validity beyond local constraints** (2608.14783, 2608.16485). Add global topology, intersection, articulation, and material checks while stratifying by part/token count; report pre- and post-CAD-kernel validity, memory, and latency.

9. **Validate learned–classical search outside digital oracles** (2608.15669, 2608.16435, 2608.16884). Use matched-budget repeated runs, independent certification, and—where applicable—small physical or wet-lab experiments to distinguish proposal quality, optimizer quality, and evaluator exploitation.

10. **Test external validity of latent-interface adaptation** (2608.15984, 2608.16143, 2608.15037). Evaluate unseen cameras, rigs, audio encoders, devices, non-additive corruptions, and natural rather than imitation data, with ground-truth geometry or independently sampled future environments.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All 42 supplied current-paper analyses were included. Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical-connections stages were available and treated as fallible aids; retained edges were checked against the corresponding paper analyses, and historical edges used cached analyses at both endpoints. No historical edge based only on a listing summary was retained.

Collection reported Hugging Face Markdown failures for 29 papers: 2608.15089, 2608.16859, 2608.15669, 2608.15265, 2608.15930, 2608.16798, 2608.14905, 2608.13900, 2608.16319, 2608.14441, 2608.16721, 2608.14577, 2608.14783, 2608.15304, 2608.16328, 2608.16391, 2608.16033, 2608.14718, 2608.16320, 2608.15869, 2608.15698, 2608.16003, 2608.16435, 2608.15659, 2608.15984, 2608.14667, 2608.16485, and 2608.15037. Their analyses relied on other retrieved paper material where available.

Explicitly truncated current analyses include 2608.15669, 2608.15930, 2608.14905, and 2608.14577. Several other analyses note missing table cells, figure values, appendices, artifact verification, or implementation details. Common uncertainty sources are absent repeated-run variance, unmatched public baselines, model-judge dependence, bundled interventions, incomplete cost accounting, and unverified release claims. Lower-confidence conclusions include causal explanations for metacognitive failure, attention collapse, capability–diversity association, and benefits attributed to individual components without ablations.
