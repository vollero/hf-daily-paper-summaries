# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-18

## Executive summary

- Agent infrastructure is the day’s clearest theme: StateM (`2608.15089`), ACID-Agent (`2608.13900`), and ClawGym II (`2608.16798`) externalize state, validate transitions, or reconstruct opaque trajectories to improve long-horizon reliability.
- Verifier-grounded training produced substantial author-reported gains in 3D-world construction (`2608.15265`), GUI automation (`2608.15930`), and black-box harness optimization (`2608.16798`), but each result remains tied to its environment, verifier, and execution protocol.
- Evaluation itself is under scrutiny. Papers diagnose context-induced verifier leniency (`2608.16003`), option-order effects (`2608.11947`), invalid synthetic survey populations (`2608.14606`), clustered selective-risk failures (`2608.14639`), and incomplete process evaluation of research agents (`2608.14905`).
- Streaming-video work spans architecture, latent prediction, and post-training: MOSS-VL (`2608.15045`) emphasizes continuous perception and response timing; IVT (`2608.15869`) internalizes future-state prediction; StreamOPD (`2608.16320`) improves a fixed four-frame causal policy through on-policy distillation.
- Visual-generation research divides between efficiency and diagnosis. Pixel-space diffusion (`2608.16887`), GRNEdit (`2608.16328`), and GenRouter (`2608.16721`) reduce different parts of the generation stack, while HarnessEval-W (`2608.16859`) and TRACE-Bench (`2608.16765`) localize world-state and reference-binding failures.
- Structured 3D generation advanced along complementary axes: MegaParts (`2608.14783`) scales explicit part generation to as many as 300 components, while HiFi-BRep (`2608.16485`) jointly decodes CAD geometry and topology under structural constraints.
- Several benchmarks expose large remaining gaps: the best VideoGAIA result is 58.30% pass@1 (`2608.14718`); PACE-Bench’s strongest full-benchmark configuration reaches 35.9% Pass@2 (`2608.14441`); and EntLORE retains a 30.4% latent-question residual even with gold documents (`2608.10679`).
- The collection also includes notable singletons: a certified matrix-multiplication bound (`2608.16884`), low-cost used-GPU serving (`2608.14614`), truck–drone optimization (`2608.16435`), mechanistic attention analysis (`2608.15022`), synthetic world-exploration data (`2608.15659`), harmful-output profiling (`2608.14577`), and acoustic-shift adaptation (`2608.15037`).

## Main results worth noting

- `2608.15089`: GPT-5.6 Sol with the frozen StateM profile records 424/445 successes, or 95.28% raw Terminal-Bench 2.1 accuracy. Treating four identified invalid rewarded trajectories as failures gives 94.38%; treating all nine possible reward-hacking cases as failures gives 93.26%. These are not finalized matched-control leaderboard results.
- `2608.15265`: On the 254-query VWE-Bench test split, VibeWorlder-30B-A3B improves from 13.6% base Pass@1 to 34.5% after SFT and 59.3% after RL. Collision pass rates remain only 59–68%.
- `2608.15930`: UI-Mate-27B reports 77.0% on OSWorld-Verified and 66.2% on WindowsAgentArena. On 33 paired OSWorkerBench tasks, a same-task demonstration raises strict success from 17.17% to 35.35%; this does not establish related-task demonstration transfer.

- `2608.16859`: With the same videos and GPT-5.5 evaluator configuration, HarnessEval-W raises physical-transition pairwise accuracy from 31.9% to 71.7% versus WBench and reduces draws from 52.2% to 1.8%; human validation is limited to physical and intentional transitions.
- `2608.16003`: A prior audit–repair episode reduces false alarms in all 15 model–wording combinations by 2.76–11.50 percentage points. Criterion \(c\) moves toward leniency in all 15, while no \(d'\) change survives correction.
- `2608.14606`: In the synthetic-survey audit, the best LLM reaches PSS 0.714 versus a 0.825 held-out-human ceiling. Ridge models trained on synthetic responses average \(R^2=-0.18\) when predicting held-out human work performance, versus 0.277 for human-trained data.

- `2608.15045`, `2608.15869`, `2608.16320`: MOSS-VL leads three listed streaming averages, IVT cuts mean method-pipeline latency from 6.56 s for Visual CoT to 1.22 s, and StreamOPD raises StreamingBench accuracy from 77.87% to 83.91% before ST-CueGate reaches 84.55%. ## Open research directions

1. **Isolate stateful-agent reliability mechanisms** (`2608.15089`, `2608.13900`, `2608.16798`). Run matched factorial ablations of state refresh, transition validation, isolation, retry, trajectory filtering, and durable memory using identical models, tasks, budgets, and finalized adjudication.

2. **Learn credit assignment for branches and long trajectories** (`2608.16072`, `2608.15265`, `2608.16798`, `2608.16320`). Compare objective-, turn-, token-, and branch-level credit on the same executable tasks, measuring success, variance, auxiliary-objective regression, and training cost.

3. **Validate hierarchical visual evaluators independently** (`2608.16859`, `2608.16765`, `2608.15265`). Collect blinded human labels across every metric or operator, swap evaluator-model families, and ablate routing, decomposition, evidence tools, and parent validation.

4. **Measure genuine long-horizon streaming behavior** (`2608.15045`, `2608.15869`, `2608.16320`, `2608.14718`). Introduce mid-generation state changes and hour-scale evidence gaps; compare recent-window, compressed-memory, and persistent-memory policies on correction delay, recall, false alerts, and latency.

5. **Test efficiency claims end to end** (`2608.16887`, `2608.16721`, `2608.14614`, `2608.14783`). Include generator inference, preprocessing, orchestration, queueing, energy, and tail latency under equal-quality or equal-throughput constraints rather than component-only timings.

6. **Establish transfer beyond curated generation environments** (`2608.15265`, `2608.14783`, `2608.16485`, `2608.16143`, `2608.15984`, `2608.15659`). Train on current assets or synthetic scenes and test without adaptation on independent libraries, styles, engines, camera conditions, rigs, and real captured data.

7. **Separate protocol effects from evaluated capability** (`2608.16003`, `2608.11947`, `2608.14606`, `2608.14639`, `2608.16391`). Repeat evaluations under matched presentation, repeated identical-order controls, independent labels, multiple judges, and synchronized serving windows; report calibration and utility, not accuracy alone.

8. **Convert process diagnosis into interventions** (`2608.14905`, `2608.16033`, `2608.14441`, `2608.14667`). Randomize agents to correction gates, learned budget schedulers, causal feedback summaries, or uncertainty-triggered human questions, then measure corrected errors, task quality, interaction burden, and compute.

9. **Validate empirical search outside digital or heuristic oracles** (`2608.15669`, `2608.16884`, `2608.16435`). For LDM, run a small preregistered physical campaign; for TTP-D, compare against stronger exact bounds; for the matrix optimizer, release and independently execute the exact certificate.

10. **Test robustness of latent and attention-based mechanisms** (`2608.15022`, `2608.15698`, `2608.06947`, `2608.15037`). Replicate across architectures and domains while perturbing evidence boxes, attention implementations, acoustic shifts, and donor pairings; distinguish correlation, decodability, and causal use.
