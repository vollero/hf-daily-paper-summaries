# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-12

## Executive summary

- Agent persistence was a major theme, spanning conceptual human-centered assistance (2608.10915), longitudinal evaluation (2608.10875), personal-information retrieval (2608.10692), scaffold evolution (2608.07645), and skill-library compression (2608.11079).
- Current assistants remain weak on extended, stateful work: VibeLifeBench’s best tested model scored 32.5/100 avg@3 across 200 simulated multi-week tasks (2608.10875), while SPIEval’s best configuration reached 57.3% exact accuracy across 250 cross-app tasks (2608.10692).
- Selective evidence acquisition repeatedly improved efficiency. Post-retrieval pruning cut deep-research tokens from 375.4k to 114.6k with judged quality changing from 57.83 to 56.62 (2608.08389); InSight-doc used 66% fewer tokens than its 140-DPI baseline while scoring 70.6% versus 69.4% average accuracy at 70 DPI (2608.10628).
- CoinRAG reported 41.7 average F1 at 81 ms P99 time-to-first-token under a 100 ms budget, compared with 39.6 F1 and 91 ms for its strongest reported baseline under that constraint (2608.07458).
- Visual generative priors were converted into explicit geometry in two distinct settings: general dynamic 4D scenes (2608.10744) and articulated objects observed at rest (2607.27749). Both remain limited by proxy metrics, model compatibility, or input/protocol qualifications.
- Deployment-oriented compression covered recommendation MoEs and visual retrieval. UniMoMo reported 1.28×–1.63× speedups for four-expert checkpoints while retaining 99.92%–102.30% of source mean NDCG@10 after adaptation (2608.08627); DistilVDR reduced an 8.1B teacher to a 524M dual encoder, reaching 61.74 average NDCG@5 (2608.10636).
- Controlled stress tests exposed failures hidden by ordinary endpoint scores. Decoding-Level Taboo found family- and scale-dependent robustness to forced token divergence (2608.09900), while JigShape showed a sharp puzzle-size scaling cliff despite high supervised performance at \(4\times4\) (2607.27670).
- Several contributions are frameworks, surveys, or diagnostics rather than validated end-to-end systems, including Combodied Agents (2608.10915), the co-evolution survey (2608.10299), and the checkpoint-specific PLGA collapse audit (2608.10288).

## Main results worth noting

- **Longitudinal agents:** On 200 VibeLifeBench tasks, Claude Opus 5 achieved the best reported avg@3 of 32.5/100; across all tested models, proactivity pass rates were 16.0%–33.6%, propagation/recovery 18.5%–32.0%, and persistence/bookkeeping 18.9%–28.0 (2608.10875). In SPIEval, GPT-5.5 at xhigh reasoning reached 57.3% exact accuracy over 250 tasks, while providing all relevant records directly raised the configuration-average result from 35.5% to 66.8% (2608.10692).

- **Interactive environments:** In 360CityArena, the best tested LMM scored 17.1% overall versus 77.3% for five locally familiar humans across 175 tasks; the human comparison is an in-domain reference, not a population estimate (2608.08814). In DSAgentBench, Claude-4.6-Sonnet with screenshot and accessibility-tree input reached 56.70% success versus 85.09% for three humans across 275 desktop data-science tasks (2608.10366).

- **Research and document efficiency:** Post-retrieval MMR reduced average tokens from 375.4k to 114.6k and runtime from 3422.6 s to 1379.8 s, with judged quality 56.62 versus 57.83 on 100 DeepResearchGym reports (2608.08389). InSight-doc’s SFT+RL model improved four-benchmark average accuracy from 50.5% to 66.9% at resolution ratio 0.25 relative to Qwen3-VL-8B, under a 40-page limit (2608.10628).

- **RAG latency:** Under a P99 TTFT ceiling of 100 ms, CoinRAG reported 41.7 average token-level F1, 65 ms mean TTFT, and 81 ms P99 TTFT across HotpotQA, 2WikiMQA, and MuSiQue. TurboRAG reported 39.6 F1, 75 ms mean, and 91 ms P99 under the same selection rule (2608.07458).

- **Ranking-system compression:** UniMoMo’s four-expert checkpoints retained 99.92%–102.30% of source mean NDCG@10 and achieved 1.28×–1.63× speedups across nine dataset/depth pairs after supervised adaptation (2608.08627). DistilVDR-HiRes reached 61.74 average NDCG@5 over 22 ViDoRe datasets, versus 71.05 for its 8.1B teacher; Fast processed 99.04 documents/s on one H200 at batch 8 (2608.10636).

- **Dynamic geometry:** Latent-to-4D achieved DINO-F1 61.60 versus 55.79 for a matched RGB-reconstruction cascade on 200 I4D-200 cases; this is a projection-similarity result, not metric geometry accuracy (2608.10744). Rest2Art reported ACD part-segmentation IoU/mAP of 0.88/0.89 and HSSD joint-type accuracy of 74.49%, with angular and positional errors evaluated only for correctly typed joints (2607.27749).

- **Visual objectives:** AdvFD improved JiT-B from FID 1.00 to 0.79, FD-r6 5.53 to 3.92, and held-out FD-r3 8.45 to 6.03 under ImageNet-1K, 256×256, one-step, 50,000-sample evaluation (2608.11205). iFAN improved COCO mask AP for PMT from 45.4 to 46.6 at 640² and 48.8 to 50.6 at 1280², with final-layer-only inference unchanged architecturally (2608.03216).

- **Reasoning stress tests:** At top-1 word-initial masking on GSM8K, conditional retention changed base→instruction-tuned from 8%→48% for Qwen2.5-7B, 15%→45% for OLMo-2-7B, and 25%→90% for Gemma-3-12B, using \(n=500\); Llama-3.1-8B showed only 14%→16% (2608.09900). JigShape’s supervised models exceeded 97% piece accuracy at \(4\times4\), fell to 27.34%–33.58% at \(8\times8\), and to 3%–5% at \(12\times12\) (2607.27670).

- **Self-evolving agents:** MGM scored 93.2% on Polyglot-60 versus HGM’s 77.9% under matched 200-evaluation, 24-expansion budgets, but full-run variance is not established (2608.07645). SkillZip compressed skills by 31.2% on average with macro task score 0.577 versus 0.570 for uncompressed skills, without validation rollouts; no uncertainty estimates were reported (2608.11079).

- **Multimodal generation:** Ex-Omni-2D’s four-step streaming configuration produced 26.512 FPS on four GPUs, but complete-request E2E RTF was 1.293 and first playable video arrived after 3.142 seconds (2608.10720).

- **Translation:** Across three model scales on WMT24++, reference-free post-training improved mean XCOMET by 2.75 points and COMETKiwi by 2.44 points over v0.1. Across FLORES+ groups, spBLEU fell by 1.21 points on average; interpolation partly recovered it (2608.10812).

- **Time-series similarity:** The best correlation depended on the downstream setting: MAD reached \(r=0.619\) for Time-MoE OOD forecasting, OT \(r=0.417\) for classification, DBA+DTW \(r=0.420\) for Lag-Llama OOD forecasting, and DBA+ED \(r=0.358\) for Time-MoE transfer (2608.08119).

- **Attention audit:** PLGA exactly reduces to scaled dot-product attention when \(G_{\mathrm{LM}}=I\). In one audited 110M checkpoint, all 560 sampled generator instances had numerical rank one, and cached versus recomputed operators caused no token changes across tested decoding paths; this does not establish reliable collapse across training runs or superiority over SDPA (2608.10288).

- **Conceptual contributions:** Combodied Agents specifies a governed loop for human-state perception, memory, personal world modeling, and admissible intervention, but reports no empirical results (2608.10915). The co-evolution survey formalizes persistent reciprocal adaptation and organizes it into three stages, but its cross-paper synthesis does not support a universal causal effect or aggregate effect size (2608.10299).

## Open research directions

1. **Causally separate retrieval, persistence, and planning failures in personal agents** (2608.10875, 2608.10692, 2608.10915). Run matched tasks with oracle records, explicit versus silent updates, persistence on/off, and oracle decompositions; measure execution accuracy, authorization violations, late-stage degradation, and independent user capability.

2. **Test whether structured memory prevents long-horizon decay** (2608.10875, 2608.10366). Compare ordinary context summarization with validated ledgers, constraint stores, checkpointing, scheduled reconciliation, and rollback under equal token and action budgets.

3. **Evaluate selective evidence systems on shared end-to-end pipelines** (2608.08389, 2608.10628, 2608.10636, 2608.07458). Combine compact page retrieval, region zooming, and nugget caching, then ablate each stage while measuring answer accuracy, evidence recall, tokens, storage, latency, and factual errors.

4. **Replace proxy-only geometry evaluation with metric ground truth** (2608.10744, 2607.27749). Generate or capture scenes with known cameras, depth, parts, joints, and motion; report metric reconstruction error, joint-type errors including misclassification, coverage, and downstream manipulation success.

5. **Determine whether visual-objective gains survive independent human judgment** (2608.11205, 2608.03216). Use blinded human comparisons for generation and out-of-distribution segmentation tests, alongside held-out encoders and calibration metrics, with repeated seeds.

6. **Explain and mitigate reasoning scaling cliffs** (2608.09900, 2607.27670). Independently vary injected surprisal, puzzle size, visual resolution, output length, and access to external search; compare direct generation with constraint solvers and report nominal-task regressions.

7. **Measure stability and regression in self-evolving agents** (2608.07645, 2608.11079, 2608.10299). Repeat complete evolution runs across archive seeds, task-overlap levels, and compression cycles; track held-out transfer, rollback frequency, code or skill complexity, safety regressions, and parser-contract recall.

8. **Disentangle compression from adaptation and teacher choice** (2608.08627, 2608.10636). Evaluate pre-adaptation merged checkpoints, matched from-scratch compact architectures, several teachers, and equal-data/equal-compute baselines across hardware and distribution shifts.

9. **Validate reference-free translation with independent evaluators** (2608.10812). Compare SFT, pure RL, and interpolation using blinded human adequacy/fluency judgments and metric families excluded from training, stratified by direction frequency and language resource level.

10. **Establish whether attention-operator collapse is reproducible and useful** (2608.10288). Train matched PLGA and SDPA models across seeds, scales, and datasets; monitor operator variance during training and compare cached, learned-constant, identity, and fully generated operators under equal compute.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All 23 supplied current papers were analyzed and appear in both “Papers at a glance” and “Sources.” Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical connections were available and were checked against the supplied endpoint evidence. Twelve cross-day edges met the evidence threshold; they express technical similarity or complementarity only.

Collection failures affected Hugging Face Markdown retrieval for nine papers: 2607.27749, 2608.10875, 2608.10720, 2608.11079, 2608.09900, 2608.10636, 2608.08814, 2608.08119, and 2608.07458. Their supplied analyses nevertheless contained substantive paper evidence, often from alternative or complete retrieved text.

Source truncation materially affected 2608.10915, 2608.10299, 2608.07645, 2608.10812, and 2608.10288. Other missing evidence includes numerical human-preference and ablation tables for 2608.10744; several result tables and uncertainty measures for 2608.08627 and 2608.10692; complete model-by-grid tables for 2607.27670; and statistical uncertainty for many papers.

Lower-confidence conclusions include:

- Whether small score differences for SkillZip, UniMoMo, and several pruning configurations are statistically reliable.
- Whether AdvFD’s encoder-metric gains imply human-perceived improvements.
- Whether reference-free translation gains survive independent human evaluation.
- Whether PLGA operator collapse generalizes beyond the audited checkpoints and workloads.
- Whether simulated personal-assistant, urban-navigation, and desktop-agent rankings transfer to live deployments.
- Whether claimed artifact releases were available at collection time where the supplied text used future-tense or omitted access details.
