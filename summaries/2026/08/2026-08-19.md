# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-19

## Executive summary

- Agent engineering dominated the day: three papers studied harness-native optimization (2608.17310, 2608.17528, 2608.17393), while two exposed lifecycle-level indirect-instruction risks (2608.17597, 2608.16393).
- Agent skills and memory remained useful but conditional. Skills primarily stabilized procedures rather than adding facts (2608.14036); memory performance varied sharply with task, retrieval breadth, backbone, and cost (2608.15008); repository skills transferred poorly across executors (2608.18933).
- Long-horizon evaluation moved toward executable artifacts. ASI-Bench (2608.17271) found a large guidance-removal gap in scientific projects, while StartupBench (2608.17800) found that no tested general-purpose agent completed one-third of runs under its strict threshold.
- Video representation work questioned pixel reconstruction as the governing objective. AVA-Encoder (2608.12313) used editable film knowledge graphs, while V-RAE (2608.13556) found semantic latent diagnostics more associated with downstream generation than reconstruction FVD in its controlled study.
- Visual generation advances covered data, training paths, scaling, editing, restoration, and safety (2608.17566, 2608.18076, 2608.05811, 2608.17286, 2608.18063, 2608.16793, 2608.17067), but several results depend on synthetic data or automated evaluators.
- Systems papers reported substantial serving or inference gains in specific hardware settings: FreeToken (2608.16157) served oversized MoE models on single machines, and dynamic multi-byte prediction (2608.15454) accelerated byte generation at 373M scale.
- Mathematical work ranged from verified Lean autoformalization (2608.14221) to literature-scale conjecture triage (2608.16977) and a conceptual personalized research-agent proposal without empirical validation (2608.14881).
- Structured 3D generation took two different forms: sparse voxel latents for aerial 3D Gaussian scenes (2608.17988) and executable relational programs for editable assets (2608.17975).
- Singleton contributions included embodied navigation (2608.17512), efficient MoE vision encoding (2608.17402), a theoretical GNN layer equation (2608.16097), PTX kernel adaptation (2608.17379), terminal-task synthesis (2608.18580), and multimodal cardiac representation learning (2608.12944).

- **Agent skills (2608.14036):** Skill achieved 61.9% success versus 55.9% for matched Workflow Memory over 528 paired triples, a +6.06-point paired difference with 95% bootstrap CI [+0.76, +11.36]. Against Raw at 59.1%, the +2.84-point CI [−2.27, +7.95] was inconclusive. Exact annotated-skill precision nevertheless fell from 29.6% at pool size 5 to 3.3% at 100.

- **Harness-native training (2608.17528, 2608.17393):** Agent Lightning raised Qwen3.5-9B mini-SWE-agent performance on SWE-bench Verified from 41.8% to 56.4%. LEGO-RL raised Qwen3.5-35B-A3B solve rates from 64.0% to 70.4%, 62.4% to 68.2%, and 57.2% to 66.6% across OpenHands SDK, Claude Code, and OpenCode, respectively. These are separate settings, not a head-to-head comparison.

- **Agent optimization with limited evidence (2608.17310):** Agentic ESOpt reports a 6.69% WebArena-Lite improvement over “No Skill” for Qwen-3.5-27B and wins in 28 of 36 co-evolution settings. Because only the abstract was available, neither the underlying metric nor the magnitude and uncertainty of the 28 comparisons can be verified.

- **Agent benchmarks (2608.17271, 2608.17800):** ASI-Bench’s 18 configurations averaged 50.91 with detailed procedures, 29.10 with method-level guidance, and 26.62 without methodological guidance across 60 executable projects. StartupBench’s best general-purpose system completed 31.27% of runs at the ≥90 threshold across 97 market-derived workflows, despite leading mean scores around 73.6.

- **Harness safety (2608.17597, 2608.16393):** HarnessRisk observed ASR from 12.6% to 80.87% across 14 model–harness configurations while utility stayed between 75.0% and 97.6%. In DeepSeek Harness, 819 of 14,560 runs (5.6%) met the deterministic full-success criterion; hidden-Unicode file carriers reached 25.5% in one channel-mode cell but 0% in text mode.

- **Serving and decoding (2608.16157, 2608.15454):** FreeToken produced 77–83 tok/s for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B on an RTX 5090 and served a 753B GLM-5.2 model at 14.9 tok/s on one RTX PRO 6000, versus 7.3 for llama.cpp in that test. LCA-MBP’s verified drafting preserved verifier outputs while giving 1.74×, 1.56×, and 1.42× speedups on DailySum, Spanish–English, and French–English at 373M scale.

- **Video representations (2608.12313, 2608.13556):** AVA-Encoder reached 49.0% Overall reconstruction accuracy versus 28.3% for soap2soap on its 18-video common-decoder benchmark. V-RAE’s tFVD correlated with gFVD at 0.621 on UCF101 and 0.919 on K600, versus rFVD correlations of 0.200 and 0.473 across the evaluated tokenizer configurations; these are associations, not causal evidence.

- **High-resolution editing and restoration (2608.18063, 2608.16793):** EditBridge led its reported 4K columns with HaarPSI 0.4279, mPSNR 24.1380, mSSIM 0.8347, mMSE 0.0049, and mLPIPS 0.2112 at 61.10 seconds. PixRestore-S used 53.70M parameters, one function evaluation, 658 GFLOPs, and 44 ms at 512×512 on one A800; its aggregate superiority did not extend to every degradation or metric.

- **Visual editing data (2608.17566, 2608.18076):** CoinVE-Edit led the evaluated models on compositional semantic, scope, and persistence accuracy—87.97, 89.45, and 89.60—but Seedance 2.0 led several perceptual measures. The capability-centric image pipeline’s 3B and 6B models scored 3.93 and 3.94 overall on CPI-General/Practical; without external baselines or curriculum ablations, those scores do not establish the proposed data design’s causal value.

- **Diffusion training (2608.05811, 2608.17286):** EG-FM reached ImageNet-256 FID 1.55 at 200 epochs with PixelDiT-XL, versus 1.61 at 320 for matched baseline training. Abra estimated an EMA-loss optimum of 199 image tokens per parameter, but metric-specific optima ranged from 121 to 294 TPP, so there is no single objective-independent optimum.

- **Mathematics (2608.14221, 2608.16977):** MathForm-8B achieved macro Pass@8 of 88.06% compilation success and 72.37% semantic consistency across six benchmarks, beating the strongest specialized 32B baseline by 6.45 and 3.96 points, respectively. FAR reduced 51,110 papers to 4,717 attempts and 77 recommended artifacts; only 15 interest-selected artifacts received human review, and one duplicated a prior result.

- **Embodied navigation (2608.17512):** Automatic reasoning reached 66.2% R2R-CE success with a 26.3% CoT rate, close to dense CoT’s 66.8% at 100%. On 300 physical trials—100 per system—it reached 60.0% success versus 53.0% and 49.0% for the two comparison systems.

- **Memory portability (2608.15008, 2608.17050):** Memory-substrate rankings reversed across tasks and backbones; for example, structural memory improved LoCoMo quality but incurred roughly 26–29 seconds per query versus under three seconds for flat retrieval. Frozen cross-model memory reduced WikiText-103 perplexity by 1.6%–15.7% across nine source–target pairs, but consistently reduced TruthfulQA by 0.2–0.8 points.

- **Executable systems data (2608.18580, 2608.17379):** FACET SFT improved Qwen3.5-4B/9B/27B on Terminal-Bench 2.1 by 7.12/8.24/6.75 points. PTXBench found no consistently library-competitive model; the only reported result above a frontier-library baseline was Claude Opus 4.8’s 1.012× cuBLAS on B200 GEMM, while backward-attention success was much lower.

- **Cardiac representation learning (2608.12944):** With frozen linear probes, CardioState-JEPA reached CirCor murmur AUROC×100 of 97.9±0.1 versus AudioMAE’s 79.1±2.7 and reduced its aggregate PPG regression MAE from the strongest listed baseline’s 10.9 to 9.1. The aggregate regression mean mixes heterogeneous targets and units.

## Open research directions

1. **Call-level credit in harness-native RL — 2608.17528, 2608.17393.** Compare uniform rollout-reward inheritance with verifier-grounded call rewards and answer-backtracked scoring, holding model, harness, rollout count, and compute fixed.

2. **Skill routing that predicts applicability, not identity — 2608.14036, 2608.18933, 2608.15008.** Evaluate canonical-ID retrieval, usefulness-labelled retrieval, and an abstaining applicability router on growing skill pools; measure task success, misapplication, tokens, and executor transfer.

3. **Causal harness-safety decomposition — 2608.17597, 2608.16393.** Independently swap system prompts, provenance encoding, tool schemas, approval gates, persistent state, and carrier normalization while measuring benign utility and attack success.

4. **Artifact-level process diagnosis — 2608.17271, 2608.17800, 2608.16002.** Add relational risk tracing and lifecycle failure labels to scientific and professional workflow runs, then test whether interventions based on early warnings improve strict completion rather than merely predict failure.

5. **Representation-versus-generator causality in video — 2608.12313, 2608.13556.** Run a factorial study crossing VAE/V-RAE/graph representations with identical decoders, generator heads, data, and compute; evaluate regeneration, human temporal quality, and edit locality.

6. **Human-grounded evaluation for visual transformation — 2608.17566, 2608.18076, 2608.18063, 2608.16793, 2608.17067.** Rescore a shared, independently sampled output set with multiple fixed VLM judges and blinded humans, covering instruction fidelity, preservation, safety, and perceptual quality.

7. **Diffusion optimization across both paths and budgets — 2608.05811, 2608.17286.** Repeat Abra-style iso-FLOP ladders with standard and energy-guided paths, multiple optimizers, and human-preference evaluation to see whether path design changes compute-optimal allocation.

8. **Scale and concurrency for edge MoE serving — 2608.16157, 2608.17402.** Sweep concurrent sessions, batches, memory contention, accelerators, and routing distributions while reporting throughput, tail latency, energy, cache interference, and output equivalence.

9. **Verified mathematical discovery at reviewable precision — 2608.14221, 2608.16977.** Randomly audit the full recommendation pool with independent experts and formalize eligible claims in Lean; report correctness, novelty, formalization success, and review cost per accepted artifact.

10. **Structured 3D quality beyond demonstration cases — 2608.17988, 2608.17975.** Establish a common evaluation for condition fidelity, geometry validity, edit locality, long-range consistency, runtime, and human preference across voxel-latent and executable-program outputs.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All 34 supplied current papers were analyzed and appear in both the taxonomy or singleton notes and the paper table.

Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical connections were supplied and available. They were treated as editorial aids; the retained edges above have concrete support at both endpoints. Historical service dates were not used to infer chronology, publication order, influence, or citation.

Collection was partial:

- Hugging Face Markdown was unavailable for 27 papers: 2608.14036, 2608.17310, 2608.16157, 2608.17271, 2608.17512, 2608.13556, 2608.18063, 2608.17528, 2608.17067, 2608.17393, 2608.15454, 2608.17402, 2608.18933, 2608.17597, 2608.17800, 2608.17286, 2608.16002, 2608.14221, 2608.16393, 2608.17050, 2608.17988, 2608.17975, 2608.16977, 2608.18580, 2608.16097, 2608.17379, and 2608.12944.
- For 2608.17310, PDF extraction also failed; its analysis is abstract-only. Its comparative, memory, and horizon claims therefore remain low-confidence.
- The analyses for 2608.15008, 2608.16977, and especially 2608.16097 report truncated source material. Conclusions for omitted appendices or theory are necessarily incomplete.
- The collection log records HTTP 404 responses for Hugging Face Markdown associated with 2608.17286 and 2608.16097; their supplied paper analyses were still usable.
- Several studies lack repeated-run uncertainty or statistical tests. Automated evaluators are prominent in video/image editing, safety, benchmark scoring, and mathematical semantic assessment.
- 2608.14881 is conceptual rather than empirical. Its inclusion describes a proposed system and evaluation agenda, not demonstrated performance.
- The historical evidence blocks were service-truncated in places, but every retained cross-day edge uses concrete cached-analysis evidence from both papers. No edge relies only on a listing summary.
