# Code as Worlds: Agentic Discovery of Executable World Representations for Physical Reasoning

> [← Daily summary](../2026-08-31.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.27549) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27549)


## One-sentence takeaway

Code-as-World iteratively reconstructs executable physical simulations from text or video and uses their verified states as supervision, yielding a 27B vision-language model with 58.6 MRA on QuantiPhy-validation.

## Problem and motivation

Vision-language models can describe physical events but often lack explicit representations of object states, physical parameters, dynamics, and intervention outcomes. The authors identify a “phenomenon–mechanism” gap: pixels preserve detail, 3D reconstruction preserves geometry, and language captures semantics, but none alone explicitly combines quantitative state, compositional structure, and executable dynamics (Sections 1–2; Figure 2).

Recovering such mechanisms from incomplete text or monocular video is also an inverse problem. The paper addresses both representation and recovery, then tests whether reconstructed worlds can supply otherwise scarce metric labels for quantitative physical reasoning.

## Approach and contributions

- **Executable world representation:** An EWR is code comprising physical composition \(C\), dynamic evolution \(E\), and visual appearance \(A\). It exposes entities, geometry, physical parameters, trajectories, events, cameras, and rendering controls through a simulator interface (Section 3; Figure 13).

- **Agentic discovery:** Modality-specific evidence—semantic constraints from text, or RGB, depth, masks, tracks, and generated meshes from video—drives a propose–instantiate–execute–render–verify loop. Candidates are refined for up to five rounds or rejected (Sections 4.1–4.3; Algorithm 1).

- **Physical supervision for VLMs:** Training first establishes image-space measurement through supervised fine-tuning, then applies GRPO to world-space questions generated from verified text- and video-driven EWRs (Sections 5.2–5.4).

- **Empirical assets and findings:** The reported training data include 73,335 image-space QA pairs plus 1,585 text-driven and 988 video-driven world-space VQA samples; Code-as-World-VL reaches the strongest reported QuantiPhy-validation result (Appendix A; Table 1).

These results support the authors’ claim that executable-world supervision improves the evaluated measurement and calibration tasks, and that iterative refinement is generally more effective than independent sampling under the stated budget. They do not establish general physical understanding, mechanistically correct recovery from arbitrary videos, or that reasoning traces cause the 27B gain: model size and response protocol change together.

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Author-stated:** Simulator misspecification can yield a locally plausible reconstruction without the correct mechanism, especially when terrain, contacts, materials, or latent factors are not modeled (Section 4.4).

- **Author-stated:** The implementation primarily covers rigid-body dynamics; fluids, deformables, fracture, and other regimes remain future work (Section 7.1).

- **Author-stated:** QuantiPhy covers a narrow slice of physics—mostly monocular calibration for size and kinematics under constrained motion—and omits many complex interactions (Section 5.5).

- **Author-stated:** Code-as-World-VL learns from discovery outcomes but does not internalize hypothesis construction, simulation, diagnosis, or iterative revision (Section 5.5).

- **Analyst inference:** The 27B comparison does not isolate reasoning or scale because both change relative to the 9B model; the authors explicitly acknowledge this confound (Section 5.4.3).

- **Analyst inference:** Reconstruction validity is difficult to assess quantitatively because Figures 5 and 11 lack exact values, sample counts, uncertainty estimates, and statistical tests in the supplied text.

- **Analyst inference:** There is an internal reporting error: Table 4 lists the full 9B average as **55.4**, while the accompanying sentence claims improvement to **56.8**. The table agrees with Table 1, so 55.4 is better supported.

- **Analyst inference:** Reproducibility is incomplete from the text alone: it does not identify the base checkpoints, discovery-agent LLM, reconstruction-set size, human-review agreement, training duration, or complete 4B/9B optimization settings. The sim-to-real pipeline also uses an unspecified internal video model.

## Open research questions

1. **Can discovery recover the true mechanism rather than merely an observationally adequate simulation?** Section 4.4 says this distinction can fail. A next experiment could use synthetic scenes with known masses, friction, contacts, and interventions, then score parameter and counterfactual recovery directly.

2. **Which part of the 27B result comes from scale, reasoning-format training, or stochastic inference?** The current comparison changes these jointly. A factorial experiment could train matched-size direct-answer and reasoning variants under identical data, compute, and decoding.

3. **Does the method generalize beyond rigid bodies and constrained cameras?** These cases are excluded or deferred. A next evaluation could introduce controlled camera motion and one deformable or fluid simulator with regime-specific verification metrics.

4. **Can a VLM internalize the discovery loop?** The current model consumes supervision produced by an external loop. A next experiment could train it to propose and revise EWRs, measuring both final-answer accuracy and executable-hypothesis fidelity.

## Confidence and missing information

**Confidence: medium.** The supplied text is complete and provides detailed method and benchmark descriptions, but material information remains unavailable or inconsistent: exact reconstruction sample counts and plotted values, uncertainty or significance tests, base-model identities, discovery-agent configuration, complete 4B/9B training hyperparameters, human-review reliability, artifact licensing, and details of the internal video generator. The Table 4 prose/table discrepancy also reduces confidence in one reported ablation summary.
