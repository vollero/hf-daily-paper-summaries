# EvoUndo: Recoverability-Constrained Self-Evolution for LLM Agent Harnesses

> [← Daily summary](../2026-08-31.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.28363) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.28363)


## One-sentence takeaway

EvoUndo shows that capability-improving agent-harness mutations often remain unrecoverable unless verification, pre-state witnesses, exact state grounding, and an expressive recovery language are designed together.

## Problem and motivation

LLM agents can persistently modify prompts, tools, configurations, middleware, files, and resources, but optimizing only the forward task objective does not ensure those changes can later be reversed. Static inverses may fail because mutations overwrite information or because recovery depends on the pre-mutation state.

The authors identify a gap in self-evolving-agent research: existing procedures generally validate capability gains without independently checking whether persistent effects can be removed across counterfactual states. They therefore formulate recoverability as a relational property of the mutation, witness semantics, recovery language, observational contract, and tested state distribution (Sections 1 and 2.2–2.5).

## Approach and contributions

1. **Recoverability-constrained representation.** EvoUndo represents each candidate as a forward mutation, pre-state witness program, recovery program, and typed effect contract. During repair, the forward mutation is frozen to prevent evasion by weakening its behavior (Sections 3.1 and 3.4).

2. **Independent counterfactual verification.** It executes mutation–recovery round trips over development and hidden harness states, checks typed observational equivalence, audits declared effects against runtime-observed effects, and admits candidates only when the hidden-state 95% Wilson lower confidence bound reaches 0.85 (Sections 3.2, 3.5, and 4.2).

3. **Diagnosis and bounded synthesis.** Failed candidates receive either coarse typed or exact-address diagnostics, followed by up to four attempts to repair witness capture, recovery operations, and the effect contract (Sections 3.3 and 3.5).

4. **Recovery-language extension.** The base calculus \(L_0\) covers configuration, prompts, tools, and routing; \(L_1\) adds ordered middleware restoration, listeners, files, managed sockets, and ordered multi-surface recovery (Section 3.4; Table 1; Appendix C).

5. **Mechanistic evaluation assets.** The authors construct a 120-case controlled defect benchmark, a natural bank of 197 recovery failures drawn from 600 one-shot tasks, deterministic oracle strata separating grounding from expressivity failures, and a protocol-locked \(2\times2\) factorial evaluation (Sections 4.3–4.5).

The results support the authors’ claim that state grounding and recovery-language expressivity are distinct empirical bottlenecks within the implemented harness and counterfactual generator. They also show that more detailed diagnostics are not uniformly beneficial.

They do not establish formal recoverability, completeness of either calculus, robustness to arbitrary state distributions, or superiority to snapshots when affected pre-state is known and directly restorable. The oracle strata are empirical and use information unavailable to a deployed synthesizer.

## Limitations and validity risks

**Author-stated limitations:**

- \(L_0\) and \(L_1\) cover only the modeled state types and have no formal completeness guarantee; transfer to richer production stacks is untested (Section 8).
- Repair freezes the forward mutation, so joint optimization of capability and recoverability was not evaluated (Sections 2.5 and 8).
- Distributed databases, multi-host state, third-party APIs, unmanaged processes, and external network state are outside the observational model (Section 8).
- Irreversible physical or financial effects require compensation rather than exact restoration (Section 8).
- Evaluation primarily uses gpt-oss-120b and Qwen3.8-27B; broader model generality is unknown (Section 8).
- Witness capture introduces access-control, encryption, retention, least-privilege, and redaction requirements (Section 8).
- Counterfactual verification supplies empirical evidence rather than deductive guarantees (Section 8).

**Analyst inferences:**

- The oracle-defined strata may partly reflect the implemented oracle and generator, not intrinsic task classes. The paper explicitly limits the stability result to resampling from that generator (Section 4.4; Appendix G.6).
- Some primary factorial repair feedback included aggregate hidden IID/OOD summaries, weakening the separation between repair and final hidden evaluation; the fresh-holdout study uses stricter isolation (Sections 3.2 and 4.2).
- Fresh-holdout performance varies sharply by family: under \(Q_{20}\), listener rescue was 0/50 despite 32/50 satisfying the hidden criterion post hoc, indicating substantial development-test conservatism (Table 12).
- The negative diagnostic interaction has only descriptive trace evidence; diagnostic curricula and constrained address use were not ablated (Section 6.3).
- Reproducibility cannot be independently assessed from the supplied text because release is promised in the future and no code or artifact location is provided (Reproducibility Statement).

## Open research questions

1. **Can diagnostics adapt to the model and recovery language?** The exact-address effect changes sign across the two tested backbones. A next experiment could compare coarse-to-exact curricula, learned diagnostic selection, and constrained address use across more models.

2. **How should forward capability and recoverability be optimized jointly?** EvoUndo freezes the forward mutation, leaving capability–recoverability trade-offs unresolved. A factorial study could compare fixed-mutation repair with joint mutation/recovery search under a shared capability budget.

3. **Can empirical round trips be combined with stronger guarantees?** Current verification is distribution-dependent and not proof-complete. A next experiment could formally verify a restricted subset of recovery programs and compare proof coverage, empirical failures, and runtime cost.

4. **How can recovery handle concurrent and external effects?** Both EvoUndo and effect-scoped snapshots degrade under same-surface concurrency, while distributed and third-party state is unmodeled. A next experiment could add versioned effects or transactional logging and test interleaved mutations across local and distributed state.
