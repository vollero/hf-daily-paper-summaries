# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-08-25

## Executive summary

- Agent evaluation shifts from plausible outputs toward executable state, runtime traces, and repeated reliability. MobilePA-Bench (`2608.23035`), Thinkingbox (`2608.19741`), and ClawProBench (`2608.22510`) all find large gaps between occasional and dependable success.
- Long-horizon agent systems increasingly externalize persistent state and verification. Apodex 1.1 (`2608.23283`), Prime Agent (`2608.23552`), and AutoResearch (`2608.17906`) report promising system-level outcomes, but component attribution and compute matching remain weak.
- Interactive world models improve camera control and bounded spatial memory, yet remain generators rather than rigorous simulators. EchoWM (`2608.23189`) and ReWorld (`2608.23565`) show long-rollout or revisit capability; the survey audit (`2608.23070`) highlights missing physics guarantees and queryable state.
- Post-training papers replace indiscriminate supervision with more selective signals: query-level regularization (`2608.23311`), strategy-conditioned comparison (`2608.13622`), and reasoning-progress filtering (`2608.19408`). Evidence is strongest in math or tool-use settings, not broad deployment.
- Retrieval robustness is a recurring concern. ASR entity corruption (`2608.22872`), web-content pollution (`2606.13610`), and corpus expansion (`2608.22856`) can materially change RAG answers even when absolute performance or aggregate accuracy appears acceptable.
- Efficiency gains span 3D generation, harness search, quantization recovery, and mixed-precision attention (`2608.19567`, `2608.20169`, `2608.20953`, `2608.17336`), but most results remain tied to specific hardware, model families, or evaluation pools.
- Several domain-specific studies provide useful counterexamples to standard assumptions: face-free anti-spoofing training transfers to face PAD (`2608.21455`), long-context RNA pretraining improves frozen representations (`2608.22849`), and astronomy supplies a controlled interpretability calibration setting (`2608.22614`).

## Main results worth noting

- Agent-team gains are consistent within reported matched Apodex modes: `2608.23283` raises APEX-Agents from 34.4 to 38.5, GDPVal win rate from 69.5 to 78.8, and FrontierScience-Research pass rate from 55.0% to 63.3%. These comparisons do not match total compute.

- Reliability gaps appear under two distinct protocols. In Thinkingbox (`2608.19741`), GPT-5.4 records 65.36% pass@1, 91.12% pass@20, and 25.25% all-20 success over 507 tasks and 20 attempts. In ClawProBench (`2608.22510`), 37 clean holdout entries average 0.6638 pass@k-any but 0.2890 strict 3/3 reliability.

- `2608.22876` localized all 192 injected causal leaks exactly across eight checkpoints and eight fault types. Its released-model diagnosis is narrower: the two observed failures share a wrong-axis chunked-recurrence lineage, and the tested reference patch reduces the measured prefix differences to zero.

- `2608.19567` reports a controlled Cube comparison on 100 TRELLIS-500K prompt–mesh pairs: CD-L1 0.078 versus 0.094, normal consistency 0.668 versus 0.632, and mean A100 latency 4.99 versus 25.71 seconds. CLIPScore is slightly lower, 23.24 versus 23.87.

- `2608.20953` reports that its 60B MXFP4 student matches or exceeds the compressed 60B BF16 source on seven of nine benchmarks, including AA-LCR 42.7 versus 35.3 and AIME 2025 76.3 versus 70.7. It still trails the 120B teacher on several tasks, and every reported run is single-seed.

- `2608.17336` reaches 31.80K prefill tokens/s with SpTrans75 versus 14.33K for FlashAttention and 29.80K for uniform INT8 One on LLaMA 3.2 3B, A100, batch 8, 4K tokens. Numerical deviation increases with INT8 coverage, and quality depends strongly on routing layout and task.

- `2608.22872` illustrates why robustness and absolute accuracy must be reported separately. On Nigerian 2WikiMultiHopQA, IRCoT+HippoRAG2 has higher ASR-input F1 than Naive RAG, 0.450 versus 0.331, but also a larger clean-to-ASR gap, 0.195 versus 0.137.

- `2608.22856` finds 10.25 percentage points of semantic excess churn on NQ, 95% CI [7.188, 13.438], while aggregate EM changes only −1.50 points. Forty of 400 questions are repeat-stable semantic flips, accounting for 10.00 points of that effect.

- `2608.23252` reports portfolio recall 0.397 for 12 sequential generations with two documents each versus 0.253 for one 24-document generation under the same 24-slot evidence budget. The cited latency is 17.5 versus 1.7 seconds, so this is a coverage–latency trade-off rather than a free gain.

- In embodied work, `2608.20430` reports 90.8 EPDMS at 2.40 average rollouts and 287.429 ms for its adaptive scheduler on NAVSIM, while `2608.22591` reports Blocks Ranking strict success of 94%, 92%, 49%, 27%, and 24% at contexts of 608, 288, 128, 64, and 32 tokens. The former studies future imagination; the latter studies observation history.

- Domain-specific results should remain scoped. `2608.21455` reaches 92.70% mean AUC across four face-PAD datasets after vegetable-only downstream training, but only for visible-spectrum print/replay attacks. `2608.22849` improves 10,240-token RNA reconstruction accuracy from 0.93083 to 0.94763 over a short-context extrapolated model, but reports no biological-function evaluation.

## Open research directions

1. **Compute-match persistent agent systems** (`2608.23283`, `2608.23552`, `2608.17906`). Equalize total tokens, tool calls, wall time, and model FLOPs while ablating persistence, delegation, verification, recovery, and retained skills.

2. **Measure reliability with enough repetitions** (`2608.19741`, `2608.22510`, `2608.23035`). Run 10–20 trials per task for selected configurations and estimate task-level success distributions, not only pass@1 or three-run strict success.

3. **Test world-model memory on dynamic state and non-retraced returns** (`2608.23189`, `2608.23565`, `2608.23070`). Revisit the same pose after object motion, state changes, or a different return route; score external geometry and state correctness rather than visual similarity alone.

4. **Compare adaptive imagination against fixed-depth quality–latency frontiers** (`2608.20430`, `2608.22591`). Match measured latency or FLOPs across every rollout depth and adaptive policy, then repeat in a manipulation domain to test transfer beyond driving.

5. **Validate selective post-training beyond current niches** (`2608.23311`, `2608.13622`, `2608.19408`). Use matched backbones and budgets across math, code, multilingual reasoning, dialogue, and open-ended tool use; report eligibility coverage, multi-seed variance, and long-run collapse.

6. **Build perturbation-aware RAG release tests** (`2608.22872`, `2606.13610`, `2608.22856`, `2608.23252`). Cross ASR corruption, corpus updates, polluted evidence, and context allocation in one factorial design, reporting accuracy, excess churn, attack success, abstention, and latency.

7. **Separate algorithmic from systems gains in efficient deployment** (`2608.20953`, `2608.17336`, `2608.19567`, `2608.20169`). Reproduce on multiple hardware generations and model families with matched precision, data, compute, and evaluation pools.

8. **Test whether specialized representation gains affect real downstream outcomes** (`2608.23392`, `2608.22614`, `2608.22849`, `2608.21486`). Pair frozen-probe or capacity results with interventions and independently sourced downstream tasks; for RNA, include biological assays, and for face recognition, include human audit utility.

9. **Strengthen benchmark-label validity** (`2608.15242`, `2608.21833`, `2608.22817`). Retain multiple defensible root labels, independently audit generated QA and evaluator correctness, and report how rankings change under alternative labels or rubrics.

10. **Test privacy and fairness claims directly** (`2608.13914`, `2608.21455`, `2608.21486`). Add formal leakage attacks or differential privacy to federated ECG learning, and measure demographic error disparities for face-only, face-free, and mixed PAD training.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All 35 supplied current papers were analyzed and appear in the taxonomy discussion, overview table, or both.

Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical-connection stages were available. Their assignments were treated as editorial suggestions and retained only where both endpoints supplied concrete supporting evidence. The historical graph was kept sparse; only previously seen papers appearing in retained edges are included below.

Collection reported unavailable Hugging Face Markdown for 23 papers: `2608.23283`, `2608.23189`, `2608.23552`, `2608.22876`, `2608.19567`, `2608.23392`, `2608.20430`, `2608.13622`, `2608.21833`, `2608.15242`, `2608.20953`, `2608.17906`, `2608.23200`, `2608.22872`, `2608.17336`, `2608.23252`, `2608.22856`, `2608.23070`, `2608.22614`, `2608.13914`, `2608.21455`, `2608.22591`, and `2608.21486`. Full or partial alternative analysis was nevertheless supplied for each.

Important partial inputs include `2608.20958`, whose result tables lacked exact numerical cells; `2608.23070`, whose source omitted 72,583 characters; and `2608.23565`, whose main control, VBench, runtime, and cache-ablation tables were incomplete. Several other analyses explicitly lacked uncertainty estimates, multi-seed results, or complete rendered tables. Historical evidence was cached and sometimes truncated, so historical edges should be read as technically supported comparisons, not exhaustive literature relationships.

Low-confidence conclusions include TLive-Omni’s comparative ranking without exact scores, ReWorld’s long-rollout performance without complete table values, broad simulator-gap prevalence beyond the truncated survey evidence, and any causal attribution from combined agent harnesses, post-training recipes, or synthetic-data pipelines without component ablations.
