# Execution-Aligned Progressive Noise for Consistent Asynchronous Replanning in Generative Robot Policies

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-07.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.06090) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06090)


## One-sentence takeaway
EAPN combines execution-aligned noise history with temporal correlation within action chunks, improving reported asynchronous-control success and continuity, although missing table contents limit verification of several comparisons.

## Problem and motivation
Generative robot policies predict action chunks while robots continue moving during inference. Independent noise initialization can make overlapping predictions choose different valid behavioral modes, creating inconsistent continuation even when committed actions remain continuous.

The authors identify a gap in approaches that align action prefixes or execution-time context: they leave stochastic dependency between successive generations insufficiently modeled. Their motivation is that persistent, execution-aligned stochastic context could stabilize the unconstrained suffix (§I).

## Approach and contributions
- **Latent-structure evidence:** Flow inversion of a standard π₀.₅ policy reveals correlations between neighboring action windows whose peaks shift with execution displacement (§IV-A, Figs. 1 and 3). This supports the proposed prior but does not independently establish causality.
- **Structured stochastic source:** EAPN mixes a persistent shared AR(1) noise trajectory with a private AR(1) process within each chunk. Actual execution progress aligns the shared trajectory across calls; separate parameters control temporal decay and shared-history strength (§III-B).
- **Explicit historical conditioning:** A conditioner encodes aligned previous noise, displacement, inference delay, and history validity. Training retains committed actions, applies the flow-matching loss to the generated suffix, and sometimes drops history to handle resets or unavailable context (§III-C–D).

Individual noise tokens retain standard Gaussian marginals, but their joint distribution changes. The authors therefore train the model for this structured source; EAPN is not presented as a training-free sampler (§III-B).

The reported results support benefits across several control settings. Direct mode-switch evidence comes from D3IL Avoiding; success improvements elsewhere do not by themselves establish that reduced mode switching caused the gains. The authors also claim preserved multimodal capacity, but the supplied text gives no quantitative diversity result. The two LIBERO regimes cannot establish one model’s performance across a continuous delay sweep.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Explicit scope constraints:** EAPN requires training for the changed joint noise distribution, and training samples delays and displacements from evaluation support (§III-B, §III-D).
- **Missing evidence:** Tables I–V appear only as captions and narrative summaries. Full baseline results, suite-level breakdowns, and numerical continuity ablations cannot be inspected.
- **Analyst inference—limited mechanism validation:** Component ablation covers one Kinetix task; mode-switch diagnostics cover one D3IL task. Their generality across manipulation settings remains uncertain (§IV-B, §IV-D).
- **Analyst inference—limited real-world breadth:** Evaluation covers two tasks, with Cloth Folding using three garments. Broader object, garment, and disturbance generalization is not established (§IV-F).
- **Analyst inference—uncertain responsiveness:** One illustrated folding sequence and qualitative motion profiles support the authors’ observations, but do not quantify response latency or establish a general absence of lag (Figs. 6–7).

## Open research questions
- **Does EAPN generalize beyond trained timing conditions?** Training matches evaluation support (§III-D). Test unseen, variable inference delays and execution displacements without retraining.
- **When does stochastic persistence hinder adaptation?** Responsiveness is supported qualitatively (§IV-F). Introduce sudden obstacle or garment changes and measure recovery time, success, and mode switching while varying correlation strength.
- **How broadly do the components help?** Ablation is restricted to `mjc_swimmer` (§IV-D). Repeat component and history-conditioning ablations on LIBERO and real manipulation, measuring both success and behavioral diversity.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The method and narrated results are clear, but the supplied text lacks full table entries, most evaluation uncertainty estimates and rollout counts, correlation and history-drop hyperparameters, conditioner architecture, detailed training settings, runtime overhead, and confirmation of released code or weights.
