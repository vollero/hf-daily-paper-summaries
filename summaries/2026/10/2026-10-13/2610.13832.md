# SpatialASM: Internalizing Active Mentalization for Spatial Reasoning

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-13.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.13832) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.13832)


## One-sentence takeaway
SpatialASM trains vision-language models to plan hypothetical viewpoints and describe imagined observations without an inference-time simulator, improving mean accuracy on seven spatial benchmarks by 1.2–3.5 percentage points across four backbones (Table 1).

## Problem and motivation
Spatial questions involving cross-view correspondence, perspective changes, and unseen regions require reasoning beyond the supplied images. The authors identify a missing capability—**Active Spatial Mentalization (ASM)**—combining task-relevant viewpoint planning with view imagination. Existing answer-only spatial supervision does not demonstrate this process, while external simulators add inference overhead (Sections 1–2).

## Approach and contributions
- **ASM reasoning formulation:** A question-conditioned plan–imagine–reason procedure, learned through standard post-training without architectural changes (Sections 2–3).
- **Training-time simulation and filtering:** An image-editing model generates views specified by an anchor image, pixel location, and viewing direction. Geometry Perturbation (GeoP) measures changes in reconstructed point clouds as a consistency proxy (Section 3.1).
- **Two complementary supervision paths:** SFT trajectories retain cases where generated views change an incorrect answer into a correct one, then replace those views with captions. GRPO subsequently trains generated-view localization and pose-conditioned view selection (Sections 3.2–3.3).

The constructed assets include SpatialASM-edit140k, SpatialASM-128k, its SFT subset SpatialASM-66k, and SpatialASM-RL-58k. A code repository is listed; dataset and checkpoint release availability is not established by the supplied text (Section 4; Appendices B–C; p. 1).

These results establish improved benchmark accuracy under the reported evaluation protocol. They do not directly establish faithful internal scene reconstruction or prove that explicit ASM causes the gains. The authors’ broader transferability interpretation is plausible but not isolated experimentally. Improvement is also not universal: Qwen3.5-9B drops on 3DSRBench (Table 1).

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated proxy limitation:** GeoP depends on a reconstruction model rather than ground-truth geometry. Backward views require a different RL threshold because newly exposed content can raise GeoP despite similar apparent consistency (Section 3.1; Appendix B.3).
- **Author-stated scope:** Dynamic scenes, embodied interaction, and longer-horizon reasoning remain future work. ESI uses passive-single evaluation (Sections 4–5).
- **Analyst inference—unseen-content reliability:** NVG training permits plausible objects absent from the inputs. Geometric consistency and a correct final answer therefore cannot guarantee that imagined content matches the actual hidden scene (Appendix B.1.3; Section 3.2).
- **Analyst inference—causal isolation:** No reported matched answer-only or ordinary-CoT control isolates ASM-specific supervision. SFT filtering also combines answer-transition and geometry checks, preventing attribution solely to GeoP (Table 4; Appendix C.5).
- **Analyst inference—uncertainty and efficiency:** No repeated-run variability or confidence intervals are reported. Table 2 reports reasoning duration and NVG synthesis duration separately; Among reasoning takes longer after internalization, limiting the claim of negligible inference increase.
- **Analyst inference—comparison limits:** Representative models use different backbones and prompts. General-VQA preservation concerns averages over three benchmarks, with individual scores moving differently (Appendix C.3; Table 7).

## Open research questions
- **Does ASM supervision outperform equally sized conventional supervision?** The reported ablation lacks a matched answer-only or ordinary-CoT control. Train those controls with the same source questions, compute budget, and evaluation prompts.
- **Do imagined observations preserve factual hidden-scene content?** GeoP measures reconstruction stability, while unseen objects may be synthesized plausibly. Compare imagined captions against withheld posed images and annotated object relations.
- **Which filtering criterion drives improvement?** The SFT ablation removes multiple checks together. Separately ablate answer-transition filtering, GeoP filtering, and their combination while matching retained dataset size.
- **Does internalized ASM transfer to active, dynamic tasks?** These settings remain future work. Evaluate controlled moving-object scenes and embodied exploration tasks, measuring accuracy and total inference cost.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The supplied text supports the method description and reported benchmark gains, but causal and statistical conclusions remain limited.

Material missing information includes run-to-run uncertainty, complete compute and hardware costs, matched supervision controls, systematic caption-fidelity evaluation, comprehensive scene-overlap auditing, and confirmed dataset/checkpoint release status.
