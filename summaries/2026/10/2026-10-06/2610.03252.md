# COSMI: COmpositional Synthesis of Multi-object Interactions

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-06.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.03252) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03252)


## One-sentence takeaway
COSMI composes single-object motion captures into a large multi-object interaction dataset and trains a variable-object diffusion transformer that improves text alignment and contact accuracy on held-out combinations and one unseen chair instance.

## Problem and motivation
Everyday human activities involve several objects, but capturing their combinations is expensive, and most interaction datasets cover one object at a time. The authors identify two gaps: multi-object training data at scale, and an end-to-end generator whose object count can vary and whose object motions explicitly follow the relevant body parts (§1).

## Approach and contributions
- **Compositional dataset pipeline:** Extract contact-based atomic clips, annotate actions and attention requirements with Qwen3, mirror interactions, and transfer limbs together with joint-relative object poses. A compatibility graph and geometric checks filter simultaneous and sequential compositions (§3; §§C.2–C.8).
- **COSMI dataset and benchmark:** Construct 221,677 sequences covering up to five objects, including articulated and worn objects. The benchmark holds out a black chair and five interaction combinations while retaining their constituent actions (§3; Tables 1, 4).
- **Variable-object generator:** A text-conditioned diffusion transformer uses shared object slots, geometry and initial-pose conditioning, a driving-joint assignment head, auxiliary consistency losses, and contact guidance. Longer outputs use overlapping motion windows (§4; §§D.4–D.10).

Code, models, and the dataset pipeline are promised for release; the supplied text does not establish their availability (§1).

| Better all-object text alignment and contacts | Across three generations per test sequence: COSMI FID **0.049**, top-1 R-precision **0.696**, T-Acc/B-Acc **0.980/0.968**, versus PriorMDM **0.077**, **0.664**, **0.938/0.936**. | Stronger held-out contact accuracy | On the unseen black chair, COSMI T-Acc/B-Acc is **0.975/0.960**, versus PriorMDM **0.895/0.916** and MDM **0.823/0.917**. COSMI also leads both metrics on each of five held-out combinations. | Table 4 |
| Physical quality remains imperfect | All-object penetration is **33.96%** for COSMI versus **34.60%** for ground truth, counting frames with body intrusion deeper than **2 cm**. Hinge error is **0.437 radians**, but hinge sweep is only **0.665** of ground truth. | Table 2; §B.2 |

These results support improved benchmark performance and recombination of familiar interaction components. They do not establish generalization to arbitrary object categories, unfamiliar people, or complex real-world activities. Learned metrics are comparable within a shared evaluator, not directly across Tables 2 and 7 (§B.1).

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated:** The pipeline covers sustained interactions rather than dependent multi-stage activities or interactions embedded in scenes. Finger motion remains less precise than dedicated grasp synthesis, and text does not specify paths or action timing (§6).
- **Observed failures:** Articulated motion has reduced range; the unseen chair can be misplaced and its armrests intersected. Training seats lack armrests (§A.6; Fig. 7).
- **Analyst inference:** Physical filtering does not guarantee continuous contact or collision-free motion: retained objects need rule contacts in only half the frames, and walking compositions tolerate specified self-intersections in up to a third (§C.7).
- **Analyst inference:** The composition-based split deliberately tests recombination, rather than subject or source-clip independence. The unseen object is a new seat instance within a represented category (§5).
- **Comparison boundary:** MDM and PriorMDM are adaptations with shared COSMI representations and losses; HIMO uses separate tier-specific training. SK-HOI is excluded because its full code was unavailable and its optimization cost was prohibitive (§5).

## Open research questions
- **Does composed-data training transfer to independently captured combinations?** The main benchmark uses composed sequences. A next experiment could evaluate models trained with and without composition on a separately captured multi-object test set.
- **Can object generalization extend beyond a familiar category?** Only one seat instance is held out. Test several unseen categories and geometries, including supports with armrests.
- **Can articulation improve without suppressing motion diversity?** Hinge sweep is reduced and HIMO multimodality is low. Compare articulation-specific objectives while measuring hinge error, sweep, and multimodality.
- **Can composition represent dependent activity sequences?** Multi-stage tasks remain future work. Test scene-conditioned cooking or assembly sequences with explicit state transitions and timing controls.
