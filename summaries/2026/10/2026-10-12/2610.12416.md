# MAMHOI: Factorizing Scene-Aware Human-Object Interaction through Affordances

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-12.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.12416) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12416)


## One-sentence takeaway

MAMHOI connects separately trained scene understanding and human–object motion generation through spatial affordances, with reported improvements in indoor-scene feasibility whose numerical magnitude cannot be checked because the supplied text omits the result-table entries.

## Problem and motivation

Scene-aware human–object interaction (HOI) requires both feasible movement through scene geometry and realistic coordinated manipulation. The authors identify a supervision gap: human–scene data capture environmental constraints, while scene-free HOI data capture interaction dynamics, but diverse paired human–object–scene data are scarce (§1).

Their proposed solution is an explicit interface between these capabilities, intended to preserve HOI motion quality while transferring scene constraints from a different training source.

## Approach and contributions

- **Separate learning through an affordance interface.** A scene-to-affordance model learns from LINGO, and an affordance-to-HOI generator learns from OMOMO; neither training stage requires paired human–object–scene manipulation data (§§3.1, 4.1; Appendix C.5).
- **Task-specific spatial conditioning.** A diffusion U-Net predicts a binary horizontal affordance region from scene depth, a planned object path, and language. The region is lifted and queried around the pelvis, object, and goal; a ViT encoder supplies features to the motion diffusion model through cross-attention (§§3.2–3.3; Appendices B.1, C.3).
- **Affordance-sensitive motion learning.** An SDF penalty and hard-negative ranking objective encourage motions compatible with the affordance despite similar endpoint constraints. Generation proceeds through overlapping autoregressive segments (§3.3.2; Appendices C.4–C.5).

The backbone and interaction guidance build on prior methods; the authors position the factorization and its spatial interface as the central contribution (§3.3.2).

It does not establish effect sizes for the geometric or motion-distribution metrics because Tables 1–6 contain captions but no entries. Scene-aware evaluation lacks paired ground-truth HOI, so it tests geometry and perceived naturalness rather than reconstruction accuracy (§4.1).

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Acknowledged trade-offs:** Ranking improves scene compatibility while sacrificing some scene-free fidelity and path adherence; geometric guidance improves penetration without improving reported human preference (§§4.3–4.4; Appendix D.2).
- **Author-stated auxiliary validity issue:** TRUMANS has correlated scene–motion distributions and a smaller evaluation, so the authors explicitly avoid using it to establish the advantage of unpaired training (Appendix D.3).
- **Analyst inference—scope:** The primary benchmark uses static indoor scenes, manually chosen feasible starts and goals, and planned paths. Robustness to dynamic obstacles or infeasible inputs is not established (§4.1).
- **Analyst inference—representation:** The factorization assumes affordance makes detailed scene information approximately redundant. Vertical minimum pooling and broadcasting may restrict sensitivity to height-dependent geometry; no dedicated test of this assumption is supplied (Appendices A, C.3).
- **Analyst inference—comparison and uncertainty:** Methods retain different native trajectory interfaces and guidance. The preference study covers CHOIS variants, not InfBaGel, and supplies no confidence intervals or analysis of repeated participant responses (§§4.1, 4.4; Appendix E).

## Open research questions

- **Does the affordance retain enough vertical geometry?** Height-dependent validity is not isolated. Compare the current pooled representation with full 3D conditioning on overhangs and vertically constrained manipulation (Appendix C.3).
- **How robust is generation to affordance errors?** Training uses motion-derived affordances while inference uses predictions. Perturb masks and compare predicted versus oracle affordances using penetration, contact, and path error (§3.3.2).
- **Which component drives the gains?** Supplied ablations address coordinate frames and ranking, but do not isolate the full factorization against matched direct scene conditioning. Run that comparison with identical backbone, data budget, and guidance (Appendices D.1–D.2).

## Confidence and missing information

**Confidence: medium.** The architecture, training separation, and evaluation scope are well described; quantitative superiority is only partially assessable.

Material missing information includes result-table values, uncertainty estimates, standalone affordance-prediction accuracy, inference latency, exact hard-negative selection details, and confirmed availability of code, weights, and evaluation configurations.
