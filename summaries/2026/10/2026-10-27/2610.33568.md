# Trajectory2Gym: Decision-Grounded Compositional Interaction Synthesis for Tool-Use Agents

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-27.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.33568) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33568)


## One-sentence takeaway
Trajectory2Gym combines dependency-targeted interaction synthesis, decision-level supervision filtering, and trajectory-normalized SFT, reporting gains across five Qwen3 scales while component evidence remains limited to single-run 8B controls.

## Problem and motivation
Successful tool-use episodes can contain poor imitation targets: actions may depend on collector-private identifiers, ignore updated user intent, or fail to demonstrate recovery and state-dependent ordering. The authors argue that outcome filtering therefore misses two requirements: adequate coverage of cross-step dependencies and evidence supporting each action in the learner-visible history (§§1, 3).

The motivation is concrete: in their common certified corpus, 40.9% of interactions have at most two supervised decisions, and only 10.0% include a grounded decision after an authentic error (Appendix B.1).

## Approach and contributions
The proposed contribution is the combination of three stages:

1. **Compositional synthesis:** Identify under-supported capability paths, construct compatible requests and initial states, and execute them in native environments. Only dependencies actually realized during execution count toward support (§4.1).
2. **Grounded supervision:** Admit targets only when execution, visible provenance, terminal success, and applicable workflow-order requirements pass. Failed calls remain context without imitation loss; subsequent recovery can be supervised (§4.2; Appendix C).
3. **Structural-support learning:** Preserve task and capability breadth, then allocate repeated examples to deficient paths. Parent-normalized SFT gives each semantic interaction equal aggregate weight regardless of how many training examples it produces (§4.3; Appendix D).

The theoretical results clarify hidden-information uncertainty, an idealized selection objective, and rendering invariance; the authors explicitly do not present them as generalization guarantees (Appendix E).

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Authors’ qualifications:** Each evaluation task runs once, and each ablation arm is a single run. Small ablation gains are explicitly described as directional (§§5.1, 5.4).
- **Generalization boundary:** Tau1 Airline uses the same task identities for construction and fresh-dialogue evaluation, making it transductive rather than task-disjoint (Appendix A.2).
- **Confounded interventions:** Targeted expansion does not fix the token budget; depth sensitivity changes both repetition and volume. Model scaling also changes training views, with a broader corpus for 30B-A3B (§§5.4–5.5; Appendix B.1).
- **Incomplete certification:** Provenance checks do not prove that every admitted action is uniquely justified or verify every possible reason for choosing it (Appendix C.1).
- **Analyst inference:** Transfer beyond the evaluated Qwen3 tool-use settings remains uncertain; web, software, terminal, and on-policy extensions are proposed future work (§6).
- **Observed residual weakness:** Live Simple regressions and a telecom failure show that gains do not eliminate immediate-call or workflow-completion errors (§5.3; Figure 11).

## Open research questions

1. **Does targeted synthesis help at fixed supervision volume?** The expansion control changes candidate supply and token volume. Compare targeted and untargeted synthesis with matched parents, target tokens, and training compute (§5.4).
2. **How should support depth depend on learner capacity?** The fixed schedule and nonmonotonic 8B sweep do not resolve this. Run a multi-scale depth sweep while holding target-token budgets constant (§5.5).
3. **How reliable and portable is certification?** The checks establish specific provenance requirements, not complete action justification. Audit accepted and rejected decisions manually, then test certification in a new environment class (Appendix C.1; §6).
4. **Can stateful gains coexist with immediate-call accuracy?** The explanation for Live Simple regressions is hypothetical. Test controlled mixtures of direct-call and compositional demonstrations, tracking both Live Simple and stateful success (§5.3).
