# ReGE: Stronger Agents through Experience Synthesis

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-27.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.33861) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.33861)


## One-sentence takeaway
ReGE improves fresh agent execution by synthesizing the same model’s prior attempts into guidance, increasing average benchmark success while adding source-generation cost.

## Problem and motivation
Repeated agent attempts uncover useful facts, commands, artifacts, and failures, but these discoveries are scattered across long records of uncertain correctness. Reset environments also require agents to repeat necessary actions rather than inherit earlier progress. The authors address how to compress this task-specific experience into actionable guidance for another attempt (§1).

## Approach and contributions
- **Joint experience synthesis:** ReGE-Syn presents bounded records from multiple attempts together to a coach in one call. It produces self-contained guidance connecting findings, operations, and pitfalls; raw per-step observations are omitted (§§2.1–2.2).
- **Fresh, same-model execution:** The student starts from the initial environment, checks guidance against current observations, and performs required actions. Source generation, coaching, and execution use the same base model without parameter updates or correctness labels (§§2–2.3).
- **Empirical comparison of reuse strategies:** The paper evaluates joint synthesis against iterative aggregation, independent summaries, direct history, and trajectory selection, including guidance-format and source-information ablations (§§3–4.6).

## Evidence and main results
| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| Higher average success, with greater source-inclusive usage | Across four benchmarks, GPT Control → ReGE: **54.7% → 64.6%**; Claude: **58.5% → 67.3%**. Mean source-inclusive token-cost ratios versus Control: **5.05×** and **4.93×**, respectively. | Table 2 |
| Much cheaper construction than adapted AggAgent | On matched BrowseComp-Plus queries, construction tokens decrease **98.2%** for GPT and **95.5%** for Claude. Combined construction/execution tokens decrease **15.7%** and **35.4%**, excluding sources. Success is **75.2% vs 75.3%** and **86.2% vs 87.0%**, respectively. | Table 3; §§1, 4.1 |
| Competitive observed success against a higher-priced model | On **468 paired tasks**, benchmark-average success is **67.4%** for GPT luna + ReGE versus **67.0%** for GPT sol; mean list-price cost is **$0.214 vs $0.766 per task**, including ReGE sources. | §4.2; Table 5 |
| Process records help beyond final artifacts | Claude, **300 BrowseComp-Plus queries**: full-input synthesis achieves **88.3%** success versus **85.0%** with final artifacts only; mean student steps fall from **14.6 to 9.1**. | §4.3; Table 7 |
| Joint synthesis compresses guidance | Claude, **300 matched BrowseComp-Plus queries**: ReGE-Syn versus Direct history yields **5,063 vs 20,571 characters**, **215.8k vs 268.9k** combined tokens, and **88.3% success for both**, excluding source generation. | §4.5; Table 9 |
| Repeated execution shows a reliability–coverage tradeoff | On **35 mixed-pool Spider2-Lite tasks**, three Claude executions yield mean success **63.8% vs 48.6%** for Control, but tasks solved at least once are **65.7% vs 71.4%**. | Appendix B.3; Table 14 |

The evidence supports better fresh execution from task-specific experience and substantially cheaper guidance construction than the adapted iterative baseline. It does **not** establish equal-compute superiority over unguided execution: ReGE consumes multiple source attempts. Close observed success rates also do not establish statistical equivalence.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated scope:** Evaluation covers four benchmarks, with ablations on selected subsets; broader settings and aligned repeated executions remain necessary (§6).
- **Cost interpretation:** Headline construction/execution savings exclude source generation. Source-inclusive usage exceeds Control in Table 2; existing-source economics depend on those records already being available (§3; Tables 2–5).
- **Comparison controls:** Direct history uses a different rendering and delivery header. Source-count ablations increase input length alongside experience coverage, preventing attribution at matched inference cost (§§4.3, 4.5).
- **Analyst inference:** Detailed mechanism findings may not generalize beyond BrowseComp-Plus, where most ablations occur. Same-task reuse does not demonstrate transfer to unseen tasks (§§4.3–4.6, 5).
- **Reproducibility boundaries:** Truncation can discard artifacts, some student request paths omit explicit output caps, and case-study linkage lacks injected-guidance hashes (Appendices A.1, A.3, E.1).

## Open research questions
- **Does ReGE win under equal total compute?** Current gains use additional source attempts. Compare against repeated unguided execution and selection under matched source-inclusive token or monetary budgets.
- **Which guidance format works across domains?** Selection and explanation-plus-answer guidance compete with plans on BrowseComp-Plus (§4.6). Test all formats across the other benchmarks using identical pools and repeated runs.
- **How can guidance preserve exploration?** Repeated Spider2-Lite runs improve mean success but reduce at-least-once coverage (Table 14). Test guidance that explicitly preserves competing hypotheses.
- **How much execution detail should synthesis retain?** Process records help, but observations are omitted and fields truncated. Compare bounded observation-inclusive records at matched context budgets.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: high** for the reported method and descriptive results; **medium** for generalization and comparative efficiency.

Material gaps include broad confidence intervals or paired significance tests for main results, exact matched-query counts for Table 3, a fully specified AggAgent adaptation, and verification of repository contents or released execution records.
