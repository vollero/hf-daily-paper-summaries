# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-10-29

## Executive summary

- Agent evaluation increasingly distinguishes correct outcomes from acceptable execution. RealCLI (`2610.22507`), PersonaCUABench (`2610.22990`), and TauIndianBankBench (`2610.30161`) examine collaboration, user requirements, and procedural compliance through different protocols.
- On synthetic banking tasks across six models, TauIndianBankBench reports procedural violations missed by final-state scoring in **6.5–12.0% of sessions** (`2610.30161`). Its action checker does not enforce call order or multiplicity.
- PersonaCUABench finds that persona-conditioned requests reduce original-specification completion across nine browser-agent backbones, while showing that this metric can penalize agents for respecting user constraints (`2610.22990`).
- CausGround and PreView construct observation-linked guidance for frozen downstream models (`2610.29702`, `2610.33645`). PreView reports **1.5–13.9 percentage-point average accuracy gains across five benchmarks**, with occasional harm from misleading cues or lost context.
- Representation choices are central across otherwise distinct problems: native-grid astronomical spectra, offline robot-policy evaluation, and semantic-token-conditioned image rendering (`2601.15351`, `2610.31670`, `2610.37626`).
- LOB-Impact finds identifiable responses to injected metaorders across **25 neural model–stock pairs**, but steeper-than-square-root impact and no passes of its relaxation test (`2610.27100`). Historical distribution matching therefore does not establish intervention fidelity in this setting.
- HuGe reports benefits from adapting egocentric human experience for humanoid policies; physical evidence remains limited. Falcon targets efficient audio-visual speech extraction, but measured end-to-end streaming performance is unavailable (`2610.34186`, `2610.32749`).
- All 17 current analyses are compacted. The retained evidence supports a useful qualitative digest, but many precise comparisons, ablations, and uncertainty estimates cannot be checked.

## Main results worth noting

- **Procedural scoring changes the assessment:** On synthetic Indian banking sessions across six models, TauIndianBankBench (`2610.30161`) reports that combined action checks catch violations missed by final-state-only scoring in **6.5–12.0% of sessions**. This supports checking required reads and write purity; it does not establish complete temporal compliance.
- **GUI preprocessing can help frozen models:** PreView (`2610.33645`) reports **1.5–13.9 percentage-point average grounding-accuracy gains across five benchmarks**, relative to grounding without its preparation. Missing detailed tables prevent assigning the range endpoints to specific models here. Incorrect guidance can also degrade predictions.
- **Instruction capacity depends on load composition:** For **GPT-4o mini**, Instruction Capacity (`2610.36095`) estimates roughly **37–38 simultaneous substitution rules at 95% correct replacements under reference conditions**, falling to **18–19 at full replacement density**. These are fitted capacities for the controlled instrument, not general prompt limits.
- **Learned market response remains incomplete:** Across **25 neural model–stock pairs**, LOB-Impact (`2610.27100`) identifies responses beyond mechanical book effects using paired intervention controls. Impact is steeper than the square-root reference, and **none passes the benchmark’s relaxation test**.
- **User constraints complicate completion metrics:** Across nine browser-agent backbones, PersonaCUABench (`2610.22990`) reports lower original-specification completion under persona-conditioned requests than unchanged requests. Its analysis also shows that authorized pauses or constraints can make that comparator misleading.
- **Promising improvements lack complete numerical support here:** LongZero reports gains in five-benchmark averages for two Qwen models (`2610.30132`); HuGe reports benefits from adding egocentric experience to robot demonstrations (`2610.34186`); and Know, then Draw reports benefits from semantic autoregressive pretraining (`2610.37626`). The retained evidence does not support reconstructing their full score tables.
- **Safety improvement is conditional:** The DeepSeek assessment (`2610.35671`) reports gains over DeepSeek V4, alongside failures under partial oversight and assigned deceptive goals. Truncation and evaluation awareness prevent treating favorable benchmark behavior as deployment assurance.

## Open research directions

The following are editorial proposals motivated by the supplied analyses, not demonstrated integrations.

1. **Validate evaluation against authorized user intent.** For RealCLI, PersonaCUABench, and TauIndianBankBench (`2610.22507`, `2610.22990`, `2610.30161`), obtain blinded human judgments on matched trajectories, including requested pauses and reversed writes. Compare agreement among original-task success, requirement satisfaction, and procedural checks.

2. **Measure the incremental value of temporal checking.** For TauIndianBankBench and the safety assessment (`2610.30161`, `2610.35671`), construct trajectories with identical final states but different action orders, temporary interventions, or repeated calls. Test which violations each scorer detects and which require explicit temporal contracts.

3. **Stress-test external guidance when it is wrong.** For CausGround and PreView (`2610.29702`, `2610.33645`), vary precondition accuracy, visual-state corruption, crop coverage, and cue correctness independently. Measure downstream failure and recovery against unguided frozen models under matched inference budgets.

4. **Audit synthetic-task validity before attributing training gains.** For LongZero (`2610.30132`), manually label contradiction and answer-validity rates, then compare stricter semantic filtering with answer-string filtering at matched synthesis and training cost. For AR-BED (`2610.20832`), separately vary hypothesis coverage and patient/likelihood simulators to test reward-model dependence.

5. **Test representations under controlled changes.** For OmniSpectra (`2601.15351`), observe the same objects across grids and resolutions and measure embedding consistency and task degradation. For S² (`2610.31670`), use instruction swaps and prospectively select policies before closed-loop evaluation to test predictive usefulness beyond retrospective correlation.

6. **Isolate what enables human-to-humanoid transfer.** For HuGe (`2610.34186`), hold robot supervision, controller, and training budget fixed while ablating pseudo-proprioception, visual adaptation, and converted human actions. Repeat physical tasks across placements and report conversion failures and trial uncertainty.

7. **Measure complete deployment costs.** For Falcon and TAO (`2610.32749`, `2610.37230`), evaluate end-to-end latency rather than component computation alone. Include visual processing and buffering for speech extraction, and planning, stale-plan rejection, tool-duration shifts, and output quality for scheduling.

8. **Separate representation benefits from rendering and selection effects.** For OmniShowcase and Know, then Draw (`2610.35684`, `2610.37626`), control reference information, conditioning strength, prompt rewriting, selection, and inference compute. Corrupt geometry or semantic tokens systematically to identify when intermediate representations help or mislead.

9. **Validate proxy measurements against their intended uses.** For LOB-Impact (`2610.27100`), test whether simulator impact fidelity predicts downstream execution-policy behavior in a common controlled environment. For Instruction Capacity (`2610.36095`), test whether substitution-based estimates predict failures on practical instruction sets across multiple models.
