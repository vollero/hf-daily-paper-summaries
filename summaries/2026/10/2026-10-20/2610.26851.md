# Manga2Animate: An Agentic Framework for Faithful Manga Animation with Script and Storyboard Planning

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-20.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.26851) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.26851)


## One-sentence takeaway
Manga2Animate uses source-grounded script and storyboard review to improve manga-to-animation fidelity, with promising results on eight short stories but incomplete numerical comparison tables in the supplied text.

## Problem and motivation
The task is to turn manga pages into coherent animation while preserving plot, character identity, composition, and art style. The authors identify two gaps: text-first storytelling systems discard visual evidence, while direct manga-to-video generation can misread panel order and dialogue or animate the page layout itself (§1, Fig. 2).

## Approach and contributions
- **Source-grounded planning:** Ordered panels, OCR transcripts, and character references become aligned script clips and storyboard panels, allowing each intermediate output to be checked against its source (§§3.1–3.3).
- **Representation-specific review:** Across two refinement rounds, agents compose scripts from the best descriptions at each panel position and select complete storyboards for the closed-source backend. Script and storyboard feedback jointly guide targeted script revisions; the open-source image backend instead selects panels individually (§3.4, Appendix B).
- **Generation and evaluation framework:** Reviewed plans condition either complete multi-shot generation or individual first-frame-conditioned clips. MangaEval adapts existing fidelity, consistency, motion, aesthetic, and cost metrics to this task (§§3.5, 4.3, Appendix F).

The authors claim the first automated framework for faithful manga-to-animation generation; the supplied evidence does not independently establish that priority (§1).

These results support the authors’ claim that reviewed visual planning helps short-form adaptation, especially identity and content preservation. They do not establish reliable long-form narrative understanding or correct motion: DD measures movement magnitude, and PA evaluates adherence to the shared refined script rather than independently verifying manga events (Appendix F). Missing table cells prevent assessment of most effect sizes.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated:** Outputs vary across stochastic runs; hosted models can restrict content; sketchy, cluttered, or complex panels and scenes requiring outside context can be misinterpreted (§5).
- **Analyst inference—limited scope:** Eight six-panel stories provide narrow evidence for generalization to chapters, longer sequences, and difficult styles (§4.1).
- **Analyst inference—confounded reviewer ablation:** Removing a reviewer also reduces its stage to one candidate and removes feedback. This does not isolate reviewing from candidate diversity or generation budget (§4.7, Table 2).
- **Analyst inference—measurement limits:** Identity scoring depends on successful detection and matching; PA shares the pipeline’s refined script as its reference. Neither alone establishes event-level faithfulness (Appendix F).
- **Missing evidence:** Most comparison-table values, uncertainty estimates, repeated-run results, and actual prompt contents are unavailable in the supplied text. Appendix H contains prompt figure captions only.

## Open research questions
1. **Does the approach scale to chapters?** Evaluation covers short sequences, and missing context is an acknowledged failure mode. Test progressively longer excerpts with controlled access to preceding pages.
2. **How much benefit comes specifically from reviewing?** Reviewer removal also changes candidate count and feedback. Compare random selection, reviewer selection, and reviewer-guided refinement under equal generation budgets.
3. **Does greater visual similarity imply correct story events?** Current metrics do not directly establish event or motion correctness. Annotate actions, speakers, and event order, then compare those judgments with MangaEval and human ratings.
4. **How robust is generation across runs and difficult styles?** Both variability and complex artwork are acknowledged limitations. Repeat generation on stratified clean, sketchy, and cluttered inputs, reporting failure rates and score dispersion.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The method and several ablation results are described clearly, but evidence for the overall performance margin is incomplete.

Material missing information includes most numerical comparison tables, uncertainty and repeated-run statistics, exact user-study preference breakdowns, full story identities and selection criteria, actual agent prompts, and confirmed code or evaluation-set availability.
