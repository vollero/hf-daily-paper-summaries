# SpaceFlow: Locally Controllable 3D Generation

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-09.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.12399) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12399)


## One-sentence takeaway
SpaceFlow adds training-free, part-specific geometry strengths and text/image appearance routing to TRELLIS, with reported gains in local adherence but texture-quality and routing limitations.

## Problem and motivation
Asset creation requires preserving selected shapes while revising other parts and assigning different materials to different regions. The authors identify a gap between globally controlled geometry generation and global or post-hoc appearance transfer: neither provides unified control over **where** constraints apply and **how strongly** geometry should follow them (§1–2).

## Approach and contributions
SpaceFlow uses editable primitives as control regions for a pretrained TRELLIS generator, without updating network weights (§3.3).

- **Local geometry control:** Encode the complete scaffold and its high-control subset, then inject the high-control latent through spatially masked blending and repeated noise/denoise resampling. Low-control regions retain greater generative freedom (§3.4).
- **Local appearance control:** Cluster generated geometry using PartField descriptors, match clusters to input primitives, and route each voxel’s cross-attention to its assigned text or image cue. An intra-condition self-attention bias and test-time contrastive optimization encourage part coherence (§3.5; Appendix S2).
- **Unified control:** Reuse the primitive decomposition for geometry and appearance conditioning within one generation pipeline. The authors also construct a multipart evaluation benchmark; its public release is not established (§3.3; Appendix S4).

The contributions extend existing generation and guidance components rather than introduce a newly trained backbone.

The evidence supports a useful local-control capability in the tested setting. It does not establish universal superiority: human preference against weak global control is below half, texture detail favors appearance baselines, and image conditioning has no comparative evaluation. Missing table bodies prevent assessment of most effect sizes and uncertainty intervals.

## Limitations and validity risks
**Author-stated limitations:** Conflicting text and geometry can produce distorted shapes; imperfect cluster boundaries can misroute materials or leave discontinuous textures (Appendix S8; Figs. S10–S11). Low-control changes can also pull neighboring high-control geometry, and nearest-primitive metric assignment can mislabel newly generated structures (Appendix S7.1).

**Analyst inferences:**

- Generalization remains bounded by a curated benchmark spanning seven categories and 2–18 primitives per asset; validation excludes low-control primitives contained within high-control ones (Appendix S4).
- Two-view VLM assessment and limited calibration probes do not establish full-surface correctness or agreement with human appearance judgments (Appendix S5).
- Appearance comparisons isolate texture generation on SpaceFlow geometry, so they do not establish superiority of complete competing pipelines (§4.2).
- The supplied results do not separately quantify the effects of routing, attention bias, contrastive guidance, and small-part recovery. Missing settings-table bodies also restrict reproducibility (Appendix S2).

## Open research questions
- **Can finer or soft routing reduce boundary failures?** Appendix S8 documents misassigned materials. Compare current routing with finer clusters and distance-weighted boundaries on annotated material regions.
- **Which appearance components drive adherence and texture loss?** Component-specific results are unavailable. Ablate routing, attention bias, contrastive optimization, and small-part recovery on identical structures.
- **How robust is geometry control outside curated inputs?** Conflicts fail and contained low-control regions are excluded. Test controlled prompt–geometry conflicts and overlapping or nested scaffolds.
- **Do image-reference gains survive independent evaluation?** Current evidence is standalone VLM scoring. Run blinded human comparisons against a defined multi-reference baseline with full-view inspection.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The method and explicit human-study results are clear, but much comparative evidence is available only as author interpretation.

Material missing information includes numerical bodies of Tables 1–4 and S1–S4, exact plotted geometry values, image-evaluation sample count and uncertainty, calibration-probe counts, runtime/hardware costs, component ablation results, and confirmed code/dataset availability.
