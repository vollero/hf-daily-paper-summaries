# LiveMACE: Process-Aware Evaluation of LLM Agent Capabilities in Evolving Markets

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-19.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.09872) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09872)


## One-sentence takeaway
LiveMACEBench finds that trading returns can diverge from trace-measured agent capabilities, supporting evaluation that separately measures mechanism access, use, and realized benefit (§§3.1–3.2).

## Problem and motivation
Outcomes alone cannot identify which capabilities produced an agent’s success. In evolving environments, external changes and persistent consequences of earlier actions further complicate attribution. The authors identify a gap in evaluating how agents use tools, memory, rules, and collaboration throughout a trajectory, rather than primarily ranking final performance (§1).

## Approach and contributions
- **Process-aware evaluation:** Combine realized trading outcomes, matched configuration comparisons, and mechanism-specific trace diagnostics (§§1–2.3).
- **Controlled mechanism variants:** Extend a common ReAct Base with dynamic tool routing, persistent memory, hierarchical rules, or manager-led specialist collaboration. Diagnostics separate stages such as tool discovery versus invocation and rule execution versus auditability (§§2.2–2.3).
- **Persistent live-market benchmark:** Evaluate five backbones during April 13–May 13, 2026, using paper-trading accounts, four-hour decision rounds, and persistent portfolio state across six cryptocurrencies and seventeen U.S. equities (§§2.1, 3).

The contribution is chiefly an evaluation framework and its diagnostic evidence; the supplied text does not establish a public code or trace release.

These reported comparisons support separating observed outcomes from specific behavioral measurements. They do not establish general capability rankings across environments or causal explanations for every return difference. More stable cumulative rankings also do not independently validate the metrics: cumulative averaging may contribute to stability **(analyst inference)**.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Truncated evidence:** The supplied source omits a substantial middle segment, and many tables appear only as captions. Coverage is incomplete, including parts of the scoring and runtime details.
- **Restricted external validity:** The authors identify longer horizons, broader regimes, and other evolving environments as future work (§5). Generalization beyond this asset universe and evaluation window remains untested in the supplied evidence **(analyst inference)**.
- **Incomplete comparisons:** Gemini’s Tool Use run was discarded after stale state observations caused repeated executions; the authors report fixing the defect. Grok’s Multi-Agent run was excluded because repetitive calls and timeouts prevented a reliable trajectory (Appendices C.3, C.6).
- **Metric scope:** OCS measures participation and source use, explicitly excluding semantic accuracy. A case achieves maximal OCS while risk warnings fail to constrain execution (Appendices B.4.4, D.4).
- **Judge dependence:** Cross-judge checks preserve several conclusions, but absolute scores and some orderings vary (Appendices C.3–C.5).
- **Attribution limits:** Repeated independent runs and uncertainty estimates for return effects are not established in the supplied text. Matched configurations therefore do not quantify how much reported benefit depends on stochastic variation **(analyst inference)**.

## Open research questions
- **Do the capability–outcome gaps persist across regimes?** Only one evaluation window is reported. Repeat matched configurations across multiple market windows and compare both rankings and risk effects.
- **Does evidence citation measure substantive collaboration?** OCS can be maximal despite unheeded warnings (Appendix D.4). Experimentally vary specialist warnings and measure corresponding changes in the manager’s plan.
- **Can memory preserve downside protection without suppressing momentum opportunities?** The authors’ Qwen case illustrates missed upside (Appendix D.2). Compare the current memory design against regime-conditioned retrieval under matched conditions.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The supplied narrative supports the central diagnostic findings, but the source is truncated.

Material missing information includes complete table cells, some metric definitions and execution-budget details, repeated-run uncertainty for trading effects, public code/trace availability, and independent human validation of judge scores.
