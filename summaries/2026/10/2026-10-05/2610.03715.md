# 4DCodeBench: Benchmarking Agents on Inverse Graphics of Dynamic Scenes

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-05.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.03715) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03715)


## One-sentence takeaway
4DCodeBench evaluates video-to-executable-scene reconstruction on 200 dynamic scenes and finds that all 18 tested model configurations recover appearance and initial geometry more reliably than motion, while fidelity scores leave physical generalization untested (§§2, 4.2, 6).

## Problem and motivation
The task is to reconstruct a video as code that generates explicit 3D geometry evolving over time. Unlike fitting a prescribed scene model, agents must choose representations for objects, materials, interactions, and dynamics themselves (§1).

The authors identify a benchmark gap: existing code-based reconstruction tasks cover static scenes or restricted physical regimes. Their proposed benchmark combines real footage with synthetic scenes spanning rigid, deformable, co-dimensional, and flowing matter, with direct geometry and motion evaluation where ground truth exists (§5; Appendix B, Table A1).

## Approach and contributions
- **Benchmark and dataset:** 100 curated real videos and 100 simulated scenes with 4D ground truth, covering interacting materials, deformation, flow, and fracture (§§2.2–2.3).
- **Executable reconstruction task:** Agents receive RGB video and a fixed prompt, then produce a standalone program, Blender render, per-frame meshes, camera parameters, and persistent material trajectories. Dynamics can be analytic, keyframed, or simulated (§2.1; Appendices G.2–G.3).
- **Evaluation suite and model study:** The authors evaluate 18 configurations using perceptual, image-space dynamics, estimated depth, synthetic 3D geometry, and synthetic 3D dynamics metrics, complemented by VQA and human/VLM preferences. Executable submissions also support analysis of modeling strategies and geometry defects (§§2.4, 3–4).

The central contribution is a benchmark and diagnostic evaluation framework, rather than a new reconstruction model.

## Evidence and main results
| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |

| Dynamics lag behind appearance and initial geometry | All 18 configurations have higher average static-family than dynamic-family scores. Astra [Max] scores 0.91 averaged over Perceptual and 3D Geometry, versus 0.67 over 2D and 3D Dynamics. These families use different metrics and scene subsets. | §4.2; Figs. 8, A20 |
| Automated rankings closely track human rankings | Human study: 3,587 judgments from 76 participants covering 17 configurations. Human–VLM Elo Spearman correlation is 0.980; human Elo–Overall correlation is 0.96. | Agreement weakens for close competitors | Individual human–VLM agreement is near chance within 50 Elo points, reaching 75% at 132-point separation and 90% at 343 points. | §4.1 |
| Higher reasoning effort improves Astra’s reconstruction | Low → High → Max raises Overall from 0.73 → 0.77 → 0.79, 2D Dynamics from 0.48 → 0.55 → 0.60, and 3D Dynamics from 0.63 → 0.69 → 0.73, with increased token use and cost. | §§4.5; Fig. 4 |

The evidence supports a reconstruction-fidelity benchmark with strong **model-level** human alignment and a consistent static–dynamic score gap. It does not establish recovery of correct physical mechanisms, reliable discrimination between closely matched models, or a causal advantage from simulation. The VQA baseline also limits interpretation of headline accuracy; the text does not clarify whether that baseline receives equivalent gating.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated:** Prescribed trajectories can score highly without transferable physics. Initial-condition interventions, force interventions, and longer-horizon prediction remain untested (§6).
- **Author-stated:** Only final submissions are evaluated; intermediate reconstruction improvements and the value of visual feedback are not measured (§6).
- **Analyst inference:** One run per configuration per scene leaves stochastic repeatability unmeasured. Native proprietary harnesses and Stirrup for open-weight models mean comparisons include harness differences (§3.1; Appendix G.1).
- **Analyst inference:** Real-video geometry and motion references depend on estimated depth, flow, and tracks, rather than measured 4D truth. Six transparent-object real scenes are excluded from specified rasterized-geometry metrics (Appendices D.1, D.3).
- **Analyst inference:** Generalization is bounded by curated footage favoring stationary cameras, limited occlusion, and continuous shots, with humans and animals excluded. Synthetic truth also inherits simulator assumptions (§2.2).
- **Analyst inference:** Synthetic registration corrects some camera errors, and static 3D scoring evaluates frame-zero visible surfaces; strong scores therefore do not establish complete hidden-geometry recovery or accurate camera export (Appendix D.4).

## Open research questions
1. **Does simulation improve transferable physical understanding?** Fidelity leaves mechanisms underconstrained (§6). Next experiment: perturb initial conditions or forces and compare analytic versus simulated reconstructions against new ground-truth trajectories.
2. **Would enforcing simulation change model rankings?** Models adopt different strategies, but strategy choice is not experimentally controlled (§4.4). Next experiment: rerun matched scenes under unrestricted and simulation-required conditions.
3. **Which render-and-compare iterations provide useful gains?** Intermediate outputs are not evaluated (§6). Next experiment: score saved checkpoints against cumulative cost and iteration count.
4. **Can VQA discriminate successful reconstructions beyond answer imbalance?** The leading accuracy nearly matches always-Yes (Fig. 5). Next experiment: evaluate balanced questions and controlled defective renders with identical gating.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: high** for the reported task, protocol, and descriptive results; lower for conclusions about physical understanding or strategy effectiveness.

Material information unavailable includes repeated-run variability, controlled strategy/harness ablations, intervention and forecasting results, equivalent-gating details for the VQA baseline, participant demographics, GPU hardware specifications, and independent verification of the stated released assets.
