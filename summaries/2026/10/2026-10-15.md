# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-10-15

## Executive summary

- Embodied research emphasizes the interfaces between perception, memory, and action: calibrated camera tokens, spline trajectories, register tokens, and elapsed-time memory offer different ways to structure robot policies (`2606.24472`, `2610.05230`, `2610.17638`, `2610.20524`).
- Evaluation increasingly separates plausible behavior from completed work. Tool selection, procedural execution, software-bug discovery, rule application, and harness conformance receive explicit diagnostic treatment (`2610.02089`, `2610.14020`, `2610.14096`, `2610.19698`, `2610.20344`).
- Distillation papers address unreliable supervision through teacher agreement, privileged value estimation, critique-guided repair, semantic action matching, and reasoning translation. A comparative study cautions that agent self-distillation gains remain setting-dependent (`2610.13521`, `2610.20516`, `2610.20572`, `2610.19820`, `2610.20828`, `2610.19789`).
- Safety evaluations distinguish legitimate task completion from unauthorized actions. Workspace attacks, action monitoring, decision attacks, and adversarial co-training offer complementary evidence, without establishing deployment-wide protection (`2610.13623`, `2610.17301`, `2610.19118`, `2610.19422`).
- Efficiency claims require separate accounting for quality, preparation, hardware, and complete workloads. KV4-RL reports measured training speedups; JSSA reports B200 decoding throughput; Latent Compaction leaves practical speed and memory savings unmeasured (`2610.14448`, `2610.19026`, `2610.20582`).
- Persistent visual-world state is becoming an explicit system component. DAO and ATAS maintain information beyond recent frames, but DAO’s persistence evidence is qualitative and ATAS’s quantitative tables are incomplete (`2610.19726`, `2610.20571`).
- Exact synthetic uncertainty, verified social outcomes, and task-specific visual judgments expose limitations that aggregate accuracy or fluency can conceal (`2610.17016`, `2610.20195`, `2610.19551`).
- All 88 supplied current-paper analyses are partial, with omitted spans. The digest therefore favors visible methods and qualified findings over rankings or reconstructed numerical comparisons.

## Main results worth noting

- **Bug discovery is a distinct bottleneck.** On SWEeper-Bench’s 200 interaction-dependent web-software tasks, the best tested agent passes **59.0%** without the original bug report. Supplying detailed bug descriptions raises five agents to **96.0–96.5%** (`2610.14096`). This intervention supports the importance of discovery; it does not isolate every discovery or repair component.

- **Rollout acceleration translates only partly into training acceleration.** KV4-RL reports **1.80× rollout** and **1.27× full-iteration speedups** relative to its BF16-rollout comparison in **Qwen3-8B mathematical RLVR**, with final six-task mean accuracy close to BF16 (`2610.14448`). Limited training replication qualifies the quality-preservation conclusion.

- **Sparse decoding has a hardware-specific measured result.** JSSA reports **up to 6.43× decoding throughput** at **128K context on B200**, while preserving much of full-attention accuracy (`2610.19026`). This is a decoding-throughput comparison, not a demonstrated reduction in complete reasoning time or full KV storage.

- **Two software and reasoning benchmarks retain substantial unsolved work.** Doc2Harness’s best evaluated model reaches **53.05% mean behavioral-test pass rate across three builds** (`2610.20344`); RuleTrek’s best tested agent reaches **55.29% end-to-end score** (`2610.19698`). These different metrics should not be ranked against each other.

- **Verified social outcomes improve under paired-world training.** PaCPO raises **Qwen3-8B Verified Success from 20.42% to 28.33% on 240 HICO test tasks** (`2610.20195`). Verified Success requires both feasible coordinated records and judged integrity; main-comparison training-seed uncertainty is missing.

- **Multimodal initialization can avoid paired alignment training in the tested recipe.** PORTAL achieves **96.3–106.2% of the supervised initialization baseline’s average downstream score across seven configurations**, after **identical instruction tuning** (`2610.18598`). The result does not eliminate subsequent multimodal training.

- **A fusion comparison is informative but confounded.** Late LLM-guidance fusion obtains **39.4% exact match versus 31.8% for attention injection** on a **filtered, paraphrase-augmented OK-VQA validation split** (`2610.17079`). Because backbone trainability changes too, the difference does not establish that late fusion is inherently superior.

- **Game-control transfer has a substantial within-model comparison.** Player-1’s **9B MineDojo-300 success rises from 10.54% after supervised initialization to 38.33% after synthetic-environment RL** (`2610.20584`). Incomplete SFT/evaluation overlap information limits stronger generalization claims.

- **Some useful results are diagnostic rather than leaderboard gains.** ProbabilityBench provides exact probabilities in three synthetic worlds (`2610.17016`); Thinking Leakage uses interventions to challenge attribution of no-think RL gains (`2610.14270`); the self-distillation comparison shows why endpoint quality should be accompanied by stability and training-cost measurements (`2610.19789`).

## Open research directions

The following are editorial experiment proposals grounded in explicit limitations of the supplied analyses.

1. **Test physical composition without oracle assistance.** For HumanoidToolBench and RoboCombine (`2610.02089`, `2610.15425`), compare learned versus oracle selection/order/handoff under matched atomic policies. Report errors at each boundary, final completion, and retention of isolated skills.

2. **Separate timing-aware memory from generic history benefits.** For Temporal Flow Policy and Vela (`2610.20524`, `2610.05230`), vary observation cadence, jitter, dropout, and execution query grids while holding demonstrations and policy capacity fixed. Measure physical timing error, contacts, and uninterrupted task completion.
