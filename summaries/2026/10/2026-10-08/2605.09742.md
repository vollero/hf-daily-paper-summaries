# TIDES: Implicit Time-Awareness in Selective State Space Models

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-08.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.09742) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09742)


## One-sentence takeaway
TIDES separates input-dependent dynamics from physical-time discretization, with reported improvements in time-series modeling and sampling-rate extrapolation, although its Physiome-ODE advantage over LinODEnet is statistically inconclusive (§§3–5).

## Problem and motivation
Irregularly sampled sequences require models to account for elapsed time between observations. The authors identify a tradeoff: S5 preserves physical sampling intervals but uses fixed linear dynamics, while Mamba gains token-dependent dynamics by learning its discretization step, requiring physical time to enter as an additional feature. They argue that this learned relationship can fail outside the training sampling regime (§§1–2).

## Approach and contributions

- **Architecture:** TIDES makes decay rates, input projections \(B\), and output projections \(C\) input-dependent while keeping oscillation frequencies static and using physical elapsed time directly in discretization. The authors claim novelty in combining selective eigenvalues with physical-time discretization (§§3.1, 6).
- **Implementation:** HiPPO-derived initialization, low-rank \(B,C\) projectors, normalization, optional deep projectors, and stable eigenvalue parameterizations support the architecture (§3.2).
- **Diagnostic and evaluation:** Fading Flash isolates conditional-decay modeling and timestep extrapolation; EigenWorms random-drop experiments test sampling-density shifts; broader benchmarks assess classification and forecasting (§§4–5, Appendix D).

These results support the architecture’s usefulness under the tested sampling shifts and benchmark protocols. They do not establish universal extrapolation, dominance across forecasting systems, or superiority to optimized Mamba implementations in runtime. Most result-table bodies are absent from the supplied text, so their aggregate claims cannot be independently reconstructed.

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Author-stated:** No language-modeling or other regular-grid long-range evaluation; additional tuning choices; generic scan rather than a hardware-aware kernel; limited empirical support and no formal expressivity result for keeping oscillation frequencies static (§7).
- **Analyst inference:** Fading Flash uses the same ZOH dynamics that motivate TIDES and only one training seed, limiting evidence beyond this controlled setting (Appendix D).
- **Analyst inference:** Sampling-shift ablation is concentrated on EigenWorms. Official Mamba comparisons also differ in weight decay and, for Mamba-3, discretization, limiting attribution solely to selectivity placement (Appendix H; Table 9 notes).
- **Analyst inference:** Reproduced baseline scores and baseline-pool-dependent ranks constrain comparability; Physiome-ODE uses synthetic random masking, and its normalization includes all instances rather than training instances alone (Appendices E.2, G).
- **Text ambiguity:** Appendix C describes complex S5/TIDES states, while Appendix D describes real-diagonal toy variants. The abstract advertises available code, whereas the checklist describes a future public release.

## Open research questions

- **Does robustness survive structured missingness?** Random dropping does not represent all measurement processes (Appendix E). Next experiment: compare models under burst outages and value-dependent observation schedules.
- **How far can arbitrary-horizon forecasting extrapolate?** The authors explicitly leave horizon degradation unresolved (§7). Next experiment: sweep forecast gaps beyond training horizons and report masked MSE.
- **When are selective oscillation frequencies useful?** Evidence against them is limited to the reported ablation (§7). Next experiment: test static versus selective frequencies on controlled frequency-changing signals at matched capacity.
- **Does the resource advantage survive optimized implementations?** Current timing uses a generic scan (§7). Next experiment: benchmark optimized kernels against S5 and official Mamba variants across lengths and batch sizes.

## Confidence and missing information
