# StepCAD: Mesh-to-CAD Code Generation via LLM Policy and Geometry-Guided Search

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-08.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.03799) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.03799)


## One-sentence takeaway
StepCAD combines intermediate-geometry-conditioned CAD code generation with execution-scored local search, reporting substantially better geometric reconstruction on CADBench while leaving design-intent recovery and practical editability unestablished.

## Problem and motivation
Mesh-to-CAD reconstruction converts unstructured geometry into executable parametric construction programs for engineering workflows. The authors identify two gaps: complete-program prediction offers limited opportunities to correct intermediate errors, and existing training corpora insufficiently support diverse operations and construction-state supervision. Their objective is faithful shape reconstruction, without claiming recovery of the original construction history, dimensions, or constraints (§§1–3.1).

## Approach and contributions
- **ARCADE-1.5M:** A synthetic corpus of 1.5M executable CadQuery programs and approximately 12.5M intermediate state-action transitions. Its contribution is operation composition, scale, and trajectory supervision—not a new operation vocabulary. Programs average 40.3 counted operations but 8.5 solid operations (§3.2; Table 1).
- **State-conditioned policy:** Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B receives Point-MAE embeddings of target and intermediate point clouds, geometry metadata, and prior code. It generates and executes one construction action at a time, with a 12-action inference cap (§3.3.1; Appendix I).
- **Geometry-guided refinement:** Beam search ranks executable edits by target-mesh IoU. Its four operators refine continuous parameters, replace sketches with mesh-derived profiles, remove overshoot through complement cuts, and skip harmful operations (§3.3.2; Appendix J). This refinement also works on compatible programs from another generator (Table 10).

The evidence supports improved geometric fidelity and executable validity against the evaluated baselines. Within-system comparisons support intermediate-state conditioning and refinement. They do not isolate the dataset’s causal contribution across different pretrained systems, establish recovery of original design intent, or demonstrate successful user editing. Statistical uncertainty is unreported.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Authors’ limitations:** Fixed edits, bounded parameter neighborhoods, and a finite beam can fail to repair poor seeds; the action cap limits inference capacity. Synthetic training introduces a domain gap (§5).
- **Authors’ limitations:** IoU/CD do not establish dimension, constraint, or semantic correctness. Coarse selectors restrict entity-specific editing, and constraint preservation or user editing success is not evaluated (§5; Appendix K).
- **Comparison and reproducibility:** Released baselines use different training corpora; the authors explicitly reject a data-only interpretation of those comparisons. Code/data availability is deferred, and error bars are absent (§4; checklist, p. 39).
- **Analyst inference:** Table 6 uses winner-attribution rather than leave-one-operator-out experiments, so its contribution shares do not establish each edit’s independent causal benefit.
- **Analyst inference:** Search specifications are inconsistent: Figure 10 retains the parent among proposals, whereas Algorithm 1 lists only edited candidates. This leaves exact beam behavior ambiguous.
- **Analyst inference:** The supplied evaluations do not establish robustness to noisy or incomplete scans despite scan reconstruction being a motivating application.

## Open research questions
- **Can geometric fidelity coexist with preserved feature semantics and constraints?** §5 leaves this unestablished. Compare IoU-only search with feature-aware objectives on dimension- and constraint-annotated parts, followed by editing tasks.
- **How does performance scale beyond the action budget?** Long training programs do not establish inference capacity beyond the cap. Sweep action budgets and beam widths on complexity-stratified targets, measuring fidelity and runtime.
- **What is the independent benefit of each search operator?** Winner-attribution does not answer removal effects. Run matched-budget leave-one-operator-out experiments with repeated seeds.
- **How robust is reconstruction to scan degradation?** The synthetic domain gap remains unresolved. Evaluate controlled noise, missing surfaces, and real scans against the existing mesh setting.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The supplied text provides substantial quantitative and implementation evidence, but lacks uncertainty estimates, precise ablation subset/aggregation details, explicit train/validation split and overlap controls, detailed numerical entity-matching tolerances, and an unambiguous parent-retention rule for search. Released code/data access, scan robustness, and practical editing outcomes are not established.
