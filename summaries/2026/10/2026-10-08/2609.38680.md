# ReGain: Restoring Subject Fidelity in Personalization on Synthetic Images

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-08.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.38680) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.38680)


## One-sentence takeaway
ReGain calibrates sampling-time guidance by frequency and denoising step, recovering a reported 51–64% of the SD1.5 subject-fidelity gap caused by synthetic-image personalization without requiring real photos or retraining (§§3.3, 6).

## Problem and motivation
DreamBooth personalization on generated subject images can produce oversaturated colors and excess fine texture, reducing fidelity to the subject. The authors isolate this effect by training real-photo and synthetic-image models from the same base with the same recipe (§3.1).

Their identified gap is miscalibration of the **guidance term itself**, rather than merely an unsuitable guidance weight: synthetic personalization increases the angle between conditional and unconditional predictions, with inflation varying across frequencies, time, and related prompts (§3.2).

## Approach and contributions
- **Diagnosis:** The authors characterize guidance inflation as angle-driven, semantically localized, persistent on shared noisy inputs, and strongest in high-frequency bands (§3.2).
- **Correction:** ReGain evaluates the personalized and base models on forward-noised synthetic training images. It estimates gains from their masked guidance-energy ratios, comparing the personalized subject prompt with the base model’s class prompt (§3.3).
- **Compact sampling schedule:** Dynamic programming compresses gains into contiguous frequency and time groups. Sampling applies these gains inside an attention-derived subject mask and retains ordinary CFG elsewhere. Calibration and correction require no training (§3.3).
- **Evaluation:** The method is tested with DreamBooth and DreamBooth-LoRA across SD1.5, SDXL, and SD3.5. Segmentation components are reused from existing methods rather than introduced as new contributions (§4.1; Appendix B).

These author-reported results support partial restoration under the tested protocol. They do not establish complete identity recovery or generalization to arbitrary synthetic-data sources. The supplied text contains table captions and narrative summaries, but omits the numerical table bodies, preventing inspection of exact per-setting scores and uncertainty.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated:** The base model is an imperfect reference, so ReGain cannot fully match real-photo personalization. Mask errors apply correction to the wrong regions. The internal network origin of inflation remains unresolved (§5).
- **Analyst inference:** Scope is limited to one synthetic-data round generated by the real-photo-personalized model. Other generators, mixed real/synthetic sets, and repeated generations are not established by these experiments (§§3.1, 4.1).
- **Analyst inference:** Comparable CLIP-embedding diversity does not rule out every training-set difference. The lower-generation-guidance and prior-preservation controls use only `dog6` (Appendix A.1–A.3).
- **Analyst inference:** Baseline rankings apply to the reported default configurations, without establishing performance after tuning each baseline for this task (Appendix C).
- **Reporting ambiguity:** The mask ablation reports a CLIP-T drop of 0.008 to 0.282 from 0.289; those rounded endpoints differ by 0.007 (§4.4).

## Open research questions
- **Can training-time correction close the remaining gap?** The authors identify residual fidelity loss (§5). A next experiment could compare guidance-calibrated training with ReGain sampling on the same subject/model pairs.
- **How sensitive is recovery to mask quality?** Mask errors are acknowledged but not quantitatively isolated (§5). Compare attention masks with annotated masks and controlled erosion or dilation.
- **Does the correction transfer to other synthetic-data sources?** Evaluation uses images generated by the real-photo-personalized model (§4.1). Repeat the protocol with edited images, other generators, and mixed-provenance sets.
- **Where does inflation originate inside the network?** Measurements characterize output predictions (§5). Layerwise activation and attention interventions could test which components drive the angle increase.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The method and evaluation protocol are clearly described, but exact numerical contents of Tables 1–6 are unavailable. Material gaps include complete fine-tuning parameters from Table 6, uncertainty or significance tests for main fidelity results, human fidelity judgments, and an explicit ReGain code/checkpoint release link.
