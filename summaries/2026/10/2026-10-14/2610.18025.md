# SHELDON: Reasoning through Dual Search over Hypothesis and Observation Spaces

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-14.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.18025) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18025)


## One-sentence takeaway
SHELDON explicitly searches both hypotheses and observations, achieving stronger reported benchmark scores than evaluated baselines, with ablations supporting the value of its observation space and comparative analysis (Tables 2–5).

## Problem and motivation
The authors address investigative tasks where agents must discover explanations or improve designs through tool use. They argue that existing agents collect evidence mainly around current hypotheses, encouraging local refinement while leaving shared assumptions and unexplored phenomena untested (§§1–2).

The proposed gap is therefore in how agents direct investigation: organizing existing evidence is insufficient if the search rarely encounters observations that motivate different explanations.

## Approach and contributions
- **Explicit dual-space state:** Hypotheses and observations each occupy evolving axes and regions, connected by support, refute, neutral, or unresolved evidence relations. Observations can assess multiple hypotheses (§§4.1–4.2).
- **Observation-driven investigation:** The harness alternates exploration, individual candidate testing, comparative testing, and refinement or shelving. Unexplained observations and underexplored regions guide subsequent hypotheses (Algorithm 1; §4.2).
- **Evidence-management safeguards:** Newly encountered observations remain provisional and cannot refute hypotheses until encountered again; shelved hypotheses can later be reconsidered (§4.2).
- **Empirical evaluation and analysis:** The paper evaluates diagnosis, root-cause analysis, abstract reasoning, and optimization, alongside component ablations and exploration measurements (§§5–6). The dual-space idea draws on prior scientific-discovery models; the contribution is its agent-harness implementation and evaluation.

The results support improved performance for the evaluated harness configurations and a contribution from explicit observations and comparison. They do not establish that broader search alone causes success: the authors find no task-level correlation between breadth counts and score on the diagnostic benchmarks (§6.1). Equal budget caps also do not imply equal actual expenditure.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated scope:** Diagnostic results concern controlled benchmarks and do not establish clinical suitability (Ethics statement).
- **Author-stated measurement limits:** Projected embedding hulls are breadth proxies; neither hull area nor cosine diversity accounts for cost. Some EvoX program embeddings are truncated (Appendix E).
- **Analyst inference—uncertainty:** ADRS reports three-run means, but the supplied tables provide no confidence intervals or significance tests. Reliability of smaller differences remains unclear (Table 4).
- **Analyst inference—comparison sensitivity:** GoS is modified, including ORCA prompt rewrites; ScientistOne is represented by released programs rather than fresh agent runs. ORCA ground truth excludes post-report faults, and human-reference systems scores use different hardware (Appendices A–B).
- **Analyst inference—implementation consistency:** Appendix E describes an earlier QNN/ADRS loop without hypothesis regions, making it unclear whether every result evaluates the same representation described in §4.
- **Analyst inference—mechanism isolation:** The reported ablations do not separately isolate provisional observations, shelving, or region construction. Evaluation uses one specified reasoning model configuration (§5; Table 5).

## Open research questions
- **Does the advantage persist at equal actual spend?** Shared caps permit large expenditure differences. Next experiment: compare accuracy-versus-spend curves with enforced spending checkpoints (Appendix F).
- **How portable is the harness across models?** The evaluation specifies one reasoning model configuration. Next experiment: repeat the main comparisons and ablations across multiple model sizes (§5).
- **Does independently verified evidence improve investigation?** Evidence relations are model judgments, and provisional status relies on repeated encounters. Next experiment: introduce controlled erroneous observations and compare repetition-based confirmation with independent verification (§4.2).
- **Which observation-space mechanisms matter most?** Removing the whole space does not isolate mapping from evidence sharing or observation-driven generation. Next experiment: ablate these mechanisms separately under matched resources (Table 5).

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The supplied text includes detailed methods, prompts, budgets, metrics, and ablations. Material gaps include statistical uncertainty, repeated-run results outside ADRS, separate validation details for the discovered QNN circuit, and a precise mapping between earlier and current harness implementations. The authors state that code and artifacts are released; their contents were not inspected.
