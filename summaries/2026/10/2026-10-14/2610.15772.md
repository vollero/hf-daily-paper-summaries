# RSIPO: Recursive Self-Improving Policy Optimization for Agentic Reinforcement Learning via Skill-Policy Co-Evolution

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-14.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.15772) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.15772)


## One-sentence takeaway

RSIPO uses skill-conditioned trajectory replays to jointly improve skill generation and calibrate self-distillation, reporting strong ALFWorld gains but smaller, non-uniform gains on search-based question answering (§§3.2, 4.2).

## Problem and motivation

Long-horizon agent training combines sparse outcome rewards with dense supervision from a teacher given privileged skills. The authors identify two gaps: skill generators trained before RL can become mismatched to the improving policy, and skill-conditioned teachers sometimes perform worse than their students (§1, Figure 2). Consequently, fixed skill generation and uniform trust can provide increasingly irrelevant or unreliable supervision.

## Approach and contributions

- **Replay-based skill optimization:** Randomly split task rollouts into evidence and control groups, generate a skill from an evidence trajectory, and replay the skill-conditioned policy from the same initial state. Reward skill tokens using the replay utility gain relative to controls, centered by task-type average gain (§3.2, Eqs. 4–10).
- **Adaptive teacher trust:** Apply task-type empirical Bayes shrinkage to noisy replay gains, then map the estimates to bounded weights on token-level self-distillation (§3.2, Eqs. 6–7, 11–12).
- **Joint training:** Combine GRPO-style RLVR, weighted self-distillation, and skill-generation optimization in one update. External LLM annotations initialize skill generation; subsequent skill optimization uses the current policy. No skills are supplied at inference (§§3.1–3.2, 4.1).

The distinctive contribution is using measured execution gains for both on-policy skill improvement and teacher weighting; the RLVR and skill-conditioned self-distillation components build on prior methods.

## Evidence and main results

All entries below are author-reported; “pp” denotes absolute percentage-point differences.

| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| Strong ALFWorld performance | Average success: **95.7%, 98.6%, 95.0%** for Qwen2.5-3B, Qwen2.5-7B, Qwen3-1.7B; respectively **+3.9, +2.5, +3.0 pp** over SEED and **+20.7, +17.4, +48.9 pp** over GRPO. | §4.2; Table 1/3 referenced, but cells absent from supplied text. |
| Search gains are not universal | Average exact match: **45.3%** for Qwen2.5-3B, second to SEED by **0.4 pp**; **50.8%** and **42.6%** for Qwen2.5-7B and Qwen3-1.7B, highest reported averages for those backbones. | §4.2; Appendix D.1. |
| Uniform distillation can hurt | GRPO+OPSD underperforms GRPO on ALFWorld with Qwen2.5-7B and Qwen3-1.7B. | §4.2. |
| Both proposed components help in the reported ablation | On ALFWorld with Qwen2.5-3B, the SFT-initialized hybrid reaches **86.4%** success versus **95.7%** for full RSIPO; removing either proposed component costs **3.7–5.7 pp**. | §4.3, Figure 4(b). |
| Teacher guidance can be unreliable | In preliminary Qwen2.5-7B ALFWorld analysis, the teacher underperforms on **5.5–22.4%** of tasks across student-competence groups. | §1, Figure 2(b). |

These results support improved task performance in the evaluated settings and component benefits in one ALFWorld ablation. They do not establish universal superiority: the smaller search model trails SEED. The authors’ explanation that procedural skills matter less for search is plausible but is not causally isolated (§4.2).

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Author-stated:** Extra replay rollouts increase training cost; same-state resets restrict applicability to resettable environments. Live website interaction remains unresolved (Limitations).
- **Analyst inference—comparison fairness:** No compute-matched comparison or measured runtime overhead is supplied, so gains per unit of training compute remain unclear.
- **Analyst inference—component attribution:** The supplied ablation covers Qwen2.5-3B on ALFWorld and does not separately isolate empirical Bayes shrinkage from other trust-weighting choices (§4.3).
- **Analyst inference—generalization:** Evaluation uses Qwen-family models, ALFWorld, and search QA. Larger models, coding, and co-working are proposed future work rather than demonstrated settings (§5).
- **Analyst inference—leakage control:** Search skill prompts prohibit answers and copied documents, but the supplied text reports no systematic audit of compliance (Appendix E).
- **Evidence availability:** Tables 1 and 3 lack result cells, and figures appear mainly as captions and narrative descriptions, limiting independent assessment of detailed comparisons.

## Open research questions

1. **Do gains persist at equal compute?** Replay adds training work without a supplied compute-matched comparison. Compare RSIPO against baselines given equal GPU time or total environment interactions.
2. **How much does shrinkage contribute?** The reported component ablation does not isolate it. Compare fixed trust, raw-gain weighting, and shrunk-gain weighting under identical replay budgets.
3. **Can replay be relaxed?** Exact resets are an acknowledged requirement. Test approximate-state replay with controlled reset errors and measure reward-estimation bias and final success.
4. **Are search skills reliably answer-free?** Prompt restrictions alone do not establish compliance. Audit generated skills for answer leakage and repeat evaluation with independently filtered skills.

## Confidence and missing information

**Confidence: medium.** The method and headline findings are clearly described, but important empirical details are unavailable: complete result tables, per-dataset scores, seed counts and uncertainty estimates, precise evaluation splits and averaging procedures, compute overhead, and the actual prompt contents shown only in figures. Code availability is claimed, but implementation contents and released checkpoints cannot be assessed from the supplied text.
