# Agent-Reflex: Teaching Language Agents to Act Reflectively

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-14.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.17953) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17953)


## One-sentence takeaway
Agent-Reflex trains Qwen3-8B on mined recovery skills and skill-guided distillation, reporting stronger tool-use and noisy-interaction performance than compared small-model baselines, while broader transfer remains untested (Tables 1–2; Appendix G).

## Problem and motivation
Tool-using agents must recover from unreliable feedback and respond appropriately to user corrections. The authors argue that clean synthetic environments provide insufficient experience with these situations, while existing reflection methods typically offer inference-time advice, individual trajectory repairs, or sparse outcome rewards rather than reusable environment-level recovery knowledge (§§1–2).

## Approach and contributions
1. **Reflection Skill Discovery (RSD):** Construct database-backed executable environments from MCP service specifications, inject failures grounded in observed tool errors, and contrast successful and failed trajectories to extract environment-indexed recovery rules. The authors report 1,170 skills across 557 environments (§3.1; Appendix D.2).
2. **Reflective supervised fine-tuning (ReSFT):** Generate verifier-approved continuations for local self-repair, whole-task recovery, and user correction. Qwen3-8B generates all training trajectories; stronger models assist construction, diagnosis, and user simulation. Privileged skill prompts are excluded from stored policy inputs (§3.2; Appendices D.2–D.3).
3. **On-Policy Reflective Policy Optimization (ORPO):** Combine clean/noisy outcome-based RL with token scoring under a privileged environment skill card. The contribution is the mined skill context and its integration: the authors explicitly reuse SEED’s distillation estimator unchanged. Deployment requires no skill library (§3.3; Appendices A.3, D.4).

The results support improved benchmark task completion and trajectory validity. Ablations support contributions from the tested components, but do not establish that every improvement specifically reflects better diagnosis. The authors themselves note that task success alone cannot identify whether recovery occurred for the right reason (Appendix E.1). Agent-Reflex also remains below the proprietary reference models on aggregate results (Tables 1–2).

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated:** The offline library can become stale as interfaces and environment dynamics change; updating it may sacrifice generality when new experience lacks diversity (Appendix F).
- **Author-stated:** Cross-capacity transfer, skill composition across environments, and temporal faults such as concurrency and partial writes remain future work (Appendix G).
- **Analyst inference:** Broad baseline comparisons are less controlled than the optimizer study. Most baseline scores are imported, some methods are reproduced, and NoisyAgent’s noise result uses evaluated-domain training (Appendices C, D.5).
- **Analyst inference:** Environment diversity versus privileged-context quality is not isolated; the authors explicitly acknowledge the need for controlled experiments explaining the advantage over SEED (Appendix F).
- **Analyst inference:** Measured scores average three runs, but main tables provide no uncertainty estimates. Diagnostic bootstrap intervals are described separately, limiting assessment of aggregate differences (Appendices D.5, E.1).
- **Analyst inference:** Selection for verifiable recovery limits evidence about irreversible or unrecoverable failures (§3.1.1).

## Open research questions
- **Can skills survive service changes?** The library is static. Next experiment: introduce interface and behavior changes, then compare frozen skills, periodic re-mining, and incremental updates (Appendix F).
- **Does environment diversity drive the gain over SEED?** This factor remains unresolved. Next experiment: hold data volume and optimization fixed while varying discovery-environment diversity and privileged-context source (Appendix F).
- **Do skills transfer across models and compose across environments?** Neither is established. Next experiment: train different-capacity policies with the same library and evaluate held-out multi-environment workflows (Appendix G).
- **Can reflection handle temporal and partially executed failures?** Current failure mining is textual. Next experiment: inject delayed responses, concurrent changes, and partial writes, measuring recovery success and unintended state changes (Appendix G).
