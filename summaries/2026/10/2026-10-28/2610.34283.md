# ChronoReplay: One Answer Interface Is Not Enough for Streaming Spatial Intelligence

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-28.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.34283) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34283)


## One-sentence takeaway
ChronoReplay improves one spatial VLM across categorical, metric, and temporal benchmarks by combining chronological supervision with explicit loss balancing, while controlled experiments show that chronology and answer serialization have distinct, limited effects (§5; Tables 9–12).

## Problem and motivation
Streaming spatial questions require a model to retain object identities, observer motion, and reference frames using only observations available before each query. Categorical answers, distances, and timestamps constrain different aspects of this history.

The authors call uneven transfer after single-family training **interface lock-in**: improving one readout can leave others unchanged or worse. They identify a second problem: a spatial target can become ambiguous when detached from the chronological history that defines it. The proposed gap is therefore in supervision design, rather than necessarily in memory architecture (§§1, 3).

## Approach and contributions
- **Chronological replay:** Preserve source episode order and attach each target to its legal observation and interaction prefix. Spatial state remains implicit in the existing VLM; no memory module or auxiliary state loss is added (§4.2).
- **Native targets and balanced learning:** Retain categorical options, numerical units and precision, and temporal answers. Normalize answer losses by length and explicitly balance preservation against trajectory supervision. The main mixture assigns equal total loss mass to categorical preservation, numerical preservation, and trajectory targets (§4.3; Table 7).
- **Controlled transfer analysis:** Compare single-family training, ordinary joint SFT, natural turn weighting, lossless answer reserialization, and cyclically shifted episodes. Separately test a fixed, query-text-based frame selector at inference (§5; Appendices D–E).

The implementation post-trains Qwen3-VL-8B-Instruct’s language model and visual merger while freezing its visual encoder (§5.1).

These results support the complete recipe and several matched interventions. They do not establish that answer format alone causes interface lock-in, that chronology explains the full improvement, or that the model learns a particular internal spatial map. Published external models use their own protocols and serve as reference points rather than matched superiority tests (Appendix B.3).

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated attribution limits:** Single-family variants differ in sources and task coverage. Cyclic shifting changes both origin and preceding context, so it does not isolate origin representation (§5.3; Appendix D.7).
- **Author-stated scope:** Evaluation uses indoor scenes and one base model. Other environments remain untested; safety-critical deployment is unaudited (§2; Ethics statement).
- **Limited replication:** Headline scores use one seed. Selected objective and chronology comparisons receive additional seeds; topology replication is explicitly not an independent-seed variance estimate (Appendices D.1, D.6).
- **Incomplete component isolation:** Objective comparisons do not separately isolate every normalization level. Population-level theory does not certify finite-model optimization (Appendices A, D.2).
- **Residual failures:** Global layout remains difficult, including a documented fixed-observation regression. Auxiliary relation discrimination also fails to consistently improve native transfer (Appendices F.5, H).
- **Analyst inference:** Lower frame count establishes reduced visual input, but does not establish lower end-to-end latency or total computation; those measurements are absent.

## Open research questions
1. **How much specialization comes from interface versus task coverage?** The single-family comparison confounds these factors. Next experiment: match source scenes, queries, and target quantities while varying only the supervised readout.
2. **Which aspect of chronology matters most?** Cyclic shifts jointly alter origin and accessible context. Next experiment: vary origin cues while preserving legal history, then separately remove selected historical observations.
3. **Does the recipe generalize across models and environments?** Evidence is restricted to one backbone and indoor benchmarks. Next experiment: repeat matched training on another VLM and an outdoor causal-video evaluation.
4. **Can layout failures improve without sacrificing other readouts?** Aggregate gains coexist with a layout regression and unsuccessful auxiliary relation transfer. Next experiment: test targeted layout supervision while tracking all native benchmark endpoints.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: high** in the reported comparisons and their stated attribution boundaries; broader generalization remains uncertain.

Material missing information includes cross-backbone and outdoor results, end-to-end runtime and memory measurements, uncertainty estimates for headline gains, and independent reproduction. The text reports released artifacts, but their availability was not independently checked; raw media and saved predictions are explicitly excluded from the bundled release.
