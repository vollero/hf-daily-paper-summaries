# How Much Does the Hint Matter? Scope Conditions for Hindsight Self-Distillation in Financial Reasoning

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-28.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.36082) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36082)


## One-sentence takeaway
On FinanceReasoning Hard, localized self-distillation improves GPT-OSS-20B’s mean accuracy, while generic feedback matches targeted critiques, suggesting—but not proving—that supervision placement matters more than hint content in this setting (§§5.1–5.3).

## Problem and motivation
Financial institutions facing data-residency and hardware constraints may need locally hosted models that struggle with multi-step quantitative reasoning. The authors investigate whether these models can improve without distilling from an external teacher or degrading general reasoning and compliance (§1).

The specific gap is how much privileged information self-distillation requires: prior approaches use full reference solutions or targeted feedback, but the necessity of detailed feedback remains unclear (§§1–2).

## Approach and contributions
- **Fin-SD recipe:** Mine incorrect student solutions, use the ground-truth answer to locate the first erroneous clause, and truncate immediately before it. A frozen student copy receives the prefix plus a critique; the student receives the prefix alone. Training minimizes reverse KL over a short student rollout, with failure mining and teacher refresh repeated each epoch. Arithmetic-only errors receive no gradient (§3).
- **Hint scope experiments:** Compare targeted critiques with fixed generic feedback, self-written hints with a larger same-family writer, and hints with full-solution OPSD. These experiments probe when detailed or stronger feedback adds value (§§5–6).
- **Financial training corpus:** Construct manually reviewed synthetic problems requiring financial conventions to derive formula inputs, followed by computation and a uniquely verifiable numerical endpoint (§4.1).
- **Retention evaluation:** Check general reasoning and financial compliance after training, although these evaluations use single runs (§§4.3, 5.4).

## Evidence and main results

FinanceReasoning results below use the same 238-problem Hard subset, an 8,192-token completion budget, and means over five seeds/runs unless otherwise stated (§4.3).

| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| Improvement over base | Fin-SD accuracy **72.19% vs 69.16%**; completion **92.19% vs 91.18%**; cumulative evaluation tokens **508.5k vs 525.8k**, a **3.3%** reduction. | §5.1 |
| Generic feedback matches targeted critique | Generic-feedback accuracy **72.52% vs 72.19%**; completion higher by **0.17 pp**; tokens lower by **4.7k**. Authors say all differences lie within seed spread. Error localization remains enabled. | §5.3 |
| Competitive with full-solution OPSD | Fin-SD accuracy **72.19% vs 70.42%**; completion **92.19% vs 89.75%**; tokens **508.5k vs 562.1k**, a **9.5%** reduction. | §5.2 |
| Larger hint writer adds no measured accuracy benefit under reverse KL | Self-written and GPT-OSS-120B-written hints both yield **72.19%** accuracy. Authors report no accuracy contrast between writers approaching significance. | §6.1 |
| Reverse KL is directionally better | With self-written hints, accuracy is **72.19% vs 70.93%** under forward KL; completion improves **0.76 pp**, with **20.6k** fewer evaluation tokens. | §6.2 |
| Short rollout provides a modest gain | A **100-token** rather than **8,192-token** training rollout yields accuracy **72.19% vs 71.09%** and completion **92.19% vs 91.77%**, with **5.0k** fewer evaluation tokens. | §6.3 |
| Retention evidence is limited | Single-run MMLU-Pro evaluation reportedly shows near-identical overall performance, but chemistry drops **5.47 pp**. Authors report no large regression across **14** FinTrust metrics; numerical table entries are absent. | §§4.3, 5.4 |

These results support competitive performance with less informative teacher context in this particular setting. They do **not** establish that hints are universally unnecessary or that localization alone causes the improvement: the generic-feedback control retains localization, prefix truncation, and the remaining training machinery. Likewise, “matches OPSD” should be read as the authors’ interpretation of observed results, not a demonstrated statistical equivalence. Evaluation-token savings do not establish total training-cost savings.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated scope:** Self-distillation is bounded by knowledge the student can already elicit; self-diagnosis may fail beyond a difficulty threshold (§7).
- **Author-stated overlap:** Formulas used to seed training appear in some evaluation solutions, making this in-domain transfer rather than evidence of entirely novel formula generalization (§7).
- **Author-stated data and tuning limits:** The corpus is small, only a mined subset contributes gradients, ambiguous problems are excluded, and hyperparameters/prompts were not extensively tuned (§§4.1, 7).
- **Analyst inference—mechanism remains unresolved:** No reported control removes or randomizes error localization, so the proposed explanation about supervision placement remains suggestive (§5.3).
- **Analyst inference—deployment claims exceed the evaluation:** Single-run retention checks cannot establish broad compliance assurance. The implemented pipeline also uses external models for data construction and answer judging, despite having no external teacher contribute gradients (§§4.1, 4.3).
- **Reproducibility gaps:** Retrieved Tables 1–5 contain captions without their numerical bodies; §9 provides no code or data details. Exact hyperparameters, uncertainty estimates, and several baseline results are therefore unavailable.

## Open research questions
1. **Does accurate localization cause the gain?** The generic-feedback experiment preserves localization. Compare correct, randomly shifted, and unlocalized supervision sites while keeping training budget and feedback fixed.
2. **When does detailed feedback become necessary?** Only one student and a larger same-family writer are studied. Test multiple student sizes and separate convention-selection failures from genuinely missing-knowledge failures.
3. **Does improvement survive formula-disjoint evaluation?** Reported formula overlap limits transfer claims. Rebuild the split with disjoint underlying formulas and repeat the baseline comparisons.
4. **How robust is behavioral retention?** Single-run checks leave uncertainty about regressions. Repeat MMLU-Pro and FinTrust across trained seeds, reporting uncertainty and investigating the chemistry decline.
