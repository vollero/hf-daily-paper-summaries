# What Outcome RL Buys: Finishing, Reliability, Rarely Reach

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-28.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.34894) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34894)


## One-sentence takeaway
Across twenty RL–base pairs on physics and math, outcome RL mainly improves finishing within a token budget or correctness after finishing, while expansion beyond the base’s sampled solutions remains small under the paper’s operational definition.

## Problem and motivation
A pass@1 gain mixes answering before truncation, answering correctly, and solving previously unsolved problems. The author argues that this obscures whether outcome RL changes behavior or adds knowledge, especially when training and evaluation budgets differ. Prior coverage studies did not separate finishing from correctness or address the same range of thinking bases (§§1–2).

## Approach and contributions
- **Budget-aware decomposition:** Exactly splits pass@1 change into finishing, reliability, and net reach. Reach measures RL success on problems the base finishes but fails in every sample, subtracting the reverse comparison; matched reliability restricts evaluation to problems both models finish (§3.1).
- **Released-model audit:** Compares twenty RL models with their thinking bases using shared prompts, checkers, sampling settings, and multiple token budgets, including training caps and the next larger budget (§3.2).
- **Controlled knowledge-supply probe:** Tests five ways of supplying physical setups to Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking, including hints and teacher derivations, with held-out problems and easier text-only twins. These channels claim no algorithmic novelty (§5; Appendix B).
- **Evaluation recommendation:** Report finishing alongside pass@1 and compare each recipe with its base at or above its training cap (§6).

## Evidence and main results

| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| Tight-budget changes mainly reflect finishing | Finishing accounts for **82%** of change at **4k and 8k** token budgets in the released-pair study. This is a decomposition share, not an accuracy gain. | Abstract; §§4.1, 6 |
| More tokens recover much of the base’s deficit | On problems selected with separate samples for RL finishing and score advantages: at **4k**, RL scores **0.68**, base **0.44**, and base at **64k** scores **0.80**; corresponding **8k** scores are **0.65, 0.38, 0.59**. | Appendix A |
| Reliability improves when finishing is already high | On physics at training caps, all **16** non-cap-bound recipes improve reliability; their bases finish **93–100%** of derivations. Matched reliability rises in all sixteen, with intervals excluding zero in **12**. | §4.2 |
| Net reach is small under the specified definition | Reported net reach is at most **0.021 pass@1**, or **2.1 percentage points**, across the four physics/math evaluation sets. Reach eligibility uses eight finished, incorrect samples from the losing model. | §§3.1, 4.3 |
| Most audited physics lone wins do not survive scrutiny | Of **144** RL lone wins examined through resampling or reading, **5** remain substantive physics wins. In the random human-check sample, **9 of 10** upheld RL steps also appear in base samples. | §4.3; Appendix A.1 |
| Plain RL’s held-out probe gain comes mainly from finishing | Qwen3-VL-8B-Thinking’s held-out mean score rises **0.16→0.26**; finishing rises **0.39→0.69**, while conditional correctness is reported flat at **0.38**. Training uses a **12k** cap. | Appendix B.1 |
| Supplied setups show no established advantage over plain RL | On **87** held-out hard problems, hint-arm difference is **+0.01 [−0.01, +0.03]**, teacher-derivation difference **−0.05 [−0.08, −0.03]**, and written-setup difference **−0.03 [−0.05, −0.01]**; intervals are 95%. | §5, Table 5 |

The evidence supports behavioral improvements within these models, tasks, budgets, and grading protocols. It does **not** establish that outcome RL cannot acquire knowledge: finite sampling cannot identify everything a base can solve, and “reach” is a deliberately restrictive operational measure.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated scope:** Thinking bases and physics/math dominate; recipes share bases and differ simultaneously in data, algorithm, and training duration, preventing clean explanations of recipe differences (§§4.2, 6; Appendix A).
- **Definition sensitivity:** Gains on problems with any unfinished base sample count as behavior. Including such problems as unsolved would raise ProRL v2’s net reach from **0.001 to 0.067** in the regenerated at-cap read (Appendix A.4).
- **Sampling and contamination:** Most comparisons use eight samples; the main physics set is base-filtered. Unfiltered checks help, but recipe-training overlap was not checked (§6).
- **Grading and trace interpretation:** Answer form explains some gains. Trace searches rely on language-model readers, and their positive step matches received no human verification (§6).
- **Controlled-probe limits:** One seed per treated arm, two controls, limited training, different training/held-out sources, and text-only presentation of problems often containing figures. Only hint and teacher channels ran at full scale; the questioner failed its gate before RL, and setup-reward training gamed its scorer (Appendices B.1, B.5–B.10).
- **Analyst inference:** Finding a step somewhere in base samples supports repertoire overlap, but does not establish that the base can reliably combine all necessary steps into a solution.

## Open research questions
- **Does genuine reach increase with scale or training duration?** Larger recipes show suggestive gains confounded by grading, while prolonged controlled training is untested. Next experiment: matched multi-seed runs varying model size and updates, with extensive base sampling and substantive-answer audits.
- **Can supplied knowledge transfer between problems sharing a physical principle?** Current twin results remain uncertain, and the source-matched recurring-content split was not tested. Next experiment: preregister principle-based training/held-out groups and measure transfer where both models finish.
- **How stable are the conclusions under alternative coverage definitions?** Unfinished base samples materially affect reach classification. Next experiment: report reach across sample counts, budgets, and eligibility definitions using the same audited outputs.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The supplied text supports the central pattern and describes substantial checks, but several full tables and figure values are absent despite their captions being present. Also unavailable are the complete released artifacts, all per-recipe intervals, independent human validation of step-search matches, and adequately replicated long-scale knowledge-transfer experiments.
