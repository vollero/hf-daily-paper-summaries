# LociArt: A Unified Paradigm for Free-Style Local Video Stylization

> [← Daily summary](../2026-10-28.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.36456) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36456)


## One-sentence takeaway
LociArt combines paired training data, spatial style codes, and region-aware evaluation to improve local video stylization on its benchmark, while multi-region generalization remains qualitatively demonstrated.

## Problem and motivation
Local video stylization must apply a requested style within moving regions, preserve everything else, and maintain temporal consistency. The authors identify three gaps: binary masks do not encode style identity; compressed mask representations can lose boundary precision; and global stylization data and metrics do not adequately supervise or evaluate local edits (§1).

## Approach and contributions
- **GRNStyle:** Extends GRN/GRNEdit binary-state refinement with polar style-aware masks. Two channels encode a fixed style direction and a third encodes occupancy, explicitly binding style to location (§§4.1–4.2).
- **Representation–geometry conditioning:** Combines encoded mask features, subtraction of the empty-mask encoding, and pooled binary spatial support. Gated evidence injection combines these conditions with source content (§§4.3–4.4).
- **Data and evaluation:** Constructs LociArt-100K through global stylization, object removal, separate source/stylized matting, compositing, and human verification. Introduces a benchmark and Style Prototype Similarity (SPS), using visual style prototypes alongside preservation and temporal metrics (§3; Appendix C).
- **Compositional inference:** Combines disjoint masks carrying learned styles in one generation trajectory after single-region training; the supplied evidence is qualitative (§4.4).

These results support improved regional style fidelity with competitive preservation on the authors’ benchmark. They do not establish superiority on every metric, arbitrary unseen styles, or quantitative multi-region reliability. SPS classification validation establishes category discrimination in the tested data, rather than direct agreement with human judgments of editing quality.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated scope:** Compositional inference assumes disjoint regions and learned styles (§4.4). The referenced limitations appendix is absent from the supplied text.
- **Analyst inference—comparison attribution:** Fine-tuning VACE on the same dataset helps assess task-specific supervision, but its different architecture and model size prevent isolating conditioning design alone (Table 1; §5.2).
- **Analyst inference—evaluation dependence:** SPS prototypes come from outputs accepted by the data pipeline, and regional metrics depend on output matting. Metric preferences and localization errors could affect rankings; leave-one-source-out validation does not resolve these issues (Appendix C.2).
- **Analyst inference—temporal coverage:** TC measures global adjacent-frame embedding similarity, so it cannot alone establish local boundary or flicker quality; the authors explicitly describe it as complementary (Appendix C.3.1).
- **Evidence gaps:** The user study has one rating per case and lacks supplied baseline means or uncertainty values. Numerical component ablations, quantitative mask-robustness results, and multi-region evaluation are unavailable (§§5.3–5.4).
- **Reproducibility:** The text identifies an internal fine-tuned stylizer and commercial judges, while detailed training-table values and confirmed artifact availability are missing (§3; Appendix D).

## Open research questions
- **How reliably does composition scale?** Multi-region evidence is qualitative (§4.4). Evaluate increasing numbers of disjoint regions, repeated styles, and neighboring style boundaries using per-region scores and human judgments.
- **Does SPS track human preferences beyond its prototype distribution?** Validation tests style classification rather than perceptual agreement (Appendix C.2.2). Compare SPS rankings with repeated human ratings on outputs from independently sourced stylizers.
- **Which geometry component supplies the preservation gain?** Reported RG results bundle anchoring and spatial support, and numerical ablations are unavailable (§5.4). Run a factorial ablation separating both components, including small and thin masks.
- **How far can replay-free expansion continue?** Evidence covers one expansion from 10 to 15 styles (§5.5). Test repeated additions and alternative code-slot spacing while tracking retention and new-style acquisition.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The supplied text supports the main design and benchmark results, but omits numerical Tables 2–5, complete style lists, user-study uncertainty, efficiency results, mask-robustness results, the limitations appendix, and confirmed code/data/model releases. Some figure references are inconsistent, particularly in the user-study section.
