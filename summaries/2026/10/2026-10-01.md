# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-10-01

## Executive summary

- Selective supervision is a recurring theme: papers change which token positions, verified trajectories, advice decisions, or failure states receive distillation signals. **2609.29142** reports a 0.95-percentage-point mean accuracy gain over Direct-OPD across eight mathematical teacher–student settings; training-seed robustness remains untested.
- Agent improvement spans both model training and external support. **2609.38143** reports an 8.95-point macro-average gain from learned harness-design guidance across two benchmarks and three Target models, with Builder computation excluded from Target budgets.
- Scientific and software benchmarks emphasize delivered behavior and artifacts. **2609.34274**, **2609.39903**, and **2609.39564** show why plausible workflows or runnable applications do not establish task correctness.
- Persistent memory requires evidence management. **2609.34276** carries maps and structured search records between navigation sessions; **2609.39166** explicitly forecasts moving-object locations and softens negative observations according to visibility.
- Evaluation can change under incidental inputs. System dates affect scores and rankings (**2609.36931**); ignored images change roughly one-fifth of idiom labels without much aggregate agreement change (**2609.37863**); candidate presentation and ordinal granularity expose decision biases (**2609.38827**).
- Restricted adaptation can alter safety even with most weights frozen. Harmful-gradient removal reduces generated emergent misalignment in a specified single-layer LoRA setting (**2609.34970**), while learned latent communication links introduce—and can partly repair—harmful compliance (**2609.39788**).
- Efficiency claims need complete accounting. Low-bit KV quality, persistent cache reuse, recurrent computation, retrieval, and faster speech generation address different costs; several papers leave deployment latency or total expenditure unmeasured.
- Mathematical and scientific findings remain bounded. No evaluated model surpasses the 30 published-frontier references in **2609.24555**; LANTERN’s screened relations lack independent expert proof checking (**2609.32264**); brain-to-text results concern constrained perceived speech, not demonstrated clinical communication (**2609.40359**).
- All **86 current papers** have supplied analyses, but every analysis is compacted. The connection output’s assertion that only 64 analyses are present is inconsistent with the supplied blocks.

## Main results worth noting

- **Selective mathematical distillation — 2609.29142:** S²D-OPD improves mean held-out mathematical reasoning accuracy by **0.95 percentage points over Direct-OPD across eight teacher–student settings**. The default retains 10% of high-JSD states; reward and anchor-KL masking change together, and training-seed robustness is unavailable.
- **Rubric reward allocation — 2609.35646:** For **Qwen3.5-4B**, RRT gains **1.7 percentage points in macro criterion score over Vanilla GRPO**. Its half-criterion-budget configuration is **within 0.1 points of full judging**. These automated criterion results do not establish human preference validity or complete cost savings.
- **Patch selection — 2609.38807:** With **Qwen3-235B on fixed candidates for 809 GitHubAD CVEs**, Recall@1 increases from **32.63% to 59.95%** in the reported comparison. This measures shortlist selection, not prospective end-to-end recovery of missing patch links.
- **Harness guidance — 2609.38143:** Learned support-design principles yield an **8.95-percentage-point macro-average score gain over harness construction without learned guidance**, across **two benchmarks and three Target models**. Builder computation is outside the Target budgets.
- **One-step image alignment — 2609.30840:** **SANA Sprint GenEval increases from 0.73 to 0.80** under RWTD. The reported reward–diversity trade-off remains central; the result is not a universal image-quality improvement.
- **Restricted safety repair — 2609.34970:** Harmful-subspace removal reduces **generated emergent misalignment by 55.4%–80.0% relative to the full update** for **single-layer LoRA on Qwen2.5-14B**. Broader probability interventions and selected capability checks do not establish deployment safety.
- **Evaluation sensitivity — 2609.36931:** Changing only the system date produces **worst-to-best GSM8K score differences reaching 14.42 percentage points** in the controlled evaluation. **2609.37863**, in a separate setting, reports roughly **one-fifth of English idiom labels changing across 13 VLMs** when images are added despite ignore-image instructions; aggregate human agreement stays nearly unchanged.
- **Long-media retrieval — 2609.39938:** LEAP gains **4.5–16.8 percentage points in long audio-video QA over same-stack Qwen3-Omni official-style baselines**. Context per pass is duration-independent, while total block scanning remains duration-dependent.
- **Interactive auditing — 2609.40325:** The best evaluated VLM auditor achieves **42.3% success versus 83.4% reported human success** on WorldAuditBench. Unequal exploration budgets qualify causal comparisons between auditing approaches.
- **Brain-to-text controls — 2609.40359:** Timing-only and synthetic-signal controls show that overlapping aligned windows can support shortcut decoding. The independent recipe reports **36.6% WER on constrained perceived-speech sentence reconstruction**, using repeated observations and an LLM; the retained evidence does not expose a complete numerical comparator.

Qualitatively, **2609.24555** supplies a useful boundary on mathematical construction performance: none of its 30 published-frontier references is surpassed. **2609.36484** similarly qualifies teacher-superiority claims because most margins fall below training-seed variability. These limits matter as much as the reported gains.

## Open research directions

1. **Separate supervision selection from budget and regularization effects.** Motivated by **2609.29142, 2609.33711, 2609.37915, 2609.38792**. Run replicated comparisons of distribution-based and outcome-based selectors at matched teacher calls, rollout expenditure, retained positions, and checkpoint rules. For 2609.29142, independently mask reward and anchor KL.

2. **Validate correction signals by their executed consequences.** Motivated by **2609.38142, 2609.40111, 2609.40285**. From restored states, randomize advice or repair versus an original-action retry and repeat both continuations. Measure recovery success and harmful corrections, then test whether predictive selection scores or diagnosis labels anticipate those effects.

3. **Measure memory revision under changing environments.** Motivated by **2609.34276, 2609.39166**. Compare persistent records with and without timestamped belief propagation in matched static, routine-motion, and random-motion worlds. Track stale exclusions, reopened locations, travel distance, and complete-task success.

4. **Test visual intermediates for causal grounding.** Motivated by **2609.34563, 2609.38177, 2609.38426, 2609.38716, 2609.38717**. Use answer-changing image interventions and human-validated geometry; intervene on intermediate states and regenerate subsequent computation. Separate representation quality from answer accuracy, recurrence, and sampling budget.

5. **Audit graders against independent deliverable checks.** Motivated by **2609.34274, 2609.39903, 2609.39564, 2609.40325**. Blind experts to agent identity, introduce plausible but defective artifacts, and compare source inspection, execution tests, visual judges, and human verdicts. Report false acceptance and false rejection by failure type.

6. **Evaluate stability and useful-context sensitivity together.** Motivated by **2609.36931, 2609.37863, 2609.38827, 2609.39578**. Pair irrelevant perturbations with genuinely useful or misleading guidance while holding tasks fixed. Repeat dates, image conditions, and candidate permutations; distinguish aggregate accuracy from item-level decision stability and selective reliance.

7. **Test restricted repair across safety and utility distributions.** Motivated by **2609.34970, 2609.39788**. Repeat adaptation and repair across seeds, domains, and communication topologies. Include diverse benign tasks, benign requests resembling harmful ones, and independent harm judgments to measure residual compliance and over-refusal.

8. **Report complete cost–quality frontiers.** Motivated by **2609.34162, 2609.38121, 2609.38143, 2609.38658, 2609.39358, 2609.39938, 2609.40316**. Include initialization, calibration, construction, scanning, storage, network, and serving costs on disclosed hardware. Compare equal-information and equal-quality operating points rather than token counts or stored weights alone.

9. **Establish external validity for generated knowledge and data.** Motivated by **2609.32264, 2609.37539, 2609.39827, 2609.40295**. Independently check mathematical derivations, audit task verifiers with varied correct/incorrect solutions, and validate text provenance. For pretraining, separately test synthetic warm-ups and wild-text additions across larger models and human-target evaluations.

10. **Extend demonstrations beyond favorable protocols.** Motivated by **2609.38537, 2609.38814, 2609.39344, 2609.39841, 2609.40359**. Evaluate continuously advancing control with real delays, preregistered dynamical parameter grids, real multi-day speaker identity, real displaced-view radar ground truth, and neural decoding with fewer repetitions or unknown onsets. Preserve each task’s native success and resource measures.
