# Enhancing Stock Market Information Retrieval with Retrieval-Augmented Generation

> [← Daily summary](../2026-11-04.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.03747) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.03747)


## One-sentence takeaway
A Vietnamese stock-news RAG pipeline reports high reference-text overlap and 1.16-second average latency, but its small evaluation without baselines does not establish improved factual accuracy or retrieval quality.

## Problem and motivation
The authors address answering stock-market questions using current financial news. They identify hallucinations, outdated model knowledge, and limited domain expertise as barriers to reliable financial responses, and propose retrieving external news instead of updating model parameters (§1). The application focuses on VnExpress stock-market coverage.

## Approach and contributions
- **Domain-specific pipeline:** Preprocess Vietnamese news, encode documents and queries with LaBSE, retrieve candidates through FAISS using L2 distance, rerank with BM25, and condition Gemini-1.5-Flash on retrieved text (§§3–4).
- **Small application evaluation:** Assess reference-answer overlap and response latency using stock-market queries (§5).

The supported contribution is an application and evaluation of existing components. The supplied text does not establish a new retrieval algorithm, model architecture, or publicly released benchmark.

These results support strong lexical overlap with the chosen references and a reported average response time in the authors’ setting. They do not establish factual correctness, reduced hallucination, retrieval relevance, investment usefulness, or improvement over another system. Although Tables 1–3 are referenced, their bodies are absent from the supplied text.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated future needs:** More complex financial queries, additional knowledge sources, and computational efficiency remain targets for improvement (§6). The authors provide no dedicated limitations discussion.
- **Analyst inference—unisolated component effects:** No non-RAG, dense-only, or BM25-only baseline or ablation is reported (§5), so gains from retrieval and reranking cannot be attributed.
- **Analyst inference—limited validity:** One news source and a small corpus provide limited evidence for broader financial or changing-market settings (§§3.1, 5.2).
- **Analyst inference—measurement gap:** ROUGE measures reference overlap; no human factuality assessment, hallucination measure, or retrieval-specific evaluation is reported (§5).
- **Reporting ambiguities:** Section 3.2 names Underthesea tokenization, while §4.1 names Vietnamese-adapted Jieba without explaining their relationship. Section 5.1 defines recall-style ROUGE equations, whereas §5.2 explicitly labels ROUGE-1 as F-measure.
- **Reproducibility gaps:** Query/reference construction, document dates, retrieval settings, exact prompts, generation settings, and latency infrastructure are unspecified.

## Open research questions
1. **Does retrieval improve answer factuality?** No controlled baseline establishes this. Compare the same generator with and without retrieval using blinded factuality judgments and source-supported answer checks.
2. **Does BM25 reranking add value?** Its contribution is not isolated. Compare FAISS-only, BM25-only, and the proposed cascade using retrieval relevance and downstream answer quality.
3. **Does performance persist on fresh, complex queries?** The small evaluation does not test temporal generalization. Use a dated, multi-source corpus and held-out later queries, measuring factual accuracy and response latency.
