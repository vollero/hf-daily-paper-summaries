# Words Caught in the Heart: Simulating Person-Specific Expression for Self-Opaque Clients in Psychotherapy

> [← Daily summary](../2026-11-04.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.00617) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.00617)


## One-sentence takeaway
Pandora’s explicit expression planner improves reference-matched client language and reduces perceived AI-like expression in selected psychotherapy simulations, without establishing clinical authenticity or training effectiveness (§4.3; Limitations).

## Problem and motivation
Clients may feel distress before they can explain it coherently, and disclosure depends on trust, safety, and emotion. The authors argue that simulators driven by clinician-organized formulations can produce unrealistically articulate self-analysis; style labels alone do not address the gap between available case knowledge and what clients currently understand or express (§1).

## Approach and contributions
- **Situated psychological planning:** Pandora uses rule-based intervention assessment and persistent state updates, then constructs subjective appraisal, attribution, content, stance, and response strategy (§§3.1–3.3).
- **Separate individual expression planning:** An offline profile captures the client’s habitual markers, hedging, and utterance statistics. An online planner combines this profile with the current interaction to guide certainty, emotional explicitness, wording, and length before LLM realization (§§3.4–3.5).
- **Combined evaluation:** The study evaluates disclosure timing and structure, paired stylometric similarity, and human judgments of AI-like expression, including an ablation removing the expression module (§4). All LLM-based simulation uses GPT-5.4 mini (Appendix A.6).

## Evidence and main results
| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| Highest automatic expression similarity | On 115 cases, content-reduced/raw similarity is **0.566/0.385**, versus **0.512/0.352** for strongest baseline Consistent MI and **0.458/0.311** without expression planning. Scores are unweighted case means. | Table 2 |
| Highest overall disclosure alignment | Pandora’s GDS/LDDA/DS is **0.871/0.852/0.875**, versus **0.664/0.690/0.417** for Consistent MI and **0.828/0.811/0.795** for the ablation. | Table 2 |
| Bundling gains are mixed | Interactive Agents has lower bundle-frequency error: **0.099**, versus Pandora’s **0.102**. Pandora has the lowest bundle-size error, **0.127**, versus Consistent MI’s **0.128**. | Table 2 |
| Much lower perceived AI-like expression | Four evaluators judged 45 matched disclosure items, producing 180 ratings per system. Pandora receives **13/180 AI-like votes (7.2%)**, versus **43.3–67.2%** for baselines and **35.6%** for the ablation. | §4.3; Table 3 discussion |
| Human ablation contrast has reported uncertainty | Removing expression planning increases AIR by **28.3 percentage points**, with an exploratory case-bootstrap **95% CI of 20.6–36.1 points**. | §4.3 |
| Evaluator agreement is limited | Across 360 candidates, mean pairwise agreement is **62.9%**; positive agreement on AI-like judgments is **53.7%**. | §4.2.2 |

These results support the complete expression module’s contribution within this evaluation. They do not isolate its individual conditioning signals, demonstrate psychological authenticity, or establish clinical equivalence. AIR measures perceived expression; its negative label also includes insufficient evidence to judge an utterance AI-like (Appendix G).

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Authors’ stated scope:** The collection contains 97 Chinese and 18 English cases, including reconstructed or generated dialogue. Generalization to independently collected client speech, other languages, and cultures remains untested (§4.1; Limitations).
- **Measurement limits:** Chinese-oriented masking gives coarser evidence for English. Similarity is measured against each case’s reference, without cross-client retrieval tests (§4.2.2).
- **Human-study limits:** All four evaluators are co-authors; judgments concern isolated utterances, and unlogged source-reveal usage prevents confirming complete blinding (Appendix E.2; Limitations).
- **Mechanism uncertainty:** State values are modeling choices, and the ablation removes expression planning as a whole rather than separating profile, length, and interaction conditioning (Limitations).
- **Analyst inference:** Disclosure scores establish conformity to source trajectories rather than independently validated therapeutic appropriateness: prerequisites can be inferred from reference ordering, and all methods share coverage controls and claim verification (Appendices A.5, D.2).
- **Analyst inference:** Because language profiles and evaluation references originate from the same client dialogues, performance on unseen client sessions remains uncertain; no held-out-session profile evaluation is described (Appendix A.1; §4.2.2).

## Open research questions
1. **Which expression signals drive the gains?** The bundled ablation cannot separate individual profiles from interaction conditioning. Next experiment: factorial ablations of profile, length control, and state-conditioned realization.
2. **Does individual voice transfer across sessions?** The reported evaluation does not establish held-out-session performance. Next experiment: construct profiles from one session and evaluate against another, including cross-client discrimination.
3. **Do gains persist in full conversations and improve training?** Human judgments use isolated utterances, and downstream benefits remain untested. Next experiment: independently blinded full-dialogue assessment followed by a controlled therapist-training study.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The supplied text supports the main descriptive comparisons and clearly states important validity limits.

Material gaps include the repository URL, detailed baseline prompts and adaptation settings, complete numerical contents of several caption-only tables, uncertainty for automatic metric comparisons, and a clear held-out protocol for style-profile construction. Data-bearing releases are partly prospective; independent clinical validation and downstream training outcomes are unavailable.
