# Beyond the Happy Path: Generalizing Robot World Models from Simulated Failures

> [← Daily summary](../2026-11-26.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.33925) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.33925)


## One-sentence takeaway

On Reforge-Eval, post-training Cosmos3 Nano with simulated grasp perturbations and geometry-based supervision reduced mean object trajectory error by 28.2% versus the pretrained checkpoint, with a 35.02% reduction on objects unseen during post-training (§4.2, Appendix B.3).

## Problem and motivation

Action-conditioned robot world models need to predict how different actions change an interaction. The authors argue that training data dominated by successful demonstrations leave models poorly prepared for misaligned or mistimed grasps that push, tilt, or slip against objects. Existing failure-aware methods often retain a global reconstruction objective that underweights small contact regions or use outcome labels that say whether a grasp failed without describing how the object moved (§1).

## Approach and contributions

- **Data and diagnosis.** The authors compare pretrained models with a static predictor and build Reforge by perturbing successful grasps in translation, rotation, and closure timing. It contains 62,125 simulated trajectories over 96 object assets with synchronized physical annotations, plus 300 real failure trajectories over 10 objects for evaluation (§§1, 3.2, 4.1).
- **Training objectives.** WorldReforge adds two losses to flow matching. Interaction Alignment normalizes latent reconstruction error over projected object regions and their immediate surroundings. Surface Correspondence uses simulator-projected tracks to teach backbone features to locate the same visible object surface points across wrist-view frames. Both losses use the existing backbone and add no inference-time parameters (§3.3, Fig. 3, Appendix B.1).
- **Evaluation and data composition.** The authors introduce a paired-action protocol that holds the initial observation fixed and scores differences between predicted futures. They also vary the training mix to test how failed contact and successful holding contribute to prediction (§§5.1–5.3).

The matched-budget comparison supports an incremental gain from physical supervision beyond training on Reforge alone. The paired-action results support better short-horizon prediction of action-dependent changes in RGB. Real-world PSNR and SSIM establish improved reconstruction on the evaluated recordings. Quantitative real-world motion accuracy and downstream policy or control performance remain unmeasured.

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Author-stated scope.** The authors leave longer-horizon manipulation and closed-loop control to future work (§6). The experiments use recorded actions and evaluate 16 future frames (Appendix B.1).
- **Analyst inference.** Simulation motion scores depend on CoTracker3 extracting points from generated frames, so tracker errors could influence measured ADE and FDE. The paired-action metric measures full-frame RGB differences and may reflect gripper, camera, and object changes together (§5.3, Appendices B.4 and D).
- **Analyst inference.** Held-out-object gains cover nine simulated assets. Reforge-Real uses a single Franka Panda and parallel-jaw gripper setup, and its reported quantitative gains concern reconstruction. Transfer across robot embodiments and contact conditions remains uncertain (§4.2, Appendices A.5 and B.3).
- **Analyst inference.** The supplied implementation specifies one training seed, leaving run-to-run variability unclear, especially for the smaller gains over matched Reforge training (Appendix B.1).

## Open research questions

1. **Do reconstruction gains correspond to more accurate physical motion on real robots?** The real-world quantitative results focus on image similarity. Evaluate the same checkpoints against independently measured object and gripper trajectories on held-out real failures.
2. **Do the short-horizon gains improve multi-step recovery?** The paper evaluates 16-frame predictions conditioned on recorded actions. Compare Pretrained, Reforge, and WorldReforge inside the same closed-loop controller on longer tasks with induced grasp misses and slips, measuring recovery and task completion.
3. **How sensitive are the gains to the failure-data mix and training budget?** The composition studies use smaller training recipes than the main experiments. Repeat a matched-budget sweep over failure proportions and contact types across multiple seeds, measuring accuracy on both failed contact and stable holding.

## Confidence and missing information
