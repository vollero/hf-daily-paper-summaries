# GEPA-Curator: Curated Learning for Self Improving Agents

> [← Daily summary](../2026-11-02.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2610.37651) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37651)


## One-sentence takeaway
GEPA-Curator improves reflective agent optimization by selecting informative tasks and supplying reliable prior successes, with reported gains across several agent benchmarks but incomplete numerical tables in the supplied text.

## Problem and motivation
Failed agent rollouts can reflect prompt weaknesses, broken environments, missing tools, ambiguous tasks, or unreliable judges. Reflecting indiscriminately on these failures can produce harmful prompt changes. The authors argue that validation after mutation cannot fully address this problem because validation is itself noisy and does not improve the evidence used to generate mutations (§1).

## Approach and contributions
- **Task curation:** An LLM Curator examines per-task execution histories and temporarily skips tasks judged redundant, infeasible, or unreliable. Skipped tasks are reconsidered in later epochs (§3.1).
- **Prior success retrieval:** For a current failure, the Curator selects a reliable successful trajectory from the same task’s history, or abstains. This gives the Reflector a concrete comparison; selection shares the task-curation LLM call (§3.2).
- **Optimizer integration and extensions:** The outer harness supplies curated minibatches and metadata. Experiments use a greedy GEPA variant; further contributions extend curation to harness-code optimization and introduce compact histories, skip intervals, and retained success references to reduce costs (§§4.1, 5.3–5.4).

The central contribution is selecting and contextualizing optimization evidence, rather than a new actor model or benchmark.

These results support improved mean performance under the reported settings, with complementary evidence from component ablations. They do not establish universal gains, statistical significance, or accurate causal diagnosis of every failure. Most table bodies and standard deviations are absent, so many comparisons can only be assessed through the authors’ prose.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-acknowledged uncertainty:** Impossible/flaky diagnoses can be speculative or later overturned; historical successes can also be unreliable. Reconsideration and abstention mitigate these risks without proving their elimination (Appendix C).
- **Analyst inference—limited component evidence:** The reported component ablation is confined to GitLab validation tasks, so complementarity across other benchmarks remains untested in the supplied evidence (§4.3).
- **Analyst inference—scope restrictions:** Main agent experiments use one actor model. τ-bench removes user interaction, limiting conclusions about interactive agent-user behavior (§4.1).
- **Analyst inference—selected motivating cases:** Task categories were constructed using preliminary optimization outcomes and manual inspection; these experiments illustrate a mechanism rather than estimate its prevalence broadly (Appendix A.1).
- **Author-identified confound:** Different serving hardware may advantage the Opus experiments on time-sensitive WebArena tasks (Appendix B.1).
- **Analyst inference—extension uncertainty:** Meta-Harness results use only one optimization experiment per method and setting. Repeated test samples do not measure optimization-run variability (§5.3).

## Open research questions
- **How accurate are curation decisions?** Diagnosis analyses include uncertain and reversed judgments but no comprehensive accuracy measurement. Next experiment: independently adjudicate sampled skips and compare against controlled reruns.
- **Does selective success retrieval outperform simpler anchors?** The ablation tests retrieval’s presence, not semantic selection against simpler alternatives. Next experiment: compare selected, random, latest, and all available successful trajectories under matched budgets.
- **How broadly do gains transfer?** Actor diversity and interactive evaluation are limited. Next experiment: repeat across additional actors and restore τ-bench user interaction.
- **Are harness-code gains stable?** Single optimization runs leave search variance unresolved. Next experiment: repeat paired Meta-Harness searches across seeds with equal total budgets.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The method and reported trends are clear, but most numerical table bodies, standard deviations, actual Curator/seed/judge prompts, detailed cost breakdowns, and license entries are missing from the supplied retrieval. Code and artifact release availability is not established. The headline maximum gains cannot be assigned to a specific benchmark/model setting from the available table contents.
