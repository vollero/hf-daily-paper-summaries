# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-11-23

## Executive summary

- Recognizing the same entity across observations remains difficult when several candidates look alike. ReSpaceBench tests this with rooms, PURE separates product design from variant identity, and Doppeldwarf-306 shows how photographer overlap affects statue retrieval (`2611.21485`, `2611.22343`, `2611.27490`).
- Controlled video studies tested different kinds of fidelity. GameWM preserves engine-defined events while varying their animation, a motion-aware editing reward checks whether source motion survives an edit, AVM retrieves prior views on camera returns, and NoPeWorld filters commands that would cross obstacles (`2611.19247`, `2611.22495`, `2611.23417`, `2611.27963`).
- AeroRSI improved navigation success after training on replay-verified repairs in simulation. AgentRoboCritic found that recovery varied with the kind of manipulation error, with alignment and failed contact effects harder to recover from than grasp failures in the analyzed rollouts (`2611.24077`, `2611.26613`).
- Tool execution depends on capturing the user’s intended targets and latest arguments. Intent Contracts made proposed scope inspectable but left ambiguous targets unresolved. Duopra improved task completion with the same backend in a prerecorded speech evaluation (`2611.24025`, `2611.25773`).
- Written guidance can change what agents deliver. Transferred modeling plans shifted held-out predictor scores and reduced one local agent’s delivery rate, while repeated feedback-free skill rewrites preferentially removed details that models rarely supplied on their own (`2611.25252`, `2611.25534`).
- QueryLM and MemOnce evaluate retrieval from different stores. QueryLM’s greater responsiveness to retrieved evidence extended to false passages, while MemOnce reported gains on memory evaluations and lower prefill time for a million-token history (`2611.26809`, `2611.27973`).
- StereoVLM’s right-view controls test whether predictions use valid binocular evidence. RadioVISTA shows why a coverage-ranking score alone gives an incomplete picture of propagation reasoning (`2611.27915`, `2611.28123`).

## Main results worth noting

These are author-reported results within the stated evaluation settings.

- **Recorded vulnerability intervals, `2611.22479`.** In one Vulnerability-Lookup snapshot, recorded patch evidence followed recorded exploitation evidence by a median of **7.6 days** among **59 vulnerabilities** with that order after excluding catalog batch dates and unordered pairs. The authors attribute most of the observed gap to catalog listings and downstream fixes.
- **Motion-preserving edits, `2611.22495`.** The motion-aware reward ranked the original edit first against progressively frame-held versions on every evaluated VE-Bench motion ladder and achieved **Spearman ρ = 0.57** against human quality ratings in the reported evaluation.
- **Simulated navigation repair, `2611.24077`.** TravelUAV test success increased from **46.4% to 67.5%** after three repair-training rounds relative to the starting policy. The collision rate roughly halved in the same simulation setting.
- **Streaming speech execution, `2611.25773`.** Prerecorded FDB v3 **Pass@1 increased from 4.7% to 44.7%** from RV-Audio plus Harness to Duopra with the same Qwen3-8B backend.
- **Manipulation recovery, `2611.26613`.** In GPT-6 Astra rollouts across 12 manipulation benchmarks, **57.7%** of analyzed failed actions recovered by episode end. Recovery for alignment and failed contact effects was lower than for grasp failures within those rollouts.
- **Retrieval under photographer separation, `2611.27490`.** On answerable photographer-disjoint Doppeldwarf-306 queries, learned geometric reranking increased top-1 accuracy from **81.8% for frozen DINOv2 retrieval to 85.5%**. The separate leave-one-out DINOv2 result was **93.1%**.
- **Obstacle-aware video generation, `2611.27963`.** Relative to the unfiltered generators, NoPeWorld reduced the mean geometric violation rate from **74% to 16%** across six generators on geometry-evaluated NoPeBench rollouts.
- **Personalized long-form writing, `2611.27211`.** SP-Refine exceeded the same-sample retrieval baseline in **25 of 27 ROUGE and METEOR comparisons** on the English LongLaMP user split. Those comparisons measure overlap with references. A separate author evaluation would establish perceived style fidelity.

## Open research directions

1. **Test recall when a scene has changed (`2611.21485`, `2611.23417`).** Replay matched camera-return routes with either no change or one annotated object change. Toggle retrieval and score layout fidelity and updated-object accuracy separately.
2. **Measure whether diagnosed action failures are repairable (`2611.24077`, `2611.26613`).** Compare critic-guided candidate repairs with ordinary policy retries from the same saved simulator states and at equal rollout budgets. Score repeatable repair and eventual task success by failure type.
3. **Track user corrections through execution (`2611.24025`, `2611.25773`).** Cross unambiguous and ambiguous targets with early and late argument corrections in a sandboxed simulator. Compare logged intent, emitted arguments, and executed changes against independently labeled user intent.
4. **Test when procedural detail helps a different implementer (`2611.25252`, `2611.25534`).** Randomize held-out folds across unmarked plans, SkillDiff-marked plans, and a no-plan control. Repeat runs at the same time budget and score executable delivery separately from predictor quality.
5. **Separate evidence retrieval from evidence trust (`2611.26809`, `2611.27973`).** Hold the question and evidence position fixed while substituting correct, conflicting, or irrelevant passages. Report whether the relevant chunk was retrieved and whether the answer followed each evidence condition.
6. **Validate motion rewards on natural editor failures (`2611.22495`).** Blind raters to the source of unmodified outputs from held-out editors. Compare reward rankings with human motion-fidelity rankings within each source video and edit instruction.
7. **Test sensitivity where spatial shortcuts fail (`2611.27915`, `2611.28123`).** Expand paired cases where corrupted stereo evidence or a propagation intervention changes the correct answer. Report accuracy on changed-answer and unchanged-answer cases separately, with uncertainty for each.
8. **Separate essential transcripts from other caption detail (`2611.15449`, `2611.27229`).** Hold the restorer and transcript list fixed. Compare full captions, requirement-preserving pruning, and length-matched random deletions using ReasonText’s crop accuracy at each difficulty level.

## Coverage and uncertainty

- **Current coverage.** All 26 supplied current papers were analyzed for this digest. All 26 analysis blocks were marked `COMPACTED`, so the omitted spans were unavailable. Detailed figures found only in editorial aids were excluded where the retained analyses could not verify them.
- **Collection failures.** Hugging Face Markdown retrieval returned HTML for `2611.21485`, `2611.22343`, `2611.25773`, and `2611.27398`. Each paper still had a supplied analysis and is covered above.
- **Downstream stages.** Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical connections were all available. Historical candidate evidence consisted of compacted cached analyses. Nine cross-day edges are retained, with claims restricted to visible evidence at both endpoints. Historical observations establish neither publication order nor influence.
- **Lower-confidence conclusions.** Proposed transfers and experiments are editorial inferences. The available evidence leaves particular uncertainty around dynamic-scene revisit fidelity, physical transfer of AeroRSI, live Duopra reliability, end-to-end runtime and artifact availability, and estimates from narrow subsets such as RadioVISTA’s changed-winner cases. Missing table bodies and repeated runs limit several other comparisons.
