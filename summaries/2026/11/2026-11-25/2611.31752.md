# Separating Measurement Failures from Defence Failures in Split Language-Model Privacy

> [← Daily summary](../2026-11-25.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.31752) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31752)


## One-sentence takeaway

A retrospective audit of split language-model privacy shows that omitted channels and misaligned targets can invalidate privacy assurances while calibrated probes still establish leakage in the tested defences.

## Problem and motivation

Split learning keeps raw training text on the client, but activations and returned gradients can expose information about it. The authors identify a gap in reconciling privacy claims across campaigns when observers see different tensors, probes target different token positions, or calibration covers only some endpoints. These differences determine whether a result supports a breach, a bounded non-detection, or no verdict on the claim as stated. (§§1–2, 6)

## Approach and contributions

- **Claim-level admissibility framework.** Bind each verdict to the observer’s access, key custody, target index, baseline, calibration controls, budget, and independent resampling unit. The framework assigns five outcomes: breach, non-detection, undetermined, invalid, and out-of-scope. (§2)
- **Cross-campaign reconciliation.** Recompute archived seed contrasts and gate decisions under each campaign’s original rules. Distinguish leakage detected by individual qualified probes from composite eligibility, and preserve source attribution for upstream findings. (§§3–4, Appendices A–B)
- **Scope analysis.** Formalize how recovery evidence transfers to an observer with broader access under a matched budget, apply that result to host key custody, and use a constant-critic counterexample to show why a near-zero variational lower bound cannot certify low leakage. (§5)

## Evidence and main results

| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| Forward-only evaluation missed a backward-channel breach | Source-reported logs show exact real-versus-decoy partition recovery in all 4,096 inspected frames per run across nine seeds. Across six utility-compliant cells, forward-only probes stayed within the frozen +1.0 percentage-point gate and joint probes exceeded it. Partition recovery is a separate endpoint from token recovery. | §3.1, Appendix A |
| Shifted labels invalidated the current-token privacy claim | The probe actually scored next-token prediction. In separate utility comparisons, standalone clients trained with zero remote return outperformed the split-trained model in all six clean corpus-and-seed pairs. One reviewed checkpoint passed the held-out loss rule with a roughly 0.034-nat increase, while teacher-forced answer-token top-1 match fell from 34.79% to 18.15%. | §3.2, Appendix A |
| Every exploratory configuration failed a frozen gate | Only 4 of 24 required probes qualified. They established breaches in 13 of 19 configurations, and all six configurations with undetermined privacy failed utility or remote-learning gain independently. The exploration used four training seeds per cell. | §§3.3, 4.1, Tables 2 and 4 |
| The redesign pilot detected leakage in every cell despite incomplete composite coverage | Across seven cells with three paired seeds per cell, next-byte five-class accuracy advantages were 0.097–0.112 against a 0.02 ceiling, with all 21 next-byte seed bindings qualified. Qualified worst-seed punctuation-fraction correlation advantages were 0.79–0.89 against the same ceiling. The all-bindings rule yielded four composite privacy failures and three undetermined verdicts. Two quantized cells also failed utility, and all seven passed remote gain. | §§3.4, 4.1, Table 2, Fig. 1 |
| Wire observations and host access support different claims | Wire decoders recorded zero top-1 recovery on rotated epochs in the bounded capture test. A compromised restoring host can access the restored activations. In the Mistral-NeMo-12B matrix, session 1 at cadence 16 and zero emulated RTT fell from 7.6 tokens/s on the static baseline to 2.8 tokens/s under ratcheting. | §§5.1–5.2, Appendix A |
| A near-zero variational score leaves leakage unconstrained above | The population objective lower-bounds mutual information. The paper’s plaintext-copy example gives a zero score with a constant critic even though the output reveals the entire input. | §5.3, Eq. 3 |

The calibrated probes establish leakage for the specified targets, observers, and budgets. The frozen rules establish candidate rejection within each campaign, even where some endpoints remain undetermined. Results from finite probes leave untested targets and worst-case recovery open, and the archive provides no field-wide estimate of evaluation error.

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Authors.** Cases were selected after outcomes were known and share corpora, code, and design choices. Seed cohorts were small, intervals condition on fixed panels, and the reported analyses used no multiplicity adjustment. The exploration’s proposed confirmation stage remained unexecuted. (§§2, 3.3, 4, 7)
- **Authors.** The scripts recompute saved outputs. Decoy frame counts and parts of the boundary review remain source-reported. Semantic targets were omitted from the pilot, and multi-step accumulation and exact batch reconstruction were untested against the noisy gradient mitigation. (§§3.1, 3.4, 7, Appendix A)
- **Analyst inference.** The reviewed checkpoint shows that the loss gate can miss a substantial drop in teacher-forced answer-token match. The size and frequency of that mismatch across checkpoints and tasks remain unknown. (§3.2)

## Open research questions

1. **How reliably does the framework classify claims outside this programme?** The archive was selected retrospectively. A prospective study on held-out model families and corpora could score verdicts against deliberately injected channel omissions, label shifts, and known leaky controls. (§7)
2. **What does the boundary-replacement defence reveal about current tokens?** The archived probe scored next-token prediction. A follow-up could evaluate separately calibrated current-token and next-token probes on correctly aligned targets under the same observation contract. (§3.2)
3. **How does perturbation strength trade off against semantic leakage and task quality?** The pilot omitted semantic targets and judged utility on its declared endpoints. A prospective sweep could use synthetic text with labelled semantic properties and prespecified loss and answer-quality caps. (§§3.4, 7)

## Confidence and missing information

**High** for the reported verdicts and their stated scope. The supplied text lacks the full per-cell and per-seed arrays, raw tensor captures for independent rescoring, complete corpus and model specifications for every campaign, and prospective external validation results. Appendices A–B identify companion records for some of these details.
