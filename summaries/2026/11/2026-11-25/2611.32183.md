# ThinkMorph-SSL: Scaling Interleaved Multimodal Reasoning with Pretext Tasks

> [← Daily summary](../2026-11-25.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.32183) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32183)


## One-sentence takeaway

Adding instruction-free visual restoration practice before interleaved instruction tuning improved BAGEL’s accuracy by an average of 6.49 percentage points over instruction tuning alone on three representative benchmarks at a matched instruction-tuning budget (§4.4, Fig. 6).

## Problem and motivation

Unified multimodal models can generate images and text in the same trace, but learning to use a generated image to answer a question typically requires demonstrations tailored to each task. The authors identify this dependence on task-specific pipelines and review as a barrier to extending interleaved reasoning to new tasks. They test whether images can supply reusable training targets before question-driven tuning (§§1–2).

## Approach and contributions

- **Instruction-free pretext reasoning.** Pretext-270K contains 270,896 traces built from seven transformations of unlabeled images. For each transformed input, the model learns to describe the visible evidence, generate the restored or canonical image, and describe the result. The original image supplies the visual target, while a separate multimodal model writes the text targets (§§2.2, 3.1).
- **Question-driven instruction tuning.** Instruct-180K contains 180,490 traces from nine automated pipelines. They teach the same backbone to generate and use question-relevant images, such as crops, depth maps, and alternate views, before answering (§3.2, Fig. 2, Appendix D).
- **Training comparisons.** The authors measure the incremental benefit of pretext training at matched Stage 2 exposure and test whether training order and explicit Stage 1 instructions affect transfer (§4.4, Figs. 6–7).

The matched Stage 2 comparison supports an incremental benefit from adding pretext practice in the tested settings. The full-suite gains measure both stages together. Since the trained model chooses when to emit images, the within-question trace comparisons are observational. Forcing image generation in the unadapted BAGEL backbone offered little benefit in the tested settings, and substituting a verified image recovered little on the spatial subset (§4.2, Table 2). The latency result covers V⋆.

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Authors’ limitations.** Scaling with pretext data, training compute, or model size was not systematically measured. Evaluation concentrates on perception and spatial reasoning. Image generation adds inference cost, BAGEL’s private pretraining corpus prevents an image-level overlap audit, and dataset and backbone biases were not audited (§6, Appendix H.2).
- **Analyst inference.** Matching Stage 2 sequence exposure leaves total training compute unmatched. The full-data comparison also uses different world sizes and checkpoint step counts, so it does not isolate pretext content from additional training or other configuration differences. The cross-backbone and training-order results use a spatial training subset and MindCube’s tiny split (§4.4, Appendix G).
- **Analyst inference.** Defective images can undermine later reasoning, as the panorama and mask examples illustrate. Main model comparisons also use each baseline’s decoding defaults, so they do not provide a matched inference-budget comparison (§4.3, Appendix G.2, Appendix H.1).

## Open research questions

1. **Which pretext tasks account for the transfer gains?** The main ablation tests the pretext stage as a bundle. Compare single-task, leave-one-out, and full-mixture runs at matched Stage 1 compute and Stage 2 exposure (§§3.1, 4.4).
2. **How much does the image itself change the trained model’s answer?** The trained model selects when to generate images. Fix the reasoning prefix and randomly substitute an intact, deliberately degraded, or omitted intermediate before continuing the trace (§4.3, Table 5).
3. **Does the recipe transfer beyond perception and spatial reasoning?** The reported suite stays within those areas. Hold the backbone and tuning budget fixed and compare staged and Stage-2-only training on a held-out family such as diagram-based geometry (§§1, 4.1, 6).
