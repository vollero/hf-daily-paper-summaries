# DexDuet: Learning Contact-Preserving Bimanual Dexterity from Human Demonstrations

> [← Daily summary](../2026-11-24.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.30495) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.30495)


## One-sentence takeaway

DexDuet learns a bimanual policy from one human demonstration and preserves contacts better than DexMachina in 117 of 122 simulation pairs matched for object-tracking accuracy (Table 2, §C.4).

## Problem and motivation

Human motions require retargeting because robot hands differ in size, kinematics, and actuation. The authors argue that object-trajectory tracking leaves a critical ambiguity: two hands can move an object along the demonstrated path while touching the wrong parts or sliding during a hold. They also identify a training bottleneck when a difficult phase stalls the withdrawal of object assistance across the entire demonstration (§§1–2).

## Approach and contributions

- **Partner-aware control.** Two per-hand actors share weights and observe each other’s state. PPO clips each hand’s probability ratio separately, with a centralized critic providing a value head for each hand (§3.2).
- **Object-relative interaction rewards.** A hand-graph reward matches palm and finger directions and scaled fingertip gaps. A contact reward matches each corresponding hand region’s distance to an object part and its nearest point on that part (§3.3, §A.4).
- **Adaptive training curriculum.** Assisted reference-state initialization (A-RSI) sets assistance by episode start segment, reuses states reached in successful episodes, and samples starts around difficult phases. A declining ceiling brings assistance to zero (§3.4, §A.5).
- **Contact evaluation.** Contact-AUC scores object-relative drift of the corresponding robot hand during human hold windows, assigning zero when the robot hand misses the part at the window start or the object is lost (§4.1, §§C.1–C.3).

The matched analysis shows that similar object-tracking scores can coexist with different contact quality on the DexMachina suite. The rater study supports the metric’s perceptual relevance for selected pairs with large contact-score gaps. Hardware evidence establishes replay feasibility for the shown motions. Closed-loop robustness, performance under disturbances, and transfer to unseen motions remain untested.

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Author-stated.** DexDuet trains one policy per demonstration, takes about a day on one GPU per policy, and does not generalize to new objects or motions. It requires accurate demonstration trajectories and observes simulator object state. Hardware execution uses open-loop replay (§E).
- **Analyst inference.** Contact-AUC is a geometric proxy based on endpoint drift during human hold windows. It also penalizes object loss, and robot contact detection differs by benchmark. These choices limit interpretation as continuous physical contact or direct comparison across benchmarks (§§C.1–C.3).
- **Analyst inference.** Matched pairs can reuse runs, and the perceptual study selects large contact-score gaps, all favoring full DexDuet on that metric. The counts describe the selected comparisons and leave agreement on subtle differences uncertain (§§C.4–C.5).
- **Analyst inference.** The main policy and curriculum ablations replace several features together, leaving their individual effects unresolved. V2D uses one training seed per method, so repeated evaluator runs do not measure training-seed variability (§§D.4, B.4).

## Open research questions

1. **Can estimated object state support reliable closed-loop hardware control?** The hardware trials use open-loop replay. Compare replay and state-feedback control under controlled pose perturbations, measuring task success and contact drift (§§4.3, E).
2. **Can one policy preserve contacts on unseen objects or motions?** Each policy is trained for one demonstration. Train a shared policy across demonstrations and evaluate on held-out objects and motions using ADD-AUC and Contact-AUC (§E).
3. **Which A-RSI mechanisms account for the gains?** The reported curriculum ablation swaps the whole package. Vary segment-specific assistance, reached-state resets, and failure-aware sampling independently under a common environment-step budget (§3.4, §D.4).
