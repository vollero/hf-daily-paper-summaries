# GPBam: A Retrieval-Aware German Public Law Exam Benchmark

> [← Daily summary](../2026-11-17.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.18981) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18981)


## One-sentence takeaway

On GPBam’s German public-law practice cases, closed-book statute recitation strongly tracked essay scores across 32 models, while supplying reference-cited statutes raised scores by only about 2–6 points out of 100 across seven tested generators (Tables 5, 8–9).

## Problem and motivation

A complete German legal opinion (*Gutachten*) requires identifying issues, selecting governing provisions, applying them to the facts, and reaching conclusions on each issue. A correct short answer can hide errors in those intermediate steps. The authors identify a gap between benchmarks that score statute retrieval and those that grade legal answers: neither type, as reviewed in the paper, measures how statute retrieval affects a complete civil-law examination opinion on the same cases (§§1–2).

## Approach and contributions

- **Benchmark and retrieval targets.** GPBam pairs 81 held-out public-law practice cases from the University of Passau with full reference solutions. The provisions cited in each reference solution serve as retrieval targets for the same case whose generated opinion is graded (§§3.1–3.3, 3.6).
- **Separate statutory recall task.** Models reproduce provisions from identifiers alone, using two sets of 100 selected by citation frequency in GPBam solutions and in German court decisions. ROUGE-L F1 measures overlap with official wording (§3.3.2, Table 2).
- **Joint retrieval and writing evaluation.** The study evaluates 32 models without retrieval and seven with eight retrieval configurations and an oracle that supplies available reference-cited provisions. It combines reference-guided grading by three LLM judges with statutory citation overlap and reports judge agreement (§§3.5–3.8, Tables 8, 11).

## Evidence and main results

| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| Wide spread in unaided essay scores | Across 32 models on 81 cases without retrieval, mean scores ranged from 4.81 to 76.42 out of 100. The panel scored the reference solutions 100 and both wrong-case solutions and fact-pattern echoes 0. | §4.1, Table 4 |
| Statute recitation tracked essay scores | Across 32 models without retrieval, Spearman ρ between mean essay score and recitation ROUGE-L F1 was 0.92 for GPBam provisions and 0.81 for court-frequent provisions. Citation Jaccard from the graded essays correlated at ρ = 0.94. | §4.2, Table 5 |
| Higher retrieval recall yielded limited essay gains | Across retrieval arms, pooled gold-norm recall ranged from 3.50% to 9.61% at \(k=10\) and from 8.52% to 23.92% at \(k=50\). The largest non-oracle gain over no retrieval was 4.13 points out of 100 for DeepSeek-V4-Flash. Three of seven generators had no gain meeting the paper’s uncorrected threshold of twice the paired standard error in any retrieval arm. | §4.3, Table 8 |
| Oracle context helped every generator by the point estimate | The reference-citation oracle reached 91.31% gold-norm recall and improved scores by about 2–6 points out of 100 versus no retrieval. Six of seven gains met the paper’s threshold. | §4.3, Tables 8–9 |
| Judges agreed more on model rankings than on individual essays | Pairwise Spearman ρ was 0.95–0.99 across the 32 model means and 0.78–0.81 across individual no-retrieval essays. | §5.1, Table 11 |

The results show that large improvements in citation retrieval can coexist with small improvements in judged essay quality. The authors interpret the remaining gap as evidence that applying statutes is difficult even when their text is available (§6). The experiment leaves the relative roles of issue spotting, rule selection, and applying rules to facts unresolved. The recitation correlation is descriptive, and the retrieval comparisons also change prompt framing.

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Authors’ limitations.** The cases come from one Bavarian preparation course, and retrieval covers a cost-selected subset of seven models. The corpus has limited EU coverage and may contain provisions amended since the solutions were written. The contamination check tests exact text overlap and leaves paraphrased exposure and prior exposure to case facts unresolved (§§3.2, 3.5, 7).
- **Authors’ limitations.** GPBam has no human grades. The judges also appear as generators, score correlates with answer length, and Claude Opus 5 alone received a harness system prompt. The private cases, reference solutions, and generations prevent independent reproduction of the full essay evaluation from the released materials (§§3.3.3, 5.2, 7).
- **Analyst inference.** A legally sound alternative analysis may cite provisions absent from the reference solution, so citation-based recall and overlap can undercredit it. The uncorrected comparisons across retrieval arms also make isolated gains less conclusive (§3.8, Table 8).

## Open research questions

1. **How much of the essay gain comes from statutory text versus prompt framing?** Both changed between conditions, and the wording-withheld check covered only three generators. Cross prompt framing with the presence of the same statutory passages on identical cases (§3.3.1, Table 10).
2. **Do qualified examiners agree with the panel where it is most lenient?** The external LLM judge diverged at higher scores, particularly on fabricated norms and reversed legal conclusions. Have multiple examiners independently grade a blinded, stratified sample and compare both scores and error judgments (§5.2).
3. **Does retrieval from full court decisions improve legal application?** The strongest recall arm used headnote citation chains, and the study did not test full decision text. Compare statute-only search, headnote routing, and full-decision retrieval under matched context budgets, with separate scores for issue spotting and application (§§3.6, 7, Table 8).

## Confidence and missing information

**High** confidence in the reported benchmark design and descriptive results. Material gaps include independent examiner grades, a full prompt-matched retrieval comparison, and access to the private cases, reference solutions, and generations. Figure 2 and Table 5 give slightly different bootstrap interval bounds for the recitation correlations, so the results above use their shared point estimates.
