# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-11-10

## Executive summary

- **Execution quality is a recurring concern.** Auriga (`2611.01112`) and Spatial Harness (`2611.11344`) make geometry executable; InfraRSI (`2611.11558`) and Infrathon (`2611.11995`) require infrastructure improvements to satisfy correctness and execution constraints.
- **Retrieving evidence and using it remain separate problems.** TRIO (`2611.02810`) trains query, stopping, and answer roles separately; Reframe (`2611.12886`) addresses poorly grounded tool actions and neglect of returned observations.
- **Serving improvements come with different guarantees.** Flide (`2610.42148`) preserves isolated-query semantics in exact arithmetic; RidgeFill (`2611.09709`) approximates prompt caches and accepts model-dependent quality losses. Their speedups are not directly comparable.
- **ReproBridge (`2611.08452`) reports a substantial artifact-reuse benefit:** mean coding-agent reproduction success rises from 76.4% to 92.6% across four models. This measures re-execution of released materials, not independent scientific validation.
- **Several evaluations expose misleading proxies.** MotionGraphicsBench (`2611.10592`) validates executable grading against human judgments; SIRIS (`2611.10329`) separates detecting a simulator limitation from making the right decision.
- **Deployment claims remain setting-specific.** InstantHMR (`2611.09195`) reports 5.49 ms mesh inference on a Galaxy S23 NPU, excluding detection and cropping. LCM-Promo (`2610.37570`) reports production benefits in one platform’s one-month experiment.
- **Evidence attribution matters.** EmoHarness (`2611.11994`) attributes most gains to transcript and prosody evidence; its automated evolution increments do not survive multiple-testing correction.
- **The collection supports concrete findings, but incomplete inputs limit comparison.** All 39 current analyses are compacted; missing tables, limited replication, and six reported collection failures constrain confidence.

Construction, GPU execution, auditing, maintenance, and human costs are not fully included.

- **A production allocation result with limited statistical detail — `2610.37570`.** LCM-Promo reports **1% relative conversion lift and 6% lower promotion cost versus Segment-EMA** in a **one-month iFood churn-reactivation A/B test**. Missing sample sizes, absolute outcomes, and uncertainty prevent assessing precision or broader transfer.

- **Serving gains require distinct interpretations — `2610.42148`, `2611.09709`.** Flide reports **up to 9.5× lower latency than the better of vLLM and SGLang** for tested shared-context decision workloads, with standalone-query equivalence in exact arithmetic. RidgeFill reports **1.25–1.60× faster prefill than ordinary exact prefill in two deployments**, with approximate caches and model-dependent quality losses. These are different workloads and fidelity regimes.

- **Clustering efficiency at a specified quality target — `2611.09501`.** e-means reportedly matches **Flash-KMeans’ 25-epoch held-out MSE in 1.4–2.6 epochs**, delivering **9.5–45.5× GPU wall-clock speedups** in the evaluated settings. This does not establish equal improvements in downstream retrieval.

- **Phone inference excludes important pipeline work — `2611.09195`.** InstantHMR reports **5.49 ms mesh inference on a Galaxy S23 NPU** and **48.8 mm EMDB-1 PA-MPJPE**. It has the best reported accuracy among evaluated models meeting the phone real-time threshold, but detection, cropping, sustained power, and thermal behavior are outside that measurement.

- **Executable spatial reasoning improves an end-to-end benchmark — `2611.11344`.** With **Qwen3.5-27B on MindCube**, Spatial Harness reports **71.14% accuracy versus 52.70% for direct reasoning**. The integrated result does not isolate the executor’s contribution from improved scene construction.

- **Verifier agreement and model success measure different things — `2611.10592`.** MotionGraphicsBench reports **93.3% agreement with held-out human judgments**, while the best tested model reaches **67.7% pass@1** on its SVG-animation tasks. High verifier agreement does not establish perfect grading or generalization to newly constructed task verifiers.

- **Mismatch tolerance is empirical and local — `2611.11450`.** In the tested math-distillation setting, **FP8 plus asynchronous generation reaches the synchronous BF16 baseline’s final held-out student-to-teacher KL 1.62× sooner**. The admission threshold is model- and configuration-specific.

- **Infrastructure throughput is not long-run training equivalence — `2611.11558`.** InfraRSI reports a **1.83× geometric-mean speedup versus workload baselines across four LLM workloads**. Its verification supports short-window comparisons, leaving full-training quality and checkpoint-resume equivalence unresolved.

- **Careful decomposition weakens an automation claim — `2611.11994`.** EmoHarness’s automated evolution adds **at most 0.95 percentage points over its human scaffold**, and **none of those increments survives multiple-testing correction**. Most reported benefit comes from transcript and prosody evidence.

- **Mechanic learning is measured on changed cells — `2611.12324`.** WARC reaches sustained **≥80% changed-cell accuracy on 56 of 69 recurring mechanics** in the supplied held-out game protocol. “Sustained” means through the available recording horizon; it does not establish indefinite retention or general rule recovery.

- **Two qualitative distinctions deserve preservation — `2611.10123`, `2611.10329`.** The injected-state study separates spontaneous reporting from probe decodability and trainable reporting. SIRIS separates limitation identification from decision correctness. Editorially, both caution against substituting an accessible proxy for the behavior actually being evaluated; their constructs remain different.

## Open research directions

The following are editorial proposals grounded in stated limitations, not established cross-paper results.

1. **Measure evidence acquisition and use independently.** Motivated by `2611.02810`, `2611.08935`, and `2611.12886`: hold evidence budgets fixed while varying acquisition policy, stopping, and answer training. Score evidence relevance, sufficiency errors, observation dependence, final correctness, latency, and cost separately.

2. **Characterize fidelity under realistic state reuse.** Motivated by `2610.42148` and `2611.09709`: test growing histories, unrelated follow-up questions, and concurrent requests under matched workloads. Report latency, memory, probability/output differences, and distant-context failures rather than retrieval scores alone.

3. **Extend infrastructure verification beyond short tests.** Motivated by `2611.11558` and `2611.11995`: evaluate accepted changes through longer training, checkpoint resume, precision checks, and adversarially modified implementations. Measure false acceptance alongside speed and final model quality.

4. **Audit executable graders independently.** Motivated by `2611.10304` and `2611.10592`: draw blinded samples of final tasks and outputs, commission independent requirement judgments, and estimate false acceptance and rejection. Include new task construction, not only new outputs for established tasks.

5. **Separate geometric perception from execution benefits.** Motivated by `2611.01112`, `2611.11344`, and `2611.12875`: compare annotated, estimated, and systematically perturbed geometry. Match intervention budgets and measure which errors computation, recovery, or human correspondences can actually repair.

6. **Test sensor transfer beyond familiar families.** Motivated by `2611.08969` and `2611.12806`: hold downstream data and encoder size fixed while introducing new sampling rates, layouts, sensing principles, and contact regions. Report adaptation cost and full-success metrics alongside partial credit.

7. **Isolate automated search and metacognitive control.** Motivated by `2611.11994`, `2611.11740`, and `2611.12762`: repeat construction across seeds with untouched evaluation sets and matched total compute. Compare added evidence, human scaffolds, automated edits, and control components; use independent human or experimental validation where appropriate.

8. **Measure what video supervision contributes to control.** Motivated by `2611.12870` and `2611.12324`: vary action labels, caption quality, and adaptation exposure while measuring predictive accuracy and action outcomes separately. For SWAM, directly test retained language–video alignment rather than inferring it from manipulation success.

9. **Evaluate deployment costs and external validity.** Motivated by `2611.09195`, `2611.08452`, `2610.36980`, and `2610.37570`: measure end-to-end phone latency and thermal behavior, artifact maintenance costs, simulator sensitivity, and replication across commercial settings. These experiments would test whether reported local gains survive complete operational accounting.

10. **Connect internal signals to consequential behavior without conflating constructs.** Motivated by `2611.10123`, `2611.10329`, and `2611.12167`: use controlled, counterbalanced interventions and separately score reports, detection, decisions, and actions. Validate each proxy against its own target behavior.

## Coverage and uncertainty

**All 39 supplied current papers were analyzed and included.** Taxonomy, same-day connections, historical connections, 50 historical candidates, and both source catalogs were available. Seven historical papers are retained in supported cross-day edges.

**Every current PAPER_ANALYSIS contains a COMPACTED marker.** All 50 historical evidence items are also compacted. Only retained passages were treated as evidence; omitted numerical tables, ablations, or connection details were not reconstructed from downstream summaries. WorldFoundry (`2611.10771`) is especially limited to abstract-and-introduction coverage. FewStepRL (`2611.08240`) has incomplete empirical coverage.

The collection reports Hugging Face Markdown failures for:

- `2611.12806`, `2611.11995`, `2611.11994`, `2611.09195`: unexpected HTML content type.
- `2611.12762`, `2611.12870`: responses did not appear to contain full paper text.

Analyses were nevertheless supplied for all six; the failures remain collection limitations, and no missing source material was recovered here.

Recurring uncertainties include absent table bodies, unverified release contents, limited repeated-run statistics, incomplete cost accounting, and unresolved component attribution. Specific reporting concerns include KG-RAG4SM’s conflicting metrics, TRIO’s aggregate-score rule, PhysioWave-T’s seizure-sensitivity attribution, e-means’ finalization-regime wording, and Click3R’s inconsistent human-subset scores.

Clinical reliability, subjective experience, real-world fraud incidence, sustained recursive improvement, and broad deployment readiness are not established by the supplied evaluations. Historical connections express technical similarity or proposed experiments, not citation, priority, dependency, or influence. No outside facts or web evidence were used.
