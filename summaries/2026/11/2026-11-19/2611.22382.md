# RICoT: Recurrent Interleaved Chain-of-Thought for Page Stream Segmentation

> [← Daily summary](../2026-11-19.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.22382) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22382)


## One-sentence takeaway

RICoT interleaves page-level reasoning and boundary decisions while carrying compact state across windows, matching or beating same-backbone pairwise and windowed baselines on FinancePSS-205 even before finance-specific post-processing (Table 4).

## Problem and motivation

Page stream segmentation splits a bundled file into contiguous documents. In accounts payable and receivable (AP/AR), missed boundaries can merge fields from different invoices, duplicate line items, and disrupt reconciliation. The authors identify a context gap: adjacent-page classifiers can miss evidence from earlier pages, fixed windows lose context at their edges, and processing the full stream in one call strains long-context inference (§§1–2).

## Approach and contributions

- **Recurrent interleaved prompting.** RICoT packs consecutive pages into overlapping, token-budgeted windows. It emits a rationale followed by a decision for each page and passes recent rationale–decision pairs plus an active-segment summary to the next window (§§3.2–3.3, Figure 2).
- **Two-axis boundary decisions.** The schema records top-level starts and nested starts separately. The finance-specific *expand* rule promotes predicted nested starts of selected document classes to top-level boundaries without another model call (§§3.2, 3.4).
- **Dual-mode distillation.** One Qwen-3.5-2B student learns from Kimi-K2-Instruct outputs on finance-filtered FinePDFs and can run with or without emitting page-level rationales (§4).
- **Production benchmark and component evaluation.** FinancePSS-205 contributes 205 curated, pseudonymised production streams. The experiments test reasoning format, recurrent state, decision fields, and *expand* (§5.1, §§6.4–6.5, Appendices A and K–M).

## Evidence and main results

| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| FinancePSS gains vary by backbone and scoring rule | Against the stronger of the same-backbone pairwise and windowed baselines on FinancePSS-205, DocF1 gains with *expand* were +0.154, +0.069, and +0.057 for GLM-4.7-flash, GLM-4.7, and GLM-5, respectively. Without *expand*, gains were +0.133, +0.032, and a tie. | Table 4, §6.6 |
| Wider local context explains part of the gain | On FinancePSS-205 with GLM-4.7-flash, independent decisions with five-page context averaged 0.651 DocF1 over five runs, versus 0.506 for the adjacent-pair baseline. RICoT scored 0.741 without *expand* and averaged 2.15 calls per stream versus 7.6 for the five-page control. | §6.3, Appendix D |
| Interleaved reasoning helped more consistently than recurrent state | Removing pagewise reasoning from RICoT with *expand* cost 0.048–0.088 DocF1 across the three GLM backbones on FinancePSS-205. Removing all recurrent state cost 0.048 on GLM-5, while changes on the other two backbones fell within the authors’ estimated noise band. | Table 6, Appendix B |
| GLM-5 led the reported zero-shot TABME comparisons | Text-only RICoT scored 0.847 DocF1 and 0.915 B-F1 on TABME-250. The cited GPT-4o zero-shot result was 0.703 DocF1 on a different subset, and the best listed TABME++ result was 0.887 B-F1. TABME-trained LLMs reached 0.933 and 0.967 DocF1 in the published comparison. | §6.2, Table 3 |
| The distilled student was competitive in both modes | On FinancePSS-205 with *expand*, the student averaged 0.785 DocF1 with rationales and 0.773 without over five inference runs, with overlapping intervals. The strongest prompted configuration scored 0.832 in a single run. On full TABME, the student scored 0.770 and 0.774 DocF1, respectively. | Table 2, Appendix B |

The same-backbone comparisons support a FinancePSS advantage for the complete pipeline, and the matched-context control shows that enlarging the independent decision window recovers only part of that advantage in the tested setting. The advantage varies by dataset and model. GLM-4.7-flash favored pairwise prompting on TABME-250, and published TABME comparisons differ in test subsets and OCR versions. The student comparison measures whether one jointly trained model benefits from **emitting** rationales at inference. The value of rationale supervision during training remains open (Tables 3–4, §4.2).

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Author-stated.** FinancePSS-205 is a curated English-language sample from one domain, labeled by the research team without an agreement study. Tobacco800 uses a single near-ceiling stream. TABME scores source-file boundaries, so a predicted split can be a real document start under a different labeling convention. Reused source files also make full TABME stream-level intervals too narrow (Limitations, Appendices A, B and I).
- **Author-stated.** Prompted headline results are single runs, with variability measured on GLM-4.7-flash and extrapolated to other backbones. The student was trained once, with optional-field omission effects unisolated and reasoning-sampling probability and LoRA rank unswept (§4.3, Limitations, Appendices B and O).
- **Author-stated.** The method assumes the first page starts a document, uses each scanned page as one logical unit, and processes windows sequentially. It cannot regroup interleaved pages into non-contiguous documents (Limitations).
- **Analyst inference.** FinancePSS scores with *expand* reflect both boundary predictions and an AP/AR-specific promotion rule. Separate nested-boundary labels would be needed to assess nested-start accuracy directly (§§3.4, 5.3, Appendix A).

## Open research questions

- **Does rationale supervision improve the student’s boundary decisions?** Both inference modes use the same mixed-supervision checkpoint. Train decisions-only and mixed-supervision students on identical teacher-labeled chunks across several seeds, then compare both in decisions-only mode (§4.2, Appendix B).
- **How much does incorrect recurrent state cause downstream errors?** Appendix E shows that errors cluster across windows, but the analysis is observational. Replay the same long streams with corrected versus model-generated state at the first erroneous window and measure later boundary errors.
- **Does the two-axis schema transfer across document conventions?** FinancePSS lacks nested-start annotations and TABME labels source-file boundaries. Independently label top-level and nested starts in a second domain, then score each axis and test a promotion rule fixed before evaluation (§6.6, Appendix A, Limitations).

## Confidence and missing information

**High** confidence in the reported method and tabulated results. Broader effectiveness remains uncertain beyond the tested models and benchmarks. The supplied text lacks the FinancePSS files and evaluation code, exact prompt wording, complete result dumps, final training chunk-selection caps, and nested-start gold labels. Availability of the distilled checkpoint is unspecified (§1, §4.1, Appendices A, N and T).
