# Autoregressive View Model

> [← Daily summary](../2026-11-23.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2611.23417) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23417)


## One-sentence takeaway

The Autoregressive View Model streams camera-controlled views from posed images or video without fitting an explicit 3D representation, and retrieving earlier generated views substantially improves revisit fidelity on the tested WorldScore loops.

## Problem and motivation

A camera-controlled generator must reconstruct observed regions, synthesize plausible content in unseen regions, and preserve scene identity when the camera returns. The authors identify a gap between reconstruction methods that typically need dense captures and video generators that can extrapolate but may drift as earlier views fall outside their active context (§1).

## Approach and contributions

- **Unified training.** AVM fine-tunes a pretrained video diffusion transformer with flow matching so that multiple context slots condition one target clip. Slots can contain posed images, clips with timestamps discarded, or video synchronized to the target. The causal variant starts with teacher and diffusion forcing, then trains on its own rollouts using context-augmented distribution matching distillation and an adversarial loss (§§3.1–3.3, Fig. 3, Appendix B).
- **Shared retrieval during generation.** AVM builds a bounded active context from persistent input views, recent motion, and views retrieved by pose or timestamp from both the supplied inputs and generated history. The causal model reuses cached attention states across denoising steps (§4, Fig. 4).
- **Unified evaluation protocol.** The authors evaluate generation and scene persistence across single-image generation, sparse image reconstruction, synchronized video reconstruction, and revisits to generated views (§5, Appendix C).

The benchmark results support camera control, reconstruction, and repeatability at revisited poses under the tested settings. Revisit PSNR compares two generated frames, so it measures agreement with the model’s earlier output. The retrieval-policy comparison measures geometric coverage, leaving its effect on rendered video quality open (§D.4, Appendix E).

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Authors’ findings.** AVM trails every tested world-model baseline in text alignment and has the lowest dynamic degree among the models evaluated on that split. Per-sample guidance distillation improves camera control and sparse reconstruction while slightly worsening FID and motion accuracy (§§5.2, 5.4).
- **Analyst inference.** The quantitative revisit test uses a prescribed static loop, and the dynamics test holds the camera fixed. The dynamic revisit examples are qualitative, so persistence when both the scene and camera move remains weakly characterized (§§5.2–5.3, Fig. 18).
- **Analyst inference.** The supplied ablation compares stages of causal tuning, leaving the separate effects of the adversarial loss, context augmentation, and rollout-length curriculum unresolved (§3.3, §5.4, Appendix B).

## Open research questions

1. **How robust is retrieval to camera pose error?** AVM conditions on metric-scale poses and retrieves by pose, but the reported tests do not vary calibration error. Perturb input poses at controlled levels and measure sparse reconstruction and revisit fidelity against the original evaluation targets (§§3.1, 4, 5.3).
2. **Does retrieval preserve scene identity as objects move during a revisit?** The quantitative loops hold scene time fixed. Evaluate moving-camera loops on synchronized dynamic captures with advancing timestamps, and score static background consistency and moving-object fidelity separately (§5.3, Appendix C, Fig. 18).
3. **Can prompt adherence improve while preserving layout?** The text benchmark shows weaker alignment, and the qualitative example shows the input structure persisting through a requested scene change. Vary text guidance and report text alignment alongside layout consistency on the same prompt-change trajectories (§5.2, Fig. 5, Appendix D.1).

## Confidence and missing information

**Confidence: medium.** The prose describes the method, evaluation settings, and headline comparisons. The supplied text omits most numerical table cells and gives no run-to-run uncertainty, so full baseline rankings and effect variability cannot be checked. It also leaves AVM code and checkpoint availability, measured end-to-end latency and memory use, and the complete held-out MultiCam scene mapping unclear.
