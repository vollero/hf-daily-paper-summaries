# Encoded Early, Used Late: Where Transformers Begin to Act on an Inferred Partner&#x27;s Expertise

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-09.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.07139) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07139)


## One-sentence takeaway
In one Llama model on synthetic dialogues, partner expertise is most linearly decodable early, while later activation patches more strongly affect a fixed downstream readout and next-token rankings, although expertise-specific behavioral change remains unproven.

## Problem and motivation
Probes can reveal information in a transformer without establishing that the model uses it. The authors ask whether this distinction extends to partner expertise inferred through conversation, identifying a gap in prior studies of explicitly supplied attributes (§§1–2). Locating usable representations could inform personalization and steering, but those applications are motivations rather than demonstrated outcomes.

## Approach and contributions
- **Controlled dialogue corpus:** EXPERTCOLLAB contains 28 synthetic, 12-turn research-planning conversations spanning four expertise levels and four scenarios. Personas signal expertise through engagement; an LLM detector found no explicit credential disclosures (Appendix B).
- **Paired decoding and intervention analysis:** The authors probe participant-perspective residual activations in Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct, then patch activation differences between conversations matched on scenario and the focal speaker’s persona. Controls include direct-readout calibration, zero differences, norm-matched random vectors, and probe-free output diagnostics (§3; Appendices C–D).
- **Finding and hypothesis:** Early decodability differs from later downstream propagation. The authors propose—but do not test—that increasing how indirectly an attribute is conveyed moves its causal onset deeper (§5; Appendix F). They state that the corpus and analysis code are released (Appendix A).

## Evidence and main results
| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| Partner expertise decodes best early | Five-fold classification over 56 speaker instances peaks at approximately 0.79 accuracy at layer 8, speaker turn 5, versus 0.25 chance; accuracy approaches chance by layer 40. | §4; Figs. 2, 6; Appendix C.3 |
| Stated own expertise remains readable | Own-level probe accuracy stays above 0.60 at every sampled layer, with a reported peak of 1.00 at layer 0. | §4; Fig. 2 |
| Simple surface features explain less | Five-feature logistic regression reaches 0.32 accuracy versus 0.786 for the neural peak cell under the pooled partner-level protocol. | Appendix E.1; Fig. 6 |
| Later patches recover more downstream probe signal | Across 48 directed cross-level pairs, normalized effect is 0.01 at layer 8, 0.86 at layer 36, and above 0.90 from layer 40; same-layer patch/readout calibration averages 1.01. | §4; Fig. 3; Appendices D.1–D.3 |
| Readout normalization excludes unresolved cases | Source–target probability gaps below 0.05 are masked, excluding 20.1% of cells; intervals use 1,000 pair-cluster bootstrap resamples. | Appendix D.2; Fig. 5 |
| Probe-free measurements show output sensitivity | Across the same 48 pairs, top-5 next-token overlap decreases from 0.97 at the first layers to about 0.67 by layer 32; final-layer cosine dissimilarity rises from around layer 8 and saturates near 0.40 by layer 32. | §4; Fig. 4; Appendix D.4 |
| Upward and downward swaps have similar aggregate recovery | Higher-expertise swaps: 0.653, 95% interval [0.597, 0.713]; lower-expertise swaps: 0.660 [0.614, 0.704], with 24 pairs per direction. | Appendix D.5 |

These results support a separation between peak linear decodability and strong downstream propagation under the tested interventions. They do not establish that patches produce appropriately expertise-conditioned responses: token rankings and hidden-state changes measure sensitivity, not the meaning or quality of generated dialogue.

The precise transition is ambiguous. The paper describes a layer 20–36 transition band, near-complete recovery around layers 36–40, and a probe-free rise beginning around layer 8. These support a broad depth profile more clearly than a single shared onset.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated scope:** One analyzed model and a small synthetic corpus limit generalization; recognition of the generator’s own patterns may contribute. Generation uses a separately served Turbo variant, while probing uses the standard Instruct model (§5; Appendix B.1).
- **Analyst inference—intervention specificity:** Matched conversations share scenario and focal persona, but their generated transcripts differ. Full activation differences can therefore carry content and style alongside expertise. A norm-matched random control does not isolate expertise from these correlated differences (Appendices B.1, D.1–D.3).
- **Analyst inference—readout dependence:** The normalized endpoint is a late-layer expertise probe, although late-layer decoding is near chance. Masking weak baseline differences helps numerical stability but restricts the result to retained cells (Fig. 2; Appendix D.2).
- **Analyst inference—evaluation independence:** Cross-validation is described as stratified by instances, without reported grouping by conversation or held-out scenario. Both speakers from each conversation are pooled, so independence across folds is not established (Appendix C).
- **Internal reporting inconsistency:** Appendix E.2 describes adjacent-level errors, but Fig. 7 shows perfect classification after fitting on all data. That figure cannot substantiate held-out confusion patterns.
- **Incomplete control evidence:** Appendix F claims own expertise is causally available throughout “where tested,” but supplies no corresponding quantitative causal profile. Explicitness also differs together with own-versus-partner identity.

## Open research questions
- **Does inference difficulty itself move causal onset?** Explicitness is not independently manipulated here. Hold the attribute and speaker role fixed while varying direct disclosure, single cues, and distributed behavioral evidence.
- **Do patches change expertise-conditioned behavior?** Current diagnostics stop at the next-token distribution and representations. Generate full patched responses and evaluate explanation depth, deference, and technical appropriateness with blinded judgments.
- **Does the profile generalize beyond this corpus?** Same-model-family synthetic generation leaves self-recognition unresolved. Repeat with human-written and independently generated dialogues, conversation-grouped splits, and additional analyzed models.
- **Is the transferred signal specific to expertise?** Whole-conversation differences include other information. Compare tightly content-controlled dialogues and expertise-directed interventions against matched content/style interventions.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The supplied text supports the broad decoding-versus-propagation pattern, but not a precise universal onset or expertise-specific behavioral steering.

Material missing information includes an explicit release URL, numerical auxiliary-judge results, conversation-grouped validation, quantitative own-level causal controls, detailed random-control estimates, and full-response behavioral evaluation.
