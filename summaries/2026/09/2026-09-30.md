# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-09-30

## Executive summary

- Explicit agent state is a recurring design choice: evidence ledgers, need graphs, dependency graphs, and editable context guide execution in `2607.11433`, `2609.33326`, `2609.31847`, `2609.34392`, and `2609.37725`. Editorially, these papers support studying structured control state; they do not establish one universally superior representation.
- Memory evaluations expose distinct weaknesses. All 15 models tested by VoxMem score below 40% overall accuracy at its 32K reference budget (`2609.32607`); DERIVAUDIT finds that broader historical evidence can rescue valid memories without reliably preventing unsupported admission (`2609.36130`).
- Visual generation serves several roles beyond final output: inference-time reasoning aids (`2609.16409`), training-time latent targets (`2608.19669`), understanding supervision (`2609.38079`), and humanoid demonstration construction (`2609.38172`). These are separate empirical settings.
- World action models pursue cheaper predictive conditioning through adaptive denoising (`2609.33748`) and single-pass noisy-future features (`2609.34981`). Other papers examine whether predicted outcomes support useful action verification, selection, and planning (`2609.38057`, `2609.38059`, `2609.37441`).
- On-policy distillation receives complementary scrutiny: transfer scaling (`2609.32722`), representation changes (`2609.35210`), multi-teacher interference (`2609.34605`), supervision placement (`2609.36601`), rollout scheduling (`2609.36608`), and teacher adaptation (`2609.38360`).
- Efficiency claims vary in scope. Braco reports prefill reductions (`2609.35232`), FocusVTC counts crop observations in input compression (`2609.36651`), and WorldAttention reports an end-to-end speedup in a specified minute-long generation setting (`2609.34606`). Their numbers cannot form a common ranking.
- Executable evaluation distinguishes plausible artifacts from usable ones. LEGO-Anything separates Blender-scene validity from fidelity (`2609.36380`); EngiWorld checks engineering artifacts (`2609.37686`); AutoDataBench and LibraryDesignBench evaluate downstream agent use (`2609.35025`, `2609.36730`).
- Safety findings concern different failure mechanisms: behavioral secret leakage (`2609.32915`), harmful state transitions (`2609.34518`), reserved-token authority (`2609.35932`), unsupported memory (`2609.36130`), and consequential reporting omissions (`2609.36139`).
- All 92 current papers have visible analyses, but every analysis is compacted. The digest therefore retains visible methods, findings, and caveats while excluding numerical details available only in downstream editorial aids.

Qualitatively, three distinctions deserve attention: provenance is weaker than derivation support (`2609.36130`); accurate world prediction can coexist with poor goal ranking (`2609.37441`); and executable visual artifacts can remain visually inaccurate (`2609.36380`). These author-reported observations motivate separate acceptance criteria rather than a single aggregate success label.

## Open research directions

The following are editorial proposals grounded in visible limitations, rather than claims that the papers have already resolved or uniquely identified these problems.

1. **Measure causal benefits of explicit agent state.** Motivated by `2607.11433`, `2609.33326`, `2609.31847`, and `2609.34392`. Hold backbone, tools, evidence access, and token budget fixed; compare free-form history, ledger, need graph, and dependency graph. Measure answer support, recovery, unnecessary actions, and erroneous-dependency sensitivity.

2. **Audit memory across its complete lifecycle.** Motivated by `2609.36130`, `2609.32522`, `2609.37923`, and `2609.38155`. Independently label factual derivation and speaker/entity correspondence before writes, then run consolidation, retrieval, and answering. Report valid-memory retention, unsupported admission, identity errors, and downstream error persistence.

3. **Test acoustic and visual memory under natural interruptions.** Motivated by `2609.32607`, `2609.37559`, and `2609.38119`. Use natural recordings with matched evidence and distractors, interrupt and restore persistent state, and pair answerable questions with evidence-removed versions. Measure recall, refusal, restoration failures, latency, and storage.

4. **Separate useful future conditioning from expensive future generation.** Motivated by `2609.33748`, `2609.34981`, `2609.38059`, and `2609.37441`. Match backbone, training data, action horizon, and hardware across explicit rollout, noisy-feature reuse, and adaptive budgets. Evaluate held-out tasks, perturbations, goal-ranking accuracy, and full action-to-control latency.

5. **Factor supervision placement, teacher adaptation, and interference control.** Motivated by `2609.36601`, `2609.38360`, `2609.34605`, `2609.32722`, and `2609.35210`. Run replicated teacher–student scale grids with factorial controls for warm-up, correction routing, teacher updates, and subspace protection. Include teacher creation and adaptation costs, and apply representation diagnostics to both gains and regressions.

6. **Compare RL learning signals at complete, equal cost.** Motivated by `2609.33589`, `2609.36802`, `2609.36864`, and `2609.37868`. Include peer-model generation, reflection, critic updates, and reused-prefix execution in the budget. Measure success per wall-clock hour across seeds; add random branch-selection and fully tuned baseline controls.

7. **Evaluate visual repair with independent judges and preservation tests.** Motivated by `2609.33627`, `2609.34309`, `2609.36199`, `2609.35530`, and `2609.37372`. Match generation budgets, use independent blinded evaluation, and inject a single defect into otherwise acceptable outputs. Measure repaired requirements, newly broken requirements, and critique cost.

8. **Strengthen acceptance evidence for agent-authored artifacts.** Motivated by `2609.35025`, `2609.36730`, `2609.36380`, and `2609.37686`. Audit verifier false rejections using equivalent representations; test libraries with unseen consumers and scenes with held-out views. For generated training tasks, measure subsequent learning gains separately from artifact acceptance.

9. **Report complete compression and serving trade-offs.** Motivated by `2609.35232`, `2609.36651`, `2609.33591`, `2609.34606`, and `2609.37402`. Measure preprocessing, rendering, calibration, cache movement, decoding, training, and feedback acquisition where applicable. Sweep output lengths and deployment horizons while retaining matched quality targets.

10. **Validate supervision and calibration on untouched data.** Motivated by `2609.34528`, `2609.37687`, `2609.35342`, `2609.36965`, and `2609.37989`. Use real human preferences or delayed labels, enforce hard annotation caps, fit calibrators only on reserved partitions, and evaluate pipeline search with fully nested folds. Report accuracy, probability error, annotation time, and sensitivity to stream order.
