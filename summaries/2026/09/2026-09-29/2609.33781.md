# Surprising Success, Repeated Failure: Entropy-Guided Credit Assignment for Exploration in LLM Reasoning

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-29.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.33781) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33781)


## One-sentence takeaway
EAPO improves aggregate reasoning accuracy and problem coverage by reinforcing high-entropy tokens in positive-advantage responses and penalizing low-entropy tokens in negative-advantage responses, using existing rollout signals (Sections 4–5, Tables 2–3).

## Problem and motivation
RL with verifiable rewards supplies outcome-level feedback, but individual token decisions can contribute differently to success or failure. Existing finer-grained credit methods often require auxiliary models, extra sampling, or privileged information; entropy-based methods can instead concentrate both rewards and penalties on uncertain positions (Sections 1–2).

The authors identify an asymmetry: uncertain successful continuations are less repeatable, while confident failed continuations frequently remain unsuccessful. Penalizing uncertain positions most heavily may suppress alternatives that could recover from failure. Their resampling experiments motivate treating reinforcement and penalization differently (Section 3, Table 1).

## Approach and contributions
- **Asymmetric token credit:** EAPO normalizes old-policy, full-vocabulary entropy using rollout-batch percentiles, clips and detaches this signal, then assigns weights proportional to `exp(κ × sign(advantage) × normalized entropy)`. Positive advantages favor uncertain positions; negative advantages favor confident positions (Section 4.1, Equations 3–4).
- **Advantage redistribution:** Weights have a within-response mean of one, preserving the response’s mean advantage and its sign. EAPO replaces the advantage in the existing policy objective without auxiliary supervision or additional rollouts (Sections 4.1, A).
- **Mechanistic evidence and interpretation:** Continuation resampling motivates the asymmetry; allocation-direction ablations test it. Theoretical analyses connect the weights to KL-regularized allocation and show that weaker negative updates limit concentration among unchosen alternatives in a single-step logit model (Sections 3, 4.2, D).
- **Empirical validation:** The authors test mathematical reasoning, transfer to Reasoning Gym, answer diversity, sampling-budget coverage, model scaling, and fine-tuning strategy (Section 5, Appendix C).

These results support aggregate gains in the tested settings; EAPO does not win every individual benchmark metric. The ablations strengthen evidence for the allocation directions. Answer diversity and epistemic markers remain proxies for exploration, and the length comparison excludes only the tested forced-continuation explanation. Neither the empirical results nor the restricted theory establish accurate causal credit for individual tokens.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated:** Entropy reflects learned confidence and biases. Its usefulness depends on alignment between uncertainty and actual decision contributions; entropy is not token correctness (Section 2; limitations, p. 10).
- **Author-stated:** Combining discoveries across states or trajectories remains unexplored. Improved reasoning also does not guarantee reliable or safe outputs (p. 10).
- **Analyst inference:** Mechanistic resampling selects maximally separated entropy windows on moderately difficult problems from two base models. This supports a targeted association, with limited evidence for generalization to arbitrary states or models (Section B.1).
- **Analyst inference:** Main benchmark results use a fixed training seed. The additional three-seed study reports training rewards for selected methods, rather than multi-seed benchmark accuracy or coverage (Table 8; Section C.6).
- **Analyst inference:** The theory uses direct single-step logit updates and a stylized binary decision model; it does not establish the same guarantees for repeated neural-network parameter updates (Sections D.2–D.3).
- **Analyst inference:** Reported training details are substantial, but unlisted parameters inherit implementation defaults. No runtime or memory comparison quantifies the claimed small computational overhead (Sections 4.1, B.3).

## Open research questions
- **When does entropy assign credit incorrectly?** The authors acknowledge dependence on uncertainty–contribution alignment. Next experiment: compare EAPO weights with intervention-based estimates of decision impact across model confidence levels.
- **Do benchmark gains persist across training seeds?** Existing seed evidence concerns training reward. Next experiment: repeat all main methods across seeds and report benchmark accuracy, coverage, and paired uncertainty estimates.
- **Does penalty attenuation preserve useful alternatives during actual training?** The theorem covers a fixed-prefix logit update. Next experiment: track alternative-token distributions and continuation success before and after neural-network updates.
- **Can EAPO support cross-trajectory discovery?** Combining findings across trajectories is explicitly left open. Next experiment: compare iterative solution-recombination systems trained with EAPO and uniform credit under matched sampling budgets.
