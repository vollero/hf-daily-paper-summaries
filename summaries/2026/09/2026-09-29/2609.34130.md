# Training and Inference Dynamics of PLDR-LLMs: Row-Map Collapse, Renormalization, and Predictive Reduction

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-29.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.34130) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34130)


## One-sentence takeaway
The paper develops exact bookkeeping for PLDR row-map dynamics and reports finite, state-specific predictive reductions, while showing that row concentration alone does not establish autonomous training dynamics, predictive accuracy, or critical universality.

## Problem and motivation
PLDR-LLMs generate operators inside attention whose learned rows can concentrate while common-row structure and predictive variation persist. The author asks how training produces this geometry, how its evolution composes over time, and what information a reduced inference computation must preserve (§1.1).

The identified gap is the passage from an observed geometric identity to a predictive dynamical model: optimizer memory, remaining corpus, downstream transformations, and observation precision can all matter (§§1.3–1.6).

## Approach and contributions
- **Exact row mechanics:** Defines absolute centered-row energy and resolves finite changes into signed work, quadratic charge, gate/shape interactions, and arithmetic defects. Contraction requires work to exceed charge (§§3.1, 4.1.1–4.1.5).
- **Chronological composition with restarts:** Represents observed energy paths by affine maps that remain defined at exact zero energy. A face hit removes homogeneous dependence on incoming scalar energy without erasing optimizer or corpus memory (§1.4; Theorem 4.15).
- **Complete-state predictive theory:** Retains AdamW moments, clocks, corpus resource, and program state; separates exact path identities from closure conditions and sufficient attraction bounds (§§2.2, 5.1).
- **Geometry-to-inference transfer:** Introduces a PLGA response-plus-defect decomposition and analyzes conditional prediction and cache recalibration. The author explicitly identifies caching and several probabilistic tools as prior methods; the claimed additions concern state transport, error accounting, and executed closure tests (§§2.3.1, 6.1).

## Evidence and main results
| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |

The visible proofs establish algebraic identities and explicitly conditional bounds. The empirical summaries support finite, protocol-conditioned forecasting and cache fidelity; they do not establish all-future collapse, uniform generated-history accuracy, economical autonomous reduced training, or a thermodynamic critical class. ## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated:** Native floating-point zeros and float32/float64 agreement do not certify exact real-arithmetic collapse (§3.2).
- **Author-stated:** Fixed contexts, heads, layers, and checkpoints are nested observations, not additional independent training replicas. Two corpus partitions do not measure between-corpus population variability (§2.2).
- **Author-stated:** Forecast coverage is conditional on calibration and continuation laws; cache results concern fixed-panel aggregate fidelity with context exceptions (pp. xiii–xiv).
- **Author-stated:** Critical limits, self-organized selection, and autonomous reduced training remain unresolved (§1.7).
- **Analyst inference:** Truncation prevents checking most experimental controls, complete outcome grids, uncertainty calculations, and reproduction boundaries. Front-matter summaries and figure captions provide evidence of reported findings, but limited grounds for independently assessing them.

## Open research questions
1. **Which retained state supports predictive closure?** Tested row-state candidates fail (§1.4). A next experiment could compare matched-state successors after progressively adding common-row, corpus, and optimizer-memory information.
2. **How broadly does a recalibrated cache preserve predictions?** Current claims are panel- and state-conditioned with exceptions (p. xiii). Test frozen cache rules on fresh documents, longer proper prefixes, and generated histories, reporting fidelity and target NLL separately.
3. **Do fluctuation regions narrow under a specified scaling family?** Measured widths do not establish systematic narrowing (§1.7). Extend independently initialized width families while matching optimizer memory, duration, and consumed fraction.
4. **Can finite row concentration yield an all-future certificate?** Exact identities do not establish attraction, and finite zeros permit later reopening (§5.1.9). Attempt certified continuation bounds on transported forcing and within-block excursions.
