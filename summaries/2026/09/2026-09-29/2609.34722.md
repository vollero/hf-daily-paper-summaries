# Geometry as Address: Routing Attention to Visual Memory for Long-Horizon Camera-Controlled Video Generation

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-29.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.34722) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.34722)


## One-sentence takeaway
GEAR uses per-frame geometry to route attention directly to matching historical visual tokens, improving camera adherence and revisit consistency on the reported benchmarks while retaining global geometry proxies only for visibility filtering.

## Problem and motivation
Long camera-controlled rollouts must recover previously seen content after it leaves the model’s temporal context. The authors identify two difficulties: implicit memory requires searching large historical token sets, while globally fused 3D appearance memory can preserve and propagate reconstruction errors. Existing geometry-guided methods provide alignment or conditioning signals but do not directly restrict each target patch’s access to matched historical visual features (§1; Fig. 2).

## Approach and contributions
- **Geometry-addressed patch memory:** Store historical frame latents separately. Construct independent depth-derived meshes, rasterize each under source and target cameras, and match face IDs to obtain token-level memory addresses (§3.2).
- **Geometric Correspondence Attention (GCA):** Each target token attends only to its matched historical candidates through an added residual pathway. The original backbone self-attention remains active; unmatched patches receive no GCA contribution (§3.4).
- **Visibility filtering:** An Invisible Octree rejects occluded correspondences. The appendix also specifies an accumulated global visible mesh; both proxies supply visibility information rather than appearance features (§3.3; Appendix A.4).
- **Rollout adaptation:** Coverage-based keyframe retrieval bounds conditioning context, and degraded-history augmentation exposes training to imperfect historical observations. LoRA and GCA adapt a frozen Wan backbone (§§3.5–3.6, 4.1).

The central claimed novelty is direct geometric addressing of historical visual features, rather than using geometry solely to construct conditioning signals (§1; Appendix C.1).

The results support the method’s advantages in the reported protocols, and the same-capacity ablation strengthens the case for geometric addressing. They do not establish universal superiority, real-time performance, or robustness when all correspondences are wrong. Minute-long performance is supported by author descriptions and qualitative figure references rather than quantitative horizon-specific results.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated:** External depth estimation adds computational overhead (§5).
- **Analyst inference:** Avoiding fused appearance memory does not eliminate persistent geometric state. The octree and global visible mesh accumulate visibility evidence, and established observations are deliberately not repeatedly overwritten; recovery from incorrect early visibility evidence is not tested (Appendix A.4).
- **Analyst inference:** The corruption experiment preserves valid matches in multiply observed regions. It does not establish tolerance to complete match failure or systematic geometry bias (§4.3).
- **Comparison boundaries:** AnchorWeave is excluded because inference weights were unavailable. UCM’s depth estimator is replaced, and Infinite-World receives discretized actions rather than continuous poses; these details qualify comparisons (Appendix C.1).
- **Evaluation and reproducibility gaps:** The text provides no confidence intervals or seed variability, and does not quantify dynamic-scene persistence, history-bank growth, or long-horizon retrieval scaling. GPU model, code/weight availability, and some geometry thresholds are unspecified (Tables 1–4; Appendices A–C).

## Open research questions
- **Can geometry estimation be integrated into the generator?** External estimation remains an acknowledged overhead (§5). Compare jointly learned addressing with DA3-based addressing on quality, camera error, and total rollout latency.
- **How does the method behave without reliable matches?** The disturbance test retains correct candidates (§4.3). Sweep the valid-match fraction down to zero and separately introduce systematic depth and pose errors.
- **Can visibility memory recover from early mistakes or scene changes?** Updates preserve established evidence, while revisit evaluation uses static scenes (Appendix A.4; §4.2). Inject erroneous early depths or moving objects and compare conservative updates with revisable visibility memory.
- **How does total memory access scale with exploration?** Conditioning context is bounded, but the history bank grows (§3.6). Measure storage, retrieval time, correspondence construction, and revisit quality across increasing history lengths.
