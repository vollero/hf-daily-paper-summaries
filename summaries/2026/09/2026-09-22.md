# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-09-22

## Executive summary

- **Harness improvement and behavior transfer are distinct achievements.** RRSI reports better held-out transfer from regularized harness search (2609.24972); Harness-Zero transfers some harness benefits into separately trained domain policies, with incomplete retention in retrosynthesis (2609.24974).
- **Specialization helps, but consolidation still involves trade-offs.** Category-aware software-engineering training improves reported resolution, while ACLArena documents forgetting and benefits from routed experts. Neither establishes superiority under matched total training budgets (2609.23377, 2609.23989).
- **Robot results depend strongly on interfaces and supervision.** Frozen-VLM commands, 3D grounding, robotized human videos, and cached teacher features each show benefits in their tested settings. Different demonstrations, planners, backbones, and evaluation protocols prevent a common ranking (2609.22966, 2609.23863, 2609.10706, 2609.24682).
- **Recovery has useful controlled evidence; safety remains empirical.** CARE improves recovery with identical corrective training data. ShieldVLA improves reported safe completion over SafeVLA in simulation, but conflicting protocol descriptions and aggregate accounting require caution (2609.24118, 2609.13231).
- **Video progress spans different objectives.** WorldCrafter improves scene revisits; VideoGen-Agent improves tool-assisted generation; early-step RoPE modification improves human-rated physical commonsense; SVEET transfers editing controls into causal generation. These metrics measure different capabilities (2609.24984, 2609.24997, 2609.23658, 2609.24788).
- **Sparse supervision is not equivalent to cheap training.** IER-based distillation can perform well at nominal 0.1% token supervision, while still generating and scoring complete trajectories. FP8 calibration improves accuracy, but its net training speedup remains unmeasured (2609.24432, 2609.22870).
- **Evaluation mechanisms themselves need testing.** SkillSpec finds confirmed skill defects without establishing recall; kernel mutation analysis exposes checker blind spots; EDGEGEN’s human audits reveal questionable synthetic success labels (2609.06052, 2609.22220, 2609.24115).
- **Several headline claims need narrower interpretation.** Educational benchmark gains do not establish student learning, persona similarity scores do not establish human realism, and concealed-answer probes can be defeated by targeted evasion (2609.23088, 2609.22255, 2609.21996).
- **Evidence completeness varies substantially.** All 34 papers have supplied analyses, but missing tables, truncated derivations, unverified releases, and proprietary preprocessing limit some conclusions—especially independent assessment of TAPe+ML’s headline performance (2609.20869).

## Main results worth noting

- **Matched corrective training strengthens the recovery finding — 2609.24118.** On FSR-Bench, CARE raises π₀ Easy/Hard recovery success from **38.8%/15.8% to 57.2%/21.2%**, with identical corrective training, shared initializations, and **100 trials per task–regime pair**. Gains also appear for π₀-FAST and RDT-1B. The controlled comparison supports the execution mechanism; low Hard scores show its remaining boundary.

- **Memory-design ablations support WorldCrafter’s recall result — 2609.24984.** Under matched training/inference settings and generator memory-token budgets, revisit LPIPS is **0.255** for the full model, versus **0.497** for context memory, **0.305** for a frozen encoder, and **0.333** without pose-conditioned readout. This supports the tested design choices, not unlimited persistence or physically correct simulation.

- **Fixed-toolset training improves video orchestration — 2609.24997.** On VABench’s **600 prompts**, equally averaged across six normalized capability scores, Toolset 1 improves from **59.5** with a zero-shot controller to **69.2** after SFT and **75.6** after SFT+RL. This isolates training more directly than comparisons against standalone generators with fewer opportunities to retrieve or regenerate.

- **Harness transfer is useful but domain-dependent — 2609.24974.** Separately distilled Qwen3.5-9B checkpoints exceed the attached evolved harness on SpreadsheetBench pass@1 (**44.0% vs 39.0%**) and AppWorld scenario goal completion (**58.9% vs 48.2%**), but trail it on USPTO pass@1 (**30.0% vs 38.0%**). A mixed-metric macro-average should not obscure that retention gap.

- **Sparse supervision can work without reducing step time — 2609.24432.** For JustRL-Qwen3-4B → Qwen3-1.7B with thinking disabled, nominal **0.1%** TIP+IER-AND supervision exceeds full OPD on all four reported math Bayes@32 scores: **19.4/16.3/11.7/14.4% vs 14.4/12.5/8.7/13.4%** on AIME25/AIME26/HMMT25/HMMT26. In a separate **10%-retention** timing experiment, IER takes **2.152±0.270 seconds/step**, versus full OPD **2.103±0.255**, over 30 steady-state steps. Neither proportional compute savings nor universal selector superiority follows.

- **Repeated sampling supports statistical estimation gains — 2609.20758.** On PRISM’s **63 domains**, with **10% labels**, traffic-share weighting, and **100 replications**, judge-and-content PP-S achieves RMSE **1.527**, versus corresponding GREG **2.148** and HT **2.505**. Nominal 95% aggregate coverage is **0.943**, **0.949**, and **0.950**, respectively. Separately, DB-CV improves error reporting markedly but does not beat naive CV’s selected-estimator RMSE.

- **Checker acceptance misses concrete faults — 2609.22220.** Five official inputs detect **6,136/7,384 witnessed operator mutants**, leaving **16.9%** undetected. Two inputs selected using half of each problem’s mutants detect **94.8%** of the held-out half, versus **82.9%** for official inputs. This measures coverage within the constructed fault distribution, not the prevalence of bugs in submitted kernels.

- **Internal probing adds information but has an adversarial limit — 2609.21996.** On the trained Gemma-2-9B MMLU concealment setting, divergence detection improves AUC over behavioral detection by **0.073**, with **95% CI [0.048, 0.097]**. Yet anti-probe training at **λ=2** retains triggered capability **0.714** while reducing untriggered recognition to the unknown baseline. A failed readout cannot certify erasure.

- **Compact policy gains have a relatively clear within-model comparator — 2609.24682.** Cached-feature alignment raises an identical 0.8B student’s LIBERO four-suite mean from **95.3±0.7% to 97.9±0.5%**, using **500 trials per suite** across two evaluation seeds and two GPUs. The deployed architecture is unchanged. The comparison supports useful feature transfer; it does not isolate world-model-specific causal understanding.

## Open research directions

These are proposed experiments grounded in the supplied analyses, not claims that the broader literature lacks solutions.

1. **Test asynchronous result integration after intent changes — 2609.13814.** Introduce controlled backend delays, cancellations, revised requests, and out-of-order returns. Measure final-answer relevance, delivery correctness, task completion, and interruption latency separately from routing accuracy.

2. **Separate search gains from selection and compute effects — 2609.24972, 2609.24115.** Repeat harness searches with matched candidate and inference budgets, independent validation, and untouched human-curated tests. Audit synthetic assertions before testing whether stronger acceptance controls improve transfer.

3. **Measure correction and consolidation per unit of training effort — 2609.24983, 2609.24974, 2609.23377, 2609.23989.** Compare correction-based supervision, joint training, and specialist consolidation under matched annotation hours, rollout tokens, and training compute. Report each capability’s retention, including mixed-domain episodes and untouched repositories.

4. **Isolate the source of robot representation gains — 2609.23863, 2609.10706, 2609.24682.** Within each system, hold the controller and downstream demonstrations fixed while varying grounding pretraining, robotization fidelity, or teacher training history. Match extra supervision and data volume; evaluate contact errors and controlled visual shifts.

5. **Move recovery and safety beyond calibrated simulation settings — 2609.24118, 2609.13231.** Compare fixed plans with online replanning on induced structural failures, and independently audit frame-level safety labels. Extend paired evaluation to physical robots while reporting safe completion, violations, recovery, and false alarms.

6. **Evaluate long-video persistence and motion separately — 2609.24984, 2609.24788, 2609.23658, 2609.24997.** Use longer streams, multiple seeds, known trajectories, and delayed subject reappearances. Score background recall, identity, valid motion, edit fidelity, and end-to-end latency separately so static consistency cannot conceal reduced dynamics.

7. **Validate sparse and low-precision updates against realized costs — 2609.24432, 2609.22870.** Report actual token retention and full rollout–training time, including teacher scoring and BF16 shadow passes. Compare methods at matched achieved accuracy; test whether approximate IER predicts measured update noise.

8. **Strengthen semantic oracles and assess their transfer — 2609.06052, 2609.22220, 2609.24115.** Exhaustively label a small skill corpus, test kernel suites on independent optimized bugs, and independently check policy assertions. Measure recall, false acceptance, false rejection, and post-repair performance beyond the discovery cases.

9. **Connect proxy scores to intended outcomes — 2609.23088, 2609.22255, 2609.25001.** Evaluate tutoring with immediate and delayed learner assessments; calibrate persona metrics against blinded human judgments; test whether offline gameplay scores predict execution across multiple games. Preserve separate outcome definitions.

10. **Establish information-preservation and reproducibility boundaries — 2609.24220, 2609.15991, 2609.20869.** Audit source-to-Markdown facts, round-trip tokenization edge cases, and the complete TAPe preprocessing implementation. For each, include full-system storage, compute, and matched-baseline accounting before drawing broad efficiency conclusions.

## Coverage and uncertainty

**All 34 supplied current-paper analyses are covered.** The taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical connections stages were available. Six cross-day edges are retained after checking visible evidence at both endpoints; the historical candidate pool contains 50 records, all labeled `cached_analysis`. No retained edge relies on `listing_summary`.

**Collection failures:** The supplied failure log records unsuccessful Hugging Face Markdown retrieval for **22 papers**. For **2609.22870, 2609.24115, and 2609.20869**, the response had an unexpected HTML content type. For **2609.13814, 2609.24972, 2609.23088, 2609.24984, 2609.22966, 2609.23863, 2609.24997, 2609.24983, 2609.23377, 2609.24432, 2609.24118, 2609.23658, 2609.06052, 2609.24797, 2609.24682, 2609.13231, 2609.11063, 2609.20758, and 2609.22220**, Markdown did not appear to contain full paper text. These are retrieval-path failures; analyses were nevertheless supplied for every current paper.

**Partial evidence:** Missing table bodies or numerical comparison details materially affect **2609.25001, 2609.24220, 2609.23986, 2609.22255, 2609.10706, 2609.15991, 2609.21996, and 2609.23169**. Collector truncation limits theoretical assessment of **2609.24797** and **2609.11063**. The retained historical records also contain service-truncated passages; claims of complete source coverage inside those records do not restore omitted evidence.

**Reporting conflicts matter.** ShieldVLA’s supplied analysis calculates approximately **47.5%** mean relative cumulative-cost reduction from Table 2, rather than the claimed **57%**, and identifies inconsistent success/OOD definitions (2609.13231). Kernel auditing has unreconciled problem, rule, and mutant counts (2609.22220). Educational metric aggregation, ACLArena training details, and SVEET’s training schedule/theoretical steps also need clarification (2609.23088, 2609.23989, 2609.24788). This digest favors explicit, setting-specific comparisons over disputed aggregates.

**Confidence is limited most sharply for broad claims.** TAPe+ML’s proprietary preprocessing prevents independent assessment of headline results (2609.20869). Student learning, clinical-training effectiveness, formal robot safety, permanent knowledge erasure, and unlimited video persistence are not established by the reported proxies. Many papers omit independent training/search seeds, complete resource accounting, or uncertainty intervals. Reported artifact releases were not independently verified.

The synthesis uses only supplied evidence. Connections are editorial comparisons, not inferred citations or dependencies; no cross-paper numerical ranking is made across incompatible metrics.
