# HARMONY: Hierarchical Agentic Reasoning for MONocular Image-to-Scene Synthesis

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-23.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.26793) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26793)


## One-sentence takeaway
HARMONY combines hierarchical VLM object placement with geometric refinement to reconstruct indoor scenes from single images, with reported baseline improvements whose magnitudes cannot be checked because the supplied results tables lack their contents.

## Problem and motivation
Single-image reconstruction must infer hidden geometry, object scales, orientations, and support relationships from incomplete visual evidence. The authors argue that geometry-driven pipelines struggle with occlusion and small objects, while VLM-driven approaches capture semantic relationships but lack precise image alignment (§1). The goal is a compositional scene with individually reconstructed objects and faithful spatial arrangements.

## Approach and contributions
- **Hierarchical reconstruction:** Establish room geometry and camera pose, then place wall-mounted objects, furniture/ceiling objects, and decorations. Furniture follows a back-to-front order; VLM checks correct orientations, support assignments, missing objects, and matched groups (§§3.1–3.3).
- **Semantic and geometric integration:** VLM reasoning determines relationships and approximate poses; silhouette alignment and VGGT point clouds refine position and scale. Collision handling and stage-level feedback repair errors (§3.3). The contribution is pipeline integration: no foundation model is trained or fine-tuned (Appendix B.1).
- **Reusable scene collection:** The authors report releasing HARMONY300 with reference images, generated scenes, renders, cameras, segmentation, and recovered spatial relations (Appendix D). Material and lighting estimation supports relit output (§3.4).

The available prose supports reported advantages under the authors’ evaluation setup, but missing table contents prevent assessing improvement sizes, variance, or detailed baseline ordering. GPT-6 Astra comparisons are presented as qualitative evidence, with insufficient configuration details for a reproducible comparison (abstract; §4.2). Generated real-scene reconstructions do not provide independent geometric ground truth.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated failures:** Rare-object vocabulary mismatches disrupt detection; severe foreground clipping leaves geometry poorly constrained (§4.5). The section announces three failure cases but describes only two.
- **Analyst inference—scale and layout:** Metric scale depends on room height, initially estimated by the VLM, despite later being described as “known.” Manhattan-frame calibration may constrain applicability to irregular rooms; multi-room generalization is illustrated rather than quantified (§3.1; Appendix F.4).
- **Analyst inference—comparison scope:** CAST uses an unofficial implementation; VIGA and 3D-ReGen use modified components. SAM3D receives HARMONY masks, while CAST and SAM3D receive HARMONY backgrounds for perceptual evaluation (§4.1; Appendix B.6). Results therefore concern these specific configurations.
- **Analyst inference—selection and validation:** The user study excludes scenes without successful renders from every method. Input-view similarity and scripted robot demonstrations do not establish unseen-view accuracy or control-policy performance (Appendices E, F.6).
- **Evidence limitation:** HARMONY300’s real-scene geometry and relations are pipeline outputs. Its broad coverage should not be interpreted as independently verified reconstruction accuracy (Appendix D).

## Open research questions
- **How sensitive is metric reconstruction to room-height and Manhattan assumptions?** No sensitivity results are supplied. Perturb height priors and test non-orthogonal rooms against measured geometry.
- **How much improvement comes from orchestration versus stronger component models?** Reported ablations lack numerical contents, and baseline components differ. Run matched-component comparisons varying placement order, feedback, and geometric refinement.
- **Can additional views resolve clipped and hidden objects?** Clipping remains a stated failure, and multi-view input is future work (§5). Compare single-view and additional-view reconstruction using held-out views and geometric ground truth.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The pipeline and evaluation settings are described substantially, but quantitative superiority is only partly auditable.

Missing information includes Tables 1–4’s contents, numerical ablation effects, user-study standard deviation and precise percentage aggregation, exact reasoning-model configuration, GPT-6 Astra setup, failure-rate denominators, and independent real-scene geometry validation. Code and dataset release are asserted in the text but not verified here.
