# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-09-16

## Executive summary

- **Persistent improvement needs independent evaluation.** ScienceBuddy (`2609.17523`) reports biomedical gains across three model–harness update cycles; ModularRSI (`2609.14857`) demonstrates frozen-harness transfer to separate benchmarks. Neither establishes that the improvement mechanism itself becomes better, the distinction emphasized by `2609.13406` and `2609.11873`.
- **Memorization and capability retention remain separate outcomes.** Composed continual-learning mechanisms raise mean final retention from 1.2% to 34.9% after 100 QA tasks (`2609.06986`), but evaluation repeats training queries and broad capabilities deteriorate. Layer-selective adaptation and specialist distillation examine more favorable, setting-dependent specialization–retention trade-offs (`2609.13680`, `2609.13770`).
- **Runtime coordination produces useful but bounded gains.** HarnessVLN improves simulated navigation success (`2609.15195`); Gavel improves skill selection and triggering without establishing downstream completion gains (`2609.15982`); StepAudio 3 Realtime combines strong duplex control with uneven customer-service success (`2609.14005`).
- **Structured motion and geometry help dynamic generation.** ModAR’s controlled ablations support using tracks, semantic features, and depth before robot actions (`2609.17524`). PhysStream improves trajectory alignment in tabletop video generation, while remaining non-realtime and susceptible to accumulated drift (`2609.17521`).
- **Compact representations offer task-dependent trade-offs.** FLAT retains similar adapted retrieval performance across large prefix-length changes (`2609.16591`). Continuous registers carry useful reasoning state across cleared diffusion chunks, but full-context decoding remains more accurate in the reported 1,024-token comparison (`2609.16372`).
- **Strong aggregate scores do not establish the intended mechanism.** StepAudio Music’s quality evaluations leave note-level plan adherence unresolved (`2609.16034`); Mind2Dialogue’s personalization gains do not validate real-user mental-state recovery (`2609.15972`); LimiX-2’s skeleton recovery does not establish causal intervention ability (`2609.17488`).
- **Verification remains a substantive research problem.** ImpossibleRubrics exposes rewardable evidence violations (`2609.16816`), and Emergence World records action and memory contamination despite warnings and corrections (`2609.17320`). DDO sharply reduces standard refusal-ablation success but weakens under adaptive attacks (`2609.16204`).
- **The collection supports scoped conclusions rather than universal rankings.** Cross-modal ICL depends on elicitation and encoding (`2609.14011`), relation prediction on annotation matching (`2609.12552`), and directional interventions on their reference and insertion point (`2609.15975`). The games survey and architecture-elicitation study likewise caution against treating plausible outputs as validated capabilities (`2609.16679`, `2609.14803`).

## Main results worth noting

The findings below are selected for evidential clarity and informative controls, not ranked across incomparable metrics.

- **Composition improves recall but does not solve forgetting — `2609.06986`.** Across three 100-task streams, the full anchor stack with merged LoRA achieves **34.9% mean final retention versus 1.2%** for naive sequential fine-tuning. Three-seed factorial experiments report positive replay–merge interactions of **3.6, 9.4, and 11.7 percentage points** across the datasets. Evaluation repeats training queries, and the separate general-capability checks show substantial degradation.

- **Layer selection changes the specialization–retention balance — `2609.13680`.** With QA-only Qwen3-8B chemistry training, `b4t16` increases **SmolInstruct 19.34→29.61** and **General Avg. 42.22→44.03** at **KL 0.051**. Full fine-tuning reaches a much higher chemistry score, **59.65**, but lowers General Avg. to **33.60**. This is a trade-off, not dominance on both objectives. In `2609.13770`, controlled student training further shows that specialist choices propagate into student capability profiles.

- **Held-out harness gains have useful controls, but incomplete attribution — `2609.14857`.** On TerminalBench 2.0 with DeepSeek-V4-Flash-Preview, ModularRSI achieves **52.43 Acc**, versus **47.57** initially, **46.44** after non-modular evolution, and **44.19** after joint all-module evolution. The harness is evolved on separate tasks and frozen for evaluation. Independent evolution-run uncertainty and a contrastive-analysis-only ablation are missing.

- **ScienceBuddy’s component results must remain separate — `2609.17523`.** Three cycles raise held-out **single-attempt accuracy 42.2%→73.3%**. Separate fixed-weight harness adaptation raises **first-response accuracy 31.1%→51.1%**; separate fixed-harness RL raises **pass@4 48.3%→67.8%**. These results support contributions from both components, but cannot quantify an added benefit from coupling because settings and metrics differ.

- **Modality ablations support a specific control design — `2609.17524`.** At **250 demonstrations per task**, including **50 action-labeled**, six-task simulated success is **75%** for full ModAR. Removing tracks yields **61%**, DINO features **65%**, depth **70%**, and RGB **75%**. Removing context noise yields **63%**, and reversing modality order **65%**. Evaluation uses **50 trials per task**; training-seed variability is unavailable.

- **Registers convey useful state across chunk resets — `2609.16372`.** Four continuous registers outperform four-token discrete carry in **all 12 main benchmark rows** across two backbones. However, full-sequence SFT leads all eight main math rows: LLaDA GSM8K is **57.4% versus 49.1%** for registers at **8×128-token chunks**. The reported longer-budget speedups use a separate historical protocol with early stopping disabled and must not be combined with corrected main-table accuracy as a single operating point.

- **The ICL study controls demonstration pairing — `2609.14011`.** For NextTerm-440M at **64 shots**, exact-match accuracy is **48.1% clean versus 16.7% deranged**, a **31.4-point gap**, with **95% CI [25.6, 37.6]**. Each condition uses **100 tasks and 128 trials per task**. All six primary modalities show Holm-adjusted significance at their respective maximum shot counts; differences in encoding and context budgets preclude intrinsic modality rankings.

- **Rubric exploitability depends on the measurement chain — `2609.16816`.** Full-150 exploit rates are **8–26%** across 11 generators under fixed attacker, judge, and Oracle models. With **45 rubrics, attacks, and judge scores held fixed**, changing only the Oracle produces **33.3%, 75.6%, and 66.7%** exploit rates. Certificate-faithful rubrics show **0/45 observed exploits** in one tested condition, but share their specification with the certificate checker.

- **Standard-attack defense differs from adaptive robustness — `2609.16204`.** On Llama-3-8B-Instruct, DDO reduces mean standard-RFA ASR from **85.1% to 1.8%**, averaging four attack variants across JailbreakBench/HarmBench and three judges. Pre-attack MMLU changes **68.1→66.8** and MT-Bench **7.89→7.38**. A separate rank-8, eight-phase adaptive test reaches **65% worst-case ASR**; the two attack settings are not interchangeable.

- **Aggregate tabular performance has subgroup and mechanism limits — `2609.17488`.** On the **288-dataset TALENT subset**, evaluated across **15 seeds** using classification accuracy and regression RMSE, LimiX-2 obtains **Elo 1506 versus TabFM 1471**, with a **57% pairwise task win rate**. Leading Elo intervals overlap. Strong six-dataset skeleton recovery remains an undirected adjacency result, not evidence of intervention or counterfactual competence.

## Open research directions

These are editorial experiment proposals grounded in explicit limitations of the supplied papers.

1. **Measure persistent improvement under a common budget.** Motivated by `2609.13406`, `2609.11873`, `2609.17523`, `2609.14857`, and `2609.16057`: compare frozen-improver, revisable-improver, harness-only, weight-only, and alternating conditions over multiple rounds. Use protected evaluation tasks, validation-only checkpoint selection, and total compute accounting. The discriminating outcome is successor quality per budget, alongside retained regressions.

2. **Jointly test memorization, generalization, and retained reasoning.** Motivated by `2609.06986`, `2609.13680`, and `2609.13770`: extend sequential adaptation with held-out paraphrases and a fixed general-capability panel. Compare replay, layer selection, and reference anchoring at measured drift levels; independently verify sampled rationales to distinguish answer correctness from faithful supervision.

3. **Connect routing and verification to final completion.** Motivated by `2609.14005`, `2609.15195`, and `2609.15982`: use executable tasks with delayed tools, revised requests, stale evidence, and failed actions. Separately substitute oracle routing, verified observations, and alternative stopping gates, reporting final-state correctness, false calls, recovery, and latency.

4. **Test structured physical state under distribution shift.** Motivated by `2609.17524` and `2609.17521`: hold out objects, scenes, cameras, and task configurations; inject controlled depth errors, tracking swaps, and delayed memory. Measure robot success or prescribed-motion error together with drift and latency, using separate validation for checkpoint selection.

5. **Determine what bounded representations retain.** Motivated by `2609.16372`: match continuous and discrete carry by information budget and masking recipe, then test unseen horizon lengths. For `2609.16591`, sweep prefix length with fixed training and decoding while measuring fine-detail generation and both zero-shot and adapted retrieval. These are distinct experiments addressing each paper’s capacity claims.

6. **Validate intended musical and personal controls directly.** For `2609.16034`, compare generated audio with ABC notes, chords, timing, and section boundaries under paired seeds and blinded listening. For `2609.15972`, train matched students with and without privileged teacher state, then test ambiguous visible histories and longitudinal user corrections. Aggregate quality or personalization scores alone do not resolve either mechanism.

7. **Audit evidence fidelity independently of the system’s own criteria.** Motivated by `2609.16816`, `2609.17320`, and `2609.14803`: independently adjudicate rubric violations, replicate agent worlds with provenance and quarantine interventions, and test reader provenance judgments under randomized architecture formatting. Measure reward errors, delayed contamination, and misplaced trust separately.

8. **Stress-test directional claims beyond their favored intervention.** Motivated by `2609.15975` and `2609.16204`: specify projection reference and head aggregation, then repeat interventions across model size, context length, and attack rank. Test whether perpendicular compression error predicts held-out behavioral damage beyond total error, and whether decoy defenses retain utility under sustained adaptive re-estimation.

9. **Separate contextual inference from encoding and annotation advantages.** Motivated by `2609.14011`, `2609.17488`, and `2609.12552`: vary encodings and shuffled controls for ICL, isolate architecture and generator changes in tabular pretraining, and hold out predicate families plus synonyms in relation prediction. Prespecified scoring and independent matching should distinguish transferable inference from protocol sensitivity.

10. **Validate game artifacts where they are consumed.** Motivated by `2609.16679`: evaluate generated worlds, designs, implementations, and test verdicts against executable mechanics, reachable states, independently verified defects, and held-out players. A discriminating study should determine whether an improved upstream proxy changes downstream functionality or player outcomes.

## Coverage and uncertainty

**All 26 supplied current papers were analyzed and included.** Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical connections were available. Their assignments and edges were treated as proposals and checked against endpoint evidence. Eight historical edges are retained; omitted candidates are not treated as disproven connections.

**Seventeen Hugging Face Markdown collection attempts failed or returned inadequate content**, although an analysis was supplied for every affected paper:

- Unexpected HTML content: `2609.16679`, `2609.17523`, `2609.16591`, `2609.11873`, `2609.15972`, `2609.15975`.
- Response did not appear to contain full paper text: `2609.14005`, `2609.14803`, `2609.14857`, `2609.17524`, `2609.16372`, `2609.14011`, `2609.12552`, `2609.16204`, `2609.13680`, `2609.15982`, `2609.17488`.

These are collection-route failures, not evidence that the corresponding paper analyses are wholly unavailable. The supplied record does not establish how each alternative analysis was obtained.

**Explicit source truncation affects** `2609.06986`, `2609.16679`, `2609.11873`, `2609.12552`, `2609.17320`, and `2609.13770`. The games survey is especially incomplete. Additional partial inputs include missing key equations in `2609.13406`; incomplete table bodies or numerical details in `2609.15195`, `2609.17521`, `2609.16057`, and `2609.16816`; and unresolved evaluation or reporting details noted in individual rows. The historical evidence excerpts are also service-truncated, so retained edges rely only on visible endpoint material.

Most empirical papers lack uncertainty across independent training or evolution runs. Particularly low-confidence conclusions include causal explanations for architecture-narrative convergence, general rankings of agent worlds, general physical or psychological fidelity, and universal recursive-improvement claims. Proposed cross-paper transfers remain editorial hypotheses.

No outside facts, web material, or independently verified artifact-release claims were added.
