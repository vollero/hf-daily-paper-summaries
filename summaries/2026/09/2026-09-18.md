# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-09-18

## Executive summary

- **Efficiency gains need distinct accounting.** DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash reports smaller KV caches (2609.19969); SoL-Pi reduces cache-inclusive token traffic with lower aggregate task scores (2609.20519); candidate scheduling changes measured GPU energy substantially at fixed sample count (2609.19499). These measure different resources.
- **Coding harness choices remain conditional.** Recoverable history reduction connects SoL-Pi with a controlled harness study, but planning, recall, and tool-interface benefits vary by model, workload, and context budget (2609.20519, 2609.20804).
- **Distillation gains require careful controls.** EOS alignment reduces stopping failures without preventing all late collapse; privileged-reference benefits depend on checkpoint and inference mode; adaptive teacher retirement improves the tested agent-training results (2609.20511, 2609.20612, 2609.20784).
- **Single-attempt reliability and repeated-sampling coverage can diverge.** At 8B, observation-supervised initialization improves Terminal-Bench pass@16 after GRPO while lowering pass@1 relative to action-only supervision (2609.20715).
- **Reusable artifacts provide another adaptation route.** GUI skill revision and search-index revision improve reported outcomes without updating executor or reader weights, although their validation procedures and evidence quality differ (2609.17653, 2609.19656).
- **Visual quality does not establish task fulfillment.** Video DeltaNet accelerates H3 denoising with some fidelity losses; a separate H3 evaluation reports limited success on implicit multimodal tasks; UFO tests combined image–text requirements against human rankings (2609.20744, 2609.18323, 2609.12397).
- **Perception must be evaluated through its consequences.** Document parsing, region selection, active robot perception, and articulation modeling report useful gains under specific protocols, with unresolved component attribution or real-world transfer (2609.20423, 2609.19745, 2609.19554, 2609.20817).
- **Evaluation boundaries shape conclusions.** User framing, upstream input transformations, synthetic environments, and evaluator calibration materially affect assistant assessments (2609.17496, 2609.18605, 2609.16900, 2609.19879). Separately, JEPA-Anything’s cross-domain breadth does not establish cross-domain transfer, and Srijika’s structural font checks do not establish independent perceptual quality (2609.20800, 2609.05661).

## Main results worth noting

The findings below are selected for their evidential clarity and diagnostic value, not ranked across incompatible metrics.

- **Cache reduction is clearly specified; deployment savings are not.** In **2609.19969**, global KV storage is **890 bytes/token**, approximately one-quarter of DeepSeek-V4-Flash at equal sequence length. Persistent KV is approximately one-eighth under identical workloads. These figures exclude other memory requirements and do not establish proportional latency or monetary savings.

- **Harness efficiency includes capability trade-offs.** In **2609.20519**, the complete SoL-Pi stack lowers Opus 5 EdgeBench cost from **$1,741 to $1,158**, while average score falls from **44.756 to 42.224**, without further backend-specific search. On **63 CPU-only Terminal-Bench 4 tasks**, it solves **15 versus Pi’s 18**, at **$211.12 versus $286.45**. Reported costs use the paper’s fixed API prices.

- **Recoverable storage does not consistently improve coding accuracy.** In **2609.20804**, T2 versus T1 wins **15**, loses **14**, and ties **3** of 32 matched comparisons, with an equal-weight mean success difference of **−0.36 percentage points**. Half-plus of the recall-enabled context settings—**36/64**—never invoke recall. This supports a conditional reading of memory-tool usefulness.

- **Reference benefits can change sign with inference mode.** In **2609.20612**, three-seed SmolLM3 step-50 Full Trace training exceeds reference-free training by **2.00 Avg@4 points [0.82, 3.17]** with thinking-enabled evaluation, but falls below it by **10.63 points [−13.19, −8.07]** under direct-response evaluation. These are 95% problem-cluster bootstrap intervals conditioned on the observed seeds.

- **Teacher retirement improves the tested continuation.** In **2609.20784**, 3B ALFWorld success rises from **76.6% at retirement to 93.8%** after continuing GRPO; continuous joint training plateaus and OPD-only continuation does not improve. This supports removing supervision at that checkpoint, without establishing globally optimal timing or compute efficiency.

- **Observation supervision changes which success metric improves.** In **2609.20715**, Qwen3-8B on **89 Terminal-Bench 2.0 tasks with 16 attempts each** reaches pass@16 **27.0±1.3%**, versus ActionSFT’s **23.6±1.2%**, but pass@1 falls to **11.0±0.5%** from **12.3±0.5%**. The uncertainty is one bootstrap standard error over attempts.

- **Batch structure strongly affects measured energy.** In **2609.19499**, eight serial calls versus one eight-candidate batch on GSM8K/A100 yield gross GPU-energy ratios of **4.64 [4.48, 4.79]** for Phi-3 and **4.86 [4.71, 5.02]** for Qwen, using **100 prompts over three repetitions** and 95% intervals. Logical token volume varies by only **0.8%/1.0%**, respectively. Schedule-specific accuracy equivalence is not established.

- **Video speedups require matching the denominator.** In **2609.20744**, unchanged **50-NFE**, single-B200 denoising drops from **799.6 to 307.9 seconds** for a **14.3-second, 768p** video. The eight-step, eight-B200 configuration takes **6.70 seconds**; its reported **14.5×** comparison uses dense H3 on the same GPU count. The larger **119.3×** figure instead uses single-GPU dense H3. On the 103-prompt quality suite, eight-step VDN has slightly worse endpoint fidelity and World Coherence than 50-step dense H3.

- **Index validation has a substantial within-study effect.** In **2609.19656**, full Self-Index versus removal of validation yields BRIGHT nDCG@10 **20.4 vs 11.4** for BM25, **21.8 vs 12.7** for BGE, and **26.1 vs 17.8** for Qwen3 embeddings, on the 0–100 scale under matched optimization-query budgets. This supports the validation package, not error-free individual checks.

- **Execution exposes limits hidden by partial competence.** In **2609.19554**, Qwen3.8-max receives **100.0% target-localization** labels in one 280-episode annotated run, while three-run task-macro terminal success is **53.93±3.17%**, with sample SD across runs. All four tested five-object composition conditions achieve **0% strict success over 20 episodes each**, despite partial placements. The diagnostic and terminal metrics have different aggregation settings.

- **Artifact validity and perceptual improvement separate in font generation.** In **2609.05661**, all **66 fonts** pass sanitizer checks, yet template copying beats generation’s SSIM on **50/56 held-out faces**. Median internal style-gap closure across **23 held-out families** is **0.047**, versus **0.006** for reference-only geometry. The style encoder saw evaluated families, and independent perceptual validation is absent.

## Open research directions

These are proposed experiments grounded in unresolved questions in the supplied analyses.

1. **Measure complete efficiency under controlled quality targets — 2609.19969, 2609.20519, 2609.19671, 2609.19499.** Run matched workloads with fixed success requirements while separately varying cache compression, history compaction, reasoning length, and candidate batching. Report total memory, latency, GPU energy, and cost, including compaction or reference-precomputation overhead where applicable.

2. **Separate stopping semantics, privileged information, and supervision duration — 2609.20511, 2609.20612, 2609.20784.** Within each compatible training setup, control EOS alignment, reference availability, rollout horizon, and teacher-removal schedule. Compare adaptive retirement against tuned fixed switches across seeds; track termination, accuracy, and delayed collapse.

3. **Test whether consequence prediction causes broader exploration — 2609.20715.** Apply the same GRPO budget to checkpoints trained with paired observations, shuffled observations, unrelated logs, and wrapper-only targets. Evaluate both pass@1 and pass@k across training seeds and choose observation-loss weights on separate validation tasks.

4. **Make persistent-artifact updates accountable for regressions — 2609.17653, 2609.19656, 2609.20519.** Compare immediate acceptance with held-out execution or retrieval checks and rollback under equal revision budgets. Audit GUI feedback for oracle exposure, and test old-task retention after new skills, index changes, or harness edits.

5. **Separate multimodal understanding from visual realization — 2609.18323, 2609.12397, 2609.20744.** Score an explicit event or constraint prediction before judging its generated realization; remove or swap conditioning evidence. If checkpoint and input compatibility can be established, compare dense H3 and VDN at matched sampling budgets on compatible implicit tasks.

6. **Isolate what improves fine-grained document reading — 2609.20423, 2609.19745.** Hold refinement pools and supervised-token budgets fixed while varying residual allocation. Separately vary global-image and selected-region token budgets, including degraded documents and chart questions where local crops may discard necessary structure.

7. **Validate assistant judgments and pipeline losses independently — 2609.17496, 2609.18605, 2609.16900, 2609.19879.** Use blinded human audits of responses and evidence, freeze judge prompts before held-out evaluation, and repeat trials. Test actual bounded routing recovery for RiskChainBench, category-specific ASR corrections for VākQA, and human rather than simulated retellings for Fuse.

8. **Identify the cause of embodied gains under realistic observation errors — 2609.19554, 2609.20817.** For VABench, match image budgets while varying active viewpoint choice and camera feasibility. For FAMOS, vary articulation span independently of viewpoint coverage and compare reconstructed with reference point clouds on quantitatively annotated real captures.

9. **Determine which predictive factors improve downstream decisions — 2609.20800.** Use capacity- and compute-matched ablations of factorization, orthogonality, and activity losses. Measure action-sequence ranking alongside rollout error in environments where planning improves and where it does not; separately test transfer between related systems.

10. **Validate usable font quality beyond internal scores — 2609.05661.** Exclude benchmark families from style-encoder training, compare against independent embeddings and blinded reader judgments, and expand shaping inventories to cover currently unaudited feature-context variants. Report fallback rates, attachment errors, collisions, and within-font consistency together.

## Coverage and uncertainty

- **Coverage:** All **24 current papers** were analyzed and included in the overview and Sources. The supplied historical pool contains **50 candidates**; this digest retains **six cross-day edges involving six historical papers** after checking visible evidence at both endpoints.
- **Downstream stages:** Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical connections were all available. Their assignments and edges were treated as editorial aids, not independent evidence.
- **Collection failures:** Hugging Face Markdown retrieval failed or did not supply full paper text for **20 papers**: 2609.19969, 2609.20511, 2609.20519, 2609.20804, 2609.20800, 2609.16900, 2609.20784, 2609.17496, 2609.17653, 2609.20423, 2609.20744, 2609.18323, 2609.19554, 2609.20817, 2609.19671, 2609.20612, 2609.12397, 2609.20715, 2609.19745, and 2609.05661. All nevertheless have supplied analysis blocks. These failures do not establish absence of the papers or their underlying evidence.
- **Partial inputs:** Historical analyses are service-truncated; retained edges use visible passages only. All historical records are labeled `cached_analysis`, with none labeled `listing_summary`. Current analyses also report missing figures, supplements, prompts, or table bodies—particularly the scheduling study, PACT, and VākQA. Unavailable values were not reconstructed.
- **Reporting conflicts:** Precise claims are weakened by unresolved GUI oracle/round/reuse reporting, RetireOPD ablation differences, Vision-RL²’s OCRBench prose–table discrepancy, small ActObs difference-table inconsistencies, and UFO’s ambiguous percentage notation.
- **Confidence boundaries:** Many comparisons lack training-seed replication, complete cost accounting, independent judge validation, or matched baseline configurations. Causal explanations, operational transfer, artifact availability, and close leaderboard rankings therefore receive less confidence than clearly specified within-study results.
- **Scope:** No outside facts or web browsing were used. Historical candidate selection is not documented, so the cross-day map is bounded rather than exhaustive. Proposed experiments are editorial inference, not demonstrated combinations or established dependencies.
