- Student-generated states are a recurring training substrate. Teacher continuations, token-level feedback, replay, and richer-context self-teachers offer distinct ways to supervise them; the supplied evidence does not establish one universally preferable recipe.

- Several findings are explicitly bounded: encoder-free parity is extrapolated (**2609.35457**), correction exams overlap training (**2609.16145**), and scientific-creativity improvements concern automated proxies (**2609.35706**).

## Main results worth noting

These highlights represent well-specified findings in the retained text, rather than a ranking across incompatible metrics.

- **2609.29233:** With **5,664 task-unrelated single-word teacher responses**, HumanEval+ **pass@1 improves by 5.34 percentage points over an exact nuisance-matched control** in the strongest shared-ancestor setting. Broader transfer remains unreliable.
- **2609.28236:** On **2,554 simulated episodes**, structured EMem raises GPT-5.4-mini’s **equally weighted task-family success from 35.3% to 58.9%**. This is simulation evidence, with ingestion/retrieval costs and structure-only attribution unresolved.
- **2609.33378:** On held-out **SimplerEnv Bridge instances**, recursively revised intervention guidance raises light-agent **success from 43.8% to 66.7%**, with agent and VLA parameters unchanged. Playbook-development costs and independent replications are unavailable.
- **2609.32353 / 2609.34044:** LT-OPD raises Qwen3.5-4B’s **average retained benchmark performance from 68.6% to 82.3% at 5% visual-token retention**. SCOPD+ raises its reported **normalized performance from 86.37% to 92.43% across 13 image benchmarks at 10% retention**. These aggregates do not support direct method ranking.
- **2609.32712 / 2609.33586:** MassAlloc reports **23.1% fewer total training FLOPs at 14B and 32K context** versus dense computation with comparable reported quality. Rowmax-H15 reports **12.4% faster B200 FP8 attention-forward calls at causal 8K**; separate **BF16, 2K** fidelity tests show **0.091–0.492% perplexity increases across five models**. Neither establishes the corresponding whole-model wall-clock gain.
- **2609.23033:** WaveFront reports **2.42× and 3.54× end-to-end decoding speedups over autoregressive decoding** for **Ouro-2.6B and Huginn-3.5B**, respectively, across six Spec-Bench categories. Approximate KV sharing requires separate quality assessment.
- **2609.35748:** In the **1.7B post-training setting**, TaH2 reports a **53% steeper AIME accuracy–decoding-compute slope** and **3.4 percentage points higher accuracy at matched decoding compute** than its non-looped baseline. Matched training compute and repeated-seed uncertainty are missing.
- **2609.34754:** For **Qwen3-8B→Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct on MMLU-Redux**, Draft-KV reports **78.04% accuracy with matched messages**, versus **37.45% receiver-only** and **36.40% with reassigned messages**. The reassignment control supports question-specific information use, while robustness to plausible false drafts remains untested.
- **2609.33855:** VQS reports **94.4% retained-answer correctness**, versus **76.4% for majority voting** and **82.2% for model judging** in its human evaluation. Average benchmark gains are **3.18, 2.32, and 2.43 points** for Qwen3-VL **2B, 4B, and 8B**. Program computation still depends on verified visual parses.
- **2609.35504:** Explicit transition planning raises GPT-Image-2’s **SolveScore from 57.0% to 71.6% with one final generation** on SolveEdit. SolveScore combines required completion and collateral damage; planner costs and evaluator validation remain incomplete.
- **2609.35279:** A held-out probe-gated freeze **prevents 29 collapses but loses 108 corrections across 6,525 homogeneous-panel debates**. Under equal weights, preventing some harmful transitions does not compensate for the lost useful revisions.
- **2609.33645:** Exact vocabulary reduction preserves **full-vocabulary CTC loss and first-order gradients in exact arithmetic**. The finite-beam implementation introduces a separate approximation, so the theorem should not be read as blanket implementation equivalence.

## Open research directions

The following experiments are editorial proposals grounded in stated limitations, not claims that the collection establishes universal research gaps.

1. **Separate feedback direction, scale, and correction trajectory.** For **2609.35347, 2609.33791, 2609.35505, and 2609.36246**, run a shared-student, shared-teacher comparison with matched fresh-rollout, teacher-token, update, and total-compute budgets. Report accuracy, forgetting, sampled coverage, and sensitivity to erroneous teachers.

2. **Measure visual adaptation at equal deployment cost.** For **2609.32353, 2609.34044, and 2609.34972**, align pruning budgets, image resolutions, training exposure, and free-generation settings. Measure task accuracy, actual token counts, prefill/decode latency, and peak memory; compare sparse evidence against retaining all tokens with reconstructed states.

3. **Validate efficiency in the configuration used for quality testing.** For **2609.32712, 2609.32704, 2609.33586, 2609.23033, 2609.34054, and 2609.34060**, benchmark complete models with identical precision, context, concurrency, and hardware. Include selection/correction overhead and distributions of latency, rather than operator-only means.

4. **Test whether local credit identifies causal decisions.** For **2609.33781, 2609.34848, 2609.33391, and 2609.32577**, independently annotate decisions and perform controlled substitutions or continuations. Check whether assigned credit predicts outcome changes, including judge errors, cap/fallback events, and incorrectly matched sibling states.

5. **Audit persistent scaffold revision under concealed tests.** For **2609.32862, 2609.35432, 2609.33378, and 2609.32965**, repeat improvement rounds with fixed regression suites and unseen external evaluators. Measure new-task transfer, rollback frequency, human involvement, and complete development cost.

6. **Stress predictive memory with disturbance and corrupted geometry.** For **2609.30247, 2609.33148, 2609.34722, 2609.35734, and 2609.33253**, vary observation disturbances, occlusion, camera/depth error, and history length. Report failure recovery, stale-context duration, scene consistency, control success, and end-to-end latency.

7. **Evaluate edit stopping alongside preservation.** For **2609.35504, 2609.35767, 2609.35611, 2609.34470, and 2609.35134**, use independently authored required/protected conditions and repeated sessions. Measure unnecessary edits, newly introduced defects, long-turn drift, and quantitative physical counterfactual accuracy on recorded intervention pairs.

8. **Connect behavior tests to executed outcomes.** For **2609.33295, 2609.34241, 2609.32400, and 2609.34771**, compare recorded-turn detection with live guarded execution under held-out policies and defenses. Measure prevented violations, false blocks, task completion, and monitoring cost; isolate SafePO with controlled stage ablations.

9. **Test validity beyond automated or restricted endpoints.** For **2609.35706, 2609.33999, 2609.16145, 2609.23270, 2609.36218, and 2609.30074**, add blinded expert assessment, generated-speech listening tests, genuinely held-out correction tasks, functional code tests, film-knowledge controls, and repeated extraction panels. These checks discriminate proxy improvements from the intended capability.

10. **Establish operational transfer in specialized domains.** For **2605.12435, 2609.31669, 2609.33487, 2609.34479, and 2609.33312**, test prospective wildfire ranking, calibrated streaming forecasts, new EEG corpora/fine-tuning, clinical subgroup and paraphrase robustness, and realistic auction ledgers. Preserve the original budget or accounting constraints when evaluating transfer.
