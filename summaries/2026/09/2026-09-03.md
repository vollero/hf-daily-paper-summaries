# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-09-03

## Executive summary

- Agent self-improvement was a major theme, but the evidence is sober: HarnessDev (`2609.01437`), Aspire (`2608.31111`), and S3Gym (`2608.31100`) all report noisy feedback, weak transfer, or negative transfer alongside localized gains.
- WHALE (`2609.00196`) provides stronger controlled evidence for jointly optimizing model weights and executable harnesses, outperforming weight-only, harness-only, and prompt–weight baselines on three verifier-backed tasks; proposer compute and test-based selection remain important caveats.
- Repo-To-Skill (`2609.02749`) reports large gains from reusable operational knowledge, including MLE-bench Any-Medal improving from \(31.11\%\pm2.22\) to \(72.89\%\pm1.18\), but skill-construction cost was outside the matched runtime budget.
- Long-context efficiency work targets complementary phases: CRISP (`2609.01925`) sparsifies prefilling and reaches up to 5.30× attention-only speedup at 512K tokens, while Declarative Attention (`2609.02737`) reduces decode-time attended tokens by 31.1%–52.0% with modest aggregate accuracy losses.
- Retrieval papers increasingly diagnose what ordinary similarity misses: entity identity (`2608.21450`), implicit perspective coverage (`2608.30949`), multimodal corruption (`2608.29607`), and execution-correct code (`2609.01865`).
- Several compact representations show promising trade-offs without universal downstream wins: ZipTok3D (`2609.01740`) reconstructs complete shapes from very short prefixes, NeoMME (`2609.01657`) compresses late-interaction document embeddings substantially, and PIXELLINGUISTII (`2609.01147`) retains useful document representations under token reduction.
- SolarWM (`2609.02886`) contributes a 1.43M-clip video corpus and selected hour-scale rollouts, but its model-quality evidence is qualitative. Kirin (`2609.01823`) offers quantitative animal-motion results, with better alignment and fidelity than its tested AniMo baseline but reduced diversity.
- Process-localized post-training is represented by Cliff (`2609.02817`), IDA-OPD (`2608.29846`), and small rubric judges (`2608.30005`); each improves its tested setting, but their objectives, metrics, and supervision costs are not directly comparable.
- A competitive-programming system (`2609.02849`) reports an unofficial IOI 2026 score of 535.4/600, above the reported 361.12 gold threshold and 498.27 top-human score, but used up to 760 GB300 GPUs and was not an official ranked entry.
- Two financial papers (`2608.29692`, `2608.29669`) use Wasserstein geometry over firm-level news-embedding distributions for different purposes—portfolio-risk certification and directed peer-field construction—while explicitly limiting causal and out-of-sample interpretations.

## Main results worth noting

- Repo-To-Skill (`2609.02749`) reports MLE-bench Any-Medal increasing from \(31.11\%\pm2.22\) to \(72.89\%\pm1.18\) across 75 competitions and three runs with the same GPT-5.5 Codex backbone, harness, and downstream runtime budget. Offline skill construction was separate and excluded.

- WHALE (`2609.00196`) reaches 48.34% SearchQA, 24.79% Math, and 29.83% chess best test mean@8 under its main schedule, versus weight-only results of 38.27%, 15.42%, and 22.17%. The comparison matches target-agent update budgets but excludes external proposer compute and uses test performance for best-pair selection.

- EarlyEval (`2609.02783`) reduces SWE-bench Verified steps by 26.0%, input tokens by 32.7%, and output tokens by 28.7% at threshold 0.95, while halted-run accuracy is 95.0% and mean absolute Pass@1 deviation is 1.1 points. Across its reported settings, Spearman rank correlations with full evaluation range from 0.959 to 0.994.

- Declarative Attention (`2609.02737`) reduces macro-average attended tokens per response from 13.43M to 6.45M for Gemma-4-31B and from 22.54M to 15.52M for Qwen-3.6-27B across 15 tasks. Accuracy falls by 1.27 and 2.75 percentage points, respectively; projected decode-time reductions are not measured wall-clock results.

- CRISP (`2609.01925`) records 34,111 ms attention-only latency at 512K tokens on one H100 with \(\alpha=1.25\), a 5.30× speedup over dense attention; at 8K, its routing overhead makes it slower than dense attention. At \(\alpha=1.0\), its aggregate InfiniteBench results are 48.7 for Llama and 28.7 for Qwen, compared with dense-attention scores of 48.6 and 24.0 under the paper’s 131K truncation setting.

- ExecRetrieval (`2609.01865`) shows a sharp top-\(k\)/rank-1 gap: Gemini Embedding 2 has exec@1 0.331 with 95% CI [0.299, 0.362], exec@5 0.997, and exec@10 1.000 over 939 Python queries. Among its rank-1 misses, 624/628 are the planted paired mutant.

- SnapBench (`2608.29607`) finds that mean R@1 across 16 models falls from 63.2% clean to 57.9% under image corruption, while six joint-corruption conditions produce a 13.6-point observed drop versus a 6.0-point additive prediction. MOOR reaches 69.80% mean R@1 across 54 states, compared with 60.72% for whitened uniform fusion and 80.07% for an oracle.

- NeoMME (`2609.01657`) reports 0.5226 and 0.5560 nDCG@10 for its 260M and 800M late-interaction models on the eight-task ViDoRe v3 public mean. For the 260M model, pooling by eight plus asymmetric quantization reduces stored representation size from 1,536.7 kB to 6.0 kB per document while retaining 95.19% of the float32 retrieval quality.

- ZipTok3D (`2609.01740`) reconstructs ShapeNet with one token at 96.8% IoU, 0.012 CD, and 97.8% F1, close to COD-VAE with 32 tokens at 97.1%, 0.012, and 97.8%. On TRELLIS, the four-token CD reduction and F1 gain over COD-VAE-32 have paired bootstrap intervals excluding zero, but the IoU interval does not.

- SimLoss (`2609.00591`) reaches IIW-400 F1 0.7023 with precision 0.8485 and 5.77 seconds/image on A100; CapMAS reaches 0.7025 and 115.31 seconds/image. The 20× measured latency ratio is notable, but the 0.0002 F1 difference has no uncertainty analysis.

- Cliff (`2609.02817`) is the best reported method in all 12 student–teacher–domain cells. For Qwen3-4B math, its averages are 65.66, 64.62, and 63.70 across three teachers, versus 62.37, 61.20, and 62.02 for teacher-GRPO. Human first-error validation covers only 100 balanced mathematics rollouts.

- IDA-OPD (`2608.29846`) improves pass@16 over sampled-token OPD at both math scales in every reported Table 1 comparison; gains range from +1.8 to +8.3 points. On code, it adds +2.1/+0.9 pass@1/pass@16 on MBPP+ and +1.3/+1.0 on LiveCodeBench for the 4B setting.

- VibeVoice-ASR-Streaming (`2609.02812`) reports a five-condition mean WER/CER of 24.66 for its 7B, 22-frame model, versus 25.23 for Gemini 3.5 Transcribe Live under the paper’s chunking protocol. Its 2.00-second expected attribution latency is not directly comparable to the cloud systems’ measured end-to-end latency.

- The competitive-programming pipeline (`2609.02849`) raises Nano’s IOI 2025 Score@1 from 130/600 to 291/600, ICPC Pass@1 from 16.9% to 51.0%, and LiveCodeBench Pro Pass@1 from 17.6% to 71.6%. GenCorrect lifts five-run IOI 2025 means to 468.2 for Nano and 502.0 for Ultra, above the reported 438.3 gold threshold.

- Debias-SparseGPT (`2609.02496`) improves LLaMA 1:4 UnQover from 35.60% to 60.46% while MMLU changes from 59.11% to 59.76%. Conversely, StereoSet-only Qwen 2:4 pruning reduces UnQover from 28.45% to 24.94% and worsens several other measures, so the result is calibration- and regime-dependent.

- Small rubric judges (`2608.30005`) use a Qwen3-1.7B probe to raise the RaR-Science policy score from 0.232 to 0.643, with 95% CI [0.573, 0.708]; an 8B generative judge produces 0.594 [0.523, 0.662]. Probe judging uses 2.33 hours through the comparison checkpoint versus 24.98 judge-hours for generation, but total RL elapsed time differs only 1.26×.

- Kirin (`2609.01823`) improves AiM3D Top-1 R-Precision from \(0.032\pm.001\) to \(0.043\pm.001\) and FID from \(11.889\pm.024\) to \(6.248\pm.014\) when adding image conditioning to text. Diversity falls from \(4.693\pm.071\) to \(4.319\pm.093\), preserving the fidelity–diversity qualification.

- PaperCompiler (`2609.02272`) improves reference-based fidelity from 3.647 to 4.152 on a 1–5 model-judged scale over 90 papers, while token use on a ten-paper subset is 1.71M per repository versus PaperCoder’s 0.98M. High-severity critique frequency falls from 13.2% to 6.1%, though API/schema mismatches rise from 2.3% to 4.0%.

- The portfolio certificate (`2608.29692`) places its Qwen3-Embedding-8B allocation at the 0.69%–1.33% in-sample variance percentile across four specified 20,000-draw reference populations. It remains 35.56% above the ex post long-only sample GMV and has no prospective backtest.

- The interaction-field paper (`2608.29669`) estimates \(\hat\lambda=3.461579\), bootstrap interval [2.890711, 4.168088], on 885 return dates in 2023–2026 using a field frozen from 2018–2022 text. Its conditional quasi-log-likelihood exceeds the tested RBF-Wasserstein and equal-support alternatives, but the non-nested comparison is descriptive and noncausal.

## Open research directions

1. **Select self-improvement checkpoints on genuinely untouched evidence.** Motivated by `2609.01437`, `2608.31111`, `2609.00196`, and `2608.31100`. Run multi-seed campaigns with separate development, selection, and one-shot confirmation slices; report retained gains, regression frequency, and cross-executor performance.

2. **Disentangle operational knowledge from added compute and context.** Motivated by `2609.02749` and `2609.02272`. Remove verification, scripts, provenance, graph links, reconciliation, and file contracts one at a time under matched total tokens, tool calls, and offline construction cost.

3. **Measure full-system long-context efficiency.** Motivated by `2609.02737` and `2609.01925`. Combine prefill and decode interventions in one serving stack and measure time-to-first-token, inter-token latency, throughput, memory, and accuracy across batch sizes, context lengths, and accelerators.

4. **Test whether retrieval diagnostics predict downstream task failure.** Motivated by `2608.21450`, `2608.30949`, `2608.29607`, and `2609.01865`. Hold the downstream generator fixed and compare answer or program correctness after entity-aligned training, perspective steering, adaptive fusion, and execution-aware reranking.

5. **Develop deployable adaptive compute for compact representations.** Motivated by `2609.01740`, `2609.01657`, and `2609.01147`. Train confidence-based policies to select prefix length, pooling, quantization, or token retention without ground-truth reconstruction metrics, then evaluate quality–latency frontiers end to end.

6. **Validate localized credit with independent labels and matched costs.** Motivated by `2609.02817`, `2608.29846`, and `2608.30005`. Build expert-labeled first-error and rubric datasets for both math and code, repeat training across seeds, and include teacher/judge inference cost, semantic solution diversity, and reward-hacking audits.

7. **Replace qualitative long-horizon video demonstrations with population-level evaluation.** Motivated by `2609.02886` and `2609.01823`. Evaluate many complete rollouts using camera adherence, temporal drift, identity retention, physical plausibility, blinded human ratings, and failure-time distributions; stratify Kirin by reconstruction quality.

8. **Stress continuous specialist interfaces outside board games.** Motivated by `2609.00474` and `2609.00591`. Compare latent tokens with bandwidth-matched learned textual summaries across several specialists and real-world tasks, using independent judges and ablations of projector, layer, pooling, and teacher choice.

9. **Test pruning fairness without conflating debiasing and abstention.** Motivated by `2609.02496`. Measure justified versus unjustified abstention, subgroup task accuracy, and calibration across languages and repeated calibration samples, while separately ablating bias-aware mask selection and compensation.

10. **Move financial representation results to prospective, changing universes.** Motivated by `2608.29692` and `2608.29669`. Freeze embeddings, transport geometry, fields, and portfolios at each rebalance date; admit entry and exit; include transaction costs; and test alternative ground metrics, unbalanced clouds, sector controls, and covariance-aware hybrids.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All 36 supplied current-paper analyses were covered. Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical connections were available; their claims were checked against the supplied current analyses and, for retained cross-day edges, the matching cached previous-paper evidence.

Collection failures affected the Hugging Face Markdown path for 27 papers: `2608.31111`, `2609.02886`, `2609.00638`, `2609.02737`, `2609.01147`, `2609.00196`, `2609.01657`, `2609.01740`, `2609.02817`, `2608.29846`, `2609.02812`, `2609.02849`, `2608.18972`, `2609.01925`, `2608.30949`, `2609.02272`, `2609.01865`, `2609.00474`, `2608.29607`, `2608.30751`, `2609.01823`, `2609.00377`, `2609.02496`, `2609.01936`, `2609.00834`, `2608.30005`, and `2504.15476`. Full or detailed paper analyses were nevertheless supplied for all 36, so these are source-collection failures rather than missing digest entries.

Several analyses remain partial:

- HarnessDev (`2609.01437`) and S3Gym (`2608.31100`) lack many underlying table cells.
- KBMR (`2608.21450`) lacks most numerical table bodies.
- Sparse Readout Prism (`2609.01936`) is explicitly missing 138,833 appendix characters.
- Some papers contain internal inconsistencies, notably LLAMIA (`2609.00474`) task counts and rationale scores, SnapBench (`2608.29607`) gallery counts and selected table values, and CRISP (`2609.01925`) implementation descriptions.
- SolarWM (`2609.02886`) has low-confidence comparative-effectiveness evidence because model evaluation is predominantly qualitative.
- Claims based on one training seed, one adaptive trajectory, model judges, small human audits, validation-set selection, theoretical latency estimates, or in-sample finance evaluation were kept qualified.
- Historical evidence was service-truncated in places, but every retained historical endpoint had cached analysis rather than listing-summary-only evidence. Directional edges are proposed technical compatibilities, not demonstrated integrations.
