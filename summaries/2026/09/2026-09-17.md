# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-09-17

## Executive summary

- Agent evaluation infrastructure was a major theme. ScienceIDE (`2609.19134`) packages scientific repositories into verifier-backed environments, while ProgramDistill (`2609.18805`) derives replay-verifiable tasks from interactive web applications.
- Several papers made adaptation depend on accumulated outcomes: CERA-MoA (`2609.18779`) co-trains agents and their router; EvolveTrade (`2609.17632`) rewrites a trading policy from realized returns; HypoEvolve (`2609.15938`) evolves hypothesis populations; and Agora (`2609.18094`) records research evidence in Git. Their evidence ranges from controlled ablations to a single uncontrolled case study.
- Long-context work addressed distinct layers of the stack. SpectralShift (`2609.14320`) changes recurrent retention, Fathom (`2609.17652`) reduces reads from host-resident KV indices, and the MoE training stack (`2609.14306`) bounds several memory peaks to reach one-million-token training.
- Two inference papers target hardware limits directly: VC-Attention (`2609.15810`) accelerates low-bit video attention, while Edge0 (`2609.18063`) streams a 35B MoE from SSD using predicted routing.
- Robotics contributions span action tokenization (`2609.18487`), in-context control (`2609.19138`), and a hand that locomotes and manipulates using the same fingers (`2609.17172`). The first has broad simulated comparisons; the latter two rely on smaller physical evaluations.
- Zing-0.5 (`2609.17909`) reports real-time action-conditioned video generation and competitive WBench navigation performance, but text-control adherence and persistence remain qualitative.
- PANORAMA (`2609.19143`) and EventEgoHands++ (`2609.17189`) both use segmentation masks as intermediate structure, respectively for phrase-grounded captions and 3D hand meshes. Their targets and evaluation settings remain distinct.
- Methodological caveats recur across the collection: missing repeated-run uncertainty, limited causal ablations, narrow hardware or benchmark settings, and metrics that omit important deployment behavior such as missed-hand penalties, agent task completion, or persistent world state.

## Main results worth noting

- **Structured data — `2609.17488`:** LimiX-2 reported TabArena Elo 1935 over 51 datasets, versus 1818 for TabFM+, and mean causal-skeleton F1 0.7972 across six datasets, versus 0.6591 for EXAONE Tabular. These are aggregate prediction and undirected-skeleton results; they do not establish intervention or counterfactual ability.

- **Agent environments — `2609.19134`, `2609.18805`:** ScienceIDE’s strongest evaluated system reached 67.1% strict success on 85 one-hour tasks, with overlap among leading-agent intervals. ProgramDistill’s Astra result was 84.3% binary success over 300 deterministic web tasks, declining from 100% at restoration depth 1 to 64.0% at depth 8. The benchmarks cover different software and verifier regimes, so these rates are not comparable.

- **Post-training — `2609.18708`, `2609.19144`:** SP3O improved Qwen3-4B seven-task avg@32 accuracy to 45.57%, from PPO’s 37.60%, under DAPO-Math-17k training. ComPO raised Llama-3-8B AlpacaEval 2 length-controlled win rate from 32.59% with DPO to 35.79% after clean-pair DPO plus ComPO, but reduced one Mistral Arena-Hard result from 14.4% to 10.5%.

- **Confidence — `2609.17708`:** XConf reported 21 wins, two ties, and one loss in AUROC against ten-sample self-consistency across six non-agent datasets and four models. Its strongest concrete coding result was AUROC 0.887 on LiveCodeBench with Gemini 2.5 Flash, with 95% CI [0.867, 0.907].

- **Agent adaptation — `2609.17632`, `2609.18779`:** EvolveTrade led its LLM comparators in three of six model-regime pairs and lost in others; in April 2026 with Gemini, its Sharpe ratio was 4.73 versus 9.07 for Static Base. CERA-MoA’s Qwen3-4B adaptive router nearly matched fixed Top-2 ID/OOD averages while using 367.77 rather than 666.37 generated tokens.

- **Collective search — `2609.18094`, `2609.15938`:** Agora’s community reduced development-evaluator loss from 3.3923 to 1.899044 bpb, but about 98% of the reduction occurred in the first 18 scored contributions and there was no control arm. HypoEvolve improved DepMap support over Tree of Thoughts by 0.0567 with \(p=0.0113\); its Open Targets margin over that baseline had \(p=0.126\).

- **Robot learning — `2609.18487`, `2609.19138`, `2609.17172`:** ActionPiece reached 94.8% LIBERO and 68.8% unseen LIBERO-Plus success under shared Qwen3-VL-4B policy conditions. GPT-Policy improved bottle opening from 0/3 to 3/3 when video, state, and actions were supplied, but used only three trials. The locomoting hand recovered from 21/25 hardware falls and pressed 29/32 requested keys in one 72.5-second sequence.

- **Video systems — `2609.15810`, `2609.17909`:** VC-Attention delivered Wan2.2 end-to-end clip speedups of 1.13–1.70× across four GPUs, with hardware-specific fidelity tradeoffs. Zing-0.5 scored 81.0 on WBench Navigation and generated eight independent 832×480 streams at 24.63 FPS on eight RTX 5090 GPUs.

- **Long context — `2609.14320`, `2609.17652`:** SpectralShift’s staged 128K extension improved mean RULER from 52.38 to 55.18, while Fathom reduced A100 GPU decode time from 296 ms to 177 ms for a 1M-token synthetic, host-resident scan. Fathom’s speed benefit disappeared when the index was in GPU memory.

- **MoE systems — `2609.14306`, `2609.18063`:** The bounded training stack reached 1M context for 120B, 241B, and 667B models on 16, 32, and 64 H200s. Edge0 served a 35B int4 MoE at 20.4 tok/s with 2.9 GiB peak active memory on a 24 GB M4 Pro, while reporting a 3.9-point average quality gap from the fp16 base.

- **Structured vision — `2609.17189`, `2609.19143`:** EventEgoHands++ achieved 34.18±0.22 mm MPJPE on EEH-R and 35.57±0.18 mm in darkness, excluding missed detections from reconstruction error. PANORAMA-4B reached 45.6 gPQ on PanoCaps, versus 44.5 for SAMTok-8B; the latter retained higher caption-quality and recall metrics.

- **Behavior and clinical generalization — `2609.18011`, `2609.15524`:** Gaze features yielded modest macro-F1 gains of 0.060 on MapTask and 0.020 on MUNDEX, with sensitivity to grouped splits. The nnU-Net study found a −0.0747 mean-regional-Dice source-to-validation difference, with 95% interval [−0.0976, −0.0523], but hidden population labels prevent cohort attribution.

## Open research directions

1. **Test environment-derived training across unseen repositories** (`2609.19134`, `2609.18805`). Hold out complete repositories and application families during task generation and training, then evaluate with independently authored verifiers and human audits of valid alternative solutions.

2. **Separate critic mechanisms under matched weighting and compute** (`2609.18708`, `2609.19144`). Compare dense, random, and spaced supervision with identical response weights, then evaluate held-out likelihood displacement, value error, generated tokens, and repeated-seed policy performance.

3. **Combine early and post-episode uncertainty** (`2609.17708`). Compare XConf, within-trajectory structural uncertainty, and a combined estimator on chronological agent runs; measure calibration, intervention timing, retry success, and the cost of delayed or noisy labels.

4. **Establish causal benefits of agent adaptation** (`2609.17632`, `2609.18094`, `2609.15938`, `2609.18779`). Use matched budgets and randomized controls for individual prompt edits, evidence views, parent selection, and routing exploration. Report repeated-run performance and total model or environment calls.

5. **Evaluate persistent state in interactive video** (`2609.17909`). Modify an object through text, navigate away beyond the active cache, and return by a different path. Score instruction success, state retention, contradictions, navigation quality, and latency.

6. **Compare long-context interventions as one system** (`2609.14320`, `2609.17652`). Evaluate a recurrent model with context extension and offloaded sparse decoding under the same RULER, tracking, and coding-agent tasks, including quality, host traffic, latency, and GPU memory.

7. **Test MoE memory systems under realistic workloads** (`2609.14306`, `2609.18063`). Measure long-context training quality over sustained runs and inference under batching, concurrent requests, different SSDs, CUDA backends, and varying cache temperature.

8. **Move robot results from narrow trials to controlled physical replication** (`2609.18487`, `2609.19138`, `2609.17172`). Run matched policies across seeds and physical layouts, recording task success, collisions, contact failures, execution time, and intervention counts. Separate demonstrations, measured state, and action-token representations factorially.

9. **Make structured visual metrics recall-sensitive** (`2609.17189`, `2609.19143`). Penalize missed hands in mesh evaluation and omitted entities in grounded captioning, then test whether improved proposal recall increases downstream structured accuracy without degrading precision.

10. **Resolve population and participant generalization** (`2609.15524`, `2609.18011`). Obtain verified cohort labels for brain-tumor validation and collect more independent dialogue participants; evaluate fully held-out groups with uncertainty from both model retraining and sample composition.

## Coverage and uncertainty

All **23 supplied current-paper analyses** were reviewed and every paper appears in the taxonomy, at-a-glance table, results, or research directions.

The taxonomy, same-day connection stage, and historical connection stage were available. Same-day edges were retained only when both current analyses supported a concrete shared problem, compatible asset, or method transfer. Twelve historical edges were retained after checking the matching current and previous-paper evidence. Historical evidence was cached analysis rather than listing summaries, although several records were truncated by the service; retained claims use evidence visible at both endpoints.

Collection reported unavailable Hugging Face Markdown for **20 papers**: `2609.17488`, `2609.19134`, `2609.18708`, `2609.17708`, `2609.18805`, `2609.18094`, `2609.18487`, `2609.15810`, `2609.17632`, `2609.17909`, `2609.15938`, `2609.14320`, `2609.19144`, `2609.18011`, `2609.17189`, `2609.19143`, `2609.18779`, `2609.15524`, `2609.17172`, and `2609.17652`. Full PAPER_ANALYSIS blocks were nevertheless supplied for all 23 papers. The remaining three current papers were not listed as collection failures.

Lower-confidence conclusions include Agora’s coordination benefit, the causal role of ActionPiece’s PRC metric, component attribution in SpectralShift and ComPO, EvolveTrade’s long-term robustness, GPT-Policy’s generalization and safety, and any claim that Zing’s text-directed changes persist. Several papers also lack repeated-seed uncertainty, matched compute, complete artifact access, or end-to-end deployment evaluation.
