# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-09-21

## Executive summary

- **Reusable external guidance is a recurring theme.** EvoOntology (`2609.15779`), Code2Skill (`2609.05571`), Designer-RSI (`2609.22086`), and GraphSkillEvo (`2609.21749`) report improvements from semantic or procedural resources. Their grounding, validation, and cost accounting differ substantially.
- **Executable references support agent training.** CodeMidas (`2609.22068`) reports gains on five external coding benchmarks; RecreationWorld (`2609.22000`) reports transfer from verified application-recreation trajectories. Neither isolates every construction or filtering component.
- **Functional completion remains difficult.** RecreationWorld’s leading direct-MCP configuration passes the entire programmatic suite on only 2.8% of applications. CADWorld (`2609.16251`) reports 17.5% best-agent success on its full 200-task suite, versus one expert reference pass at 87.0%.
- **Dialogue improvements expose explicit trade-offs.** OmniVChat (`2609.21465`) improves audiovisual-input response scores while its brevity reward reduces correctness. SteerDuplex (`2609.12623`) improves acoustic steering and selected interaction metrics, but selected-run reporting and premature yielding limit conclusions.
- **Visual control benefits from more specific evaluation.** Paint-Anything (`2609.20816`) improves object-color scores; OmniVBench (`2609.22069`) validates factor-specific checklists against human ratings. RefineEdit (`2609.20633`) reports preservation gains, but evaluation-set tuning and simulated ablation placeholders weaken its evidence.
- **Robot-policy gains need task-specific interpretation.** PARTS (`2609.21788`) improves physical manipulation through local residual learning; Movement Trend Guidance (`2609.20669`) benefits from future-state supervision. Active rollout time, task progress, binary success, and rollout-seed variation must remain distinct.
- **Evaluation can reveal misleading success signals.** TeleAntiFraud 2.0 (`2609.18748`) shows how fraud-class F1 can conceal zero lawful-call recall. TrustReviewer (`2609.20942`) improves agreement with official ratings without establishing better scientific critique.
- **Efficiency and enforcement claims are conditional.** IntBMoE (`2609.21346`), MoME (`2609.15126`), SiliconBench (`2609.19169`), and Cellpose-SAM quantization (`2609.21038`) expose different quality–resource trade-offs. APort Vault (`2609.22076`) prevents observed forbidden-recipient transfers under configured policies, without establishing benign-task utility.

## Main results worth noting

- **BI tools have comparatively substantial statistical support — `2609.20886`.** On BI-Bench, ten-run o4-mini SQL accuracy rises from **48.2% to 61.9%** with specialized tools: **+13.7 pp**, 95% CI **[7.7, 19.7]**. Across ten backbones and SQL/Python settings, 19/20 tool comparisons are significant; project-level analysis and false-discovery correction support the finding. This remains raw-table-to-answer evaluation over curated public projects.

- **Coding transfer spans distinct tasks — `2609.22068`.** Against the initial MiMo-V2.5 policy under identical reported evaluation settings, CodeMidas RL raises SWE-bench Pro pass rate **50.3% → 54.4%** and Terminal-Bench v2.1 **63.7% → 72.2%**. ProgramBench **Almost Solved** rises **4.5% → 21.5%**, but that metric requires at least 95% of tests, not complete task passage. Main-result uncertainty is absent.

- **Future supervision has a useful capacity control — `2609.20669`.** At the fixed epoch-1000 checkpoint on LIBERO-40, success is **71.93 ± 0.49%**, versus **44.87 ± 0.33%** for a parameter-matched latent without future loss and **65.28 ± 0.19%** for explicit future-point conditioning. Each uses 50 demonstrations/task and the same three-rollout-seed protocol; the deviations do not measure independent training variability.

- **Physical-task improvements should remain separate — `2609.21788`.** PARTS raises earbud binary success **10% → 40%** and cable binary success **50% → 95%** against SFT, with 20 evaluation episodes per method per task. LEGO’s **54% → 82%** is mean fraction of ten bricks sorted. Combining these into one “full-task success” number would mix incompatible metrics.

- **Dialogue rewards visibly change the objective — `2609.21465`.** Full OmniVChat-RL scores **0.652** on the synthetic benchmark and **0.632** on the recorded Chinese probe. Removing its efficiency reward raises those scores to **0.697/0.691**, while synthetic mean response length grows **36 → 99 counted words**. Checkpoint-selection qualifications prevent treating this as a fully controlled convergence comparison.

- **Checklist validation is more informative than close leaderboard gaps — `2609.22069`.** On 965 outputs from 100 cases, each rated by three annotators, checklist scoring improves Spearman agreement over holistic scoring from **0.67 to 0.77** for reference fidelity and **0.69 to 0.74** for instruction realization. No uncertainty establishes a meaningful difference between overall generator scores **72.68** and **72.41**.

- **Reported distillation accuracy and speed diverge — `2609.21619`.** Cal-OPD versus OPD improves six-benchmark mathematical Avg@16 from **50.8 to 53.1** for Qwen3-4B teacher → 1.7B student, and **65.9 to 69.0** for 30B-A3B teacher → 4B student. On eight H20 GPUs, minutes/step improve **18.80 → 14.90** in the smaller setting but worsen **31.26 → 48.76** in the larger one.

- **Authorization has a strong, narrow endpoint — `2609.22076`.** The matched Levels 2–4 comparison records **105 versus zero** forbidden-recipient transfers across **68,970 pairs**. For guarded evaluations spanning 790 source sessions, the reported rule-of-three session-level upper bound is **0.380%**. Similar aggregate request rates are not evidence of equivalent benign-task utility.

- **Retention is meaningful only alongside baseline competence — `2609.21038`.** Mixed W4/W8 reduces weight storage **6.76× versus FP32** and passes the per-modality requirement that the entire 95% interval for paired instance-F1 change at IoU 0.5 exceed **−0.02**. Plain W4 also passes, at **6.90×** storage reduction. The study does not establish mixed precision’s superiority, useful absolute iPSC segmentation, or hardware speedups.

These findings are selected for the clarity of their comparisons and controls, not ranked by numerical magnitude across unrelated metrics.

## Open research directions

The following are editorial experiment proposals grounded in limitations documented by the supplied analyses.

1. **Independently audit code-derived supervision — `2609.22068`, `2609.05571`, `2609.22000`.** On source units with executable interfaces, compare judgment-only and execution-audited acceptance using size-matched training pools. Measure verifier false acceptance/rejection and downstream performance on repository-disjoint tasks.

2. **Test whether memory updates generalize beyond their admission cases — `2609.15779`, `2609.22086`, `2609.21749`.** Repeat optimization across independent validation splits, freeze accepted resources, and evaluate fresh tasks plus controlled data/tool changes. Match optimization budgets and include construction, replay, retrieval, and serving costs.

3. **Turn artifact checking into a controlled intervention — `2609.22000`, `2609.16251`.** Randomize final relaunch, saved-artifact inspection, and repair feedback under equal interaction budgets. Grade with independently authored checks to determine whether verification improves functional completion beyond benchmark-specific compliance.

4. **Measure dialogue gains without favorable run selection or shared judges — `2609.21465`, `2609.12623`.** Preregister seeds, report every run, and obtain blinded human ratings on independently collected conversations. Sweep reward weights while measuring correctness, brevity, acoustic compliance, interruption handling, and answer completion separately.

5. **Evaluate visual control jointly with preservation — `2609.20816`, `2609.20633`, `2609.22069`.** Freeze configurations on separate development data, then test held-out colors, materials, edit types, and reference combinations. Report requested-change success alongside unrelated-region preservation; validate brief temporal failures with denser human-reviewed video evidence.

6. **Separate robot-learning gains from supervision and restaging effects — `2609.21788`, `2609.20669`, `2609.18620`.** For PARTS, vary full-task-generated versus restaged entry states and record human labor. For trend conditioning, repeat independent training seeds and controlled occlusions. For DeformSmith, compare simulated forces/deformations with measured interactions and match refinement simulation-call budgets.

7. **Test decision quality beyond aggregate agreement — `2609.18766`, `2609.18748`, `2609.20942`, `2609.21094`.** Fraud studies need lawful/fraud recalls under disjoint calibration and held-out generators/voices. Review steering needs expert-rated valid concerns and actionability. Ethical-vector reversal needs larger independently authored dilemmas, invalid-output rates, and explicit transitivity tests. These are separate task-specific evaluations, not a common score.

8. **Compare efficiency at retained quality and complete cost — `2609.21346`, `2609.15126`, `2609.19169`, `2609.21038`, `2609.21619`.** Match storage and compute where possible, report quality–cost curves, and include cache construction, teacher rescoring, compression metadata, runtime memory, and repeated measurements. For serving and quantization, pre-specify task-appropriate retention margins against competent references.

9. **Stress the correctness of external action rules — `2609.19315`, `2609.22076`.** Introduce incorrect grounding, incomplete action semantics, policy changes, and controlled dispatch bypasses. Measure physical feasibility or authorized-task completion alongside blocked invalid actions, rather than treating rule compliance alone as end-to-end success.

10. **Validate mechanisms and reproducibility directly — `2609.09206`, `2609.19122`, `2609.22039`.** HEAL needs compute-matched random/all-head interventions; TA-CSC needs separate learned-coefficient, regularization, and batch-normalization ablations. Gricea needs independent teams comparing executable artifacts with conventional materials on configuration errors and reproduced outcomes.
