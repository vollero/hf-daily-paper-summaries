# VeriPhy: Agentic Physical Reasoning for World Model Evaluation and Refinement

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-04.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.03153) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.03153)


## One-sentence takeaway
VeriPhy makes generated-video judgments traceable through precompiled checks and evidence records, but its development-set recall barely exceeds a monolithic VLM and its reported refinement gains lack independent confirmation (§§4–5).

## Problem and motivation
Generated videos can look convincing while violating motion, contact, event-order, or prompt requirements. The authors identify an integration gap: existing evaluators provide complementary judgments, localization, and measurements without consistently connecting prompt obligations to evidence and final decisions in one auditable execution chain (§§1–2).

The benchmark covers broad prompt adherence—including counts and rendered text—rather than physical validity alone (Appendix A.5).

## Approach and contributions
- **Auditable verification system:** A text-only planner extracts prompt-grounded claims before viewing frames and compiles a statically validated dependency graph. Observations gate or localize declared calls to frozen perception and measurement tools. Records distinguish numerical measurements from learned judgments; fixed rules compose supported, contradicted, and unknown states. A learned reviewer can override some judgments, with both decisions retained (§4).
- **Localized flaw corpus and protocol:** The authors assemble 1,500 generated clips with 2,582 human-written flaw records, including prompt spans, rationales, severity, confidence, and optional temporal and spatial localization. Semantic matching assigns whole, partial, or missed outcomes (§5.1).
- **Simulation-conditioned generation testbed:** MuJoCo trajectories become depth or silhouette controls for frozen Wan 2.2-VACE generation. Separate withdrawal experiments probe when generated motion remains stable after removing control (§§3, 5.2).
- **Context and refinement experiments:** Curated lessons from recorded misses modify planner context without weight updates. A separate prompt-rewrite experiment tests critic-guided regeneration (§§5.4–5.5). Operator growth, external-knowledge admission, and control-edit refinement remain roadmap components (Appendix B).

The authors position the novelty primarily as integration under a shared verification contract, acknowledging that planning, tools, and memory mechanisms predate this work (§2).

## Evidence and main results

| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| Higher core recall than structured baselines; small gap over single-pass VLM | On 149 development clips with 304 flaws: VeriPhy 228/304 (75.0%; 191 whole, 37 partial), question decomposition 164/304 (53.9%), scene graph 161/304 (53.0%), same-backbone single-pass VLM 222/304 (73.0%). Mean model/tool calls: 14.1, 21.9, 52.1, and 1.0, respectively. | §5.3.1; Table 8 |
| More matches do not imply higher finding reliability | Human-flaw match rate: 26% of VeriPhy’s 871 findings versus 29% of question decomposition’s 557. These are annotation-match rates, not established precision. | §5.3.1 |
| Learned review and specialist abstention materially shape results | Review overrides account for 83 of 228 matched flaws; physical specialists abstain on 166 of 227 checks and contradict on 61. | §§5.3.1–5.3.2 |
| Lessons improve recall on a separate corpus split | On 306 clips with 502 flaws, excluding experience identifiers and restated prompts: 340/502 (67.7%) without lessons versus 375/502 (74.7%) with 33 lessons; paired sign-test \(p=3×10^{-4}\). Four lesson conditions find 374–377 flaws. | §5.4; Table 5 |
| Better rank correlation, worse discrete agreement | On 200 VideoPhy-2 clips, VeriPhy versus VideoScore2: SRCC 0.465 versus 0.327; exact agreement 31.0% versus 33.0%; within-one agreement 76.0% versus 83.0%. Semantic adherence only. | §5.3.3; Table 4 |
| External physical-report matching remains limited | Interim evolving-state Physion-Eval run: matched-report rate 18.4% among 543 reported-glitch clips; evidence-conditioned flag rate 26.8% across 601 clips. Published evaluator comparisons use different samples. | §5.3.4; Table 9 |
| Early control withdrawal particularly disrupts post-contact motion | Separate backbone, thrown-ball scene, 16 paired seeds: release at \(\sigma=0.952\) yields opposite-direction post-contact horizontal motion on 9/16 seeds versus 0/16 without withdrawal; airborne horizontal agreement drops only 0.003. | §5.2.3 |
| Refinement improves its own critic score without independent corroboration | Across 589 completed Cosmos3-Nano comparisons, 63/87 acted-on clips increase critic plausibility; overall mean rises 0.797→0.862. AutoRater reports physical-commonsense mean 3.59→3.58 and joint pass rate 22.8%→23.1%, but Appendix A.9 identifies unequal baseline/rewrite denominators. | §5.5; Tables 6, 11 |

The evidence supports a concrete audit-trace architecture and improved annotation recall under some settings. It does not establish reliable physical certification, cost-matched superiority, or independently verified generation improvement. Traceability also includes learned judgments; it does not mean every verdict follows from a numerical physical measurement.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated evaluation limits:** The core is repeatedly used for development, contains only flawed clips, and has single-annotator labels and unknown source generators. One failed clip is excluded. The question-decomposition baseline was specified after inspecting critic outputs (§§5.1, 5.6–7).
- **Author-stated measurement limits:** Cross-tool identity binding can fail; intermittent grounding can produce misleading counts; spatial and negative-event predicates are limited. All measured claims are visual, despite the audio-capable architecture (§§4.4, 6–7).
- **Author-stated attribution limits:** Lessons increase claims per prompt; no matched-question-budget experiment separates better targeting from simply asking more. The proposed lesson-admission gate was not used (§7).
- **Author-stated generation limits:** Small single-body probes do not establish multi-object fidelity. Simulation limits scene diversity, and rendered playback does not preserve simulator absolute speed (§5.2).
- **Analyst inference—internal inconsistencies reduce reproducibility:** §4.5 says no refinement loop was run, while §5.5 reports one. Algorithm 1 reviews unknown claims, whereas §4.5 describes reviewing supported claims. Table 6 claims paired evaluation, but Table 11 specifies 600 baseline versus 589 rewrite clips and incomplete baseline-score availability. These discrepancies prevent a fully consistent reconstruction.
- **Analyst inference—independent-rater interpretation is unresolved:** Flat AutoRater scores do not establish that the rater is insensitive; they also remain compatible with no physical improvement (§5.5).

## Open research questions
- **Does the critic retain its advantage at matched resources?** Current comparisons mix tool usage, review, and call budgets. Test a frozen system against single-pass and decomposition baselines on independently annotated clean and flawed clips, with matched compute and separate reviewer/tool ablations (§§5.3, 7).
- **Do lessons improve targeting beyond increasing claim count?** Recall rises alongside plan size. Compare taught and untaught planners under identical claim budgets on a new generator population (§§5.4, 7).
- **Can feedback improve independently measured physics?** Prompt rewriting improves self-scores only. Compare blind resampling, prompt edits, and localized control edits using complete paired outputs and blinded human defect annotation (§5.5; Appendix C).
- **Can audiovisual evidence resolve failures missed visually?** Audio support is untested. Evaluate visual-only and audiovisual configurations on localized contact–sound timing faults, reporting both detection and abstention (§6).

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The supplied text supports the architecture and reported comparisons, but inconsistent implementation and refinement descriptions weaken interpretation.

Material missing information includes verified-negative evaluation, inter-annotator agreement, complete paired refinement baseline scores, the precise plausibility-score construction, matched-budget component ablations, full compute costs, and audiovisual results. Public completeness of code, checkpoint revisions, and trace artifacts cannot be established from the supplied text alone.
