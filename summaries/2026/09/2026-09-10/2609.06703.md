# DianShi-RxnDB: A Large-Scale, Fine-Grained Organic Reaction Data Platform Built via a Fully Automated Pipeline for Researchers and AI Agents

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-10.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.06703) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06703)


## One-sentence takeaway
DianShi-RxnDB organizes approximately 24 million patent-derived reaction instances with source links and researcher/agent interfaces, reporting 92.95% pooled accuracy across five manually evaluated fields in a sample restricted to automatically qualified records (§§2.4–2.5).

## Problem and motivation
Reaction structures, conditions, procedures, and yields are scattered across patent text, images, and cross-referenced experiments. The authors identify a gap between open datasets with limited experimental detail and provenance and richer commercial resources with access restrictions. Their goal is fine-grained reaction retrieval, experiment comparison, and source verification for researchers and AI agents (§1.1).

## Approach and contributions
- **Automated data construction:** Process USPTO/EPO patents using DeepSeek-V3-0324 primarily for experimental text and MinerU.Chem for chemical images and schemes; normalize structures and experimental fields; apply RXNMapper-based atom-mapping, atom-conservation, and other qualification checks. Both passing and non-passing records are retained (§§2.1–2.2).
- **Instance-preserving organization:** Link individual experiments to substances and source locations. Group instances by normalized reactant–product identity while preserving their separate conditions and procedures; associate transformation templates generated with LocalRetro and RDChiral (§2.3).
- **Shared researcher/agent access:** Provide a Web workbench for search, comparison, and source inspection, plus MCP tools for composable retrieval. An aspirin example demonstrates both paths (§§3–5).

The contribution is primarily the data platform and its organization; the report defers detailed extraction methods to a future technical report (§2.2).

| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |

| Finer exposed representation in one example | The inspected Intermediate 4 record in US10975080 exposes more separate participant, process, workup, provenance, and validation features. | §§2.6.2, 12.3 |

These results support substantial database scale and good agreement for selected fields within the evaluated populations. They do not establish whole-record correctness, extraction recall, experimental reproducibility, or improved downstream prediction or planning. The external comparison measures a separate agreement criterion and cannot be pooled with the internal manual accuracy.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated scope:** Journal articles and unpublished laboratory knowledge are excluded. Manual accuracy applies only to qualified instances and the five assessed fields (§6.1).
- **Observed extraction failures:** Workup materials can become reaction participants; roles can be duplicated or misassigned; cross-paragraph information can be missed; multiple steps can be merged (§2.5).
- **Author-stated comparison boundaries:** External results concern matched samples, an older available Pistachio release, and one representation example. Yield agreement partly reflects explicit-percentage versus inferred-yield policies (§§12.1, 12.4–12.5).
- **Analyst inference—reference and selection risks:** Exact paragraph matching restricts the evaluated population, and the reference procedure cannot recover entities omitted by both systems. LLM adjudication introduces an additional potential error source; independent human validation of it is not reported (§12.4).
- **Analyst inference—reproducibility and utility:** Deferred pipeline details prevent full reconstruction from this report. The aspirin demonstration does not measure general agent reliability, retrieval quality, or efficiency (§§2.2, 5).

## Open research questions
1. **How well does qualification separate correct from incorrect records?** Non-passing records were not manually evaluated. Next experiment: independently audit both populations for whole-record correctness and qualification errors (§§2.4, 11.1).
2. **What does image processing add to extraction completeness?** The pipeline uses text and images but reports no modality ablation. Next experiment: compare text-only and combined extraction against exhaustively annotated patents (§2.2).
3. **Do external agreement advantages survive independent reference construction?** Current references depend on matched paragraphs, candidate entities, role harmonization, and yield policy. Next experiment: use blinded expert annotations and score explicit and inferred yields separately (§12.4).
4. **Does MCP improve research-task performance?** Evidence is an illustrative aspirin workflow. Next experiment: evaluate diverse precedent queries for answer correctness, source fidelity, completeness, and task completion time (§5).

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** Scope, principal counts, and evaluation restrictions are explicit, but many tables appear only as captions in the supplied retrieval.

Material missing information includes exact external agreement percentages, several detailed comparison tables, extraction prompts and implementation details, complete qualification rules, compute cost and throughput, annotator agreement statistics, human validation of external LLM adjudication, and bulk-data/code availability.
