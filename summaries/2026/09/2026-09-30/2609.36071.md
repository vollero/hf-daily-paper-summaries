# LongCat-DeepResearch Technical Report

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-30.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.36071) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36071)


## One-sentence takeaway
LongCat-DeepResearch combines a fixed research specification, parallel section investigators, and coordinated local editing, achieving the highest overall scores among four compared products on three public benchmarks, with unequal scored coverage and no established statistical significance (§§3–4).

## Problem and motivation
Open-ended research requires evidence gathering to evolve as reports develop. The authors identify three difficulties with growing shared histories and repeated full-report revision: context competition, early narrative anchoring, and regeneration of unaffected text. Their goal is to coordinate a shared agenda while preserving detailed investigation within each section (§1).

## Approach and contributions
- **ResearchSpec execution interface:** Independent planners search and read sources; a Judge merges their proposals, and a Critic/Reviser refines requirements. Validated section assignments then remain fixed during research (§3.1).
- **Section research and editorial coordination:** Each researcher receives the full specification and one assignment, investigates independently, and writes a complete cited section. Mechanical assembly precedes global ownership/conflict decisions and local revisions (§3.1; Fig. 2).
- **Training-data construction:** Licensed review articles or frozen multi-source briefs ground questions and hidden rubrics. Evidence checks and trajectory validation support research-related mid-training and post-training data (§3.2; Fig. 3).
- **Empirical decomposition:** Product comparisons, component interventions, planning refinement, and editor scaling examine the resulting system. The distinctive contribution is this integration and its execution interfaces; the authors acknowledge precedents for structured planning, parallel research, and revision (§§2, 5).

These results support the recorded system’s report-quality advantages and suggest useful roles for planning aggregation and section-based work. They do not establish universal superiority, equal-resource efficiency, factual correctness throughout reports, or isolated training-data effects. The public comparison is among the listed products, not an established overall leaderboard rank (§4.4; App. A).

## Limitations and validity risks
**Author-stated:** Development subsets were previously inspected, sometimes selected using prior scores; editor subsets expanded after initial-result inspection. Studies generally have one completed grade per report, and bootstrap intervals exclude generation/judge variability. Retrieval windows, recovery, and realized computation differ. Missing reports are excluded; Claude’s ResearchRubrics aggregate lacks complete per-task grades for verification (§Limitations; App. B).

ResearchSpec cannot reopen the global agenda during investigation. Editing can lose detail or attribution, and full-draft inputs retain context and cost constraints. Enhanced editing jointly changes source access, instructions, and iteration count. Training-stage contributions lack matched ablations. FACT citation-support results are not reported (§3.1; §4.1; §Limitations; App. B.7).

**Analyst inference:** Unequal scored cohorts weaken direct product comparisons; simplified planning changes several components simultaneously, limiting attribution to planner count alone. In-house validity is difficult to assess because task composition and evaluator details are sparse (§5.1; App. B.1–B.2).

## Open research questions
- **Would reopening ResearchSpec recover missed evidence?** The fixed agenda leaves unresolved coverage gaps in the cases. Compare fixed planning with evidence-triggered replanning on held-out tasks under matched budgets (§5.5; App. C).
- **Which editorial changes help without damaging support?** The enhanced Editor combines several interventions and sometimes weakens attribution. Factorially vary source excerpts, instructions, and rounds; assess native quality and statement-level support (App. B.7).
- **How stable and efficient are the gains?** Existing studies lack repeated generation/judging and equal realized-resource comparisons. Repeat held-out evaluations with matched resource caps, reporting costs, failures, and score distributions (§Limitations; App. B).
- **What does research-specific training contribute?** Checkpoint comparisons do not isolate data sources or stages. Run matched training ablations with the harness fixed (§3.2; §Limitations).

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** Workflow and recorded results are well described, but causal attribution and generalization remain uncertain.

Material missing information includes exact development-subset sizes and IDs, complete per-system scored coverage, in-house task/evaluator specifications, realized costs and latency, repeated-run variability, detailed training recipes and data quantities, artifact release contents, and dedicated factual-support results.
