# StoryEngine: A State-Grounded Agentic Framework for Video Storytelling

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-30.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.33627) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.33627)


## One-sentence takeaway
StoryEngine separates planned story states from generated visual evidence and reports better narrative and visual continuity than three baselines across two video backbones on a constrained 60-story benchmark (§§3–4).

## Problem and motivation
Multi-shot video generation must preserve event consequences, object placement, character identity, and recurring environments. The authors argue that existing agentic pipelines lack explicit state propagation and can carry visual mistakes into later shots as narrative facts—for example, relocating an object that should remain stored elsewhere (§1).

The identified gap is a mechanism that specifies persistent story facts, translates them into visible shot requirements, and repairs generation errors without rewriting the intended story.

## Approach and contributions

- **Authoritative state propagation:** A structured plan records entity placements, relevant attributes, and ordered event effects. A deterministic reducer computes each shot’s intended opening and ending states; generated pixels cannot revise them (§3.2; Appendix B.2).
- **Grounded render planning:** Canonical character, state-specific object, and panoramic environment references support shot-specific camera views. A compiler produces contracts containing opening, motion, ending, and persistent visual criteria (§3.3; Appendix B.3).
- **Continuity and local repair:** A gate reuses, selectively references, or discards the previous shot’s tail frame. A VLM evaluates fixed criteria and guides bounded regeneration while preserving the plan (§3.4; Appendix B.4).
- **Diagnostic benchmark:** The authors introduce 60 stories with frozen, hidden annotations and separate suites for narrative realization, cross-shot coherence, and visual consistency (§4.1).

The principal methodological contribution is the explicit separation of semantic authority from visual conditioning, implemented through these coordinated stages; the supplied text does not establish priority beyond the authors’ related-work comparison (§2).

## Limitations and validity risks

**Author-stated limitations:** An incorrect authoritative plan persists because visual evidence cannot correct it. Canonical references and panoramas are not true 3D scenes; occlusion, large viewpoint changes, crowds, deformable objects, and hand–object contact remain difficult. Exhausting the repair budget can leave violations (Appendix E).

The benchmark excludes longer narratives, larger casts, dialogue, audio continuity, text, and interactive editing. The authors also acknowledge possible bias from using the same VLM for in-loop evaluation and final judging, although their inputs differ and annotations remain hidden. Metrics have narrower meanings than their names might suggest: APR measures anchor presence, MIR depends on detected faces, and LCS does not check the requested direction of lighting contrast (Appendices D.10, E).

**Analyst inferences:** The continuity ablation jointly removes tail conditioning and feedback-driven regeneration, so it cannot isolate their individual contributions (Appendix B.7). Excluding unreadable location evidence from LAR and dropping unreadable state probes from SPS makes their scores conditional on observable evidence (Appendices D.5–D.6). Direct I2V omits events and states from its prompts, limiting its value as a comparison against an equally informative independent-shot baseline (§4.4; Appendix B.7).

## Open research questions

- **How can incorrect plans be corrected safely?** Plan mistakes remain persistent (Appendix E). Inject known semantic errors and compare human review or validated replanning against the fixed-plan system.
- **Does coherence scale to longer, more populated stories?** Evaluation covers ten shots, two locations, and small casts (Appendix E). Increase shots, locations, and characters separately and measure continuity failures and resource use.
- **How much does each continuity mechanism contribute?** Tail conditioning and repair are bundled in the ablation (Appendix B.7). Test gate-only and repair-only variants under matched generation budgets.
- **Do gains survive independent assessment?** Shared evaluator preferences remain a stated risk (Appendix D.10). Repeat evaluation with independent judges and blinded human pairwise comparisons.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The supplied text gives detailed method and evaluation protocols, but omits full main-result tables, uncertainty estimates, human validation, generation cost and latency comparisons, and confirmed code/benchmark release status. Three evaluation runs are described for selected metrics, but generation-level variability and statistical significance are not established.
