# Beyond Dyadic Memory: Interaction-Aware Multimodal Memory with Adaptive Agentic Retrieval for Multi-Party Spoken Conversations

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-30.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.32522) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.32522)


## One-sentence takeaway
VoxPolyMem combines recurring-speaker tracking, interaction-aware memory, and evidence-gain-trained retrieval to improve multi-party conversational QA on a synthetic speech benchmark and two image-text benchmarks, while real-world spoken QA remains untested.

## Problem and motivation
Multi-party assistants must remember not only conversational facts but also who spoke, whom they addressed, and whose preferences changed across sessions. The authors identify a gap in memory systems and benchmarks that primarily cover dyadic text or image-text conversations: transcripts alone omit acoustic identity cues, and conventional memory structures may omit interaction roles (§§1–2).

## Approach and contributions
- **VoxPolyBench:** A benchmark of generated multi-session conversations with synthesized speech, participant-dependent questions, and annotated supporting turns. Event planning and human/LLM validation control temporal consistency and evidence sufficiency (§3; Appendix A).
- **VoxPolyMem:** Online voiceprint matching, confidence-gated updates, and identity reconciliation connect recurring speakers to a hierarchy of directed interaction records, extracted facts, and participant profiles. Facts and profiles preserve source-turn links (§§4.1–4.2).
- **Adaptive retrieval with EG-GRPO:** A policy selects memory layers, retrieval tools, rewritten queries, and participant filters. Training rewards newly retained supporting evidence through coverage and rank, assigning credit separately at each retrieval state. Previously retained evidence earns no additional credit; the answer model stays fixed (§4.3).

## Evidence and main results
Score is the mean of two LLM judges’ rubric scores, multiplied by 100; Recall measures annotated supporting turns represented through retained records and their source links. All QA methods share GPT-4.1-mini answering and a final budget of 15 records (§5.1; Appendix C).

| Finding | Evidence / setting | Location in paper |
|---|---|---|
| Highest overall spoken-memory QA score | On 1,327 VoxPolyBench test questions, VoxPolyMem scores **85.0**, versus **61.3** for M2A, the highest displayed external baseline. Baselines receive ground-truth speaker labels; VoxPolyMem identifies speakers automatically. | Table 2; §5.1; Table 5 |
| Gains extend to image-text QA | On Mem-Gallery’s 1,411 test questions: **89.6** versus UniversalRAG’s **77.8**. On H2HMem-Multi’s 175: **74.4** versus AUGUSTUS’s **66.0**. | Tables 2 and 5 |
| Interaction structure and repeated retrieval contribute | Against the **84.0** non-RL VoxPolyBench base, removing interaction relations yields **78.6**; single-round retrieval yields **76.1**. These ablations use the non-RL base. | Table 3; §5.3 |
| EG-GRPO improves retrieval and answering | With fixed memory, tools, and inference budget, VoxPolyBench Score/Recall/mean rounds change from **84.0/85.2/1.6** without RL to **85.0/87.4/1.2**. On Mem-Gallery, EG-GRPO achieves **89.6/93.6/1.3**, versus MoT-GRPO’s **87.1/91.5/1.8**. | Table 4; §5.4 |
| Tracking works on selected real recordings | Full tracker attribution accuracy is **97.7%** on VoxPolyBench, **95.0%** on IEMOCAP Sessions 4–5, and **87.9%** on five AMI chains, using frozen embeddings and oracle utterance boundaries. | Table 6; Appendix D.1 |

These results support the combined framework in the evaluated settings. They do not establish end-to-end diarization or real-world spoken-memory QA robustness. Recall measures source-link coverage rather than whether every retained abstraction faithfully contains the needed information. Fewer retrieval rounds also do not establish lower wall-clock latency or total computational cost.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated:** Generated dialogues and synthesized speech limit acoustic and conversational diversity. Planned events help ensure valid questions but leave reasoning over naturally occurring long conversations unresolved (Appendix E; Ethics Statement).
- **Author-stated:** Hosted-model versions and sampling variability affect reproducibility; voiceprints and participant profiles introduce privacy risks (Reproducibility and Ethics Statements).
- **Analyst inference:** Oracle utterance boundaries isolate identity tracking, so the real-recording results exclude segmentation and overlapping-speech failures (Appendix D.1).
- **Analyst inference:** Table 4 tests EG-GRPO as a combined design without separately ablating coverage, rank weighting, and state-specific credit; their individual contributions remain uncertain.
- **Analyst inference:** The human audit covers only non-RL outputs on two benchmarks and reports aggregate mean gaps, explicitly without establishing item-level agreement. Reliability for full-model comparisons remains uncertain (Appendix D.2).

## Open research questions
- **Does the QA advantage survive natural speech?** Real recordings are tested only for identity tracking. Next experiment: evaluate the complete pipeline on longitudinal real conversations with accents, noise, overlap, and predicted utterance boundaries.
- **Which EG-GRPO component drives improvement?** Reward components are not separately isolated. Next experiment: compare coverage-only, ranking-only, and combined rewards with matched rollout and training budgets.
- **Does evidence provenance overstate usable evidence?** Recall credits source IDs linked to compressed facts and profiles. Next experiment: audit abstraction faithfulness and compare linked-source Recall with direct answer-support judgments.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: high** in the reported methods and within-benchmark findings; generalization remains uncertain.

Material missing information includes scenario-level train/test separation, repeated-run uncertainty or significance tests, complete optimization hyperparameters and compute costs, baseline adaptation details, item-level human agreement, and end-to-end QA results on real speech. Artifact availability is asserted by the authors but was not independently checked.
