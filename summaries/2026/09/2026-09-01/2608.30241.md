# PaperBanana-Interact: Scientific Diagram Refinement with Multi-Turn Human Feedback

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-01.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2608.30241) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.30241)


## One-sentence takeaway
PaperBanana-Interact combines compressed interaction memory with iterative multi-objective critique to improve scientific diagrams across feedback turns, outperforming tested refiners on quality and forgetting while remaining computationally costly and imperfect.

## Problem and motivation
Scientific diagrams often cannot satisfy an author’s visual, structural, and communicative preferences in one generation. Existing systems largely lack support for iterative human feedback, despite the stricter semantic and factual requirements of scientific figures (§§1–3).

In a 14-person formative study, every participant requested revisions, average satisfaction rose from 3.2 to 4.2, and 64% of sessions produced requests inspired by seeing an earlier draft (Figure 2; §§3.2, C). The authors identify two recurring multi-turn failures: progressive quality drift and loss of previously implemented requirements.

## Approach and contributions

1. **MTPaperBananaBench:** A benchmark of 292 expert-drawn diagrams and 3,518 requirements covering content, organization, and visual representation. Requirements were proposed with LLM assistance and manually reviewed (§3.3; Appendix B).

2. **Simulated interaction protocol:** Hidden requirements are progressively converted into natural-language feedback. Evaluation measures final requirement satisfaction, reference-relative diagram quality, per-turn satisfaction, and forgetting (§3.3; Figure 3).

3. **PaperBanana-Interact:** A multi-agent refinement pipeline with:

   - a summarizer that compresses prior images and requests into textual memory;
   - a critic checking current and prior requests, source faithfulness, and presentation;
   - a refiner and image visualizer operating in an internal loop until acceptance or an iteration limit (§4.3; Figure 4).

4. **Failure analysis and validation:** Experiments expose quality drift and 14–23% forgetting among tested baselines, with ablations examining critic structure, history representation, and iteration budget (§5.1; Tables 1–3).

These results support the authors’ claim that the proposed system mitigates—not eliminates—quality drift and forgetting under this benchmark. They do not establish performance with sustained live-user interaction, independent evaluators, open or cheaper backbones, or scientific correctness beyond the supplied judging procedures.

## Limitations and validity risks

- **Author-stated risk:** Generated diagrams can contain plausible-looking factual errors; the authors require human oversight and describe intended disclosure and licensing safeguards (Appendix A).

- **Author-stated computational limitation:** Most main-table values come from a single run. Only one principal setting was repeated five times (Appendix D).

- **Author-stated residual failure:** Forgetting remains approximately 10–13% for PaperBanana-Interact (§5.1; Tables 1–2).

- **Author-stated resource cost:** Each refinement turn reportedly consumes 159.4k/11.2k Gemini input/output tokens and 7.1k/5.0k NanoBananaPro input/output tokens per diagram; 22% of examples reach the ten-iteration cap (Appendix D).

- **Analyst inference—simulator validity:** Requirements are predefined and the simulator receives evaluator rationales, so the benchmark may be more structured and informed than spontaneous human feedback (§3.3). The paper does not report a direct behavioral comparison between simulated and real users.

- **Analyst inference—evaluation dependence:** Gemini-3.1-Pro participates in requirement generation, user simulation, system components, and judging. Human agreement checks help, but both human evaluations were performed by paper authors and cover limited subsets (Appendices B, F.3).

- **Analyst inference—narrow user sample:** The formative study contains 14 internally recruited participants from one organization, limiting population-level generalization (Appendix C).

- **Analyst inference—partial ablation coverage:** Table 3 tests major components, but the supplied text does not isolate the summarizer’s quality from the rest of the system or compare against equally compute-intensive alternative refiners.

## Open research questions

1. **Does the simulator predict real-user behavior?**  
   This remains open because the benchmark uses hidden, ordered requirements and evaluator rationales. A next experiment could compare simulated and live-user trajectories on the same initial diagrams, measuring feedback diversity, satisfaction, and failure discovery.

2. **Can similar gains be achieved with much less computation?**  
   The internal loop dominates cost, while 22% of cases exhaust its budget. An adaptive stopping or critic-routing experiment could plot quality and forgetting against tokens, latency, and iteration count.

3. **How well does refinement preserve scientific truth under expert review?**  
   The authors acknowledge visual hallucination risk, but the principal metrics use model judging and reference-relative quality. Domain experts could independently annotate factual errors before and after every turn.

4. **Will the method remain stable over longer conversations?**  
   Evaluation stops after at most five turns, leaving long-horizon memory behavior unresolved. A 10–20-turn stress test could track requirement retention, quality drift, and textual-memory corruption.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: high** for the reported design and experiments because the complete retrieved text includes methods, tables, appendices, prompts, and several validation checks.

Material uncertainties remain: confirmed public availability and licensing of the benchmark/code; wall-clock latency and monetary cost; detailed benchmark domain composition; confidence intervals for most system comparisons; independent non-author human evaluation; simulator-to-human validity; and performance beyond five turns or with alternative backbones.
