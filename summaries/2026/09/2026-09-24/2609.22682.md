# Self-Organizing Agent Teams Learn to Reason Together

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-24.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.22682) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22682)


## One-sentence takeaway
Learned, frozen conversation strategies improve fixed model teams beyond compute-matched individual reasoning on average, but gains depend on selecting correct solutions from the team’s candidate pool (Tables 1–2).

## Problem and motivation
When no agent has a complete solution, selecting independent answers or assigning predefined subtasks may miss opportunities to combine partial reasoning. The authors investigate whether teams can learn reusable ways to organize roles, criticism, repair, and synthesis without specifying each new problem’s decomposition (§1).

## Approach and contributions
- **Learned conversational organization:** SAT represents teamwork through phases specifying participants, rounds, information flow, shared instructions, and persistent roles (§2.1).
- **Offline strategy learning and transfer:** A designated member reflects on collaboration transcripts and outcomes, proposes mutations, and tests successful mutations on other training problems. A source-dependence audit screens problem-specific content; coverage-greedy selection freezes ten strategies per team. Separate banks use 15 AIME-2024 problems and 25 GPQA Diamond problems (§2.2; Appendix A).
- **Generation versus selection:** At evaluation, every strategy generates a reasoning certificate; a team member audits the pool and selects an answer. Comparing final accuracy, candidate coverage, and an oracle over independent member answers distinguishes generating correct candidates from recognizing them (§§2.2–4).
- **Demonstrability analysis:** An external model panel’s ability to distinguish correct from incorrect certificates is associated with collaboration gains across benchmarks (§5).

These results support gains over the tested independent outputs and controls. Exceeding routing coverage establishes that correct answers occur beyond those observed independent answers; it does not establish that individual models could never generate them. The transcripts illustrate a plausible repair mechanism, while the demonstrability result establishes association rather than causation.

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated:** Correct candidate generation does not guarantee correct selection, especially for knowledge and logic. Demonstrability is measured post hoc and was not optimized or experimentally manipulated (§7).
- **Analyst inference:** Generalization is established for two fixed rosters and the listed benchmarks. The authors deliberately choose models with performance headroom; transfer to stronger or changed rosters remains untested (§3; pp. 3–4).
- **Analyst inference:** Tables report seed means without uncertainty intervals or significance tests for accuracy differences, leaving small advantages—particularly over knowledge-and-logic linearization—uncertain (Tables 1–2).
- **Analyst inference:** The initialization comparison changes strategy diversity and selection as well as learned organization. It therefore does not isolate the contribution of reflection alone; component ablations are not reported (Appendix B).
- **Analyst inference:** Approximate compute matching is asserted without detailed token, latency, monetary-cost, or offline-search accounting, limiting efficiency conclusions (§3; Appendix D).

## Open research questions
- **Can improved demonstrability cause higher accuracy?** The current evidence is correlational. Randomize certificate formats or audit roles while holding candidate generation and inference budgets fixed, then measure selection accuracy.
- **Which learned components produce the gains?** Existing controls do not isolate roles, phase structure, reflection, and bank diversity. Ablate each component under matched candidate counts and selection rules.
- **Do strategies survive roster changes?** Both banks retain their training rosters. Replace individual members or use stronger models, comparing unchanged banks against relearned banks.
- **How efficiently can SAT close its selection gap?** Correct candidates frequently remain unselected. Compare alternative judges and verification procedures on identical frozen pools, reporting accuracy and cost.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: high** in the reported method and descriptive comparisons; causal and efficiency conclusions remain less secure.

Material information unavailable in the supplied text includes detailed compute accounting, accuracy uncertainty estimates, complete model/API and decoding configurations, full split identifiers, and a code or machine-readable artifact release location.
