# Hugging Face Daily Papers — 2026-09-25

## Executive summary

- **Physical video consistency has promising benchmark evidence, but transfer remains unresolved.** WROP’s fine-tuned continuation model leads its interface group on human preference; training and examination share scene generators, and the untuned base-model control is missing (`2609.28654`).
- **Video improvements come from distinct interventions.** WanPE improves prompt planning, AV-GRPO targets audiovisual alignment, and ViRDM reduces final-stage post-training cost while improving motion scores. Their metrics measure different properties and do not support a common ranking (`2609.30221`, `2609.29816`, `2609.28923`).
- **Agent training increasingly incorporates execution feedback and runtime support.** AEWM, Qwen-Planner-Agent, IterSynth, Rufus-Air, and PUBG Ally report useful gains, but component attribution, retry budgets, and deployment conditions matter (`2609.28416`, `2609.29892`, `2609.29444`, `2609.29421`, `2609.29837`).
- **Reusable manipulation strategies show substantial simulation results.** Coding agents synthesize frozen policies, WAA packages visual procedures and motion checks, and DeltaWAM models compact visual changes. These are different control approaches; physical-robot evidence remains limited (`2609.30233`, `2609.29964`, `2609.28811`).
- **Recoverable information is not necessarily usable computation.** Mixed embeddings preserve some next-token information without matching independent decoding; compact SAE groups predict POS without establishing category-exclusive or causal grammatical features (`2609.29845`, `2609.29362`).
- **Discovery evaluations distinguish verified outputs from reliable discovery.** Mathematical interestingness improves within selected, verified-or-repaired cohorts; ExplorationBench exposes large variation across exploration trajectories and failures despite correctly reported rules (`2609.28603`, `2609.30199`).
- **Evaluation quality is itself a central result.** RGB-D relabeling changes measured segmentation performance, OmniEcho separates approaching a sound source from successful termination, and Jev’s strong failure ranking coexists with miscalibration and defective reference labels (`2609.29028`, `2609.23407`, `2609.29429`).
- **Efficiency and security claims require precise boundaries.** NSC optimizes an architecture proxy, perceptual RDO improves selected compression metrics at specified PSNR costs, and AgentKernel supplies an enforcement architecture without measured security gains (`2609.23087`, `2609.30077`, `2609.29647`).

## Main results worth noting

The findings below retain their individual comparators and evaluation settings; none supplies a universal “best paper.”

- **Physical continuation with uncertainty reported — `2609.28654`.** PWM-WROP’s **1679.5 Elo [1603.5, 1781.5]** exceeds the next-highest continuation system, Grok Imagine, at **1457.0 [1362.7, 1555.1]**. The human study has **361 distinct-model pairwise judgments**, **20 raters**, and **14 systems**. Reference-to-video systems have higher overall point estimates with overlapping intervals and different interfaces. This supports WROP benchmark performance, not a causal training effect or unseen-generator transfer.

- **Fixed-generator prompt benefits — `2609.30221`.** In the Wan3.0 SC-GRPO ablation, WanPE-397B’s video preference **S improves 42.70→49.69**. S counts wins plus half of “both good” judgments, with no credit for “both poor.” This is more informative about the training stage than whole-system commercial comparisons, but lacks statistical uncertainty and constraint-level rendered verification.

- **Final-stage video-training savings — `2609.28923`.** On the same eight-A100 setup, excluding shared initialization, ViRDM uses **16 GPU-hours and 48.3 GB/GPU**, versus Self Forcing DMD’s **176 GPU-hours and 77.1 GB/GPU**. Separately, four-step Wan2.1-1.3B at **832×480** achieves VBench Total **84.87 versus Causal Forcing’s 84.51**. The cost and quality comparisons have different baselines. Motion regularization changes Total excluding Dynamic Degree only **85.77→85.79**, locating much of the aggregate gain in motion.

- **Paired agent improvements with limited attribution — `2609.28416`, `2609.29892`, `2609.29421`.** AEWM-RFT improves Doc2Repo mean test pass rate **46.1%→48.6%** over matched Self-RFT for separately trained 35B-A3B agents, while average turns increase **76.3→89.8**. Qwen’s fixed 27B planner improves MobilePA-Bench Overall **71.90%→77.05%** with its combined harness. Rufus-Air improves BrowseComp pass@1 **22.7%→37.1%** over GLM-4.5-Air under matched author-run evaluation. These support their respective systems, without establishing equal total cost or isolating every component.

- **Manipulation performance and timing must remain separate — `2609.28811`.** DeltaWAM with streaming memory improves ten-task RoboTwin success to **85.4% clean / 83.9% randomized**, versus Fast-WAM **81.3% / 75.8%**, using **ten denoising steps** and **100 episodes/task/setting**. The **121.02→76.76 ms** latency comparison instead uses **single-step inference on the initial five tasks**, where clean/randomized success is **71.6%/69.6%** versus **72.8%/69.0%**. The headline success and latency figures are not one operating point.

- **Approaching a goal is not successful termination — `2609.23407`.** Across **900 OmniEchoBench-Nav episodes**, **52.89%** enter the **1 m** goal region, while **16.22%** terminate successfully; distance-estimation MAE is **2.20 m**. This diagnoses an execution gap but does not isolate stopping errors from batched actions or imperfect distance estimates.

- **Discovery gains are conditional on selection — `2609.28603`, `2609.30199`.** Mathematical training raises mean conditional interestingness **1.76→7.58** over the base model on **160 proven statements per model**, with constrained repairs allowed. In ExplorationBench, Claude Opus 5’s selected AlienCode trajectory improves **3.8%→87.6%** from M0 to M4; Best@3 selects among three complete trajectories, and each accuracy averages three answers over 70 tasks. Neither finding estimates reliable useful discovery per fixed total budget.

- **Detector ranking and calibration diverge — `2609.29429`.** Jev’s generic-question median AUROC is **0.886 [0.821, 0.952]** over **31 canonical benchmarks**, beating the better TF-IDF/length baseline on **25/31**. Yet median ECE is **0.168**, versus **0.074** under the finite-sample perfect-calibration null, and **24/31** exceed that null’s 95th percentile. Deployment probabilities require more evidence than ranking performance.

- **Compression gains are tied to the optimized metric — `2609.30077`.** VTM 23.8 all-intra, **8-bit 4:2:0**, QPs **22/27/32/37**, yields block-estimator target-metric BD-rates of **−23.6/−16.8/−15.5/−17.3/−19.1%** on Kodak and **−36.7/−16.9/−14.9/−14.2/−16.0%** on 41 CLIC images, ordered SSIM/MS-SSIM/LPIPS/DISTS/WD. All are relative to SSE-RDO and interpolated to PerceptQPA Y-PSNR costs of **+8.1%/+9.7%** respectively. Cross-metric conflicts and absent human evaluation prevent a general perceptual-quality claim.

## Open research directions

These are editorial experiment proposals grounded in the supplied papers’ explicit limitations, not claims that no related work exists.

1. **Separate physical learning from familiarity with scene templates.** For `2609.28654`, compare untuned and fine-tuned checkpoints under identical inference on entirely held-out generators and independently constructed scenes. Add human-validated semantic checks for occlusion, collision, and solidity; report each family separately.

2. **Measure rendered constraint fidelity independently of training rewards.** For `2609.30221` and `2609.29816`, annotate requested actions, speaker bindings, event order, and synchronization in final videos. Cross prompt enhancement with generator post-training under fixed budgets, using judges excluded from reward construction. For `2609.28923`, extend independent motion judgments to longer rollouts and multiple seeds.

3. **Establish agent benefits at equal total resources.** For `2609.28416`, `2609.29892`, `2609.29444`, and `2609.29421`, match tokens, tool calls, retries, and training compute. Independently vary editing, harness support, role separation, and selected stage order; evaluate on an untouched common suite. This would distinguish mechanism benefits from additional computation and checkpoint selection.

4. **Connect internal representation findings to functional behavior.** For `2609.29362`, ablate POS-selected and matched random latent groups on lexical-disjoint grammatical tasks. For `2609.29845`, compare separated decoding with independent batching at matched accuracy, measuring latency and memory. These address different mechanisms, so successful probing or rank preservation should not substitute for either functional test.

5. **Stress manipulation policies beyond their development conditions.** For `2609.30233` and `2609.29964`, freeze programs or skills and test unseen geometries, state noise, calibration error, and altered physics before hardware deployment. For `2609.28811`, repeat physical trials across seeds/platforms and sweep memory refresh intervals, reporting clean and randomized performance alongside end-to-end latency.

6. **Diagnose sensor and annotation effects with controlled interventions.** For `2609.23407`, independently vary proximity calibration and replanning frequency on the same navigation episodes. For `2609.29028`, fix independently audited, scene-disjoint test labels while varying training-label refinement, and compare SPF with capacity-matched fusion under depth corruption and misalignment.

7. **Measure useful discovery per fixed budget.** For `2609.28603`, report proposal counts, repair rates, proving cost, and prospective downstream theorem reuse against controls matched on proof cost and description length. For `2609.30199`, equally replicate autonomous and replay exploration, compare endpoint distributions rather than selected maxima, and test retention under noisy feedback.

8. **Validate protection and monitoring against distinct failure criteria.** For `2609.29647`, measure unauthorized effects, provenance under-labeling, legitimate task completion, and latency under pillar ablations and concurrent tool stress. For `2609.29429`, freeze questions and thresholds before testing human-labeled interactions under generator and prevalence shifts. These are separate enforcement and detection experiments, not an assumed integrated defense.

9. **Audit efficiency proxies against the outcomes they approximate.** For `2609.23087`, train repeated architectures at exactly matched resource budgets, including equal-score layer permutations. For `2609.30077`, compare human preferences at matched bitrate and test lower-rate/inter-frame settings. Each experiment checks whether its specific surrogate supports the intended downstream benefit.

10. **Explain offline-to-live teammate gaps.** For `2609.29837`, prospectively freeze the evaluator and compare targeted controller, command-completion, and speech–action changes in latency-matched blinded games. Record unfulfilled commands, recovery, player preference, and benign refusals, rather than relying on offline capability scores alone.

## Coverage and uncertainty

- **Coverage:** All **22 supplied current papers** were analyzed and included above. The historical input contains **50 candidates**; this digest retains **eight cross-day edges involving eight historical papers**, based on visible evidence at both endpoints. The selection is sparse, not an exhaustive relationship map.
- **Downstream stages:** Taxonomy, same-day connections, and historical connections were all available. Their assignments and edges were treated as fallible aids; none supplies independent evidence for a paper’s result.
- **Collection failures:** Hugging Face Markdown retrieval failed for **17 papers**. Fifteen responses lacked apparent full paper text: `2609.28654`, `2609.29845`, `2609.30221`, `2609.23407`, `2609.28416`, `2609.29892`, `2609.29444`, `2609.29421`, `2609.28603`, `2609.29837`, `2609.30199`, `2609.29964`, `2609.29429`, `2609.28811`, and `2609.30077`. Responses for `2609.29362` and `2609.23087` had an unexpected HTML content type. Supplied analyses were nevertheless present for all 22; these are retrieval failures, not absent analyses.
- **Partial current inputs:** The analyses explicitly identify truncated source material for AEWM (`2609.28416`), AgentKernel (`2609.29647`), PUBG Ally (`2609.29837`), and Jev (`2609.29429`). AgenticGenPlan (`2609.30233`) lacks numerical table bodies. AV-GRPO (`2609.29816`) lacks numerical ablation-figure values; other analyses note missing figures, protocol details, or inconsistent metric reporting.
- **Partial historical inputs:** Historical excerpts are service-truncated cached analyses. Only visible endpoint material supports the retained edges; statements that an original analysis once had complete text do not restore omitted evidence. No historical input is marked `listing_summary`.
- **Confidence limits:** Most comparisons lack repeated-training uncertainty. Especially tentative conclusions include near-tied rankings, causal explanations for bundled systems, real-world transfer from simulation, detector calibration under deployment shift, mathematical usefulness beyond operational metrics, and security effectiveness from architectural arguments. Proposed cross-day transfers remain untested.
- **Source boundary:** No browsing or outside facts were used. Release claims were not independently verified, and service observation dates were not interpreted as publication chronology, priority, citation, or influence.
