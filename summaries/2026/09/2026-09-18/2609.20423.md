# WeVisDoc: From Coverage to Capability for Robust End-to-End Document Parsing

> [← Daily summary](../2026-09-18.md) · [Relationship graph](relationships.md) · [Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/papers/2609.20423) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20423)


## One-sentence takeaway
WeVisDoc combines broad document supervision with error-guided refinement, leading the compared end-to-end parsers on both benchmarks while leaving the contribution of individual refinement components unresolved (Tables 2–4).

## Problem and motivation
Document parsing must recover text, tables, formulas, and reading order across varied layouts and acquisition conditions. The authors identify two distinct gaps: missing training coverage and insufficient exposure to patterns already present. Simply adding data—or prioritizing every high-error example—cannot distinguish these from annotation errors, unreadable inputs, or decoding failures (§1).

## Approach and contributions
- **Broad coverage construction:** Approximately 40 million records combine collected documents, page/region/component supervision, executable HTML/CSS synthesis, and validated appearance degradations. Derived views remain linked to source content to control duplication (§3.2).
- **Audited capability diagnosis:** A held-out probe measures residual errors within frozen clusters combining SigLIP2 visual embeddings and structural metadata. Coverage audits distinguish requests for new data from requests for more exposure; separate mining supplies verified hard examples (§§3.3.1–3.3.3).
- **Token-budgeted refinement:** An approximately 5-million-record pool combines targeted additions, replay, and hard examples. Bounded residual-based allocation specifies supervised-token shares and corrects record sampling for target length (§3.3.4).
- **Parser instantiations:** Qwen3-VL-Instruct backbones are specialized at 2B and 4B scales, producing Markdown, HTML tables, and LaTeX. Stage II freezes the visual encoder and updates the language model. The contribution is the integrated data procedure rather than a new backbone (§3.4.2).

WeVisDoc scores are means over three inference runs. Overall averages text accuracy, FormulaCDM, and TableTEDS; it excludes ROEdit (§4.1). The evidence supports improvement from the complete refinement stage, especially on degraded pages. It does not isolate residual allocation, establish equal-total-training-budget superiority, or demonstrate leadership across every model category. Baselines mix author-run evaluations with previously reported results (§§4.1–4.3).

## Limitations and validity risks
- **Author-stated:** Stage II combines additional optimization, allocation, hard examples, additions, and replay; component effects remain unidentified (§4.3).
- **Author-stated:** Handwriting, historical scans, severely damaged documents, broader acquisition conditions, languages, and domains remain insufficiently measured. Synthetic degradation fidelity limits generalization (§5).
- **Author-stated:** Diagnosis depends on the probe, representation, clustering, and evaluator, and adds computation (§5).
- **Analyst inference:** Three inference runs without reported uncertainty or independent training replications do not establish training stability or statistical significance.
- **Analyst inference:** Qualitative examples illustrate possible corrections rather than typical outcomes: selection favors high Stage II scores and low Stage I scores. Appendix B.1 explicitly disclaims prevalence estimation.

## Open research questions
1. **How much does residual allocation itself contribute?** The bundled comparison cannot answer this (§4.3). Compare natural versus residual allocation using identical refinement pools, token budgets, and trainable modules.
2. **How sensitive is diagnosis to probe and clustering choices?** The authors acknowledge these dependencies (§5). Vary probe composition, cluster granularity, and representation while measuring allocation stability and downstream scores.
3. **Do gains transfer to broader authentic degradation?** Severe damage and historical or handwritten material remain undermeasured (§5). Evaluate both stages on independently collected, audited acquisition data with language and degradation breakdowns.

## Confidence and missing information
**Confidence: medium.** The text supports the reported benchmark comparisons and overall procedure, but causal attribution and reproducibility are limited.

Missing details include numerical stage token budgets; probe size and composition; cluster count and key allocation thresholds/hyperparameters; exact refinement-mixture shares; the combined record-error formula; full preprocessing and decoding settings; training cost, latency, uncertainty estimates, and training replications. Model and repository links are supplied, but corpus availability and release completeness are not established.
